# WO-9 — TRACE THE ADDITIVITY ASSUMPTION FORWARD FROM THE FISHER SYNTHESIS
Opened 2026-09-18. CC0.

---

## FRAME — HOW THIS IS TO BE HELD

```
These are OPEN QUESTIONS. Areas to explore.
Not a critique of statistics. Not a position about the field.
Everything below is a question to be instrumented, not a charge.
```

Anyone picking this up who converts it into an argument against statistical
genetics has mis-read the work order. The tools are not in dispute. Their
inherited assumptions are the object.

## WHY THIS CASE IS SHARPER THAN ADJACENT ONES

```
Elsewhere: a commitment skews FINDINGS.
Here:      it shapes the TOOL.
```

Correlation, regression to the mean, chi-squared and much of the
analysis-of-variance framework were developed for questions about heritable
differences between populations, then handed to fields with no connection to
those questions — carrying design choices about what counts as a distribution
to be described, what an outlier is, and what between-group variance means.

## THE HISTORICAL STRUCTURE (established at search-result level; primaries not read)

```
PRE-FORMALIZATION FORK
  Naudin 1862  a hybrid consists of two specific essences separated in
               pollen and ovule -> segregation, 3-4 years before Mendel,
               in ESSENCE language
  Knight       first to OBSERVE segregation
  Mendel 1865  names Kolreuter, Gartner, Herbert, Lecoq, Wichura;
               contributes the FORM — seven pairs of DISCRETE traits
               in a plant where they behave discretely

  DERIVED: the categories were SELECTED, not found. What survived
  formalization is what made COUNTING work. Naudin's essences don't
  count. The displaced blending model was CONTINUOUS. That is the fork.

THE THIRTY-YEAR CONFLICT
  Mendelians (particulate) vs biometricians (continuous selection).
  Pearson and Weldon rejected genetics, not recognising the two
  arguments were not mutually exclusive.
  -> the statistical machinery and the discrete-unit machinery
     inherited OPPOSITE HALVES of the same fork.

THE MERGE
  Fisher reconciles them by modelling genes as gas molecules governed
  by thermodynamics, letting continuous variation, natural selection
  and Mendelian inheritance coexist.
  That synthesis is what modern statistics runs on.
```

**METHODOLOGICAL RESULT, worth more than the history.** A citation trace was
run and returned a FALSE NEGATIVE: eugenics predates Mendel's rediscovery by
seventeen years, and Galton had to be told about Mendel's work in 1900. The
precondition carried forward structurally, through the fork inherited by both
camps — not through citation. **Citation tracing cannot detect a
precondition carried by a shared structural inheritance.** That generalises
well beyond this case.

## THE LIVE QUESTION

```
In the merging, was information left out?
```

**CANDIDATES — marked as constructed, traceable rather than established:**
```
1 ADDITIVITY IS WHAT THE GAS-MOLECULE DEVICE BUYS.
  Effects sum; variance partitions into components.
  Anything NON-ADDITIVE does not survive as a modelled object:
    - interaction between loci
    - context-dependence
    - what a gene does differently depending on what surrounds it
  These remain representable only as NUISANCE.

2 THE EXCLUDED SHAPE IS THE ONE THAT RETURNED AS EPIGENETICS —
  heritable effects contingent on conditions — i.e. precisely what the
  additive frame was constructed to exclude.

3 BLENDING WAS WRONG ABOUT MECHANISM but was tracking CONTINUITY AS A
  PRIMITIVE. After the merge, continuity has to be rebuilt as a sum of
  discrete units. That rebuild is not free and its cost is unexamined.
```

**LOAD-BEARING LINEAGE CLAIM — verifiable or breakable with sources:**
```
variance partitioning is the direct descendant of this merge,
and heritability estimates inherit the additivity assumption from it.
```
If that claim is false, WO-9 collapses and the collapse is cheap to
establish. Test it first.

## RUNNABLE

```
R1  STATED-VS-INVISIBLE AUDIT
    Sample current papers reporting heritability or variance
    decomposition across several fields (behaviour genetics,
    agriculture, medicine, education).
    CODE EACH: is additivity
       (a) stated as an assumption
       (b) stated and tested
       (c) present and unstated
       (d) explicitly relaxed (variance components for interaction)
    OUTPUT: the distribution, by field and by decade.
    PREDICTION (PROPOSED): (c) rises as distance from the source
    discipline rises. If wrong, that is the finding.

R2  RE-ENTRY AUDIT
    Code the non-additive phenomena excluded at the merge against what
    later returned under other names — epigenetics, gene-environment
    interaction, plasticity, norm of reaction.
    QUESTION: did the re-entry have to FIGHT the frame?
    EVIDENCE TO LOOK FOR: rejection language, "nuisance" or "noise"
    framing in early papers, whether the phenomenon had to be
    re-expressed as a variance component to be publishable.

R3  PRIOR-ART CHECK — DO THIS BEFORE R1 OR R2
    Has any history-of-statistics or philosophy-of-biology work already
    asked the what-was-dropped question AT THE MERGE SPECIFICALLY?
    NOT SEARCHED. Plenty exists on the Mendelian/biometrician
    controversy; the question here is narrower — not who won, but what
    neither side carried forward.
    If it exists, WO-9 becomes a pointer instead of a programme,
    which is a good outcome.

R4  THE METHODOLOGICAL RESULT, GENERALISED
    Independent of the genetics: test whether citation tracing detects
    preconditions carried by shared structural inheritance, using this
    case as the known-answer instance.
    If citation tracing reliably misses this class, that is a finding
    about a standard method in history of science, and it is portable.
```
