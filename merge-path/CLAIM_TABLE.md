# CLAIM_TABLE — merge-path

Claims are properties of the machinery and of the egress-blocked survey,
not conclusions about any registrar's real record shape (no spec was
fetched). Every number is computed by `selftest.py`; the report render is
in `samples/`. `MRG_*` to avoid collision with `MF_*` (measurement-fork),
`MP_*` (model-provenance / membership-probe).

## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

A refuted claim is updated forward with a new id; the old id keeps its text
and gains an UPDATE paragraph. `WORK_ORDER.md` is not edited. No registrar
statement is promoted from UNVERIFIED without a fetched spec; the checks in
`selftest.py` are not loosened to keep a claim.
