rural -- policy / agricultural statistics / census
read off: density

  The label is read off population density. Everything else it is taken to indicate rides along unmeasured.

COMPONENTS
  * density            population density
      unit: people per unit area
    ownership_dist     ownership distribution
      unit: independent operators per 1000 acres
    functional_div     functional diversity
      unit: distinct enterprise types in region
    self_support       self-supporting capacity
      unit: share of local demand met locally

DIVERGENCE CASES
  [readings present, fewer than two quantified components] industrial-consolidation
      500 to 6000 acres held by one or two companies, roughly 300 people
      living there, roughly 50 employed. Density stays low so the label
      holds; ownership distribution and functional diversity have
      collapsed. The same word covers both configurations.

  [named only] no-alternate-check
      Independent operators across a fence line are a distributed sensor
      array: different practices, different failure timings, so soil and
      water degradation appears as divergence between neighbours before
      it appears in any assay. Single ownership removes the comparison
      case, so the depletion signal has no baseline to appear against.
      Depletion rate rises while the category is unchanged.

  [named only] employment-concentration
      One packing facility closure zeroes regional employment at once
      rather than degrading gracefully. Diversified base and
      single-employer base score identically on the label.

  [named only] service-withdrawal
      Hospitals and clinics exit; no other work is affordable in the
      area. Service presence is not a component the label carries, so
      withdrawal produces no movement in the statistic.
