COMPONENTS
  nested_containment nested containment [levels of enclosure (a is inside b is inside c)]
  organizing_abstraction organizing abstraction [categories held by an observer for sorting information, count]
  imposed_ordering imposed ordering with standing attached [positions ranked, with differential claim on decisions or resources]
  ordering_origin distance between where the ordering is established and where it is used [credentialing interval — time lag and scale gap from origin to point of use]
  cut_rate       cut-generation rate [new internal distinctions required per unit time to maintain the ordering]

DIVERGENCE CASES
  [unquantified] environment-ordered-dominance
      Dominance in some fish and reptile groups: traits favoured in that
      environment produce the dominance, and when the environment
      changes, which being is dominant changes to suit it. The ordering
      is read off the environment rather than held by anyone. Nested
      containment reads present, imposed ordering is absent, cut rate is
      zero. Same word covers this and a credentialed command structure.
  [unquantified] volunteer-fire-crew
      Roles assigned by who was available, necessary, and trained — most
      skilled for the environment. Training director and field-team
      director were sometimes the same person when that person could be
      on call. Ordering established at the point of use by conditions.
      ordering_origin near zero.
  [unquantified] credentialed-fire-service
      Same function, ordering now established off-site and instantiated
      on arrival. Hierarchies outside the instance are initiated inside
      it. ordering_origin large; cut rate has only gone up. Nested
      containment unchanged between this and the volunteer case — the
      label does not move while ordering_origin and cut_rate move to the
      opposite end.
  [unquantified] surgical-team-drift
      1900s surgical teams had little hierarchical ranking; 1960s-70s
      markedly different from present. Now surgical nurses hold certain
      credentials, support nurses hold other credentials, surgeons
      another, with more added over time. The transient in-theatre
      ordering looks unchanged; the credentialing structure it imports
      has grown continuously.
  [unquantified] naturalness-argument
      The claim 'hierarchy is natural' is supported by pointing at
      nested containment and at environment-ordered dominance, then
      applied to imposed orderings with standing. Both supports sit in
      components where cut_rate is zero. The application sits in the
      component where cut_rate is unbounded. No separate handle in
      English marks the switch.

OPEN
  - cut_rate and ordering_origin are proposed measurements with zero
    collected series. The fire service is where data plausibly
    exists — volunteer through present, credential requirements
    documented and dated.
  - The read-vs-imposed criterion must be fixed BEFORE any series is
    run. Sorting cases into 'not really hierarchy' after seeing the
    cut rate makes the finding true by construction.
  - tracked_by_label is a judgment call here as elsewhere.
    nested_containment is the reading because it is what is pointed
    at in naturalness arguments; imposed_ordering has a competing
    case if the domain is taken as organizational practice rather
    than the naturalness claim.
