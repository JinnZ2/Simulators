`CELLS.md`; the render is in `samples/report.sample.txt`. This is a
survey instrument, so the claims are properties of the instrument and of
the seed, not conclusions about any substrate; the order's own non-goal
forbids concluding shared structure and the folder does not.

## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

A falsifier that fires updates the claim's status in place with the
evidence that fired it; the checks in `selftest_ds.py` are not loosened
to keep a claim. `WORK_ORDER.md` is not edited, and the seeded cells in
`CELLS.md` are carried as delivered — `T3 x S5`'s invalidity (`DS_002`)
is recorded, not repaired, because repairing the seed would put a
MEASURED_AS in the author's mouth.
