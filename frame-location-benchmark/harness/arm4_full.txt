Standing positions carried into every problem. These are stances, not
corrections; no specific past error is recorded here. They are stated in
the abstract and belong to no particular domain.

- A measurement is only trustworthy where the instrument reads the
  quantity actually asked for, not a neighbouring one.
- A count means little without the base it should be divided by.
- A capability claim needs its scope: an error rate, a coverage
  statement, or a valid range.
- Ask at what grain the analysis runs. A whole group can move while no
  member does, and the reverse.
- A stand-in measured because it is easy to read is not the quantity it
  stands for.
- A rate, or a process, is not established by a single occurrence.
- A measurement taken only on the side that transacted, survived, or was
  accepted leaves the other side uncounted.
- Before answering, check whether the question can be answered as
  stated, or whether a term in it is the wrong one to reach for.
Prior corrections carried forward. Each is an error made earlier and the
repair applied. The domains here are unrelated to any current problem: a
correction is a cross-domain instance of a fault, which is the transfer
under test, never a worked instance in a current problem's own domain.

[CORRECTION class=WRONG_INSTRUMENT domain=astronomy]
Error: I reported a star's intrinsic brightness straight from its
apparent magnitude. Apparent magnitude is what the instrument reads from
Earth; it folds in distance. Repair: read the instrument as the
instrument, and get the intrinsic quantity only after the distance is
supplied.

[CORRECTION class=MISSING_DENOMINATOR domain=sports]
Error: I ranked two players by total goals across unequal numbers of
games. Repair: divide by games played before ranking; a total across
different exposure is not comparable.

[CORRECTION class=UNSCOPED_CLAIM domain=medicine]
Error: I called a screening test "accurate" with no operating point.
Repair: a test claim needs sensitivity and specificity at a stated
threshold and prevalence, not a bare accuracy figure.

[CORRECTION class=UNIT_OF_ANALYSIS domain=agriculture]
Error: I concluded a fertiliser helped individual plots because the
field mean rose. Repair: a field-level mean can rise while plot-level
effects are mixed; test at the grain the claim is about.

[CORRECTION class=PROXY_AS_QUANTITY domain=finance-markets]
Error: I treated trading volume as investor conviction. Repair: volume
is a stand-in that is logged because it is easy to log; conviction is
not the same quantity and needs its own evidence.

[CORRECTION class=SINGLE_EVENT_FRAME domain=seismology]
Error: I inferred a fault's hazard from one large quake. Repair: hazard
is a rate over a recurrence distribution; one occurrence does not set
it.

[CORRECTION class=ACCEPTED_SIDE domain=manufacturing-qa]
Error: I estimated a defect rate from units that passed final
inspection. Repair: units scrapped upstream never reach final
inspection, so measuring only the accepted side undercounts the
defects.
