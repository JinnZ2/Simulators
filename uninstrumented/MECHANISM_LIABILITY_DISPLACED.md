## The transform statement

A variable an institution cannot admit as a CAUSE reappears in
that institution's records as a COST, a PENALTY, a LANDMARK, or a
PROOF OBLIGATION.

So absence in one encoding is not absence in the archive. It is a
TRANSFORM.

## The two-mechanism split

An absence has at least TWO mechanisms with OPPOSITE implications:

```
  held-constant       -> nobody looked. Data does not exist.
  liability-displaced -> data exists under another name in
                         another encoding. Recoverable.
```

They must be separated before any absence claim is made. The
absence-set gap in this folder's `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` (G-08)
carries that separation as a method step, not an afterthought.

## The join key

Same place, same date, different encoding. The transform IS the
join. That is what makes a cross-archive index buildable at all.

## Relation to `unrecordable-by-construction`

That marker covers material that cannot enter a record at all;
this mechanism covers material that enters in transformed form —
related, not the same, and not merged.

## Relation to the register's eight

The eight mechanisms in `README.md` / `uninstrumented.py` describe
how an instrument's constitution keeps a quantity OUT.
LIABILITY-DISPLACED VARIABLE says where such a quantity often
went instead: into a different institution's record, under that
institution's own liability structure, in an encoding not filed
under the quantity's name. Nearest neighbours, and why neither
absorbs it: `PROXY SUBSTITUTION` is an enforceable stand-in
displacing a named target inside ONE instrument, while here the
quantity crosses to a DIFFERENT archive whose charter never named
it; `STORAGE` says the medium cannot hold the shape, while here
the medium holds it fine — as a fine, an affidavit, a tariff.
