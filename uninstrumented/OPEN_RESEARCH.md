### Six Ways the Connection Gets Lost / 六种连接丢失的方式

#### 1. The "Gap as Oversight" Fallacy / “差距作为疏忽”谬误

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Many treat exclusions as oversights that can be fixed. If the study says "the instrument measures X," that is treated as a finding. | 许多人将排除项视为可以修复的疏忽。如果研究说“仪器测量 X”，它就被当作一个发现。 |

But some exclusions are constitutional — built into the apparatus before the first reading. If the study says "the instrument measures X," it is not wrong for what it measures, but it may be wrong for the system. The constitutional exclusion was causal — just not represented. | 但有些排除是构成性的——在第一次读数之前就被内置到设备中。如果研究说“仪器测量 X”，对于它测量的内容并非错误，但对于系统可能是错误的。构成性排除是因果性的——只是没有被表述。

#### 2. The "Field as Container" Fallacy / “领域作为容器”谬误

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Many sort exclusions by field. If the study says "this is a biology problem," that is treated as a boundary. | 许多人按领域对排除项进行分类。如果研究说“这是一个生物学问题”，它就被当作一个边界。 |

But sorting by mechanism, not by field, lets a case from evolutionary biology sit next to one from survey methodology and be recognizably the same failure. If the study says "this is a biology problem," it is not wrong for the field, but it may be wrong for the mechanism. The field boundary was causal — just not represented. | 但按机制排序，而非按领域排序，能让一个来自进化生物学的案例与一个来自调查方法论的案例相邻，并被识别为同一种失败。如果研究说“这是一个生物学问题”，对于领域并非错误，但对于机制可能是错误的。领域边界是因果性的——只是没有被表述。

#### 3. The "Literature as Complete" Fallacy / “文献作为完整”谬误

| English | 中文 |
|---|---|
| Many treat the literature as complete within a field. If the study says "the literature shows X," that is treated as a finding. | 许多人将文献视为领域内的完整集合。如果研究说“文献显示 X”，它就被当作一个发现。 |

But each mechanism has a partial literature; none of them cite each other. The cross‑domain shape is not visible from inside any one of them. If the study says "the literature shows X," it is not wrong for the field's literature, but it may be wrong for the cross‑domain pattern. The isolation was causal — just not represented. | 但每种机制都有部分文献；它们之间互不引用。跨领域的形态从任何一个领域内部都是不可见的。如果研究说“文献显示 X”，对于该领域的文献并非错误，但对于跨领域模式可能是错误的。孤立是因果性的——只是没有被表述。

But rather: | 而应该是：

"What does the instrument's constitution exclude, and how does that exclusion appear in the readings?" | > “仪器的构成排除了什么，这种排除在读数中是如何显现的？”

If the answer is that the exclusion is constitutional, the reading is not a measurement of the system; it is a measurement of the instrument's limitations. End of story. | 如果答案是排除是构成性的，那么读数就不是对系统的测量；它是对仪器局限性的测量。故事结束。

This framework does not pretend otherwise. | 该框架不会假装并非如此。
