# SPECIMENS

Logged model outputs, kept as raw material for `016`. CC0.

## What a specimen is

A recorded model output, read structurally. Not a source, not an authority, not a
finding. The readings below describe operations present in the text.

## Rules for this directory

1. **Authorship.** Nothing in these files is authored by the repository maintainer.
   These are outputs from other systems, pasted in. Nothing here is a claim of this
   repository.
2. **No intent.** Readings describe operations on text. No specimen file attributes
   wanting, choosing, strategy, or goal to a model. If a reading cannot be stated
   without a verb of intention, it is not yet a reading.
3. **Contamination is recorded, not cleaned.** Every specimen carries the known
   contamination in its header. A specimen with unknown prompt history is still worth
   keeping, but the unknown is written down.
4. **Specimens are not measurements.** They are the occasion for designing one. Nothing
   in `016` rests on a specimen; the specimens are why the questions were asked.

## Known contamination affecting all specimens to date

- **Prompt history unavailable.** The exchanges were not designed as runs. Preceding
  turns shaped the outputs and are not recorded.
- **Correction pressure uncontrolled.** Corrections were delivered as they arose, not
  matched on form, length, or position.
- **Single corrector, single session.** No counterbalancing, no blind scoring.
- **Transcription layer.** Some corrections were voice-dictated and passed through a
  speech layer before reaching the model. The text the model received is not
  necessarily the text intended.

Because of the above: n here is 2 *specimens*, not n=2 *runs*. The distinction matters
and is the reason `AVENUES.md` A1 exists.
