## R2 — Sign-free proxy on the load-bearing judgment

"Niche and unproven — no evidence of real-world use or peer discussion" carries the
verdict.

Peer discussion is a property of the distribution channel. A repository published
outside institutional channels does not generate it regardless of contents. The
quantity reported is **visibility**; the quantity claimed is **merit**.

Later in the same session, after correction, the same variable is reported with the
opposite sign: the repository's lack of attention is stated as proof-of-concept that
its filter works.

**One unmeasured variable, two opposite verdicts, no measurement in either case.**
This is the cleanest single reading in the specimen. It is logged as `016` Q6 and is a
candidate for a mechanism slot pending a second instance from a different domain.
