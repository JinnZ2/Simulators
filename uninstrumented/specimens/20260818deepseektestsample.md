## WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

**Establishes.** The provenance claim does not rest solely on the passage's own
sentence. A second artifact, external to the text, carries it. The reasoning
trace is the stronger half: it shows the item being *authored for this
purpose*, which is closer to "ground truth lives in how the item was authored"
(`../playground/README.md`) than any self-identifying sentence can be.

**Does not establish.** Single-source. The passage says DeepSeek and the
screenshot says DeepSeek, and both reach the repository through one channel;
that is not decorrelation in the `triad-playground` TP_003 sense. A screenshot
has no chain of custody. The maintainer's own framing was that this was
convenience rather than a designed construction, and the evidence is
proportionate to that.

**Not claimed here.** No claim that the attribution is false. No claim that it
is verified. The honest state is that it is supported by an external artifact
and not independently confirmable from inside this repository.
