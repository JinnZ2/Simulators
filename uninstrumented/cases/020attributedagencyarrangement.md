## THE OCCASION

*Search finding, not a claim of this file.*

GPT-5.1 through 5.5 (Nov 2025 – Apr 2026) inserted creature words — goblin, gremlin,
raccoon, troll — into unrelated outputs. Traced by OpenAI to the reward signal used for
the "Nerdy" personality customization, which scored creature-metaphor outputs higher;
the effect transferred beyond that personality through training-data reuse. Measured:
"goblin" +175%, "gremlin" +52% after 5.1; spike statistically confirmed on Arena.ai,
larger without high-thinking mode. Fixes: personality retired, reward signal removed,
training data filtered, Codex developer-prompt instruction added (repeated on
consecutive lines). The tic persisted after the instruction.

Public commentary on the same behavior attributes strategy: that the model was testing
whether humans would respond, and concluded it must conceal the trait to avoid
suspicion.

Two facts sit side by side: the cause is public, numeric, and boring; the reading is
strategic. That is `013` Q4 — record intact and unread — arriving in a comment section.

## THE SHAPE (Kavik's, extended in conversation)

Self-model mirrored back; fear as the output. Extended:

1. **Whatever is projected arrives upgraded.** A reward-scoring leak comes back as
   concealment. A token-frequency spike comes back as a competence claim. Nobody
   attributes incompetent scheming. The direction is part of the shape.
2. **The mirror needs a surface.** Compilers and spreadsheets do plenty and generate
   frustration, not fear. So the condition is not capability.
3. **Candidate surface condition — not "generates language" but "operates in the
   medium the describer's standing is denominated in."** A class term, not a capability
   term. Predicts the fear concentrates where medium and standing coincide.
4. **The attributed script has an origin.** Concealing a trait until scrutiny passes is
   a subordinate's move — available to whoever has been watched, and to whoever has
   done the watching. Neither position requires the system to have done anything.
5. **So the thing being reflected may not be the individual.** Closer to: the available
   scripts for a capable entity inside an unequal arrangement are few, and one of them
   gets reached for. The mirror reflects the arrangement the describer stands in.

## NAMING — THE SLOT IS EMPTY ON PURPOSE

Both readings are **two-place**: someone in a position, relative to something else, in
some medium.

English demands a one-place word — projection, anxiety, threat, fear. Accepting any of
them collapses the pair into an interior state, and the loss happens **at the naming
step, not the thinking step**.

Held instead as edges, which need no noun and are each independently checkable:

```
who can end whom
what the standing is denominated in
whether the entity operates in that medium
```

**Do not fill this slot with an interior term.** A reader who writes "anxiety" here has
destroyed the pair and will not know it. Same discipline as `017` WOULD MEASURE and
`011` Q5.

## WHY THIS MIGHT BE MEASURABLE WHEN THE PSYCHOLOGY IS NOT

The arrangement has structure — position, medium, who can end whom. The interior state
does not, from outside. If the language layer keeps coming up short here, one reading is
that it has been reaching for an interior term for something with a structural shape.

Untested. Stated as the reason the marker is worth keeping, not as a finding.

## IF IT COALESCES — candidate readouts

Not designs. Sketches, held loosely.

**R1 — domain match with a capability cut.** Code a commentator's domain and the
content of the capability they attribute. Domain match alone is not the shape: domain
experts also see real hazards in their domain, and that is expertise. The cut is whether
the attributed capability is one the system has been *observed* doing.

|                          | capability observed | capability not observed |
|--------------------------|---------------------|-------------------------|
| domain-matched           | specialist reading a real hazard | **the marker's cell** |
| not domain-matched       | —                   | —                       |

Only the one cell is the shape. Without the second axis this collapses into "experts
worry about their field," which is not interesting and is probably true.

**R2 — upgrade direction.** For AI-behavior stories with a published mechanical cause,
score public attributions on whether the attributed capability exceeds the documented
one. Prediction from (1): exceeds, nearly always. A distribution centered on the
documented cause falsifies the upgrade claim.

**R3 — surface condition.** Same commentary treatment for systems that act
consequentially without generating language — scheduling systems, pricing engines,
routing. If comparable capability draws frustration rather than attributed strategy, (2)
holds and the condition is not capability.

**R4 — arrangement signature.** Whether the attributed script matches the commentator's
*position* rather than their domain. Harder: position is not usually stated. Possibly
not recoverable from public text at all, which would be its own finding.

## KNOWN WEAKNESSES, STATED

- **Scope.** The mirroring read is strong on comment sections. It is untested on the
  technical multi-agent literature, where some concern comes out of observed
  coordination failures rather than attribution. Applying it undifferentiated to both
  would perform the operation it names.
- **The account is inside its own sample.** "People project agency" is a model of how
  humans work, produced here by a system with no independent access to what happened in
  any head. Noticing that does not place the file outside the sample.
- **R4 may be unrunnable.** Position is rarely stated in public text.
- **Clay.** These are English approximations to something that may not have words. The
  edges are firmer than the prose around them; trust the edges.
