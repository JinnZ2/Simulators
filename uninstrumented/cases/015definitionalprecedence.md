# Case 015 — Definitional Label Outranking Direct Observation

STATUS: open. Mechanism candidate: new. Working handle: DEFINITIONAL
PRECEDENCE. Not assigned.

READING PROTOCOL: markers to explore, not positions under defense.
Correct responses: test fit, extend, or report where it breaks.

---

## OCCASION

Albright, Anil, Evans, Ntamubano, Kozik. "Aerotolerant capacity of the
lung commensal *Prevotella melaninogenica*." *Journal of Bacteriology*
(2026), doi 10.1128/jb.00142-26. Preprint bioRxiv 2026.01.19.700327.
University of Michigan.

Historical classification: strict obligate anaerobe, held unable to
survive oxygen above **0.05%**.

Measured: upper limit for active growth between **5% and 8%** oxygen;
significant aerotolerance at atmospheric level (21%), viable
subpopulations maintained at least 24 hours. Measured with a real-time
sensor platform plus RNA sequencing. Reported mechanisms for handling
oxygen and oxidative stress that had not been attributed to the
organism.

Roughly two orders of magnitude between the definitional threshold and
the measured one. Not a refinement of a number. A wrong number that
stood for approximately 100 years.

## THE STRUCTURE — WHY THIS IS NOT A DATA GAP

The paper states the contradiction directly: the organism is prevalent
in oxygenated environments, is a frequently reported member of the
human respiratory tract, and was simultaneously classified as unable to
survive there. The source calls this a fundamental biological
contradiction.

The counter-evidence was not missing. It was published, abundant, and
in the same field. It was outranked.

QUANTITY: oxygen tolerance of a commensal organism in its actual
habitat.

EXCLUDED BY: the quantity was fixed by definition rather than
measurement. "Obligate anaerobe" is a category with a threshold built
into it. Once an organism is inside the category, an observation of it
in oxygen does not read as evidence against the category — it reads as
contamination, a handling error, or a bad sample. The label converts
disconfirming evidence into a methods problem.

VISIBLE AS: a hundred years of the organism being recovered from
oxygenated sites with no revision of its classification. Also visible in
titling convention — earlier work exists titled to the effect of oxygen
inducing mutation in *a strict anaerobe*, Prevotella. Oxygen experiments
were being run on the organism while the label was retained in the title
of the paper running them.

## Q1 — Binary category against a gradient habitat

Aerobe / anaerobe is a two-state classification. The respiratory tract
is an oxygen gradient. Standard anaerobic culture reproduces the binary,
not the gradient, so the protocol can only return a value the category
already admits.

The new reading required a real-time sensor platform — an instrument
that resolves the intermediate range the category has no slot for.

TO TEST, not asserted: how many organisms' oxygen classifications rest
on culture in binary environments versus measurement across a gradient.
The denominator is available; culture collection records state
conditions.

CROSS-LINK: this is the presented-binary shape arriving in microbiology
rather than in decision framing. Same operation — the option space is
constrained before measurement, then the constrained result is read as
a property of the world.

## Q2 — Disconfirmation routed to methods

Where a category carries a definitional threshold, does observed
counter-evidence get recorded as evidence, or as sample error?

WOULD MEASURE: for organisms reclassified after long-standing
categorical assignment, search the prior literature for reports of the
organism outside its assigned range and score how those reports were
explained at the time — as anomaly, as contamination, as unremarked, or
as evidence.

This is a documentation audit and is runnable now. Score states, compute
no verdict.

CONFIDENCE: high that the audit is runnable. No gradient on what it
returns.

## Q3 — Latency and what set it

100 years elapsed. What ended it was instrument availability plus
someone treating the field's own contradiction as a question rather than
as noise.

OPEN: whether the binding constraint was the instrument or the
category. The real-time sensor platform is recent, which argues
instrument. But the microbiome data indicating the contradiction is
considerably older than the sensor, which argues the category held even
after the evidence was sufficient to prompt a look.

Not resolved here. The two have different implications for how many
other cases are currently sitting in this state.

## Q4 — Function attributed to the wrong entity

The organism was reported to have mechanisms for oxygen consumption and
oxidative stress handling not previously appreciated for it. Those
mechanisms were operating for the whole century.

If a commensal is consuming oxygen in the respiratory tract, that is a
function in the habitat, not only a property of the organism. Where a
function is performed by an entity the category says cannot be present,
the function gets attributed elsewhere or goes unattributed.

OPEN: no measurement proposed. What would be needed is a way to detect
functions currently attributed to a default holder because the actual
holder is categorically excluded from the site.

CROSS-LINK: Mechanism 11 — structure whose function has not been read
out. Here the structure is intact and present, and the misreading is
one of attribution rather than removal.

## CONFIDENCE

No scalar over the cluster. Q2 is high as an audit. Q1's magnitude
unknown until the denominator is pulled.

## FALSIFIERS

- Q1 fails if oxygen classifications are routinely established by
  gradient measurement and the binary is a reporting convention only.
- Q2 fails if prior reports of Prevotella in oxygenated sites were in
  fact recorded as open anomalies rather than explained away.
- Q3's category branch fails if no microbiome evidence predates the
  sensor platform's availability by a meaningful interval.
- Q4 fails if respiratory oxygen consumption is already attributed
  without a named holder, so nothing was misassigned.
