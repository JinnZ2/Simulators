# Case 014 — Cognitive Offloading, Evolutionary Framing, and Channel Separability

STATUS: open cluster. Mechanism unassigned.
Working handle: REFERENCE POPULATION SMUGGLED. Names Q2 only.

READING PROTOCOL: markers to explore, not positions under defense.
Correct responses: test fit, extend, or report where it breaks.

---

## OCCASION

Offloading Reduces Prospective Memory Learning. *Journal of Experimental
Psychology: Learning, Memory, and Cognition* (2026). Participants who
used external reminders later remembered fewer future tasks than those
who had not — below the no-reminder baseline, not merely level with it.
Authors attribute it to desirable difficulties: the effort of unaided
retrieval is what builds durable memory.

Stated caveat in the source: online study, students, short-term memory.
Does not speak to years of reminder-app use.

The case is not about this result. The result is the occasion for
examining how such results get framed.

---

## Q1 — NULL TEST: is a transmission channel ever specified?

Where cognitive offloading or technology-driven cognitive change is
described in evolutionary terms, is any transmission channel named?

This is a documentation audit, not a hypothesis. Score each instance:

- CHANNEL SPECIFIED — allele frequency, developmental environment,
  epigenetic regulation, or transmitted instruction is named, with a
  timescale attached
- ASSERTED — evolutionary language used, no channel, no timescale
- ABSENT — no evolutionary claim made

No verdict computed. The ratio is the reading.

EXCLUDED BY: nothing prevents it. It has not been assembled.

Same shape as the EIA denominator in Mechanism 11: the checking
apparatus exists in a neighbouring field and is closed on its own
inputs.

WHAT MAKES IT CHECKABLE NOW: the critique already exists in a different
literature. Evolution education and paleoanthropology have named the
progress framing, the pinnacle framing, and hero-worship in the prose
of the field's own writing. Pobiner (2016) names the specific corollary:
need-based rationales carry a mistaken assumption that within-lifetime
transformational change is genetically heritable. Kelemen's work
indicates the teleological default is not the product of parental
explanation, religiosity, or storybook convention — so it is a default
reading mode, which is why it survives in people who would disavow it
if asked directly.

None of that critique has been redirected at contemporary
cognitive-offloading claims. The target moved; the instrument did not
follow.

CONFIDENCE: high that the audit is runnable and that the ratio is
currently unmeasured. No gradient on what it returns.

## Q2 — The reference population

[stated by Kavik] "Human evolution led to this point" resolves in
practice to one population's recent environment. Other populations'
adaptations then get read as earlier stages of the same line rather
than as different niches. The framing is preset before any evidence is
examined: my way is the outcome, their way is the prior stage.

Two errors stacked, and only one is documented:

- PINNACLE ERROR — teleological reading of an untargeted process.
  Documented in the education literature above.
- REFERENCE POPULATION ERROR — a sampled subpopulation's recent
  conditions standing in for the species. NOT addressed in that
  literature.

The second is the one that does the work, and it is the one with no
name in the sources found.

WOULD MEASURE: in instances scored ASSERTED under Q1, whether any
reference population is stated at all. Expect the population to be
unstated rather than misstated — the smuggle runs through omission.

## Q3 — Are the channels separable?

[stated by Kavik] The channels co-vary and feed the same outcome.
Offloading arrives with a time crunch; time crunch selects convenience
food; nutritional environment during development is a transmission
channel. Attention directed at social-likability signals changes
hormonal and chemical state, which affects gene regulation. The
teaching channel transmits reliably across generations without touching
the genome — if the offspring is not taught the capacity and does not
use it, the connections are not made.

Her question, recorded as asked: how are we supposed to separate all
this.

HELD OPEN, and this is the point of the entry: the non-separability may
be the finding rather than the obstacle. If the channels are not
separable in the system, any study isolating one is measuring an
artifact of its own isolation, and the isolation is a property of the
instrument.

WOULD MEASURE: unclear. Requires a design that does not assume
independence in order to test for it. Not yet available.

DO NOT fill this in with an approximation.

## Q4 — Coupling value as a function of difference

[stated by Kavik] The value in human-AI coupling lies in the
differences between the two — that is where the interesting things come
from. And: species do not attempt to become other species. They mimic,
they camouflage, but they occupy different niches.

If the value is in the difference, then anything eroding the difference
erodes the value. Offloading runs that direction: it shifts the human
contribution toward functions the machine already performs — storage,
retrieval, scheduling — while the non-substitutable side is a body in
an environment with immediate feedback.

The niche observation is the mechanism under it. Convergence is not a
strategy anything runs, because two things in the same niche compete
rather than compose. Mimicry works because the mimic stays different
underneath; it is a surface signal, not a redesign.

WOULD MEASURE: no instrument proposed. What would be needed is a
readout of which capacities are being substituted versus extended, and
that readout does not exist on either side of the coupling. An AI
cannot self-report it, and the human side has no unaided baseline once
the tool is in use.

BRIDGE: this is the competence-residual measurement in
[[tool-off-metrology]] pointed at cognition rather than trade skill.
Same problem — the baseline is destroyed by the thing being measured.

## FALSIFIERS

- Q1 fails if offloading literature routinely specifies channel and
  timescale, and the evolutionary language is already disciplined.
- Q2 fails if reference populations are stated in the sources and the
  generalization is explicit rather than smuggled.
- Q3 weakens if a design exists that separates nutritional,
  hormonal-regulatory, and instructional channels without assuming
  their independence.
- Q4 fails if substitution and extension turn out to be distinguishable
  by an existing measurement.
