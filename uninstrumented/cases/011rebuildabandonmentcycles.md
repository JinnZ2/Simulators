## OCCASION

Kiss, Viglione, Blöschl et al., *Nature*, 12 Aug 2026.
"Cascading continental-scale floods across Europe in 1342–1343."
DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-10888-8

Reconstruction of what has been carried in the record as a single event —
the July 1342 Magdalenenflut, "the millennium flood" — as **16 distinct
flood events** across Europe within roughly 18 months. Stated implication:
current flood management is not built for sequences.

The consolidation is the first observation: a sequence was transmitted as
a singularity, in a literate culture with dated documentary records. The
compression survived the channel; the structure did not.

## THE CLUSTER

Not one quantity. At least four questions that current records cannot
separate.

### Q1 — Repair state as an initial condition

Do any flood design standards, anywhere, carry a **repair-state term**?

Design is against magnitude (N-year peak) with an implicit assumption
that the system is intact at event onset. A second event arrives into
saturated ground, unrepaired works, and spent response capacity. That is
not the first event scaled up; the failure pathway differs.

WOULD MEASURE: audit of national/regional flood design codes for any
variable representing pre-event repair completion. Expected null. A
field-wide null makes the exclusion structural rather than local.

### Q2 — Rebuild attempts before permanent abandonment

Count of rebuild cycles at a site prior to non-reoccupation.

Hypothesis to test, not asserted: the threshold is a **rate** threshold,
not a magnitude threshold — total water delivered in one event across 40
years versus the same total across 4 years produces different occupancy
outcomes.

EXCLUDED BY: outcome is recorded as depopulation, migration, or economic
decline, attributed to the most recent event or to no event. No field
collects "attempt number N." The denominator does not exist.

WOULD MEASURE: for sites with documentary or archaeological continuity,
reconstruct (attempts, inter-event interval, repair-completion fraction
at next onset) triples. 1342–1343 is a candidate corpus because the event
sequence is now separated.

### Q3 — Who holds the abandonment decision

Same recorded outcome — site not rebuilt — arises from structurally
different mechanisms:

    residents decide
    insurer withdraws coverage
    state declines to fund works
    lender declines to finance rebuild
    (list not closed)

The record does not distinguish these. Abandonment is logged as a fact
about the place, not as an output of a decision process with an
identifiable holder.

Links to the GENERATION CAPACITY REMOVED mechanism: the residents' choice
is made over whatever option set was generated and delivered to them.

WOULD MEASURE: decision-holder attribution as a required field on any
non-reoccupation record. Currently absent by construction.

### Q4 — Repair capacity as a depleting stock

Distinct from rate mismatch. The knowledge of how to rebuild the specific
works at the specific site is held by people who leave. It is not
transferable to later occupants by the same channel, and it does not
return with them.

WOULD MEASURE: unclear. Flagged as needing an instrument that does not
obviously exist. Possible bridge to the tool-off / competence-residual
spine.

### Q5 — NOT YET ARTICULABLE

There is more in this cluster than Q1–Q4 hold. Political and ownership
structure of the affected area is involved in a way not yet separable
into a question. Recorded here as an open slot rather than forced into
existing vocabulary.
