0  the delivered scanner is not the reconstructed one
========================================================================
  Four differences that move numbers:

    unit          delivered splits into SENTENCES (>= 20 chars);
                  the reconstruction worked line by line
    dedup         delivered `break`s after the first matching
                  trigger per mechanism per sentence -- at most
                  one hit per (mechanism, sentence)
    extensions    delivered reads .txt .md .rst .org .csv .json .jsonl .py
                  the reconstruction read .md only
    boundaries    delivered compiles each trigger RAW:
                  re.compile(t, re.I), no \b

  That last one was flagged [CHOICE] in the reconstruction and
  guessed the other way. Raw matching means `lean` matches
  `cleaning` and `slack` matches `slacken`. The dedup pulls in
  the opposite direction, so the two do not simply add.

  Every number below is from the delivered instrument.

1  the drop's own worked example of BUDGET BOUNDARY
========================================================================
  The register's canonical BUDGET_BOUNDARY case is leaf vs
  panel, and ../declared-frame/v2/examples/ ships both halves.
  No corpus, no threshold, no adjudication: does the scanner
  fire on the case its own trigger list was written for?

    declared-frame/v2/examples/ -> 0 candidate(s)

  Now the same case in four phrasings:

    the register's own VISIBLE AS line   SCORED AS WASTE ('inefficient', candidate)
    the delivered result string          NO HIT
    stated as a comparative              BUDGET BOUNDARY ('more efficient than', candidate)
    stated with the noun                 BUDGET BOUNDARY ('conversion efficiency', candidate)

  Unchanged from the reconstructed run. Two results:

  (a) The delivered result string does not fire. The triggers
      catch the RHETORIC of a comparison -- 'more efficient
      than', 'outperforms', 'orders of magnitude better' -- not
      the comparison. Two numbers side by side with no
      comparative is the usual result-line form, and it is
      invisible to all eight BUDGET BOUNDARY triggers.

  (b) The register's own phrasing fires under the WRONG
      mechanism: 'inefficient' is a SCORED AS WASTE trigger, so
      the reader is handed the wrong `check` question.

  Both repairs are cheap: a trigger for the bare-numbers form,
  and letting mechanisms co-fire -- which is UNI_003 arriving in
  the scanner. The delivered `break` in scan() is what enforces
  one-mechanism-per-sentence, so the co-firing repair is a
  two-line change there.

2  triage load -- the quantity the design turns on
========================================================================
  patterns.json states its own standard: every hit is a
  candidate for triage, not a finding. Under that standard the
  binding constraint is not precision but how many `check`
  questions a human must answer per unit of text.

  corpus: 959 files (.txt .md .rst .org .csv .json .jsonl .py), 827626 words
    all candidates        837   1.0 per 1000 words
    strength=candidate    551   0.7 per 1000 words
    strength=weak         286

  The `weak` band is the delivered scanner's own confidence
  gradient -- reported, never used to filter, which is the same
  discipline as the register's CONFIDENCE field. It downgrades
  a SCALAR DEMAND hit when conditioning words are nearby, an
  AUDIT ASYMMETRY hit with no hedge in the sentence, and any
  sentence over 400 chars.

  Densest files:

  hedge attached to an account. The trigger is correct and the
  corpus is adversarial to it.

  One result IS about the instrument, and it is the largest
  single number in the table. `lean` is compiled raw, so it
  matches as a substring:

    `lean`    200 hits   surrounding forms: clean x109, cleanly x26, boolean x22, snaps_clean x17
             the bare word appears 13 time(s)
    `slack`    84 hits   surrounding forms: slack x108, slack_radius x31, slack_monitor x2, slacken x2
             the bare word appears 108 time(s)

  `lean` is the most-fired trigger in the corpus and nearly all
  of it is `clean`, `cleanly` and `boolean`. Adding word
  boundaries to that one trigger removes ~200 of 837 candidates
  (24%) at no cost, because the bare word is rare here.

  `slack` is the harder case and stays: the bare word IS what
  mostly matches, and the noise is a proper-noun homograph and a
  code identifier, neither of which \b removes. That one is a
  triage cost the design already accepts.

5  the corpus is live, and the scanner is inside it
========================================================================
  patterns.json holds the trigger strings, and the scanner reads
  .json. So the definition file is in the corpus:

    candidates from the scanner's own files: 31 of 837
      uninstrumented/AUDIT_NOTES.md                2
      uninstrumented/patterns.json                 10
      uninstrumented/scan.py                       1
      uninstrumented/scan_audit.py                 18

  Not a bug to fix by excluding a path. It is the use-mention
  problem at its purest, and it is the one place in this corpus
  where it actually bites -- documents ABOUT the mechanisms
  return almost nothing, because the triggers are written in the
  vocabulary of the FAILING document, not of the mechanism.
  'apparatus in the wrong channel' is how the register names
  MODALITY; 'failed to demonstrate' is what a paper exhibiting
  it says. The two barely overlap -- except in the file that
  lists both.

  The counts in sections 2-4 are therefore a SNAPSHOT, not a
  fixture. This is ../anchor-interval/'s moving reference
  occurring rather than being described.

  And one step further, which is why EXCLUDE exists at the top
  of this file: scan.py reads .txt, and this script writes its
  own output to samples/. Left alone, run N+1 measures run N and
  the script has NO FIXED POINT -- consecutive runs disagree
  before anything in the repo has changed.

    candidates in this script's own last output: 21
    excluded from every walk above, by path

  That exclusion is a hand-broken loop, not a fix. Anyone
  running scan.py over this repo WILL see those hits, because
  the file is really there. What the exclusion buys is a script
  that converges; what it costs is that the reported corpus is
  no longer the corpus on disk. Both halves are stated here
  rather than one of them being quietly true.
