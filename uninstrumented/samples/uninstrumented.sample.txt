1  does sorting by mechanism cut across field?
========================================================================
  The register's stated reason for sorting by mechanism rather
  than by field: it lets a case from evolutionary biology sit
  next to one from survey methodology and be recognizably the
  same failure. That is checkable.

  mechanism              field                             
  ----------------------------------------------------------
  MODALITY               animal cognition                  
  STORAGE                tacit skill                       
  SCALAR_DEMAND          survey methodology                
  BUDGET_BOUNDARY        energy accounting                 
  AUTHORED_REFERENCE     ML evaluation                     
  SCORED_AS_WASTE        behavioural ecology / industrial skill
  AUDIT_ASYMMETRY        model behaviour                   

  7 entries, 7 distinct fields, 7 distinct mechanisms
  mechanisms holding more than one field: 0

  At one entry per mechanism the two partitions are identical and
  the sort is UNTESTED, not confirmed. It buys nothing until a
  second entry lands under an existing mechanism from a different
  field.

  Nearest candidate already in the repo: MODALITY currently holds
  animal cognition. ../reasoning-dial/ RD_009's G-STATE gap -- a
  self-report from a miscalibrated observer is the quantity in
  question -- is a different field with arguably the same shape.
  Filing it would be the first real test of the sort.

          boundary placed in time rather than in space

  [2 applicable] AUDIT_ASYMMETRY
      reliability of an account
      also AUTHORED_REFERENCE:
          the corpus that sets what counts as a normal account was
          produced by the side that goes unaudited

  4 of 7 entries have more than one mechanism with a claim.

  This is not a defect to resolve by tightening definitions. The
  filing decides what comparison case an entry sits next to, and
  that is the register's whole function -- so the filing is a
  CHOICE and should be visible as one. Two entries filed under
  different mechanisms that share a second mechanism are a pair
  the sort did not surface.

  Minimal repair, and it changes the schema: carry excluded_by as
  a PRIMARY plus a list, and sort under all of them. The cost is
  that an entry then appears more than once, which is the correct
  cost -- it is in more than one place.

========================================================================
3  known-null corpus: does anything file that should not?
========================================================================
  Every entry in the register states high confidence on the
  exclusion. A list that only ever admits entries is
  CONSTANT_FIRES in the ../null-harness/ sense, and the doc's own
  first line -- 'not a gap log' -- is a rule the structure does
  not enforce.

  Null corpus: the six instruments graded in
  ../instrument-epistemology/. Real apparatus, real chains,
  three of them graded 'mostly assumed'. None should file here.

  instrument                         fidelity   verdict there    files here?
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  broadband seismometer network      0.800      well grounded    no
  satellite thermal IR radiometer    0.504      partially grounded no
  airborne LiDAR biomass             0.514      partially grounded no
  camera trap array                  0.293      mostly assumed   no
  IRMS + isotopic mixing model       0.275      mostly assumed   no
  eDNA metabarcoding assay           0.165      mostly assumed   no

  false entries: 0 of 6

    broadband seismometer network
        measurand unobservable, chain long, model in the loop -- and
        reached, via traceable standards. No mechanism fires.
    satellite thermal IR radiometer
        M3, heavily model-dependent. Model dependence is not on the
        list and should not be.
    airborne LiDAR biomass
        allometry is a bridge model with known domain limits. Weak
        grounding.
    camera trap array
        detection probability is estimated rather than measured. Weak
        grounding.
    IRMS + isotopic mixing model
        mixing model underdetermined. Weak grounding, and the
        underdetermination is REPORTED.
    eDNA metabarcoding assay
        fidelity 0.165, four blind spots. Still not excluded -- the
        quantity is reached badly, and every step is named in its own
        blindness map.

  So the mechanism set discriminates on this corpus. The line it
  holds is exactly the one the doc states:

      weak grounding      the quantity is reached, badly
      constitutive        the quantity cannot appear at all,
      exclusion           and the apparatus is why

  eDNA at 0.165 is the hardest case and it stays out. Every step
  of its chain is named in its own blindness map, which is what a
  reached-but-badly quantity looks like. An excluded one has no
  blindness map, because the exclusion happens before the map is
  drawn.

  What this does NOT establish: that the register has a reachable
  fire branch on a case someone would actually bring. The null
  corpus is six cases chosen because they are well documented,
  not because they are near the boundary. The near-boundary test
  is a quantity a field believes it measures and does not, and
  none of the seven entries is currently contested by anyone.

========================================================================
READING
========================================================================

  The register holds. Seven mechanisms, a closed vocabulary, and the
  entry structure separates the stated confidence from the shape so the
  two can move independently.

  Three results on the register itself, none of them fatal and all of
  them structural:

  The mechanism sort is UNTESTED rather than confirmed. At one entry per
  mechanism the mechanism partition and the field partition are the same
  partition, so nothing yet demonstrates the cross-domain grouping the
  sort exists for.

  The mechanisms are not mutually exclusive -- 4 of 7 entries have a
  second mechanism with a claim. The filing decides which comparison case
  an entry sits next to, so it is a choice and should carry a primary
  plus a list.

  On a known-null corpus of six externally graded instruments, nothing
  files that should not, including eDNA at fidelity 0.165. The line
  between weak grounding and constitutive exclusion holds: a reached-
  but-badly quantity has a blindness map, an excluded one does not,
  because the exclusion happens before the map is drawn.
