QRNG PAIR SEARCH
unit of search = (source, bath_set), not (source, name)

SOURCES -- effective bath is source | readout
  avalanche_diode  src=TH           readout=EM,PWR,TH          eff=EM,PWR,TH  [PARTIAL]
  decay_alpha      src=COS          readout=EM,PWR,TH          eff=COS,EM,PWR,TH
  johnson_noise    src=TH           readout=EM,PWR,TH          eff=EM,PWR,TH  [CLASSICAL]
  nv_spin          src=EM,OPT       readout=EM,OPT,PWR,TH      eff=EM,OPT,PWR,TH
  photon_split     src=OPT          readout=EM,PWR,TH          eff=EM,OPT,PWR,TH
  rtd_tunnel       src=TH           readout=EM,PWR,TH          eff=EM,PWR,TH
  vacuum_homodyne  src=OPT          readout=EM,PWR,TH          eff=EM,OPT,PWR,TH

READOUT SHARING IS A DEPLOYMENT CHOICE, NOT A SOURCE PROPERTY
  single board, one ADC              0 of 10 pairs clean
  split rail/clock/ADC, same board   0 of 10 pairs clean
  fully separate chains              7 of 10 pairs clean
  decay_alpha x rtd_tunnel is source-disjoint and welds at the ADC.
  Compactness forces sharing: that is the squeeze, priced.

  rtd_tunnel x vacuum_homodyne       -          welded:EM,PWR,TH       1e+09

  nv_spin x vacuum_homodyne          OPT        welded:EM,OPT,PWR,TH   1e+06
  photon_split x vacuum_homodyne     OPT        welded:EM,OPT,PWR,TH   1e+09

  On one shared board every pair welds at TH/PWR/EM. The pairs
  worth building are those with an EMPTY STRUCTURAL overlap, where
  the weld is an engineering fact rather than a physics one -- and
  where separating the chains is therefore both possible and
  measurable. A structural weld survives any chain separation.

  separable pairs (structural empty, fielded non-empty): 7 of 10

SAMPLE COUNTS -- what a correlation sweep can actually resolve
  r >= 5e-03    needs 1.00e+06 samples at 5 sigma, 1 lag
                  1.40e+06 samples across 100 lags
  r >= 1e-03    needs 2.50e+07 samples at 5 sigma, 1 lag
                  3.51e+07 samples across 100 lags
  r >= 1e-04    needs 2.50e+09 samples at 5 sigma, 1 lag
                  3.51e+09 samples across 100 lags

ENTROPY -- min-entropy, not Shannon
  two legs at 0.8 and 0.6 bits per sample:
    XOR floor          0.80 bits  (guaranteed, needs independence)
    two-source target  1.40 bits  (budget, needs a named extractor)
    same, with 0.3 bits of measured coupling: 1.10
  Secrecy of the combiner appears in none of these lines, because
  it contributes to none of them.
