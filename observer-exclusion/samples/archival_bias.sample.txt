ARCHIVAL BIAS IN L -- computed before any archive is opened

1  L is attenuated by the archival delay
   The excluded reading is dated by when someone WROTE IT DOWN
   and the artifact survived, not by when it was held.
   Stipulated archival hazard 0.06/yr (mean delay 16.7 y):

   true lead    observed mean    recovered    P(observed > 0)
   ----------------------------------------------------------
   5            -10.6            -2.11        0.27
   10           -5.6             -0.56        0.47
   20           4.4              0.22         0.71
   30           14.4             0.48         0.84
   50           34.4             0.69         0.95

   A true 20-year lead measures as 4.4 and shows positive in
   71% of cases. A true 10-year lead measures NEGATIVE on
   average (-5.6) and is positive 47% of the time -- a coin
   flip on a real effect twice the mean archival delay.
   The bias runs AGAINST the hypothesis, which is the good
   direction: a positive L survives it. A null does not -- F2
   and F1 become the same observation, and the spec lists them
   as different falsifiers.

2  F4 is biased toward acceptance by differential archiving
   The spec's own control is field biologists' notes,
   abstracts and correspondence -- institutionally archived.
   Stipulated hazard 0.18/yr against 0.06/yr for trade press.

   true excluded lead     field appears  tie      excluded appears
   --------------------------------------------------------------
   0                      0.74           0.05     0.21
   5                      0.54           0.03     0.43
   10                     0.40           0.03     0.58
   20                     0.21           0.01     0.77
   40                     0.06           0.00     0.93

   At a TRUE lead of zero -- both held it the same year -- the
   record shows the field first in 74% of cases. F4 would be
   accepted on a difference in archiving, not in holding.
   The excluded population needs a true lead of about 8 years
   before the record shows it first more often than not.
   Also conservative. Also collapses F4 into F1.

3  what this means for reading a result
   Both principal biases run against the hypothesis, so:
     L > 0 survives them        -> the finding is strengthened
     L ~ 0 does not discriminate -> F1, F2 and F4 return the
                                    same observation
   The spec lists F1, F2 and F4 as separate falsifiers. Under
   archival censoring they are not separable on the L
   distribution alone. Separating them needs the archival
   hazard ESTIMATED -- which is doable from the same corpus,
   by dating artifacts against claimed observation dates. §4
   already says to record both. That field is the control.

4  the one bias that runs the other way, and is unguarded
   §5 pre-registers CASE selection. Nothing pre-registers
   ARTIFACT CODING. If 40% of artifacts are ambiguous and a
   coder who knows which way the literature moved accepts
   80% of them where a blind coder accepts 25%, the
   difference is 22% of the whole corpus -- entered as
   earlier dates, which inflates L directly.
   Fix is standard and cheap: code artifacts blind to the
   direction of the reversal. The spec guards selection and
   not coding, and coding is where the leniency lives.

   question-availability, three drops back:
     Q2 unaskable -- 'Posing the question costs the asker standing'
     'the label is applied prior to content, so the content never reaches evaluation'
   here:
     'Q2 is: reading held, no channel'
     'no instrument was pointed at them'

   Two different mechanisms under one label. The first is a
   channel that exists and penalises entry; the second is no
   channel at all. This spec's §1 distinguishes itself from
   'solicited and rejected' and not from the previous Q2,
   because the previous Q2 has been overwritten.
   Case 021's sense substitution inside the family's own
   vocabulary -- fourth instance in this tree after `state`,
   `parity` and 021's own cases.

   It has a consequence. `QA_003` identified the PREVIOUS Q2
   as `affect routing`, the mechanism uninstrumented recorded
   as named-in-prose and filed nowhere. That identification
   does not transfer to this Q2, so whoever files a twelfth
   mechanism has to say which one -- and if they file this
   one, `affect routing` is still unfiled.
   This spec's own title is the better name for what it
   describes: OBSERVER EXCLUSION, not 'unaskable'.
