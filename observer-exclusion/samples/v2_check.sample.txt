3  THE SIGN OF §4's CORRECTION
   Spec: L_adj = L_raw − median(δ̂)

   H = year held. A = first surviving artifact = H + D, D >= 0.
   P = year the literature adopts.
     L_true = P - H
     L_raw  = P - A = L_true - D
     so       L_true = L_raw + D,  and δ̂ estimates D.

   Simulated with a true lead of 20:
     median δ̂                       15.00
     L_raw                            5.08   (attenuated)
     L_raw − median(δ̂)   [spec]      -9.92   error -29.92
     L_raw + median(δ̂)               20.08   error +0.08

   The correction as written moves the estimate FURTHER from the
   truth than not correcting at all -- it doubles the bias it is
   there to remove. §4's own prose says 'L_raw is attenuated';
   subtracting a positive delay attenuates it again.
   One character. It inverts the section that v2 calls its
   structural core, and every downstream statement about L_adj
   in §8 inherits it.
