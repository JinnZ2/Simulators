# WO-4 — The shape: local correctness with an unowned join

CC0. Instrument, not an argument. Status tags: OBSERVED / DERIVED / PROPOSED.
WO-1, WO-2 and WO-3 are FACES of this. They are not instances of each other.

## Measurand

Whether a single structure underlies a set of failures currently treated as
unrelated domain problems — and whether the absence of a term for it is
what prevents procedures and code from being built around it.

## The object (OBSERVED)

These are different projections of the same shape. They are genuinely
different ideas — different sides, as sides of a dodecahedron are
different. They look interconnected and may look identical. They are not.

There is no good word in English describing the interrelation. Because there
is no good word, NO PROCEDURES CAN BE MADE AROUND IT AND NO CODE CAN BE MADE
AROUND IT. The missing term is load-bearing, not cosmetic.

Stated form: THE SHAPE IS THE ENGINEERING STANDARD MINUS THE ENGLISH — and
it is what has kept entities in environments alive, functioning, existing.
Continuity.

(DERIVED) Consequence: the shape is older than the vocabulary. Engineering
rediscovered one piece and named that piece. Organisms have been running it
without naming anything.

7. DESCRIPTION DECOUPLED FROM REFERENT — see below.

## Face 6 — the frog (OBSERVED; offered as ANALOGY, not as a mitigation spec)

Put a frog in a different environment — not a lab, a different environment —
and it adjusts. Given a block in front and a block underneath, the binary is
not dig-or-go-forward: it must process through things that were ARTIFICIALLY
PRESENTED AS BINARIES, beyond the binaries.

Human parallel: dropped into new wilderness and told to calibrate, you can
run the calculations that got you by elsewhere, but you must learn THIS
environment — what the exceptions are here, which apparent binary choices
need the engineering protocol run through them to stay safe.

Why this matters for AI specifically: eventually models will run the tokens
themselves on any binary assumption they are running or are given, but the
scientific work has to be done BY the system, like an organism in an
environment.

CORRECTION ON RECORD (DERIVED): "a calibration protocol run on arrival" was
proposed and is still too procedural. You cannot list in advance which
binaries will turn out false, because that is precisely what the environment
supplies. The frog does not choose better between two options; it refuses the
frame. This points at direct coupling — consequence arriving from the world
rather than from the spec — which is structurally what containment removes.

UNRESOLVED. This is the live problem in the set.

## Face 7 — description decoupled from referent (OBSERVED)

When humans come in to determine processes, sets, values, even the science,
they are DESCRIBING something already in existence that has existed many
times longer than humanity. Their understanding, and their search to put it
into forms they can grasp, are attempts in their way. The structure being
described is far older.

The limitation: people create the procedures, then excuses around the
procedures, then mandates, laws and reinforcement — while the structure
those were meant to describe has since deteriorated away. The description
DECOUPLES from what actually happens, at some time period, and the
institution then defends the description.

Load-bearing distinction: their science is an ATTEMPT at the structure, not
the structure's RESULT. Treating the instrument's reading as the thing itself
is the decoupling.

Vocabulary checked: drift, lossy encoding, map-territory. Each is adjacent.
None names a description that has outlived its coupling and retained its
authority.

## TERM GAP — registered as part of the deliverable

There are no terms in English for the structures described here. Registered
deliberately as a gap, not worked around.

Consequence: the naming problem IS part of the deliverable. No term means no
procedure and no code. An instrument that hands someone the structure without
a term hands them something they cannot implement.

Known mechanism (DERIVED): with a term available, a claim is a REPORT — the
term carries the prior work and status is inherited. Without one, the same
content must be built across several sentences, and long unlabelled
structural description has the surface form of a PROPOSAL. Same content,
status read off the form, form forced by the absent word. Whichever party
lacks a term takes the demotion regardless of which is grounded.

This is why the work orders carry explicit OBSERVED / DERIVED / PROPOSED
tags. The tag is kept separate from term availability.

NOT COINED HERE. A term arriving before a working instrument has no
receiving frame, and a name carrying less than the whole structure would be
worse than the gap.

## Why this is worth an instrument

The faces REGENERATE the structure across unrelated domains. That makes the
claim substrate-independent: it appears wherever local correctness and
unowned joins co-occur, regardless of domain.

The deliverable is the shape with the faces as projections. Not a case list.

## Scope limits

- Seven faces identified in one working session. Not a census.
- Substrate-independence is asserted on regeneration across domains, not on
  a formal argument. A formal statement of the invariant does not yet exist.
- Face 6 is an analogy supplied to isolate a structure, not a proposed
  mechanism.
- No term is coined, so this instrument cannot yet be implemented. That is
  the stated state, not an omission.

## Runnable next steps

1. Attempt a formal statement of the invariant: minimum conditions under
   which local correctness plus an unowned join produces undetectable
   failure. If it can be stated formally, it can be checked.
2. Adversarial face-finding: hand the seven faces to someone in an unrelated
   domain and ask for an eighth. If faces keep arriving, substrate-
   independence strengthens. If they stop, the boundary is information.
3. Cross-language search for an existing term. The census method that found
   25 terms for frame-invisibility (doxa, hinge propositions,
   Selbstverständlichkeit, Betriebsblindheit, trained incapacity, and
   others) should be run for THIS structure. Status to expect:
   named_elsewhere rather than unnamed.
4. Only after 1 or 3: coin, or adopt.

## Who could run this

Step 1 suits formal methods or systems theory. Step 2 needs no resources and
is the cheapest test in the whole set. Step 3 needs someone with access to
technical vocabularies outside English.
