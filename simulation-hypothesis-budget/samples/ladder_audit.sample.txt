  RUNG 2 -- 'nobody has an energy cost for a measurement outcome'
  VERDICT: LANDS, and it is standard physics rather than a doubt.

    Landauer bounds ERASURE at kT ln2. Bennett's resolution of
    Maxwell's demon is that MEASUREMENT can be done reversibly --
    the demon does not pay to look, it pays to forget.

    validated on   erasure in a thermal reservoir, ~kT, lab T
    applied to     production of a measurement outcome

    events counted                        1.29e+30
    if every event is eventually erased   3.37e+7 J
    if written once and never reclaimed   0 J
    memory-reuse factor                   UNDECLARED

    The steelman is why this is a missing declaration and not an
    arithmetic error: a simulator with finite reused memory must
    erase each outcome to make room, and then the count transfers
    unchanged. Nothing states that. Its negation pays zero.

    So the litre of gasoline is a floor on a DIFFERENT OPERATION
    than the one it is named for.
