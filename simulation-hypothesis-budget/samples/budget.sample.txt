LAYER 1 -- DECIDABLE (our physics, simulator embedded in our physics)

  observable universe volume        3.57e+80 m^3
  Planck volumes in it              8.45e+184
  Planck times since t=0            8.07e+60
  spacetime cells to step           6.82e+245

  universe's own mass-energy        2.73e+71 J
    (all components at rho_crit. matter-only is ~20x smaller;
     the choice moves the ratios by ~1.3 decades out of ~150.)

  ENERGY FLOORS, one bit-op per cell:
    Landauer  (k_B T ln2, T = 2.725 K)   1.78e+223 J
      = 6.52e+151 x the universe's mass-energy
    Margolus-Levitin (rate over 4.35e+17 s) 2.60e+194 J
      = 9.53e+122 x the universe's mass-energy

  Both floors assume the simulator sits in a universe with our
  constants and our CMB as its heat sink. That is the ONLY reading
  under which these numbers are about anything -- see LAYER 2.

  THE VOLUME COUNT IS THE WRONG COUNT.

  holographic bound on the observable universe:
    horizon area                    2.43e+54 m^2
    max information in it           3.36e+123 bits
    Planck-VOLUME count             8.45e+184
    volume count exceeds the bound by  2.52e+61

  A region's information content goes as its AREA, not its volume.
  Every 'simulate every Planck volume' estimate overcounts the state
  by ~61 orders of magnitude before any energy is assigned to it.

  redone on the holographic state, one update per Planck time:
    bit-operations                  2.71e+184
    Landauer floor                  7.07e+161 J  (2.59e+90 x universe)
    Margolus-Levitin floor          1.03e+133 J  (3.79e+61 x universe)

  Still astronomically above the universe's own energy. The
  correction changes the exponent, not the conclusion, for the
  embedded-simulator reading.

LAYER 2 -- VOID (the ratio everyone actually wants)

  'Could a simulator afford this?' requires:
      energy the job costs   /   energy the simulator has

  REFUSED:
    VOID RATIO.
    numerator is computed from OUR hbar, k_B, T_CMB and l_Planck; denominator would be a property of a parent universe whose constants are unknown and need not match.
    Nothing licenses dividing one by the other.
    State the frame or do not form the ratio.

  The numerator in LAYER 1 is built from OUR hbar, k_B, T_CMB and
  l_Planck. A parent universe running us need not share any of them.
  Its Planck length, its thermodynamics, its available energy and
  whether it even has a Landauer limit are all unconstrained by
  anything measurable from inside here.

  So LAYER 1 is not an argument against the simulation hypothesis.
  It is a measurement of one specific thing: whether THIS universe
  could host a full-resolution simulation of itself. It could not,
  by ~150 orders of magnitude.

LAYER 3 -- THE KNOB THAT DOES THE WORK

  Cost scales as (l_Planck / L)^4. Nothing in the hypothesis requires
  Planck resolution; it is assumed, and it is the assumption the whole
  number rests on.

  resolution             cells        cheaper by   Landauer J
  Planck                 6.8e+245     1.0e+0       1.8e+223
  proton radius          9.3e+166     7.3e+78      2.4e+144
  Bohr radius            5.9e+147     1.1e+98      1.5e+125
  visible light          5.1e+131     1.3e+114     1.3e+109
  1 mm                   4.7e+118     1.5e+127     1.2e+96
  1 km                   4.7e+94      1.5e+151     1.2e+72

  The rule is L^-4: every factor of 10 in resolution is a factor of
  10^4 in cost. Planck to proton radius is ~19 decades of length and
  ~79 of cost; Planck to visible light is ~114 decades of cost.
  An argument whose conclusion moves by 10^114 under a parameter
  nobody has defended is a statement about that parameter.

  And the resolution only has to beat what is MEASURED, not what
  exists. No experiment has resolved anything near l_Planck; the
  shortest length probed is ~10^-19 m at collider energies.

THE ONE CONCLUSION THAT SURVIVES LAYER 2

  Can a system simulate ITSELF at full fidelity, inside itself?
    bits available                  3.36e+123
    bits needed (copy + 1 marker)   3.36e+123 + 1
    possible                        False

    (decided exactly, not by addition: at this magnitude float
     arithmetic gives x + 1 == x, which is True here, and would
     report the impossibility as possible. caught by selftest.)

  This needs no parent-universe constants, which is why it is the only
  result here that is not frame-dependent. A full-fidelity self-
  simulation is impossible for any system whatsoever, at any scale,
  under any physics, because the copy plus the machinery distinguishing
  it from the original does not fit inside the original.

  Everything else in this file is contingent on a frame nobody can
  measure from in here.
