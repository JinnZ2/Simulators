EARTH TRANSITIONS -- the delivered count, checked
========================================================================

  The structural point is right and nothing below disputes it: the
  eight 'major transitions' are labels, each a coarse-grained envelope,
  and pricing the labels is not pricing the transitions. What is
  checked is the arithmetic, because the arithmetic is what produces
  the headline.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. THE CEILING, CHECKED FROM INSIDE THIS FOLDER

     universe mass-energy      2.73e+71 J
     Margolus-Levitin rate     1.65e+105 ops/s
     ops since t=0             7.16e+122  (10^122.9)
     Lloyd, as delivered       10^120
     agreement                 within 2.9 decades
     budget.py uses all components at rho_crit; Lloyd uses matter-only.
     SHB_006(a) already names that convention as a choice worth ~1.3
     decades, and the rest is accounting detail. Order-of-magnitude
     agreement by a second route is the check.

     The ceiling holds. It is the one number in the delivered result
     this folder can confirm by a route it did not take.

  2. WHAT PRODUCES 10^110 -- and it is not the eight

     Earth atoms                1.19e+50
     Planck ticks in 4.54 Gyr   2.66e+60

     construction               ops          log10
     labels x atoms             9.6e+50      51.0
     atoms x Planck ticks       3.2e+110     110.5
     labels x atoms x ticks     2.5e+111     111.4

     reproduces ~10^110 to within a decade: atoms x Planck ticks

     The eight labels contribute 0.9 decades to a 110-decade number.
     The count is atoms (50.1 decades) times PLANCK TICKS (60.4
     decades). So the first pass was never really a transition count --
     it was a stepping count, and the resolution assumption supplied
     more than half of it.

     That is SHB_004 on a new substrate: the resolution assumption does
     more work than anything else in the argument. It also sharpens the
     delivered self-correction rather than undercutting it -- the
     correction is worth 52 decades and the thing corrected was worth
     0.9.

  3. THE HEADLINE NEEDS 1e52 TO BE A MULTIPLIER

     1e52 is a MULTIPLIER on the stepping count
       total 10^162.5, over ceiling by 43 decades
       <- reproduces the delivered 1e42
     1e52 is the TOTAL nested transition count
       total 10^52.0, under ceiling by 68 decades

     110 + 52 - 120 = 42, exactly. So the delivered arithmetic is
     consistent under the multiplier reading and 68 decades out under
     the other. The text presents 1e52 next to 'nested transitions,
     FOUR classes only', which reads as a total. One label is wrong and
     the arithmetic is right.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

  4. BUT MULTIPLYING PRICES THE SAME PHYSICS TWICE

     A STEPPING model pays per degree of freedom per timestep, and
     every transition that occurs is already inside that cost --
     nesting adds nothing. An EVENT-DRIVEN model pays per transition
     and does not step. They are alternative architectures, and the
     product of the two is not a cost.

     cost model                           log10   cohr  verdict (decades)
     event-driven, labels only            51.0    yes   fits, 69 spare
     event-driven, nested                 103.0   yes   fits, 17 spare
     uniform Planck stepping              110.5   yes   fits, 9 spare
     stepping x nesting (as delivered)    162.5   NO    OVER by 43

     Under every internally coherent model, Planck-resolved Earth
     FITS. The overshoot appears only in the mixed one.

  5. THE CONSTRUCTIVE VERSION -- what would make it true

     event-driven base (labels x atoms)   10^51.0
     nesting used, four classes           52 decades
     nesting at which the model breaks    69 decades
     headroom remaining                   17 decades

     The delivered text says four classes only, not exhaustive. So the
     claim becomes true, without any double-count, if the full nesting
     is 69 decades rather than 52. That is a reachable falsifier and a
     better form of the delivered result: enumerate more transition
     classes and the event-driven model breaks on its own.

  6. THE KNOB, TURNED UNTIL IT FITS
     -- and this one ran against expectation

     stepping every atom, no nesting, budget 10^120:
       timestep affordable      1.71e-53 s
       Planck time              5.39e-44 s
       knob                     9.5 decades FINER than Planck

     Stepping every Earth atom for 4.54 Gyr inside the universe's whole
     ops budget affords a timestep 9.5 decades BELOW Planck time. The
     knob has headroom in the direction nobody asks for. That is the
     delivered first pass's own result -- 'uses 10^-10 of the budget.
     it FITS' -- arriving from this side, and it is not what turning
     the knob was expected to show.

     with the delivered nesting applied as a multiplier:
       timestep affordable      1.71e-1 s
       knob                     43 decades COARSER than Planck

     Under the mixed model the picture reverses and the affordable
     timestep lands at about 0.2 of a second -- not Planck time, not
     the 10^-21 s anything has ever resolved, but human-scale. So the
     delivered claim is equivalently a statement about the CLOCK: it
     says a Planck-stepped Earth carrying its full nested transition
     count would need a sub-second tick to fit, which is a
     contradiction in terms. Stated that way the double-count is
     visible without any arithmetic.

  WHAT IT REACHES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

     Reaches: this is a count over the world's own CONTENTS rather than
     over cells, and that is a different kind of bound. Under SHB_011,
     every transition that leaves a record must be computed by any
     architecture that can produce its own observation record --
     mineral grains, ice cores and fossils are records -- so a content
     count is architecture-independent in a way the cell counts of
     budget.py are not. That is the strongest thing in the delivered
     result and it is not what the delivered result claims.

     Does not reach: the hypothesis. Both operands are computed in our
     physics about a simulator embedded in our physics, so SHB_003's
     frame refusal applies unchanged and this measures self-hosting.
     And 'four classes' is a floor enumerated by us -- SHB_021 on a
     second substrate, the reference class being ours again. The
     delivered text reaches that itself: the eight-label count is a map
     artifact.
