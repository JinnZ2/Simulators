CONSEQUENCE FRAME -- what the hypothesis licenses, not what it costs
========================================================================

  THE INFERENCE UNDER TEST
    P1  this universe is a simulation
    P2  therefore a consequence propagating inside it is not real
    C   therefore the party producing it does not carry it

  P2 is the load-bearing line. 'Not real' cashes out as 'not
  computed', and multiscale.py already fixes what each architecture
  computes. So P2 needs ONE cell of a 2x2 to be non-empty:
  a consequence that is OBSERVED and NOT COMPUTED.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

  CONSEQUENCES USED (ordinary ones, placed by scale and region)
    a sentence spoken and heard              1e-2      diffuse_gas       observed
    a tree felled, the clearing after        1e+0      condensed_matter  observed

    a detector click                         1e-19     lab               observed

  RESULT

    In every architecture that can produce its own observation
    record, the cell P2 needs is EMPTY. It is empty for a different
    reason in each: in the refined stacks because the region is
    resolved, in the lazy stack because observation is the trigger.

    3 of 5 architectures do have an observed-and-uncomputed
    consequence, and each is thereby inadmissible: a listener who
    heard the sentence is a record the architecture cannot produce.
    Cheapness does not buy the cell. Contradiction does.

    The uncomputed consequences that remain are the unobserved ones
    -- a single molecule taken alone, a photon on an unvisited rock.
    Those are not the ripple effects the inference is deployed
    against; nobody is held to a consequence nothing registers.

    So P2 fails on every admissible architecture, at every cost,
    INDEPENDENTLY of whether P1 is true. The conclusion does not
    follow from the premise even if the premise holds.

    SHB_005 says the same thing from the other side: within-frame
    physics is unchanged by being hosted. A simulated fall breaks a
    simulated leg with the same arithmetic.

    file is also not a disinterested party to the conclusion; the
    interest direction is stated in declined() and left unresolved
    rather than being resolved in the comfortable direction.
