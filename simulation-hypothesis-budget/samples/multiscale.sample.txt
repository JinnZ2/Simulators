  EVERY ARCHITECTURE IS DOMINATED BY ONE LEVEL:
    the finest resolution, times the volume fraction needing it.
    Neither factor is constrained by anything measurable from inside.

  THE LAZY LIMIT -- render only what is measured

    total measurement events                          1.29e+30
    Landauer floor on that                            3.37e+7 J
    vs uniform Planck                                 1.9e-216 x

    For scale: 3.37e+7 J is about 34 MJ -- 9.3 kWh, or the chemical
    energy in roughly 1.0 litre of gasoline (~34 MJ/L).
    The Landauer floor on rendering every observation ever made, by
    everyone who has ever lived, is about a litre of fuel.
    That is what the architecture assumption is worth.

  THE SPREAD IS THE RESULT

    most expensive architecture   6.82e+245 cells
    cheapest floor considered     1.29e+30 events
    span                          216 decades

    budget.py found the answer moves 10^114 under ONE undefended
    parameter. Allowing multiple scales, it moves 216 decades under an
    undefended ARCHITECTURE.

    So 'the energy cost of simulating the universe' is not an
    underdetermined quantity. It is not a well-posed one until the
    level stack is specified, and no version of the hypothesis
    specifies it.

    What survives unchanged: budget.py's SHB_005. No system simulates
    itself at full fidelity at any resolution, because the argument is
    about state capacity and not about cost.
