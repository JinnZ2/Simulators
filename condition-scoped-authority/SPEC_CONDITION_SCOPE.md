# CONDITION-SCOPED AUTHORITY
# Status: marker under exploration.
# Companion to: STOP-AUTHORITY INSTRUMENTATION GAP

## THE CLAIM
Authority is not held by a position. It is held by a position
FOR A CLASS OF CONDITION. Rank and scope are different objects
and a single ranking cannot represent scope.

## WORKED CASE — protective detail
A bodyguard holds no authority over the principal's schedule,
finances, politics, or clientele. The instant a threat is
live, the bodyguard holds total authority within that
condition class, including physical force against the
principal's stated preference. Secret Service operates the
same way.

Rank does not invert. The domain is partitioned, and inside
that partition the principal was never the decider.

## THE JUSTIFICATION IS COMPETENCE, NOT RIGHTS
The specialist holds the authority because the reading
capacity sits there. The principal's attention is committed
elsewhere by design. Routing the decision upward adds
latency and substitutes a worse instrument. It does not add
judgment.

State it symmetrically: neither party reads the other's
domain. The specialist does not claim the executive's
domain either. This constrains both equally, which is why
it survives argument — it is not a transfer of power.

## WHAT COLLAPSED
When a scoped domain is restructured as a department
reporting upward, condition-scope is replaced by rank. Rank
has no condition column. The structure then asserts that the
top of the hierarchy holds every domain's reading capacity
simultaneously.

Nobody would defend that claim if it were written down.
The structure states it silently.

## VOCABULARY GAP
Current EHS literature describes direct-to-CEO reporting as
the strong configuration, evidenced by: visibility, seat at
executive meetings, influence on strategy. All soft verbs.
There is no term distinguishing:

  BOUND     — the finding stands regardless of who it lands
              on; reversal is not available
  ADVISORY  — the finding is heard by someone who may
              overrule it

Both are called "authority." Any measurement using the word
without stating which one is unsigned.

## THE ORGAN ERROR
The coordinating organ is not senior to the others. It is a
different organ, which cannot do what they do and cannot
sense what they sense. Coordination is a specialization, not
a rank.

Two failures follow from misreading it as rank:
  1. Reassignment by decree — instructing the hand to be a
     foot. Does not produce a degraded system; produces a
     non-functioning one. The failure lands downstream,
     where the decree cannot observe it.
  2. Measurement — scoring only the coordinating organ and
     reporting the result as the whole system's capacity.

The second is the same error as the centralized-executive
prior in consciousness and intelligence rubrics. Org chart
and measurement instrument, one shape.
