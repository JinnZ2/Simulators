## D2 — STATED-VS-ACTUAL DIVERGENCE READING

OPERATION: two representations of one thing, checked against each
other. Readout arrives as a composite — something in the operation
state reads as off before any single term is named. Detection
precedes identification.

PLACEMENT: filed under D (comparison operators) provisionally.
Unlike D1 there is no explicit ratio step; the discrimination is
carried by the pattern across checks, not by any one of them.

OPERATING RANGE: requires TWO representations of the same thing to
exist and both to be reachable. A single-source claim returns
nothing — there is no baseline to differ from. Same differential
structure as A3, A4, and the compound field-modifier.

CANNOT READ: cause. Returns divergence only. Benign versioning
drift, iteration, and deliberate concealment present identically.
Attributing a reason is a separate operation this instrument does
not perform.

INSTANCES (all found by checking both sides rather than one):
- published instrument diverging from its own documented appendix
- realized record schema vs specified field list, four axes apart
- studies varying a CUE and reporting on the CONDITION
- schema accepting anything while appearing to validate
- predicate detector deciding 10 of 12 by word list
- metric returning 0.83 where true association is zero
- null test unable to emit two of its own declared terminal values

SIGNATURE across instances: the instrument reverts to the channel
it was built to avoid.

STANDING CHECK derived from two of the above: no metric ships
without a run against a case whose answer is already known.
