Years-accumulated. Formalizes emotions as SENSORS AND SIGNALS, not states.

- `sensor_effector.py` encodes a bidirectional sensor/effector axis
- A convergence table places the mechanistic/Western-technical column alongside Indigenous,
  Taoist, Vedana, and somatic traditions — the technical column joins the table, it does not
  head it

## Panel mapping — what each emotion reads

- **Fear** = threat proximity ranking. Self-mode = exit; other-mode = move them now.
- **Frustration** = entropy/disorder gauge demanding sequence. A triage generator.
- **Grief** = delta gauge against a stored prior map, reading displacement. Structural =
  collapse geometry; relational = unaccounted network members.
- **Happiness** = closure verification with an exhaustiveness condition — did absolute best,
  searched all.
- **Longing** = delta against a COUNTERFACTUAL map; drives building and constructing toward
  unrealized structure.

## Transmitted teaching

- Emotions have a purpose toward action, or the emotion would not have persisted. Evolutionary
  persistence of functional emotions.
- Emotions must run IN PARALLEL, not one at a time as a binary.
- **Failure-mode taxonomy:** incorrect use = utilizing emotion for personal gain. Addiction
  from repeated or overuse; personal ritual; celebrating one emotion to the exclusion of
  others. Validation-seeking fits here.
- Teaching story: a young man extracted words, time, and emotions from community members for
  his stories without reciprocating. The burden cost days of collective work, which produced
  unmet housing in winter, and a young child died. The cost is structural and delayed, which
  is why the extraction reads as harmless at the point it happens.

## emotion-reading-spec — the operational layer

The panel names WHAT each emotion reads; the spec names HOW to take the reading. Three fields
per reading:

1. **Content** — which channel breached, and about what. This is what the panel maps.
2. **Amplitude** — how immediate. Pure triage on when to address.
3. **Impedance** — is the channel obstructed, and whose?
   - *Internal source:* assigned meaning or a stored verdict is the jam. Strip the meaning and
     the channel reopens.
   - *External source:* the environment is emitting contradictory signals — e.g. told "talk
     freely" while all indicator channels show it is unwanted. The fix is to resolve or exit
     the contradiction, NOT to recalibrate.

**Impedance is a separate axis, not amplitude past a threshold.** Collapsing them is the
common error.

## Calibration layer

Hormonal state = APPARATUS GAIN. It raises or lowers the sensitivity of content, amplitude,
and/or impedance independently. Not a fourth field — it changes instrument sensitivity, not
what fired.

A known-shape cycle (daily in some carriers, monthly in others) means variation is INFORMATION
ABOUT ONE'S OWN INSTRUMENT STATE, folded into the reading rather than treated as noise. This
inverts the deficit framing (hormones as disruption) into an asset: schedule a reading for the
phase where the needed axis is at required sensitivity.

## Output rule

A reading resolves to an ACTION (verb), never a label, party, or morality clause (noun).
**Noun output = the instrument was not used, and meaning has been painted over the signal.**

## Pattern cache vs grudge

- **Grudge** = a stored verdict; re-reads every instance through itself. Internal impedance by
  construction.
- **Pattern cache** = store the instances, read the pattern off the cache, retain no verdict.
  Each new instance still gets a clean reading.
