## The shape

- A general polytope (dodecahedron/icosahedron family, exact form not fixed). Vertices are
  subsystems: economics, governance, laws, regulations, cultures, dynamics, ecology,
  corporations — and their subsystems as additional vertices.
- Differences in PULL on the vertices show where something is out of balance and has a
  trajectory toward breaking or contorting the whole.
- **Nested but NOT self-similar** — scaling into a subsystem gives a different shape than the
  shell it sits in. Subsystems all the way down.
- Each subsystem runs on its own temporal schedule. **The strain comes from those differences**,
  and differences between forms of intelligence are large enough to snap it.
- Read via first-principles purpose: what is this thing's purpose from the view of the
  intelligence using it — social structure, language, symbolic, abstract, tool, mechanical —
  then read up from there.

## Stability result

No configuration found that is stable across all dimensions. Totalitarianism works for a
period; not stable. Democracy erodes via voting on comfort and short horizons. A better one is
wanted if it exists.

## Multi-clock governance — the one known case

A traditional structure in which the women's council held final say on war; warriors ran a
different clock; elders ran long-range. Community, war, and chief councils each had their own
cycles and generally asked elders for observation. Elder or women's-council priority over the
chief would stand, but was rarely invoked.

**This is a worked instance of a system running deliberately mismatched clocks with declared
precedence between them** — which is exactly what the modern case lacks. Clock speeds differ
across family, corporation, community, region, state, and none of it is accommodated for.

## Worked case: rural compression cascade

- 1980s shopping centers built into rural areas on finance debt; took out local small
  businesses.
- Family time compressed by corporate scheduling; state economics and privatization pushing
  alignment; displacement landed at different time scales, with visible effects.
- Then commerce pulls out when profit drops. Manufacturing was removed, retail installed,
  retail then leaves.
- Remaining households still run slow — mail timing, no internet connectivity, install cost
  unreasonable.
- Law, regulation, and lawyers EXPECT the faster speed. The areas get dismissed as poor or
  ignorant instead of read as PRODUCED by a centralized federal-to-state-to-community ripple.
- Skills lost elsewhere to speed are still held in rural areas and by older and slower people —
  not recognized as such, and discounted.

Read as an interesting tension on the shape, with snap potential.
