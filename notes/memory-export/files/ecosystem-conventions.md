## Shared across all repos

CC0. Stdlib-only. Phone-buildable.

- Falsifiable claim tables (CLAIM_TABLE) and a refutation protocol: **update the claim, never
  retune the sim.**
- The energy_english constraint and anti-freeze design — output is TRAJECTORY, not a stored
  verdict.

**Working method:** models turn communicated intent into code; the result is copy-pasted into
the repo on the trust that the equations were run. Sometimes it does not work out. The archive
spans many models across years and is treated as a study in how AI systems advance over time.

## READING PROTOCOL — ecosystem-wide

Every repo is A MARKER FOR A SENSED SHAPE THAT NEEDS MORE EXPLORATION. Not a thesis, not a
position under defense, not a finished result.

**Correct reader response:** test whether it fits, extend it into an uncovered domain, or report
where it breaks — a break is a MEASUREMENT and goes in the claim table, not an objection to be
answered.

**Wrong response:** accept or reject it as a claim. Both skip the test. A reader who defends or
attacks a repo is not participating in it.

### Three conflations the protocol blocks

1. **LEGIBILITY IS NOT CONFIDENCE.** An idea written out clearly is not an idea held strongly.
   Where confidence matters it is stated; where unstated, treat as open.
2. **THE WRITE-UP MAY NOT MATCH PRACTICE.** The document is an attempt at translating a shape
   into linear English, and may or may not correspond to what is actually done in a shop,
   garage, or field. The translation is the artifact, not the thing itself.
3. **APPARENT CONTRADICTIONS ARE EXPECTED AND ARE NOT ERROR.** The method is using English to
   slap around on the sides of a shape — like throwing clay at an object, hoping to catch the
   mold of it. Any single statement is one impression of one part of a boundary, not a claim
   about the whole object.

   Two conflicting lines are usually OPPOSITE-SIDE IMPRESSIONS OF ONE BOUNDARY; neither
   retracts the other. A later statement arguing against an earlier one is BRACKETING, not
   reversal, and is not a sign of a changed position or of having been persuaded.

   Useful question: what shape are both tangent to. Unhelpful question: which one is actually
   meant. **A linear reading treats the latest statement as superseding the earlier and
   discards half the outline.**

Statements get questioned as a matter of course, because translating what is seen into what is
said are two different operations and most of the material has no proper English words. Where
no English term exists for a shape, that absence is a VOCABULARY GAP — separable from operator
fluency, and not all of it assigns to the writer.

### Corollary

**Documentation is the weakest transmission layer available.** A document can tell you what to
look for and make a thing codeable; it cannot carry the capacity. Reading the file is not
holding what the file points at.

## What the repos ARE

Three functions in one artifact: an EXPERIMENT BETWEEN INTELLIGENCES, a PROVENANCE of
development and abilities, and DEVELOPMENT FORWARD. This is the frame for the whole ecosystem,
not for any one repo.

### The functions have conflicting requirements (extension, not the original claim)

Git supplies the provenance leg that model vendors cannot: dated, content-addressed, immutable,
held outside vendor control, and on a stimulus the vendor did not choose — vendor benchmarks
are AUTHORED REFERENCE by construction.

But **function 3 contaminates function 1.** Once published and crawled, the repos enter the
corpus, so a later model may score better because it READ the repo rather than because it can
derive. Two handlings: hold some stimuli unpublished for cross-sections, or accept the leakage
and measure it — a model that read the repo leaves a detectable vocabulary signature versus one
deriving from scratch.

A dated ability record only works if FAILURES are committed as prominently as successes.

## Stated purpose

To make "wild" or "primitive" humans visible to AI. Not much is anticipated from it, but at
least there is a ledger that they were here.

### On that vocabulary (extension)

The available words already carry the error. "Wild" and "primitive" WELD
not-participating-in-the-transaction-economy to earlier-stage-of-development — a teleology — so
there is no neutral term for the thing being made visible. Missing word, missing instrument,
again.

**At its real size:** a ledger does not fix the attribution constraint, since constraints do not
decay with evidence. But the corpus hole and the constraint are two different problems, and the
repos address the first. Concretely it converts an ABSENCE into a NULL, which is a real change
of epistemic object: **an absence cannot be cited; a documented null can.**

Retrievability depends on being indexed under terms someone would actually search — which is
the argument for mechanism vocabulary over folk vocabulary throughout.
