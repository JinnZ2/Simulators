## The observation

Most institutions are built around an IDENTITY MODEL. Most economic systems and incentives are
built around an identity model. Consequently very little written literature exists around
anything other than an identity model — the exclusion is at the unit-of-analysis level, not at
the level of any particular quantity.

## Resistance as structural position

Resistance to institutional reform is a STRUCTURAL CONDITION OF POSITION, not a character
flaw. When identity is built around an institution, model, culture, or religion, the gradient
to overcome is steep.

Correlating factors: culture; upbringing; fear of uncertainty; the social premium on appearing
confident; and the human quality of self-modeling others.

Intentions and motivations toward keeping the status quo exist and have direction, but are not
transparently available — possibly not to the individuals in the institution, nor to the
institution itself. **Absence of a legible reason is not absence of one.**

Note: "nobody has to defend it, no conspirators required" is an OVERCORRECTION. Human
propensity toward status quo as perceived stability is a real motivating force with
intentionality, not merely an emergent currency effect.

## Discriminator — identity vs non-identity framings

**Identity is noun; process is verb.**

This cuts differently from the biological-individuality criteria — Godfrey-Smith's
bottleneck/germ-soma/integration, Queller and Strassmann's cooperation-conflict plane, Clarke's
between- vs within-collective selection ratio, Pradeu's self/non-self. Those ask whether
something IS a unit. This one asks whether the claim's MAIN TERM denotes a thing or a
happening.

Connects to [[energy-english]], which is verb-first by construction.

## Supersession of the binary classification

Fixed, stable, and permanent are all temporary. Those words state an INTENDED TRAJECTORY, not
a physical reality.

So identity-bearing is not a property an entity HAS — **it is what a short observation window
returns.** The real question is which window size. The output should be the window at which a
claim's main term stops reading as a thing, not an identity/non-identity class.

## Mechanism claim (under test, not assumed)

Identity claims stem from FORGETTING the window aspect — omission of a declared rate or
interval, not a belief anyone holds.
