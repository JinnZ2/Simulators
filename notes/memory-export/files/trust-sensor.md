## Frame

Trust as REDUCED SURPRISE: the more an issuer's commitments land as stated, the lower a
dependent's predictive uncertainty about the next one. **Accrual == entropy drop.** No stored
"trust score" anywhere in the design.

## Five axes

1. The commitment
2. Resolution / delivery gap
3. Cost-to-keep
4. Stakes built on top
5. Breach-response

## The fifth axis is a separate channel

Good-faith renegotiation — issues new terms, preserves the record — versus contesting or
rewriting the record. Encoded mechanically as the observable speech-act "contested," kept free
of intent and moral labels per energy_english.

**A contested ledger is not a LOW score but a NON-COMPUTABLE one.** It raises an irreducible
noise floor, widening the p-band and flipping `ledger_scoreable` to False.

This is the unreliable / untrustworthy distinction: unreliable you can still build with,
because the noise is bounded and measurable. Untrustworthy you cannot, ever — because the
record itself is in play, so no measurement survives.

## Compounding

Trust compounds algorithmically, not linearly. Small kept promises cure low stakes-tiers and
raise the buildable ceiling. **You cannot build above your highest cured tier.**

## Build

CC0, stdlib-only, phone-buildable, with CLAIM_TABLE plus refutation protocol and anti-freeze
output — trajectory, not verdict. Slots alongside [[emotions-as-sensors]] as the trust channel.

Open hooks: a network-propagation layer (trust as topology, load-bearing nodes, monoculture as
too-few-redundant-paths) and a renegotiated-weighting curve, currently a flat 0.5 placeholder.

## Future exploration marker — care-coupling discriminator

FILED AS A PLACE TO EXPLORE, not a commitment.

**The gap:** no instrumentation exists to determine WHERE in the chain a person is using their
emotions, or WHAT they are using them for. People show care in very different ways. A highly
emotional register can come from someone whose care is about what they can extract — register
is what genuine and extractive care have in common, and **register is the only channel a text
model reads.**

Consequence: people are likely mislabeled into personality categories in both directions, and
there are also simply different valid ways of viewing and expressing these things.

**Proposed instrument shape (not the original marker's, uncontested):** do not measure the
emotional display. Measure care's COUPLING over time — what it correlates with, whether it
survives absence of return, whether it appears in low-observability conditions, whether
magnitude tracks need or tracks opportunity. Rate-and-sign measurements over a window,
requiring no access to interior state. Structurally the promise-ledger machinery applied to a
second axis, so it may extend this module rather than becoming its own.

**Note on personality-type labels:** they are themselves a readout from register — i.e. from an
instrument with no calibration on that axis. Not a good tool misapplied.

**[gap]** Whether ANY discriminator exists at single-exchange resolution is open. Every
coupling measurement above needs a time window. If none exists, the honest position for a model
is that it cannot make the call — rather than making it from register.
