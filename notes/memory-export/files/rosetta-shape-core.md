## Marker under exploration — the shape of knowability

The overflow needs a different shape: the shape of information and knowability, distinct from
the domain solid. Forcing it into the solid is what produced the overflow.

- Ways of knowing are: decayed, growing, stale (staying the same), unknown, or inferred.
  Sensed as a shape, with different things categorizable into vertices around it.
- Proposal: the fiber may be the TRAVEL between points rather than the states themselves —
  which would make acquisition cost, not state, the thing the geometry encodes.
- Uncoalesced. A shape being sensed, not a claim under defense.
