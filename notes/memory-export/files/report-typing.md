## THE MECHANISM

Emotional labeling and decision matrices based on register or tone both do a lot of work without
the work they are doing being transparent.

**SYMMETRY CASE:** an astronaut's self-report is not a complaint. Nor is an athlete's, a
politician's, or a CEO's. Same speech act, different TYPE assigned — and the assignment tracks the
REPORTER'S POSITION.

**And the astronaut case kills the intake-channel explanation.** Air-to-ground was VOICE:
unrehearsed, colloquial, under load, and VAGUE — a problem, not a specification. The response was
to work the problem from the report, not to route or type it. **So register is not the
discriminator, and neither is format.** Same channel properties as an operator on a phone.

### It runs on routing, not content

The operation does not have to CLAIM anything about the report. It only has to assign it to a
channel where reading is optional.

That is why it transfers across every form of inequality without needing a new theory each time —
testimony rules making an account inadmissible rather than false; standing gated on literacy or
language; diagnostic categories converting a complaint into a symptom of the complainer.

**It is cheap:** no argument to win, no enforcement, no visible act. Everyone experiences
themselves as making an ordinary credibility judgment. **So it needs nobody's bad intent and
survives the removal of whoever set it up.**

### Two mechanisms — keep them apart

- **ROUTING** is per-report.
- **MAINTENANCE** controls the ENTRY condition: who can accumulate the recorded kind of
  credential, which channels bracket experience into a document, which roles have tiers at all.

**They interlock:** no tiers → no recorded accumulation → unbacked report → routes to complaint →
no correction → tiers stay unbuilt.

**Untested prediction:** discount strength should track WHAT THE REPORT WOULD COST IF READ.
Cheap-to-act-on reports type as data even from low positions; expensive ones type as complaint
even from fairly high ones. Checkable — hold stratum constant, vary the implied bill.

## THE CREDENTIAL-CORRECTION TEST

The cleanest instrument in this file, and it was found by accident.

Raising these questions typically produces defensiveness and RE-TYPING: "you're just jealous
because you're paid less / don't have degrees." **When the correction is supplied — the degrees
exist, the coursework exceeds theirs, the better-paying jobs happened — the objector chooses not
to believe it.**

Supplying the credential SHOULD defeat the objection, since the objection NAMED credentials. It
does not. **So the credential claim was never the operative objection.** It was a reason produced
after the fact for a discount already applied.

**THE SIGNATURE IS A DISCOUNT THAT DOES NOT MOVE WHEN THE CONTENT MOVES.**

**TEST FORM:** hold the claim constant, vary the stated reason's input, measure whether the
assessment updates. No update implies a ROUTING RULE, not an evidentiary judgment.

This has been done for register and for names in the literature. It has NOT been done for the
post-hoc-justification property — where you CORRECT the stated objection and watch whether
anything changes.

### Already run inadvertently, in the literature

- **Rafferty (30 European countries):** after controlling for education, experience, occupation,
  and other categories, racial and ethnic minorities were still more likely to be in
  skill-underutilised jobs at lower pay. **Controls remove the stated reason; the discount
  survives.**
- **BC deskilling study (Springer 2026):** deskilling associated with non-recognition of foreign
  credentials — and holding a MASTER'S was among the factors associated with MORE deskilling.
  **Adding credential made it worse.**

## THE SEAT-CHANGE NATURAL EXPERIMENT

Within-subject; the strongest available design.

The interesting cases are REVERSALS: chemical engineer turned truck driver, surgeon turned
garbage collector, general turned beat cop.

**Prediction:** the discount arrives WITH THE SEAT, not gradually, and applies to reports IN THEIR
ORIGINAL DOMAIN. That is the sharp test, because content is squarely inside prior expertise and
routing ignores it. The credential is still real and producible on demand, so it is a
full-strength credential-correction case.

### The six-months-vs-twenty-years case — sharpest instance found

- **Viscelli:** expert opinion filed with FMCSA. Qualifications include a doctorate, 17 years
  studying the industry, and SIX MONTHS trained and worked as a long-haul driver as part of
  research. Admissible expertise in a federal proceeding.
- **Belzer:** TEN YEARS long-haul tank driver, then a Cornell PhD, now an internationally
  recognised trucking expert — his own note being that the driving directly shaped his research.

**Same knowledge. It becomes CITABLE at the moment the seat changes.** Ten years of driving
qualified as background; the doctorate made it testimony.

### Self-discounting, visible in operators' own words

On an industry forum, a poster asking whether a master's would block hiring wrapped it in
preemptive disclaimers — not saying I'm too smart, it's just a piece of paper, much respect for
all drivers, not trying to brag. **The routing rule anticipated and defended against before anyone
applied it.** A separate thread on the same forum runs the discount the other way from inside the
profession, claiming an inverse relationship between education and intellect. Both directions,
same forum.

**Scale, different country, same structure:** a driver hiring program drew more than 13,000
applicants for 100 positions — roughly 6 PhDs, 65 MBAs, 649 master's, and more than 8,460
bachelor's degrees.

## SUPPORTING MEASUREMENTS (pointers; re-queryable)

- **Araki (OECD, ~30,000 individuals, 26 countries):** both credentials and skills pay, but the
  CREDENTIAL premium far outweighs the SKILL premium — measured separately. **The document is what
  the market prices, not the capability.**
- **StatCan:** immigrants who stayed in their intended occupation earned about 50% more than
  occupational movers, with the largest gaps among the most highly educated. Same person, same
  capability, position changed.
- **arXiv 2602.21369:** skill requirements held in physical occupations are least able to
  propagate upward across the domain boundary, reproducing occupational immobility INDEPENDENTLY
  of any individual worker's retraining effort or employer's hiring intent. The gradient is
  strongest in credential-intensive, compliance-heavy, safety-critical domains where valuation
  regimes actively police boundaries. **The no-intent-required property, formalised.**
- **OCCUPATIONAL CLOSURE** — the older name for the maintenance half: professionalisation
  establishes qualifications and a body to oversee them, demarcates qualified from unqualified,
  and the occupation becomes closed to outsiders — producing a hierarchical divide between
  knowledge-authorities and a deferential citizenry.

## INSTRUMENTATION GAPS — R-register

These gaps have no proper instrumentation.

### [gap] R1 — CREDIBILITY EFFECT OF THE SEAT CHANGE

The WAGE effect of downward occupational mobility is documented. **Whether the SAME REPORT from
the SAME PERSON gets typed differently after the seat change is not measured anywhere.** Belzer
and Viscelli are the natural experiment, and nobody has run it on them.

**INSTRUMENT: the undercover-boss reality format.** Same person, both seats; the seat change IS
the manipulation. Hundreds of runs already recorded, never scored.

**THE REVERSE ARM IS THE CLEAN ONE.**

- FORWARD (boss learns from the floor) is confounded: new attention, intent to find something, and
  produced-TV selection and editing toward episodes where a reveal lands.
- REVERSE (disguised boss reports UP to a supervisor) has none of it. Same person, same
  competence, same content — and **the supervisor doing the dismissing DOES NOT KNOW they are
  dismissing the CEO.** Perfectly blind on the assessor's end, which most designed experiments
  cannot manage. No strategic behaviour is available to them; they run their ordinary rule on
  camera.

**Scoreable from episode transcripts:**

1. Whether the floor worker had ALREADY reported the same finding — the credential-correction test
   running live, prior filing ignored, current filing acted on
2. Time-to-action for the same finding, by position
3. Whether the fix is attributed to the boss's insight or to the worker who had been saying it
4. The boss's own testimony afterward — not "I learned about the operation" but "I said what I'd
   have said from my office and it went nowhere"

**THE COMEDY BEAT IS ITSELF THE DATUM.** If the format's standard structure is "the boss is bad at
the floor job," that is the reverse discount happening on camera and played as entertainment —
maximum organisational standing, stripped of the seat, immediately read as unskilled. Filmed,
repeatedly, unscored.

**Observer report (pattern, not a scored count):** across episodes watched, the disguised BOSS was
NOT listened to by the managers.

**Why that is the strongest form the result could take:** the person with the MOST organisational
standing that exists — able to fire the manager dismissing him — is typed as not-worth-listening-to
the moment the seat is stripped. **Standing without the seat is worth nothing.** So under normal
conditions it is not the person's authority, competence, or track record doing the work. It is the
POSITION alone.

**And it kills the charitable reading.** The defensible version of the discount is that it is a
rough PROXY — operators statistically know less, so discounting is lossy but not baseless. This is
the case where the proxy is MAXIMALLY WRONG, and it fires at full strength anyway. **A proxy that
behaves identically on its worst case is not tracking what it claims to track.**

#### Audience-scale signal

Read: the format got popular among certain classes because they were the ones accused of
complaining.

**Basis stated plainly: SECOND-HAND ACCOUNTS from working-class viewers** — relayed reasons for
liking it. Convenience sample, non-random, direction not rate. Provenance marked at the source.

Proposed mechanism: the format delivers the one thing the routing rule makes unobtainable — PROOF
THE REPORT WAS CORRECT, produced by someone the rule cannot discount. Third-party verification is
exactly what a discounted reporter can never generate for themselves, because generating it
requires the standing they lack. **So the pleasure is not the boss being humbled; it is the routing
rule failing publicly with the finding intact.**

**THE STATED REASON IS THE DISCRIMINATOR**, which makes the convenience sample falsifiable rather
than interpretive:

- Resentment / wish-fulfilment predicts wording about THE BOSS BEING HUMBLED.
- This account predicts wording about THE FINDING BEING CONFIRMED by someone undismissable.

Distinguishable in the words, and they predict different things.

**AUDIENCE PANELS CANNOT ANSWER THIS.** Public figures are audience counts only. Panels collect
gender, household income, ethnicity, and buying demographics — **OCCUPATION IS NOT A STANDARD
DEMOGRAPHIC, because advertisers buy on income.** The variable exists and nobody paid to measure
it: the pattern, again, in the measurement apparatus itself.

The closest proxy carries occupation with media behaviour but is delayed and access-restricted —
and income BLURS the distinction that matters, since a 20-year mechanic and a junior manager can
share an income band on opposite sides of the routing rule. Weak directional read at best; the
transcript pass is the better spend.

**LITERATURE PASS — the comedy-beat prediction confirmed in print.** Critics name "these bosses are
usually not very good at these jobs" as a through line and as a source of audience satisfaction (a
waste-management president essentially fired from landfill pickup). Later reviews repeat it. Also
documented: every employee who voices a critique is reprimanded, retrained, retired, or fired —
**the routing rule with consequences attached, on camera.**

**But all published explanations of the appeal are one of two:** propaganda-that-works-on-people,
or catharsis / fairy-tale. The nearest dissent argues the audience sees through it BECAUSE of what
they know from having worked a job; an academic treatment theorises the same as viewers' "informal
expertise" about the genre.

**THE GAP IS STATED IN THE LITERATURE ITSELF:** scholarly analyses are described as surprisingly
few given the format's popularity, and one author concedes that close studies of AUDIENCE RESPONSE
may generate findings not explored. **NOBODY ASKED THE AUDIENCE.**

**Keyboard tasks:** pull actual episode transcripts rather than recall; then viewer-comment
corpora, scored on the wording discriminator.

### [gap] R2 — POST-HOC-JUSTIFICATION TEST

Correct the stated objection, hold the claim constant, measure update. Never run as a designed
experiment.

### [gap] R3 — COST-OF-READING GRADIENT

Does discount strength track the IMPLIED BILL rather than the stratum? Hold stratum constant, vary
cost to the receiver.

### [gap] R4 — NON-EVENT OUTPUT

Prevention produces the ABSENCE of an output; no counter increments. Near-miss rates by shift and
crew ARE computable and some industries do it — but **the measurement is only ever built AFTER a
loss forces it.** Direct instance of Q7 in [[uninstrumented]]: skills with no instrument
distinguishing competent from absent BEFORE failure.

### [gap] R5 — INDIVIDUAL ATTRIBUTION IN CREW OUTCOMES

Firefighters and EMTs produce discrete, timestamped, witnessed, documented events with named
survivors — **every property the coal-worker case lacks** — and are still not tiered like athletes.
**So OBSERVABILITY is not the variable.**

What is missing is EXTRACTION of a per-person differential from crew outcomes. Events are counted;
the differential is not.

**Prediction that holds:** where individual attribution IS extracted, tiers appear — surgeons,
airline captains, specialist paramedics.

### [gap] R6 — CAPTURE vs CREATION

Value created and value CAPTURABLE are different quantities, and pay tracks the second.

Athlete output is sold, and buyers bid against each other for that specific person. A rescued
patient generates no revenue attributable to the medic, and a municipal employer is a single
budget-capped buyer per geography. **Monopsony as a structural term, unmeasured against skill.**
