## The symmetry test

Diplomacy and geopolitics reduced to an INVARIANCE CHECK — do-unto-others as method: swap the
actors, hold the action fixed, see if the verdict survives. **If it flips when the names flip,
the judgment was tribal, not about the action.**

## Push the comparison down the pyramid

Compare at base and INTENSIVE units — densities, per-capita, per-node, ownership concentration
— where hidden asymmetry surfaces. Compare intensive vectors, not extensive totals: 1B vs 300M
is an extensive count; the RATIO is what drives.

## Gradient as the thermodynamic driver

A potential difference. Sign gives direction (high to low); magnitude gives pressure toward
flow — migration, capital, force. **The field IS the diplomacy signal.** The engine never rules
on it.

## Oddness

Symmetry formalized as ODDNESS: gradient g(A,B) = -g(B,A), so a consistent reading must be odd
under actor-exchange.

A NON-ODD reading — for example, counting a gradient as pressure only when it runs against you
— is the propaganda shape. The audit localizes the smuggled actor-dependent term to specific
dimensions rather than declaring the whole reading biased.

## Claim-lineage protocol — the reusable spine

An upgrade to the ecosystem refutation convention:

**A refuted claim is a POINTER TO A MISSING VARIABLE, not a tombstone.** Spawn a child =
parent + exposed variable + a NEW independent falsifiable prediction. Genealogy, not graveyard.

**Epicycle guardrail:** a child is admitted only if the new variable is independently
measurable AND predicts beyond rescuing the parent. Otherwise rejected. Without this the
protocol degenerates into unlimited patching.

## Build

CC0, stdlib-only, phone-buildable. The equivalence engine self-hosts its own claims (E1/E2/E3)
in the claim-lineage spine. energy_english held throughout — no aggressor/threat/just fields,
only intensives, potentials, and parity results.
