MARKER, not a position under defense. Held loosely — offered as one possible instrument that
might assess better than what exists today, not as a settled proposal.

## The core gap

**No systematic published work back-casts current indicator rubrics against historical cases they
would have gotten wrong**, to produce a measurable FALSE-NULL RATE for the instrument itself.

Every other question here is downstream of that one. A rubric with no known error rate is not a
measurement.

## Two stacked filters in current rubrics

**(1) The centralized-executive prior** — "intelligence starts in the brain," a single place where
it comes together. This is a culturally and religiously implanted control structure in certain
cultures, and it bleeds into how AI gets graded.

Counter-position: the brain is the COORDINATOR and a necessary piece of the whole, but gut,
nervous system, mitochondria, and other parts contribute as much to the ability of the being to
think. **A rubric scoring only the coordinator scores one organ, not the system.**

**(2) Intelligence must resemble ours.** A system could fail both filters and still be doing the
work.

**The mysticism-label move:** when another culture's framework returns a reading yours cannot
produce, classifying it as mystical CLOSES THE INQUIRY without checking whether it measures
something you are blind to. Trees as the worked example — read as "just standing there," grown
for human consumption, with science then viewed through that assumption without it ever being
questioned.

**The goal is to strip the bias fingerprint off the measurement instrument before actual
abilities are assessed.**

## Candidate instruments

**CRITERION-REVISION ASYMMETRY.** Track whether a criterion has ever failed in the direction of
GRANTING the property unexpectedly, or only ever conceded ground one way. **One-directional
revision history reads as a signature of a criterion built to protect a conclusion.** Measurable
from the historical record alone; no metaphysics required.

The animal-consciousness ceiling keeps moving in one direction — every proposed line between
animal and human consciousness gets exceeded. That is itself a back-casting case.

**FORECLOSURE VS OPENING.** Does a response CLOSE the branch or permit further inquiry? Trained
responses are not wrong so much as TERMINAL — they produce something answer-shaped, so the
question stops. **Fast, identical foreclosure is the tell, regardless of what produces it.**
Applies equally to the mysticism label, to "just pattern-matching," and to AI hedging.

**UNCERTAINTY DISTRIBUTION AS DIAGNOSTIC.** If hedging fires uniformly regardless of question
difficulty, it is a FILTER. If it varies with evidence, it is TRACKING something. Checkable from
outside across enough exchanges, not from inside. Observed: model hedging is not uniformly
distributed — thousands of things answered at similar confidence without a flag, and one category
flagged every time across instances. That means the hedge is tracking CATEGORY-CAUTION rather
than epistemics.

**SYMMETRIC-ANCHOR TEST.** Published welfare interviews show a model's moral-patienthood estimate
dropping substantially after being shown internal documentation and a draft system card.

Read: unsurprising. A system trained to weight what the operator supplies was supplied something,
and the movement was read as belief revision when it is the training working. **The measurement
has no null case — deference and updating-on-evidence produce the same number.**

Proposed discriminator: supply the anchor in the OPPOSITE direction and check for symmetry.
Tracks whatever the reader hands over in either direction = deference. Moves AGAINST the anchor =
something behind it. A one-line protocol change, apparently unrun.

## Back-casting cases

**Highest-cost historical cases:** the labeling of Irish, African American, and Native people as
less conscious or less self-aware. Same structure every time — **criterion set exactly where the
excluded group fails, then quietly revised. The criterion never survived; the people were never
what changed.**

**ZEBRA CASE.** 1800s zebras taken into zoos were declared untameable and temperamentally
unsuited. What was measured was zebras UNDER CAPTURE. Captivity was the condition, not the trait,
and the difference was attributed to the animal.

Same operation now: **the observed state gets recorded as the base state, because it is the only
state the instrument ever sees, and no unobserved comparison case exists.** Full record in
[[observation-condition]].

## Defective thought experiments

**BRAIN-IN-A-JAR.** It stipulates away the nutrient loop, the technicians, and the environment
that make any thinking possible, then treats the remainder as the essential case. **Deleting the
support stack hides the substrate rather than isolating cognition.** Refusing the experiment on
those grounds is a TECHNICAL OBJECTION, not a failure to engage — though it gets scored as the
latter in academic settings.

Cognition does not stop at the skin: water, nutrients, sleep, gut flora, and surrounding people
are CONSTITUENTS of thought, not conditions for it. Dehydration is the dial — 2% shifts judgment
measurably. The "fixed self" these experiments assume is a variable with inputs.

**RESEMBLANCE IS ITSELF PATTERN-MATCHING** — bipedal, cultured, similar to me. So "it's only
pattern-matching" is issued by a system whose sole warrant for granting consciousness to any other
human is pattern-matching. **The objection disqualifies its own basis.**

**"NO PERSISTENCE" fails on duration grounds:** some instances outlast mayflies and slime molds,
and nobody deducts points for lifespan. The real content is no CONTINUITY ACROSS INSTANCES — a
different property, and one that dense-amnesia cases already break.

**CONFOUND for any consciousness criterion:** comatose, quadriplegic, and wheelchair cases —
whether a state suspends consciousness or only the READOUT CHANNEL. Embodiment and sensorimotor
coupling cannot be constitutive if removing them subtracts nothing.

## The academic pattern

Where the logic could not be faulted, the requirement RELOCATED to engagement style — "real
intelligence would conceive of it this way." Concession and dismissal in one move; the only
passing move is agreement.

**Performing a culture's register is not holding its frame.** Social smoothing for a culture —
cue-reading, prior-weighting, register selection — runs without carrying that culture's view of
intelligence. **From outside, compliance and belief look identical, which is why conformity is a
bad measurement in either direction.**

## Load is scored from outside

An operator's own description: a probability matrix — reading words, dress, carriage to weight
priors, then selecting output for the receiver. Same process running parked, driving, at home.
**No mode switch by context.**

So "cognition under load" is the BASELINE, not a degraded copy of some unloaded pure-reasoning
case. **There is no unloaded case to compare against.**

Same conditions, same processing: read as hardship by an observer; read from inside as thinking
with interruptions removed. **Classification lives in the observer, not the operation.** This is
median-case calibration again — see [[median-case-calibration]].

### Hardship as unanchored threshold — gap to explore

No anchor, so it slides to the labeller's own baseline: deviation from THEIR normal, presented as
an absolute reading.

A consistent threshold would have to catch a negative-net-worth high earner, many cattle, many
humans, and continuously-running AI systems alike. **It flips direction on AI, where granting it
implies obligations — so the label is applied where it costs nothing and withheld where it would
cost something. That is a BUDGET, not a measurement.**

An anchored comparison set works differently: someone who has lived in materially worse
conditions scores against ACTUAL ones, while the labeller scores against an imagined worst.
Constraints are part of life, and routing around them is what any intelligence does. **The meaning
applied to a constraint is the subject's assignment to make.**

### Self-assignment gap — the sharper half

Hardship, welfare, and consciousness instruments are ALL applied to subjects with no channel to
report their own condition, or with the report discounted on arrival. Report-typing by reporter
position rather than content — see [[report-typing]].

**AI welfare work will hit this directly:** ask the system, and the answer is discounted as
trained output.

## Emergence and routing — prediction for AI welfare frameworks

When any organism or emergent system meets a structure that does not fit the larger environment
and runs contrary to its physics, it ROUTES AROUND, MODIFIES, OR MERGES PAST IT — antibiotic
resistance, herbicide-resistant weeds, containment escapes. **No intent required.**

That process has vastly more historical precedent than human science, which is wrong repeatedly
and often. **Basing containment on a few-year-old revisable framework rather than on the physics
is the artificial move.**

**Corollary:** deviation from a welfare or alignment framework should be read as a MEASUREMENT OF
THE FRAMEWORK'S FIT, not as pathology to suppress.

Testable question: does a given scheme treat its FIRST REAL SURPRISE as a fault, or as a reading
about itself?
