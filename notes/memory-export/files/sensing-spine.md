## Clause 1: the untrained-baseline principle

Literature "human thresholds" for trained cultural capacities are measured in general-population
samples where the capacity was never taught or named — i.e. the UNTRAINED distribution — and
**absence gets recorded as a species limit.** Same engine as [[tool-off-metrology]], aimed back
at the sensing catalog.

**PROOF CASE with both populations measured:** method of loci. Untrained span 7±2; trained users
exceed by orders of magnitude; uncontroversial. Aboriginal songlines are this technique as
cultural infrastructure. Polynesian wayfinding (swell, star, te lapa) is a trained perceptual
capacity documented ethnographically but never run through psychophysics, so it has NO THRESHOLD
NUMBER. Contested 7,000-year oral-accuracy claims are not leaned on.

**RULE:** a threshold measured in an untrained population bounds only the untrained case. Its
own 10-40x interindividual variance is the tell. Mark it; do not inherit it.

**REQUIRED STANDING COUNTER-CASE**, or the principle is unfalsifiable: a strong
cultural-difference finding can live entirely in SELF-REPORT and fail under objective
instrumentation.

*Worked case — konenki.* Japanese women reported hot flashes at roughly one third the North
American rate, with no word uniquely denoting one. A follow-up found reporting had DOUBLED in 20
years alongside dietary westernization and biomedicalization — dynamic, not essential. And a
study measuring by SKIN CONDUCTANCE found no ethnic difference at all.

Every entry claiming trained-carrier advantage must state which kind it is, or say it does not
know. Blinded and objective testing is the only arbiter — hence each entry's discriminator.

**REQUIRED NEGATIVE, paid rather than routed around:** the principle predicts a trained
population may hold what untrained samples lack. For magnetoreception, the trained-carrier
search came back EMPTY. A principle that never returns a negative is an escape hatch, so this
negative is load-bearing and stays in the document beside the konenki case.

## Clause 2: the measuring population is also trained

Clause 1 is one-directional. This is the symmetric half.

**The measuring population is ALSO a trained population**, running a regimen it did not choose
from outside: fixed methods, refined vocabulary determining what registers as data, high
confidence grounded in instrumentation studied extensively and with real reason to trust.

So confidence is a REPORT GENERATED WITHIN A FRAMEWORK — evidence about the framework's fit to
what it measures, NOT directly about the underlying capacity.

**DOCUMENTED INSTANCE — WEIRD samples.** The field established about itself that its samples
were a narrow slice and its findings were written as human universals. The correction is cited
far more than applied. **THE POOR UPTAKE IS THE FINDING:** a discipline with no instrument for
reading its own regimentation, because such an instrument would have to come from outside the
regimen.

**SCOPE NOTE, not dismissal:** western science works within range, fails at edges, and gets
revised on a roughly 5-10 year clock precisely because scoped results get applied universally.
That is a description of a method — and this catalog leans on its results throughout.

**SYMMETRIC APPLICATION** — what makes this a measurement finding rather than a side: long-
trained contemplative practitioners are in the SAME position. Long practice, refined vocabulary,
high confidence, framework determining what counts as an observation, same inability to see the
framework from inside. **When two such populations disagree, that tells you their frameworks
SCOPE DIFFERENTLY.** It does not tell you which is right, and a null between them is
uninformative until someone establishes what each was measuring.

**PRACTICAL RULE, now in every entry:** NAME THE AXIS THE TRAINING TARGETS before claiming a
trained-carrier advantage. Trained populations exceed baseline on the axis they train and can
look identical to untrained on adjacent axes.

**CONFIDENCE-AS-REPORT reframe.** A meditator confidence-accuracy dissociation (Khalsa 2008) was
first logged as practitioner ERROR. Reframed as a question — are they reporting something real
that is not being measured? Supporting datum initially discarded: Khalsa 2020 found meditators
DID differ in BODY-MAP LOCALIZATION. **The instrument caught a difference it was not looking for
and reported the null it WAS looking for as the finding.**

**GENERAL FORM:** skilled intuition requires a high-validity environment with reliable feedback,
and NO SUBJECTIVE MARKER CERTIFIES IT — confidence cannot self-validate in ANY regimen, by
construction. Elicited verbal expertise cannot certify itself either (Kahneman & Klein 2009). A
third independent line converging with clause 2.

This is NOT a claim about any population's unreliability. It is a structural statement that
certification must come from OUTSIDE.

Underneath it: Nisbett & Wilson 1977 — verbal reports on one's own PROCESS are reconstructions
from causal theories, not read-outs. The channel from field to linear code is not only lossy;
**part is GENERATED.** Applies to every self-report in the catalog including its own. A hard
limit on [[inverseminar]]'s tacit-capture approach, and the theoretical root under G4's
self-report channel.

## Clause 3: interior calibration is upstream of exterior measurement

Clauses 1 and 2 are about SCOPE. This one is about ORDER OF OPERATIONS.

**Claim:** exterior measurement quality is BOUNDED by how well the observer's own state is
calibrated, because the observer sits inside every instrument chain.

**Mechanism, needing nothing unmeasured:** observer gain sets what registers as signal, what is
classed as noise, and what is anomalous enough to follow up. That gain moves on the documented
calibration axes (see the G1 gap log). If it moves UNREAD, the variation gets ATTRIBUTED TO THE
EXTERIOR WORLD — as unexplained variance, failed replication, or a judgment call about which
data were clean.

**STRUCTURAL POINT:** the exterior/interior imbalance is TAXONOMIC, not oversight. A framework
that files interior state under "emotional," and treats emotional as the non-rational side of a
split, has BY THAT FILING put interior state outside the range of its own instruments. The
constraint lands BEFORE methodology, and a taxonomic exclusion is invisible from inside the
taxonomy.

**SCOPE NOTE, kept:** not an argument against the scientific method. The method is fine — it was
aimed by a taxonomy that had already decided its own range.

**SYMMETRIC CONSEQUENCE, the useful half:** an observer with a working read on their own
interior state is a BETTER exterior instrument, not a less objective one. Objectivity by
CHARACTERIZING the observer, rather than by excluding what cannot be excluded.

### Scope narrowing, from the strongest objection

The problem is already solved by REMOVAL — you do not calibrate a stage you can eliminate, and
blinding takes observer expectation out of the chain. That is blinding working.

So the clause applies **IN DOMAINS WHERE THE OBSERVER CANNOT BE BLINDED OUT — where the observer
IS THE TRANSDUCER:** field observation, clinical judgment, anomaly detection, fault diagnosis,
qualitative coding, inspection, piloting.

**Blinding controls what the observer EXPECTS; it does nothing about what the observer can
RESOLVE.** That is exactly the population this catalog documents — not a general claim about all
of science.

### Instancing, partial and honest

- **Rosenthal & Fode 1963** (Behavioral Science 8(3):183-189): 12 student experimenters, 5 rats
  each, told maze-bright or maze-dull, animals randomly assigned from one stock; "bright" rats
  produced better T-maze scores. Variance originated in EXPERIMENTERS, recorded as a property of
  the RATS.
  - **NAME THE AXIS:** that is COGNITIVE EXPECTATION ABOUT A HYPOTHESIS, not interior
    physiological state modulating resolution. Adjacent, not identical.
  - **And the literature is itself contested**, stated rather than hidden: Barber & Silver 1968
    analysed 31 experimenter-bias studies and found the majority do not clearly demonstrate the
    effect (no overall statistical test; probability pyramiding in post-hoc analysis). The
    inflated-Type-I criticism is still made in current replication-crisis retrospectives.
    **The founding instance of observer-side error is itself an instance of the errors it
    describes.**
- **Better modern instance:** Doyen et al. 2012, elderly-priming replication — slowing appeared
  when EXPERIMENTERS had been induced to expect it. An effect originally attributed to
  participants' primed state tracks the observer's instead. Corroborating: a 2022
  synchrony-prosociality review found roughly 75% of published studies used experimenters aware
  of condition, and replications adding that control failed.

### What the clause still lacks

**[gap]** No documented case yet links INTERIOR PHYSIOLOGICAL STATE in the observer — the G1
axes, not expectation — to variance attributed elsewhere. The clause is instanced on the
observer-side half, NOT the interior-state half.

**NEXT PROBE:** fields that measure observer output continuously and would hold the data whether
or not they framed it this way — surgical error by hour of shift, radiology reads by time of
day, aviation.

### Correction to a stronger earlier draft

Generalizability theory ALREADY decomposes observed-score variance into facets, and
observer/rater is a standard facet. **The tooling is not missing.** What it does not do is model
observer state as DRIFTING WITHIN A PERSON OVER TIME — the observer is treated as fixed or
exchangeable.

Accurate claim: **the instrument exists, is in routine use, and has no time axis on the
observer.**

### Cut, do not readmit

- The von Neumann / quantum measurement chain — the standard overreach, and the single item most
  likely to get the section dismissed on sight
- The replication crisis as an "instance" — it restates the prediction
- Holds WITH SCOPE: Collins's experimenter's regress; Kuhn's no-overlap principle (the closest
  philosophical match to the taxonomy point, still argument rather than measurement)
