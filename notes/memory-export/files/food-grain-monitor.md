Probability field model over a four-axis condition space: heat, water, season length, soil
fertility.

Resilience scored as cell coverage plus redundancy across a geographic envelope gradient —
so a system with high yield concentrated in few cells scores below one with lower yield spread
across many, which is the inverse of how production statistics rank them.
