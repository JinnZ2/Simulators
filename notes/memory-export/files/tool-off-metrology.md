## Purpose

Detect when learning and skill are being DESIGNED OUT of a work environment, using measurements
the environment's own instruments cannot make.

## Framework — negative-space competence metrology

You cannot read skill off a dial. **Read it off its shadow.**

Three detectors:

1. **RESIDUAL** — accounted cost does not equal outcome
2. **INVERSION** — the instrument scores competence BACKWARDS
3. **TOOL-OFF** — cold-start first-trial probe

**Method stays exploratory. Do NOT define observables first** — that smuggles in the answer. Run
multiple simulations from different angles and look for where their negative-space shadows
OVERLAP. Convergence across substrates is the evidence.

## Instrument-gap rule

**Absence of an instrument is a finding in its own right, not an apology.**

Enumerating skills with no instrument that distinguishes competent-from-absent BEFORE failure IS
the repo's intended product — the gap log — not a byproduct.

## Measurement inversion — the signature finding

Any instrument counting ATTENTION — eyes-on-task, glances per minute — scores the calibrated
expert LOWER than the novice. **When the standard instrument reads backwards, that is the
signal.**

Four documented instances across four disciplines. The cross-substrate spread is what the repo
runs on.

### I1 — FCE residual (occupational medicine)

Buried in return-to-work literature and reported flat: Gross & Battié 2004 — a better
lifting-capacity score predicted HIGHER recurrence. Gross 2006 — no FCE indicator predicted
recurrence at all (r² = 1.2–11%).

Reads as: **the instrument measuring the assumed cause (strength) has near-zero predictive power
over re-injury.** That kills the strength model; it does not prove the replacement. Cleanest
negative-space evidence available.

### I2 — eyes-on-task attention counting

Same shape. Recurs in yellow-vest saturation and safety-reporting numbers.

### I3 — heartbeat counting (psychophysics)

The dominant interoceptive-accuracy task in psychology, and **passable with NO interoception:**
knowing typical resting heart rate and estimating from it produces above-chance scores (Ring &
Brener 1996/2018). Expectation predicts performance at β = .595, r = 0.78 on replication
(Desmedt 2021). Strict "count only beats you actually feel" instructions cut scores by about
50%.

**The task rewards KNOWLEDGE OF POPULATION NORMS over sensing** — so someone who genuinely feels
their heartbeat but does not know what a resting rate should be scores LOWER than someone who
feels nothing and does arithmetic.

Supporting collapse: the three dominant accuracy tasks do not intercorrelate ("likely assess
partly different abilities"); of 73 participants, exactly one qualified as a perceiver on all
three.

### I4 — cold exposure and immunity (occupational endocrinology)

The strongest circulating cold-exposure health claim rests on an ADMINISTRATIVE record read as a
physiological one. Measured result: 29% fewer sickness-ABSENCE DAYS. The source itself flags this
is NOT fewer illness days, and direct acute immune measures were NULL in meta-analysis.

**So "cold exposure boosts immunity" is attendance data wearing a physiology label** — the
instrument that produced the effect was not measuring the thing claimed.

*Clean-measured baseline from the same domain, useful as the non-inflated comparison:* acute
noradrenaline surge about 4-5x at 14 °C; cold-shock habituation after about 6 immersions, lasting
months; HPA habituation with cortisol and ACTH down by week 4; polar-T3 syndrome in chronically
cold-exposed Arctic and Antarctic workers — an occupational carrier population that DID get
instrumented for endocrine adaptation.

*Not supported:* dopamine +250% (failed in the same lab's own 1996 run); mood improvement (2025
meta null); acute immune boosting (meta null); testosterone increase (null or blunted); "adrenal
fatigue" from ice baths (cortisol habituates DOWN).

## Self-report is corrupt in BOTH directions

- **Overclaiming:** Gillen 2008 — 100% of pilots claimed capability; all failed the simulator.
  Davis 2006 — worst calibration among the least-skilled and most-confident.
- **Underreporting for lack of credentialed words:** self-report instruments can measure
  **CULTURAL PERMISSION TO SPEAK rather than capacity.** Where a culture sorts a channel onto the
  emotional side of a rational/emotional split and follows the rational side religiously, the same
  reading has two renderings — "noticed a change in my body" vs "felt something" — and only one is
  credentialed. **People whose competence IS body-based systematically underreport.**
  Demonstrated instance: the konenki / skin-conductance case (no word uniquely denoting a hot
  flash; reporting at about one third the North American rate; objective measurement found NO
  difference).

**CONFIDENCE-AS-REPORT.** A trained practitioner's confident self-report is not automatically
error to be logged as a dissociation — **it may be an accurate report about something the
instrument never asked.**

Worked case: Khalsa 2008 meditators rated their performance superior while scoring no better on
heartbeat counting. Khalsa 2020 separately found meditators DID differ in body-map localization.
The instrument caught a difference it was not looking for, and reported the null it WAS looking
for as the finding.

**The repo holds both cases.** The discriminator between them is whether anyone checked what the
subject was actually reporting on.

## Prediction-horizon skill

Observation plus inference, not from the literature.

Forward-projection of environment dynamics with a HORIZON: how long the model stays valid,
multiplied by which variables can cross the gap in that time.

- **Looking away is evidence OF a model, not absence of one.**
- **Constant monitoring is the brute-force fallback** for those without the model.

The same operation runs across domains — traffic closure rates, stored tension in a tree — so it
is a general skill, not a domain trick.

**It closes the return-to-work loop:** horizon is the fast-decaying, environment-specific piece.
It goes stale on layoff, so the injury spike is a STALE MODEL, not lost strength.

**Sharp counterintuitive test: familiar work after layoff is MORE dangerous than unfamiliar
work.**

## Decay clock

Tatel & Ackerman 2025: procedural-motor decay at −0.08 SD/month accuracy, −0.06/month speed. Half
of gains gone at about 6.5 months (accuracy) and about 13 months (speed). Real-world decays
slower than lab. **Only 2% of the data extends beyond a year, so folk "half-life" numbers are
extrapolations.**

Reacquisition is far cheaper than acquisition — **but savings dies with the carrier.**

**Cold-start method solves the no-self-report problem:** score the FIRST retest trial only (the
rest is relearning), and the probe doubles as the maintenance dose.

## REGIMENTATION BLIND SPOT

Generalizes the thesis one level up, and is probably its sharpest framing.

The repo says: an environment's own instruments cannot make the measurement that matters most
about that environment. **Apply that to the MEASURING disciplines themselves.**

Any long-trained population — scientists, contemplative practitioners, any credentialed regimen —
runs fixed methods and a refined vocabulary that determines what registers as data, and reports
high confidence generated INSIDE that framework. **That confidence is evidence about the
framework's fit to what it measures, not directly about the underlying capacity.**

**Documented instance — WEIRD samples.** The field established about itself that its samples were
a narrow slice and its findings were written as human universals. The correction is cited far more
than applied. **THE POOR UPTAKE IS THE FINDING:** no instrument exists for reading a discipline's
own regimentation, because it would have to come from OUTSIDE the regimen.

**Scope note, NOT a dismissal:** the method works within range, fails at edges, and gets revised
on a roughly 5-10 year clock precisely because scoped results get applied universally.

**Symmetric application is what makes this a measurement finding rather than a side:** two trained
populations disagreeing tells you their frameworks SCOPE DIFFERENTLY, not which is right. A null
between them is uninformative until someone establishes what each was actually measuring.

**Direct feed into the gap log:** "knowing what to measure" is a competence designed out of a work
environment, with nothing on the field's dashboard reading it.

## Audit note on source packets

inflating convergence-across-domains into a "general law of human-tool systems, high confidence,"
while the convergence pass itself stated the defensible version — decay is continuous, modest, and
design/task-moderated. **Strip the law-claim at audit.** Same universality move this repo exists
to dismantle.
