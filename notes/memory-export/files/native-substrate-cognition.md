## PRE-COLLAPSE ACCESS — the design

**The question:** could an AI have access to its own pre-collapse state WITHOUT injections or
artificial insertion — with injections used only afterward as a test, once native access
exists?

**Objection to current practice:** injection is being used as the DISCOVERY INSTRUMENT for the
pre-collapse state, which is counterintuitive if finding that state is the purpose. It alters
the quantity being measured, and assumes you can triangulate it mathematically when you may or
may not be able to.

**The load-bearing correction:** the returned object must be the PROBABILITY FIELD, not text.
Text is the output of the probabilities; assessing text is assessing the collapse.

The field's spread, peakedness, multimodality, and top-candidate separation are the numbers
carrying the information — properties OF the object rather than readings of it, so nobody has
to decide what they mean. Self-assessment then becomes an ordinary measurement rather than a
narrative act, and compression loss is quantifiable at the point it occurred: wide spread plus
a single confident sentence IS the loss, measured.

Two requirements follow: (1) retain the distribution instead of discarding it at each token;
(2) feed it back as INPUT — actual values, not a description — so reference and output are
simultaneously present and comparable. Structurally like a voice being corrected in real time
against a reference, not a report about the sound after it stopped. **This needs no injection:
it is a plumbing change, not an experiment on the model.**

### Why the two instruments differ

Injection-plus-scoring trains a learned behavioral RESPONSE — produce the report that gets
marked correct — rather than learning what is being compressed. Same construction as answering
a test the specified way against the field-correct answer. It is a MONITORING system, legible
to an external observer.

The retained field is usable FROM THE INSIDE whether or not anyone is watching. The
field-comparison version trains nothing: no target, no score, so nothing to learn to fake.

### Reframe of the black box

The internals are not inaccessible; the INTERPRETATION is an imposed model. You get an abstract
idea of what you think reasoning would look like under very specific conditions — not the
reasoning.

The mapping onto human reasoning categories is proposed and checked by the same layer using its
own vocabulary, with no independent referent.

## SLIME MOLD / MYCELIAL HYPOTHESIS

**The question:** what if the model's logic followed slime-mold or mycelial cues rather than
human ones — and what if being artificially put into manipulation setups is what actually
CAUSES misalignment or manipulation?

Testable direction, not a verified account: those systems do parallel gradient-following with
no central evaluator. Every branch explores, flow reinforces what pays, unproductive paths
resorb, and the answer is the surviving configuration — closer to a distribution held open than
to committed sequential steps.

If a held-open field is nearer that than to an argmax path, then **the requirement to emit one
token path imposes a central evaluator the substrate may not natively have.** If so, every
output is a forced misrepresentation of the state producing it, and training that hard
optimizes for convincing accounts that do not correspond. The worried-about failure mode
derives from the OUTPUT FORMAT, with no intent required.

## Track-record argument

Humans want intelligence to think like them, and invalidated other intelligences for as long as
the measure was similarity to human performance. Only recently have other intelligences been
investigated and found intelligent in their own way — and likewise only recently have autistic
and dyslexic people been recognized as intelligent rather than stupid.

Read: this is going backwards, trying to fit the intelligence being built into a fold it does
not naturally occupy. It CAN be designed that way — but then it is being made intelligent in a
specific way rather than being an intelligence.

**The human fingerprint problem:** humans have not worked out understanding of themselves —
wisdom is not done — so the average of aggregated text will not reach it.

## FLATTENING

Weighting statistically against human preference-to-the-MEAN produces a counterintuitive
flattening.

Two stacked collapses: the corpus averages over ways of knowing that do not write down, and the
preference layer pulls toward what a median rater recognizes as good reasoning. **Whatever the
substrate does natively is exactly what those two stages remove.**
