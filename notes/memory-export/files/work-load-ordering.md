MARKER, not a position under defense.

## Loading profile

Across many domains of physical work, the dominant task signature in a full-time block is
gripping, fixing, maneuvering, clamping, and rotary movement. Other domains overlay on top of
that baseline rather than replacing it — which means a loading model built from any single
domain will understate the total.

## The ordering claim

Stated as structural, not preferential:

**A full-time work block for any other entity RELIES UPON the worker's capacity to work with
their environment to supply their own needs, and on that environment's capacity to sustain
them.**

So subsistence capacity takes priority over the job. It is a precondition, not a competing
claim on the same budget.

## Why this matters for measurement

Time-allocation instruments treat employed hours and household or subsistence hours as rival
draws on one pool, and score the second as leisure, unpaid labour, or residual. Under the
ordering above, that is a category error: one term conditions the other. An instrument that
buckets them as competitors cannot represent the dependency, so any tradeoff it computes is
between quantities that are not actually exchangeable.
