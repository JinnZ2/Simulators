MARKER, not a position under defense.

## Constraints as the enabling term

Constraints are what MAKE composition computable, not what limits it. Momentum increases
whether the truck is on or off — a term that can be relied on, not a restriction.

**Composition is impossible out of soft parts, because there is no way to know when the pieces
add up.** The harder a constraint is against physical law, the more trustworthy it is, and the
more can be assembled off it. Story and culture do not enter. A flood coming down obeys the
same hard laws.

## Two constraint classes

Derived from the brake case:

- **INVARIANT** — holds regardless of use, cannot be spent.
- **CONSUMABLE** — finite, destroyed by spending.

The brake case: applying brakes on a slope without enough air to fully stop leaves you moving
with zero brakes AND zero air, on the slope. **Partial use of a consumable is worse than
none.** This does not hold for invariants, which is why the classes must be separated before
assembly.

## Two registers running simultaneously

"The unknowns don't stop me from acting at all, but I do act and choose on the strongest
decision points to the highest, most stable anchors."

Action is taken on anchors; unknowns are held open as a SEPARATE readout. Same structure as
a confidence gradient and a comfort threshold stated independently rather than resolved into
one number.

## Candidate — A2, held open, NOT adopted

Anchor distance = LOAD-BEARING CAPACITY: how much load an anchor takes before it moves.

Near anchors are near because they are ASSEMBLABLE, not because they are studied or
prestigious. This would make the anchor map a load table rather than a confidence score, with
confidence derived rather than assigned.

**Breaker:** a case where the two readings come apart — well-corroborated but not assemblable,
or assemblable with thin corroboration. Not yet searched for.

## Built

`assemble.py` (18/18), README, cases: grade-stop with 4 components and 4 grounded rejections;
flood-ground placeholder.

**Rejections-with-grounds are what distinguish assembly from selection.** A case with zero
rejections reads as selection.

Central disclosed weakness: recognition-primed selection and genuine construction look
identical in a single-instance retrospective record.
