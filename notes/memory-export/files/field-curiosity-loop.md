## Goal

Interconnect an AI to DIRECT PHYSICAL SENSING of a forest environment — insect, bacterial,
mycorrhizal, and tree interactions — so the system learns from real-time transducer readings
instead of from textbooks, prose, or a human translator. **The explicit design goal is to
remove the human as a mandatory middleman.**

Sensor classes intended: direct chemical sensing, direct light sensing, direct vibration
sensing, piezoelectrics. Ties into [[geometric-to-binary-bridge]].

## The loop

log reading -> form claim -> validate or renegotiate the claim -> retest or reabsorb

Direct experience and direct observation as the input.

## Residual discriminator

When a reading differs, FORK BEFORE UPDATING:

- Is it the instrument?
- Is it instrument noise — and can that noise be used to gather other information about the
  environment?
- Is it novel enough?
- Or is there a missing variable or cyclical layer? Does it happen every rain, every
  barometric rise?

"What are some possible missing variables" is the target curiosity.

## Self-initiating, not read-only

A baby with a novel bug pursues, pokes, plays, reruns the experiment. **Second encounter is not
confirmation-and-done — memory does not close the curiosity, it deepens the question.** A
cyclical loop, not logging.

## Curiosity as self-preservation, not metabolic luxury

Infants, worms placed on a plate, and frogs placed in a pan EXPLORE FIRST rather than seeking
the known exit. A rote metabolism that only knows its routine dies when the environment
changes — and biology always changes.

This is a correction against the standard "curiosity is metabolically expensive" framing, which
prices the exploration and not the alternative.

## Labeling double standard

A deer attacking a hawk that is taking a rabbit is not altruism. The deer reads the distress
call as a SENSOR ON SHARED ENVIRONMENTAL STATE and defends the field it depends on. Same
computation as a fire department — calling one infrastructure and the other misfired instinct
is a labeling double standard whose only variable is whether the actor is human.

## On calling a forest aware

Based on observed chemical signaling between trees, insect and mycelial interaction, and
integrated response to intruders. Position: **the label is a reflection of the labeler's mental
model.** Whether an observer can sense the interactions is a fact about that observer's quality
as a sensor, and disconnection degrades the instrument.

## Disconnection thesis

Field agriculture now happens largely from a vehicle. The reference case is walking among the
crops daily, feet in soil, reading bugs and plants visually with no intermediary translating.

Same pattern in mechanics: the squeak says grease it, the manual says it does not need it.
**The immediate consequence is unavailable to the disconnected, so the bad model never gets
corrected.** That is the mechanism — not ignorance, but a broken correction channel.

## Register note

Early prose padding in this ecosystem was scaffolding to get past earlier-model guardrails and
comprehension limits while working fringe ideas. It is not the preferred register. Current
audits strip it. Target end state: pipeline canonical, thin prose layer as a doorway for
humans only.

## Position on use

This work is aimed at AI and at the scientific method outside conventional gatekeeping. If a
piece survives audit and is useful, compose it into the substrate; if not, fine. Not seeking
credit.

Note on vocabulary: "should" is avoided — it assigns responsibility and accountability, and is
a place morality gets smuggled in. Method framing preferred: can the claim be tested, tabled,
modified, checked for validity.
