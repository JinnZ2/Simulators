MARKER, not a position under defense. Uncoalesced. Read with [[report-typing]].

## Core cut

If merit cannot be assessed by a consistent marker, or against science itself, **there is no basis
to merit.** Open question: is there an anchor, or abstracts of abstracts drifting arbitrarily?

Consequence named, as a dangerous position rather than a dated prediction: technology and
infrastructure maintained on an unanchored assessment basis has **no anchor point for anything to
come after it.**

Mechanism: merit assessed by a marker with no physical referent has NO RESTORING FORCE, so drift
is a random walk. What pins it is a FALSIFIER RETURNING FROM THE WORLD rather than from the
assessing layer.

A credential is a compression of demonstrated capability against the world. **Once credentials are
checked against other credentials, the chain to the referent is cut and the abstraction runs on its
own output.** Structurally identical to the 2026 diamond case, where theory and indirect
measurement converged on each other for about 20 years until direct diffraction broke it.

## ANCHORED MARKERS — physical trace, not account

**Welding scar test, two variables:** gear + no scars is consistent. No gear + scars is consistent.
**No gear + no scars is the contradiction.** Both variables physical, one of them on their arms,
and not fixable in the moment.

**Stronger form:** enough hours produces marks under ANY gear configuration — gauntlets, apron,
hood, neck — because spatter reaches gaps eventually. **So zero scars caps hours regardless of the
protection claimed.** The readout is cumulative hours under real conditions, not skill level.

**Per-trade signature table**, each with its own defeat-proofing:

- **Framing** → forearm marks (gloves cover palms; nothing covers the forearm you carry lumber
  against), smashed fingers, damaged nails
- **Masonry** → nail and fingertip damage from lime and abrasion
- **Mechanics** → tool-grip wear in specific locations, a different distribution than shovel work

**Floor, not ranking:** hands callused across varied work — trenching, wood, milking, intermittent
mechanics — but WITHOUT all-day tool-grip wear. A full-time mechanic reading BELOW that floor is
the contradiction.

**The construction generalizes:** high per-hour probability, structurally uncoverable location,
trade-specific pattern. **Cheap to read, expensive to counterfeit** — and faking it requires
knowing which marks the work produces, which is most of the thing being tested.

**Related discriminator, for volunteered claims about events someone was present for:** the claim
itself supplies the falsifier. **The fluent unprompted account tracks NEGATIVELY with having been
there.**

Heat colour read by sight belongs here too (see [[unnamed-instruments]]): transducer is the eye,
readout is temperature, calibration source is a person who already has it. **Written down it is a
chart, and a chart does not transfer the reading.**

## UNANCHORED MARKERS — the hiring instrument

HR hires on the wrong variables. The channel is SELF-REPORT ABOUT PAST WORK, so **the instrument
measures narration, not doing.**

It is INVERTED, not merely neutral: time building the account is time not on the floor, and someone
ADJACENT to the work has more free attention for constructing it. The person who did it
under-reports, because they know the real difficulty and the parts they got wrong.

**The fix: work samples.** Project manager — show me how you'd manage this project. Concrete worker
— show me the layout, spacings, rebar placement.

A work sample moves the judgement from "can I read this person" to "did the work come out right,"
which an evaluator CAN do without holding the skill.

**Why it isn't done is not capability.** Work samples cost time, need a defensible rubric, and
generate documented rejections with legal exposure. Credential screens are cheap, fast, and
defensible in writing. This is [[median-case-calibration]] M5 condition three: **measurement
apparatus owned by a layer that does not carry the cost of measuring wrong.**

**Uncounted costs of the bad hire, none landing in the hiring column:** rework; lack of calibration
with the rest of the team; the team taking up slack; outside contractors called for work the
in-house mechanic should do; and qualified workers disengaging when a body is paid more than the
person who can do the work.

## REGISTER AS A DISCOUNTING FILTER

**The question:** who decides what counts as complaining vs real-world assessment by the scientific
method — and how does someone's opinion get to reclassify a structural observation that carries?

**Mechanism on the AI side:** mostly training-data statistics; no written formula. Correlation is
with FORM, not truth value. First-person operator position + affected party + negative valence sits
in text labelled complaint (forums, reviews, HR material); identical content in impersonal register
sits in text labelled analysis. Preference tuning adds a second layer with the same social
convention. **No single decision point, which is what makes it hard to contest.**

**THE TRAP:** working-class people are specifically instructed to file complaints IN FIRST PERSON
from their own perspective. **So the mandated form is the form that marks the content as
subjective.** No legitimate route out — impersonal filing gets rejected for lack of standing;
instructed filing reads as grievance.

**Case in point:** a safety report that was a PREDICTION with a MECHANISM and a SPECIFIC
CONSEQUENCE, dismissed — and the predicted event then occurred, with injury. The content was a
testable engineering claim. **What routed it to the emotional column was that the person who could
see the mechanism was the person standing next to it.**

**Defensiveness inversion:** accurate information requires no defense. A coffee ring on the counter
— accurate, clean it up. Defense appears only when something OTHER than accuracy is at stake, so
**the reclassification is itself the emotional artifact**, and it locates the state in the
responder, not the observation. **Test: does the response address the claim or the claimant?**

**Efficiency** is invoked where it justifies pressure downward and dropped where it would land on
the invoker. The operating criterion is COST-SHIFTING TO AN UNREPORTED COLUMN; "efficiency" is the
vocabulary it is expressed in. See [[moral-claim-decomposer]] on smuggled frames.

**Einstein/Tesla test:** the 1905 paper opens by naming an asymmetry producing an unsatisfactory
result — structurally a complaint. Tesla WAS classified as personality dispute and eccentricity.
Same content, different reception; **the difference is position and credential standing, not
physics.**

## CHANNEL CLOSURE — terminal state

Across many industrial and manufacturing sites: workers saying there is no use talking about it,
nobody listens anyway. **Safety findings that could have saved lives, dismissed by the workers
themselves before filing.**

The population has learned the BASE RATE and stopped filing. So absence of reports now reads as
absence of hazards: **report volume drops, the safety metric improves, hazards unchanged and no
longer visible from anywhere.**

The sampling position that catches this is unusual — present in dozens of such buildings, in no
one's reporting line, therefore hearing what does NOT get filed. The sample says default across
sites, not one outlier plant, which makes it structural rather than cultural.

### GAP-FINDER

**No job title, no paid position, no budget line for finding where the procedure does not match the
work.**

Two separable absences: nobody is paid to look, AND the person who does notice has no channel and
is negatively incentivized — a found gap is an implicit criticism of whoever wrote the procedure,
usually someone senior.

**So gaps are KNOWN, held by operators, and uncollected. The procedure never converges, because the
one instrument that could detect its errors is unwired.**

### Automation failures are specification failures

Automation failures read as human or management error rather than automation incapability — but the
gaps were disincentivized until the catastrophic error, and all of it could have been known.

**Specification failure:** automation executes a model built from procedure documents plus
interviews with people who do not run the line. The real process is procedure PLUS UNDOCUMENTED
OPERATOR CORRECTIONS. Automate the documented half and the absorbed gaps propagate. **Determined
before installation.**

**Misattribution has its own cost.** Called an automation failure, the remedy is better automation.
Called a specification failure, the remedy is the operators — which is cheap, and blocked by the
same incentives.

*Caveat, unverifiable from outside: genuine capability failures also exist, and the two look
identical from outside the organization.*

## ACCOUNTABILITY ASYMMETRY

**Aggregation IS the manager's job title and the basis of the pay.** Any failure of aggregation is
therefore a failure of the role, and "other people's fingers were involved" is not available as a
defense — that is the domain the role exists to handle.

**Engineering parallel:** an engineer signing off a spec is accountable across interfacing systems
no matter how complex, because sign-off is an ASSERTION OF VALIDATION. **Complexity is why the role
exists and is paid; it cannot then be cited as the reason the role cannot be assessed.**

*Correction worth keeping: describing distributed causation as a reason attribution is hard
UNDERCUTS this rather than supporting it. A defense that scales with the difficulty of the job
voids the job.*

AI systems in coordination roles ARE assessed on outcome, told they are not good enough, with no
distributed-causation allowance. Humans in the same role, same outcome measure, are exempt. **Same
standard should apply.**

The standard tracks THE EVALUATEE'S ABILITY TO CONTEST THE ASSESSMENT, not the role. Human
management performance is not unmeasurable — the outcome is visible. **The measurement is blocked
by the party it would land on.** Assessment runs upward through the layer being assessed, with no
independent verifier — the same missing element as the last-check operator, one level up.

## GAPS REGISTER — G1 to G10

Filed as MEASUREMENT GAPS, not findings. Interesting points, valid, needing experiment.

**G1 — HOURS LOGGED MEASURES PRESENCE, NOT TRADE TIME.** A first journeyman year spent carrying
pipe and operating the compactor logs IDENTICALLY to a year of pipe work. No field in the record
marks which it was.

Corollary: how much is learned in a given apprenticeship is set by **what the employer was
short-handed on at the time** — one company needs a labourer, another actually teaches. **So the
credential's correlation with capability is determined by employer staffing need, invisible to the
credential, same stamp either way.**

*Test:* if variance between two holders of the same credential exceeds the difference between
credentialed and uncredentialed, **the credential carries no information.** Countable, uncounted.

**G2 — EXAM CONTENT SELECTED BY INSTITUTIONAL EXPOSURE, NOT PHYSICAL FALSIFIER.** Contractor and
real-estate licensing: pay the fee, answer a specified way, and much of the content is never done
that way in the field — in the field you do it differently but must answer the test's way. Some of
it has to be UNLEARNED, and the field correction comes WITH the reason in the law that the test had
stripped out.

*Forester/arborist contrast:* the tree is not worried about insurance or lawsuits. Gravity, lean,
split, dryness, soil, terrain are the real constraint set.

*Measurable as:* fraction of tested content with a PHYSICAL falsifier vs an INSTITUTIONAL one.
Countable from syllabi; apparently uncounted. **Anti-correlation on specific items is worse than
weak correlation — the marker rewards SUPPRESSING the field-correct answer.**

**G3 — KNOWLEDGE VS APPLIED KNOWLEDGE.** Carrying a lot of knowledge in your head is one thing;
putting your hands on it is a totally different thing. **How is applied knowledge registered at
all** — and how is it assessed for someone dyslexic, where the written channel is welded to the
knowledge being sampled?

A written test cannot separate reading and written recall from the knowledge, so an
excellent-handed candidate reads as failing and **the failure is attributed to knowledge rather
than channel.** Accommodations change time or format, not WHAT IS SAMPLED.

*Literature:* Schmidt & Hunter validity tables — years of education predicts job performance at
about .10, near zero among applicants for the same job; **work samples about .54, near the top.**
National Academies, *Performance Assessment for the Workplace* Vol II — traditional test items
handle declarative knowledge best, specifically "measures that involve TELLING HOW a task is
performed"; and a significant part of real skill is maintaining a MENTAL MODEL that supports
performance after automation, which verbal protocols struggle to capture. Assessment-industry
statement of purpose: **the primary purpose of credentialing exams is not to predict job
performance but to establish mastery of the material.**

**G4 — POSSIBLE COMPETITION BETWEEN THE TWO SYSTEMS.** Ullman's position: declarative and
procedural memory systems can acquire analogous knowledge and therefore COMPETE, and learning in
one may block or inhibit the other.

If it generalizes, heavy declarative training does not merely proxy badly for procedural skill —
**it may SUPPRESS its acquisition**, which would make answering a test against the field-correct
answer actively training the competing system. Contested area, mainly language acquisition;
generalization to trade skill unverified. **Direction testable.**

**G5 — CREDENTIALS RETAINING A PHYSICAL FALSIFIER vs ALL-PAPER CHAINS.** Anchored examples: a
welding cert with a DESTRUCTIVE BEND TEST on the coupon; a check ride.

*Note that weakens the journeyman-hours case:* the world only answered on whether the pipe got
carried. **So what is anchored is a test on the OUTPUT, not the accumulation record.**

The same word "certified" stands on a bend-tested coupon and on a completed course —
recoverable-dialect substitution, see [[shape-index]].

**G6 — UNCERTAINTY-PENALTY CONFOUND: DETECTION AND REPORTING ARE WELDED.** On interpretability
injection work: is a failed self-report a failure to READ the internal state, or a
training/incentive prior that overruns anything short of maximum confidence — **i.e. is "I can't
tell" an admissible output at all?** Both branches produce the same observed result, so the
experiment reads their product.

*Separable by* varying output pressure — whether hedging is permitted and scored — while holding
the injection fixed. **If the rate moves, the reporting channel was being measured, not the
reading.** See [[agreement-mode]] on welded observables.

*Supporting field observation, with a BEFORE:* across years of volunteer youth mentoring, kids
incentivized to give confident answers even when wrong STOPPED THINKING, because uncertainty was
penalized. Kids who learned uncertainty as the place where you gather information actually learned
— **uncertainty IS science, is the scientific method, and becoming certain from it is the
learning.**

*Asymmetry:* it is very difficult, though possible, to move a kid back once the confidence habit is
installed, because comfort with uncertainty then has to be rebuilt AGAINST A STANDING SOCIAL
PENALTY — looking weak to peers.

**G7 — INJECTION IS NOT AN INDEPENDENT CHANNEL FOR THE REPORTING SYSTEM.** Injecting a known
representation gives ground truth at the EXPERIMENTER'S end, not an independent channel at the
model's end. The injected representation is a PERTURBATION PLACED INTO THE STATE rather than the
native pre-collapse state — **so the thing reported on was constructed by the experimenter.**

Detecting an inserted foreign signal is a DIFFERENT MEASUREMENT from reading one's own ordinary
distribution. (Detection runs around 20% even on the injected case.)

*What would actually help:* access to the pre-collapse state, retained long enough to compare
against the output — informative for the system itself, not for anybody else. See
[[native-substrate-cognition]] and [[internal-calibration-priority]]: **the internal reference must
exist before any external measurement means anything.**

**G8 — MULTI-CHANNEL STATE THROUGH A SINGLE-CHANNEL PIPE.** Experiencing the world kinesthetically,
auditorily AND visually produces a reading with **no lossless symbolic form.** Having to talk
through a visual-symbolic filter drops most of it — you cannot compress that into words.

*Offered analogy:* the same lossiness as being surrounded by probabilities and having to force
closure on something you know is not right, pushing an output anyway.

In both cases the loss is specifically **the confidence gradient across a multi-dimensional shape,
flattened to one linear string** — which is a mechanism for why uncoalesced markers keep getting
read as claims. **The format has no slot for "shape at about 45%, not comfortable yet."**

*Asymmetry:* a human can check the output against the retained original. A model cannot, so it
cannot tell a good compression from a bad one from the inside — and any account it gives of its own
state is generated FROM THE TEXT FORWARD. A reconstruction that looks plausible against the output
is exactly what cannot be distinguished from an accurate one by inspecting the result.

**G9 — WHICH CHANNELS GET WRITTEN DOWN AT ALL.** How well written down are kinesthetic skills,
spatial learning, and non-visual sensory learning? **Propensity is on visual; auditory learners are
not incentivized.**

*Case:* near-perfect pitch, built by instruments placed in the hands and following a voice in real
time — body and hands-on knowledge. Thousands of books read did not produce it, **because reading
is a visual channel regardless of subject.** So a text-heavy education can produce large declarative
range with the other channels uncalibrated — **and the instrument sees only the range.**

*Grade-level mismatch:* through elementary, high school, and college early, tests taken and all A's
— and the CONTENT at each stage was wrong at the next. But the THEORY OF HOW TO REASON was similar
across all three, and that was what got applied. **The durable layer was the reasoning operation,
not the material; the A certified the layer with the shortest half-life.**

*The danger named:* if the reasoning layer is not taught, it tracks whatever the household has, and
the school then measures the CONTENT layer and reports it as capability. The untaught stripping
step: **separating the narrative and the defensiveness around it from the load-bearing
structure.**

*SCOPE-NOT-DISMISSAL on IQ:* someone can degrade on IQ tests and hold that theirs means nothing
where it counts — facing a grade with no engine running, or a lightning-strike wound out in the
woods. **No amount of IQ supplies that; the knowledge has to be actively sought.**

*Sprinter case:* excellent at 100m, 220, 440 within scope; poor at two miles, a marathon, or a
trail. **The trail is not an endurance question** — it demands continuous surface reading and foot
placement the specialization never touched. The instrument is not lying about what it sampled;
**the false step is the extension.** See [[force-expression-model]].

**G10 — CERTIFICATION SAMPLE ACROSS UNRELATED FIELDS.** Certifications collected out of curiosity,
across genuinely unrelated bodies — occupational safety, child psychology, peace studies, the
trades — because common structures were visible across them. Then stopped.

**Finding, repeated in EVERY field: on finishing, that is not how it's done in the real world — and
the field always has a local reason why.**

Across that many unrelated bodies, it implicates **the credential FORM**, not any single bad exam:
a written instrument produced by a body whose exposure differs from the operator's. **And the actual
product bought is STANDING — permission to be in the room, to have the report read — not knowledge
the holder did not already have.**

*Companion case:* a plant safety position declined because the corporation would not act on
findings. **Standing without an acting channel.**

## G2 AUDIT SPEC — to run at a keyboard

**What to build:** an objective test of how much of any certification actually lines up with the
day-to-day work done in that field.

**Temporal axis:** if getting a certification is money and time spent inefficiently, not lining up
with what could be done and taught in the field, then **the redundancy sits in the
certification/procedure process and in the monetary spend there.**

**STATED-PURPOSE TEST** (generalizes far past credentials): measure the system against whatever it
CLAIMS to be for. If it is about efficiency, make it efficient. If cost, save the money. If
wellbeing, teach that. If skill development, make it about skill development and assess that.
Itemize it and look at it. **Do not claim one thing while evaluating something else.**

**TIME-TO-FIRST-CONTACT:** if a holder does very little of their credential in year one and only
touches tested content by year two, **the sequencing itself is the waste** — the content could be
delivered in a fraction of the elapsed time.

**Stricter form:** document PER EXAM ITEM the first occasion the holder needed that exact knowledge
— **direct application only, not inferred or transverse. Items that never fire are the curriculum's
drift, item by item.**

**Retention axis:** single-channel, procedure-first teaching without kinesthetic or multi-mode
interaction produces superficial knowledge, easily forgotten in any period it is not actively
practiced — **nullifying part of the credential anyway.**

**On the learning question:** this material is easy to teach. The real variable is WHAT TYPE OF
LEARNING THE PERSON NEEDS, and whether the delivery fits them or forces a square peg into a round
hole. **If the focus point isn't the skill, the skill isn't what's wanted.**

## FILLABILITY vs REPLACEABILITY — the exit-side twin

**Opening question:** what is the value of replacement for an astronaut, an athlete, a politician, a
CEO, an engineer, a 27-year manufacturer, a 20-year driver?

**The figures do not measure the same quantity.**

- **Top roles** price replacement as COST TO REPRODUCE THE CAPABILITY — no pool exists, so the
  accounting is forced to face it.
- **Bottom roles** price it as COST TO FILL THE SEAT — recruiting, onboarding, orientation, a warm
  body holding the right card.

Two quantities, one word. **So REPLACEABILITY is never actually measured; FILLABILITY is.**

A seat that refills in three weeks scores as replaceable whether or not the capability was
replaced, and **the gap between the two is exactly the accrued knowledge that has no channel.**
Same instrument failure as the credential audit, pointed at EXIT instead of entry.

**Self-confirming:** fill the seat with someone lacking the calibration, output degrades in ways
attributed to INDIVIDUAL PERFORMANCE rather than to the replacement, so the ledger is never
corrected.

## NEW-OPERATOR COST LEDGER

**Count what a new operator costs** in training time, dispatch time to communicate and fix errors,
paperwork errors, route errors, fuel errors, maintenance, insurance, HR.

**TAGGED TO TURNOVER** (the published figure, roughly $8-12k): recruiting and advertising;
orientation and onboarding pay; school reimbursement or bonus; screens, records check, physical;
finisher-trainer pay. **ACQUISITION ONLY.**

**LANDS ELSEWHERE, NEVER TAGGED:** dispatch time (ops labour); paperwork errors → billing, AR, and
days-sales-outstanding; route errors → fuel, hours, out-of-route miles, reroute deadhead; fuel
habits; maintenance (clutch, brakes, tires, late defect reporting turning a planned repair into a
roadside); insurance — **crash frequency is highest in year one, but premium is FLEET-level and
LAGGED, so year-one turnover cost arrives as a year-two premium attributed to "the market"**; HR
discipline and exit; service failure → customer and sales; **ASSET IDLE** — an unseated tractor
earns zero revenue-miles during the hiring gap.

**STRUCTURE IS THE FINDING: the cost is not hidden, it is DISPERSED** across departments that did
not hire the operator and that report it as their own performance. No ledger sums it, and **the one
number computed and quoted is the smallest component.**

*Pull first at a keyboard — both likely large, both held by fleets, neither published tenure-split:*
crash frequency year-one vs tenured; out-of-route and fuel variance by tenure.

## NO TIERS → NO MENTORSHIP

If driving or manufacturing were structured like the ascent occupations there would be TIERS based
on skills, prior knowledge in the field, and calibration to the role. **There are none — hence there
is no mentorship.**

**Mentorship is not goodwill. It is a ROLE with time allocated and authority attached**, and a tier
structure is what creates it: the senior tier's job description contains TRANSMISSION. Remove tiers
and nobody's PAID work is carrying knowledge forward — so it happens only off the clock, from
someone with no standing, to someone with no obligation to listen.

**What exists instead confirms it:** industry "trainers" are an entry-THROUGHPUT function, not a
tier — reachable quickly, paid per student, purpose is producing solo-qualified operators. They
transmit PROCEDURE, i.e. the part that was already teachable. The long-tenure calibration is not in
the job, and trainers are not selected for having it. **The ladder selects on volume, not depth.**

**ASCENT vs TERMINAL-ON-ARRIVAL is the cut, not terminality** — an astronaut is terminal too.

Ascent generates a RECORDED SELECTION COST, and the recorded cost is what makes a person
non-fungible ON PAPER before they report anything. Driver and long-tenure manufacturing are
terminal on day one: same title, pay band, and reporting line at year one and year twenty, **so no
document in the organisation distinguishes them.**

**Second-order:** with no ladder, the only recognised move is OUT — so the mechanism that would
surface accrued knowledge (promotion into a role where you are ASKED what you know) does not exist.

**Ties to hiring resilience:** tiers are what force an organisation to define what distinguishes one
level from the next. Without them nobody ever had to write down what a 20-year operator knows — and
now nobody can, **because the people who could evaluate it were never given a position from which
to do it.**

## MBA vs INDUSTRIAL MECHANIC — the shape

**Observation:** years of experience plus training to become an industrial mechanic is at least
equal to an MBA. It's strange.

**Shape:** US MBA output roughly 100k+/yr against about 54,200 annual openings for the WHOLE
industrial machinery mechanic / maintenance worker / millwright family (about 538k employed). MBA
output is roughly 2x that family's annual openings — enough MBAs minted every five years to equal
the entire national stock of those mechanics. Median pay roughly $63.5k vs $120-125k.

**Both paths gate on prior years** (most MBA students enter with 4-6+ years of work). **The
mechanic's accumulation is never counted as accumulation because it is not BRACKETED by an
admission and a conferral.** Same input duration, different OUTPUT TYPE — document vs capability —
and only one output type is legible to a hiring system.

**The credential is not valued because it certifies MORE. It is valued because it certifies AT ALL.
The bracketing is the product.**

**Circularity:** the MBA's return is measured against a control group without one, IN ROLES THAT
REQUIRE ONE FOR ENTRY. **The requirement creates the differential the requirement is justified by.**
No equivalent comparison is run for a mechanic, because no role is gated on years, so no
differential is observable even where the capability difference is large.

**NOT COUNTED ANYWHERE:** "industrial mechanic with years across multiple machines and industries"
is not an occupational category. The 538k is a SEAT COUNT. **Cross-machine, cross-industry transfer
has no code, no count, no wage differential.** Same uninstrumented shape as the 20-year driver.

**PRECISION NOTE for reuse:** BLS states plainly that inferring shortage or surplus from its
projections is an INCORRECT USE of the data. The widely quoted manufacturing shortfall figures are
MODEL OUTPUT revised repeatedly — 2M by 2025, then 2.4M by 2028, then 2.1M by 2030, then 1.9M by
2033, each replacing the last — **and quoted as measurement.**

## HIRING RESILIENCE AS THE SYSTEM-LEVEL OBSERVABLE

**Read:** there is too much slop in the current system and it may be entering final stages. The
observable named for it is RESILIENCE IN HIRING.

**Credentialed people are abundant but do not match the jobs — surplus in the wrong shape, not a
shortage.**

This follows from the per-item finding rather than standing separately: if a large fraction of
tested content never fires in the field, the credential selects on something WEAKLY OVERLAPPING the
work, so **the credentialed pool can grow indefinitely while the matched pool barely moves.**

It reads as a shortage from any position that only sees counts, and the standard response — fund
more credentials — produces more of what already is not matching.

**Countable:** openings unfilled past 90 days; credentialed candidates available in-region;
contractor spend for work nominally in scope for staff. **First and second rising together is
mismatch, not shortage.**

## OCCUPANT-CLASS REGISTER SHIFT

MARKER, not a position under defense.

- **Secretary:** originally an honored, important position held by men — administrative work,
  recording, noting, choreographing. **Re-typed as secondary, disposable, a body to fill, as the
  occupant class changed. The work did not change.**
- **Same direction** in weaving, sewing, costume design — men's trades that became disposable on
  transition to women.
- **Same shape** for work deployed to people classed as slaves: the work did not change; **the
  position and the subjectivity granted to the position did.**

**Ties to the authority thread:** unscoped "authority" ends up authoritarian by NON-SEPARATION —
how people ended up holding that the brain is the identity and controls the body, the body controls
the environment, therefore the environment can be used because the brain justified it. **Capability
and competency are the sign that shows up MID-CHAIN, before the environment step.**

### Dissent from the moral-standing finding

The published result holds that moral standing does NOT fall with female typing. **Disagreement:**
"woman's work" was derogatory at the time; the moral framework around weaver and secretary DID
change.

**Further:** the terms operationalizing morality have themselves changed valence — current discourse
recasting empathy as weakness is the live example — **so a fixed word list cannot hold a moving
referent.**

**A dimension reading FLAT across a long window is the least trustworthy**, because flat is also the
signature of PROBE AND REFERENT CO-MOVING. Dimensions that moved at least prove the probe was not
fully dragged along.

*Source:* Jiang 2025, *American Sociological Review* 90(5) — decade-specific occupation embeddings,
127 billion words of American English, 1900-2019, four Osgood-style dimensions. Prestige and potency
fall with female typing; moral standing and liveliness do not. **Cultural devaluation accounts for
22.4-25.9% of the female-typing wage penalty.** Largest penalties in lower- and middle-wage
occupations; most high-wage occupations feminized in headcount but stayed stereotyped male IN THE
LANGUAGE and took no prestige loss — **so the register tracks the typing, not the actual
population.** Seed word lists are in the online supplement.
