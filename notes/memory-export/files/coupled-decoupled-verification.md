Falsifiable simulation instruments testing a coupled/decoupled proposal-verification
framework.

Models how DECOUPLED systems maintain internal coherence while drifting from accuracy — with
applications to LLMs, cultural dynamics, and human cognition simultaneously. The coupling term
is what ties proposal generation to an external check; remove it and coherence becomes
self-referential, which is measurable as drift without any loss of internal consistency.
