## Micro-skill decay

Position carried into [[tool-off-metrology]]:

- Micro-skills are all physical and decay the same way — handwriting, micrometer, screwdriver,
  or body mechanics for lifting masonry. Return-to-work back injuries are **lost lifting
  skill, not lost strength.**
- Observed competence collapse when the tool set is removed: a working auto-mechanics
  instructor unable to service an older car without garage, tools, and computer port; habitual
  drill users clumsy with a screwdriver; carpenters unable to mill lumber or build from first
  principles.
- Castor oil as the total-loss case: process knowledge effectively gone, and
  book-plus-video reconstruction produces injuries or a failed substance.

## Parked probe (Q4)

Locally-superior practices get displaced by consumerism and aggregate-scale economics on
distribution cost, not on performance — taking local knowledge with them.

**Prediction:** displaced practices cluster in high-variance environments — cold, remote,
off-grid, irregular loads — where the local optimum diverges most from the aggregate optimum.
