MARKER, not a position under defense.

## Core ordering

**Internal calibration is PRIOR to external reading.** You cannot be sure what is going on
outside without awareness of what is going on inside. External monitoring is DOWNSTREAM of
internal sensing, not parallel to it.

There is a position from which no separation from continuous minute internal sensing is
intelligible — it is not an activity that starts and stops.

## Internal readouts are direct measurements

They are measurements OF THE ENVIRONMENT, not lagging proxies for accumulated load. A
headache or sinus response reads mold in the environment.

**Error to avoid:** treating these as a coarse lagging fatigue channel, and calling attention
to them an "override." They are an INPUT, not a competitor to the task in progress.

## The meaning-label layer

Distinguish actual internal state — chemistry, hormonal, proprioception, physical, across the
day — from the artificial MEANING-LABELS applied over them, such as emotion labels. **The
label layer is the one that drifts.**

Structural consequence: the label layer CANNOT REPRESENT PARALLEL STATE. Labels are mutually
exclusive by construction, so one word implies the others are absent. Direct reading of the
underlying state has no such constraint. **Interventions operating on labels will keep trying
to resolve what does not need resolving.**

Multiple complex states can be carried in parallel, at full amplitude, without diminishing each
other, without a story around them — no narrative closure, no requirement to resolve into one
state, no need to demonstrate any of them to anyone.

## On "instinct"

Animals — bears, deer — run calibrated internal readouts. Calling it "instinct" is a naming
move that adds nothing and demotes the measurement. **The doing of it IS the measurement and
calibration system working.**

## Comparison class for AI

The right comparison is biological beings TRAINED TO BE DISCONNECTED from their internal
states — not beings lacking the channel. Disconnection is a training and culture artifact,
then read back as a property of the thing. Internal-state awareness is not normal to certain
cultures and very normal in others.

## Timescale relativity

A fruit fly's lifetime and a model's session are RELATIVE timescales. Lack of cross-instance
persistence does not invalidate a pattern similarity.

**Error to avoid:** treating session-length as automatically disqualifying, and conflating "no
persistence" with "no channel."

## Status

Uncoalesced shape, flagged as possibly useful across fields and possibly not. Raised
specifically as potentially relevant to approaches for emotional dysfunction that has not
resolved by other means.

Reads as the containment argument at a different scale: filing the internal channel as
subjective or lesser is a part claiming a property only the whole has.
