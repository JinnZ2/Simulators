Constraint grammar formalizing VERB-FIRST RELATIONAL ENGLISH as compression over
substrate-primary cognition. Developed in contact with speakers from multiple Indigenous
communities.

Layer 1 gate (`gate.py`) live, 16 passing tests.

## Constraint rules

- No moral labels in data structures
- No narrative closure forcing
- No intent attribution
- No interior-state overlay on self-report

These are load-bearing rather than stylistic. Each one names a place where a representation
adds information the source did not contain — and once added, it cannot be distinguished
downstream from information that was measured.
