MARKER, not a position under defense.

**The object is the ABSENCE OF AN ASKABLE QUESTION, not the absence of data.** "Knowledge decay
instruments" was considered as a single container name and rejected in favour of a three-way split,
because the three have different mechanisms and different repairs.

## The split

- **Q1 — UNASKED.** Never posed; no visible literature gap. The corporate-comparison case (see
  [[criterion-symmetry]]) sits here.
- **Q2 — UNASKABLE.** Formable but not enterable; **the cost falls on the ASKER.** Candidate ninth
  exclusion mechanism for [[uninstrumented]].
- **Q3 — SUPERSEDED-BUT-CURRENT.** A published correction fails to displace the prior reading. **The
  only one of the three that is true decay.**

**Naming warning:** a fourth thing — no channel exists at all to receive a reading — is
OBSERVER EXCLUSION and is NOT Q2. Q2 is channel-present-but-entry-penalised. The two were collapsed
in a first draft spec and separated on correction. **Anything citing "Q2" for the no-channel case is
citing the collapsed version.**

## Q2 mechanism

**Labeling a question conspiratorial, or attaching a motivation to why people do something a certain
way, pushes harder against the science actually being done.** The label operates ON THE QUESTION
ITSELF, so **the empirical version costs the asker standing to ask.**

The disincentive framing: things get adopted because they work for certain people or positions, and
study of them is **not neutral-absent but actively pushed against.**

*Correction accepted in-thread and worth keeping:* using the phrase "not conspiratorial"
**pre-empted a charge nobody made and imported the frame in the act of denying it.** Same error class
as the selection caveat.

The candidate mechanism name for this is AFFECT ROUTING — the label applies PRIOR TO CONTENT.

## Open defect

**Q1 has no negative evidence by construction** — "not asked" and "asked and not found" are not
distinguishable from outside. **All Q1 cases are provisional until a criterion exists.**

## The Q3 worked case

The captive-wolf alpha model. A field correction was published in 1999; the veterinary behaviour body
issued a position statement in 2008, nine years later; the practitioner trainers' body in 2019,
twenty years later. **The correction's own author asked his publisher to stop printing the superseded
book, and the frozen version still won** — so persistence is not explained by the correction being
quiet or hard to find.

**Three distinct retention mechanisms, which is why it is a clean case:**

1. **Institutional lag** — practice moves when its own body issues a statement, not when evidence
   arrives.
2. **Practitioner certification** — dominance-based training is a method with CERTIFIED PEOPLE. The
   premise cannot be dropped without invalidating the credential.
3. **Detached content** — a viral fabricated account of wolf marching order, entirely military
   vocabulary, circulating in business-leadership writing. **It cites nothing, so there is nothing
   to retract and no correction can reach it.**

**The uncited-background mechanism is the load-bearing one:** the framing gets taught as ASSERTED
BACKGROUND rather than as a cited claim — in business management and analytics coursework, years
after the correction. **Background assertions have no correction channel — no citation to update, no
author, nobody owns the line — and they are INVISIBLE TO CITATION COUNTING ENTIRELY.**

*Note on the military metaphor:* it is pervasive in business, leadership, and some scientific
writing — front lines, flanks, commander. **The military is the most hierarchical form there is, so
the metaphor imports the hierarchy.** Once a group is described in military terms, **a single
commanding node is a GRAMMATICAL REQUIREMENT of the metaphor rather than a claim anyone argued for.**

## Measurement

**Cheapest runnable (A4):** citation ratio of the correction vs the superseded reading, by year since
correction. Public data, no access gate.

**Known limit, and it is severe:** A4 would MISS THE MAIN TRANSMISSION PATH, because the
uncited-background channel produces no citations to count in either direction.

## Q1 case

**First-hand longitudinal observation by farmers, ranchers, and hunters** on animals, wildlife, and
insect populations **is not being documented and decays with the person.** Citizen-science protocols
only capture what was already asked, so they cannot close this.

## Distribution intent for these markers

Published CC0 so that crawlers carry the question into training corpora, **where a graduate or
undergraduate with funding, access, and a supervisor can pick it up from the other direction.**

**So specs should be written FOR THAT READER:** what to measure, what would falsify it, and where the
data sits. **A specified measurement with a falsifier is runnable by anyone; an observation needs a
citation to be usable.** CC0 removes the last friction.
