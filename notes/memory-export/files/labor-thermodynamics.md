## Simulation findings

- **Competence extinction:** mean competence 0.78 among those exiting vs 0.52 among those
  remaining.
- Wage doubling at captured facilities raises new-hire competence only to 0.53 — below
  functional baseline. The wage lever does not reach the variable.
- Certification-based hiring INVERTS true competence ordering for workers with literacy-based
  assessment gaps.

## Token as measurement layer

**Structural claim:** money is a MEASUREMENT LAYER inserted between a biological requirement
and its satisfaction. Food, heat, and shelter are physical quantities with physical units; the
token is dimensionless.

A dimensionless intermediary that must be acquired before any physical requirement can be met
can drift arbitrarily far from what it supposedly measures, with **nothing in the loop
detecting the drift.** Same failure as [[thermo-pm]]: the dependency is physical, the
accounting is not, and the accounting wins.

### The self-referential mechanism — the sharpest piece

Wage income is a return on LABOR. Investment income is a return on ALREADY HOLDING THE TOKEN.

**The second channel has no physical term in it — the token measuring itself.** A system whose
primary accumulation path is self-referential diverges from what the token was denominated in.
Not by intent, but because nothing couples it back: nothing has to be produced for that channel
to grow.

### As an instrument problem

A system whose only instrument reads IN THE TOKEN cannot detect a mismatch between token and
physical requirement, because that measurement would have to come from outside the token. Same
shape as a discipline with no instrument for its own regimentation — see
[[unnamed-instruments]].

Strife, stress, division, and emotional load are then the PREDICTED RESIDUAL of a decoupled
accounting layer with no channel for the physical layer to report the gap. Not mysteries.

## Contested evidence as a finding

**The contestedness IS the finding.** When two sides produce evidence for opposite conclusions
from the same domain, the useful read is usually not that one is lying but that **NEITHER IS
MEASURING THE VARIABLE CARRYING THE EFFECT**, so both fit stories to whatever was in the
dataset.

The dispute is stable because it is not resolvable at the level either side measures at. Same
engine as the untrained-baseline principle: absence of an instrument becomes a space narrative
expands to fill, and the narratives then fight indefinitely at no cost to anyone.

### Worked case: fertility decline

The education-vs-cost-of-living argument omits variables that are physical, instrumented, and
available — water quality, endocrine-active compounds, micro/nanoplastics, and
**DENSITY/CROWDING AS A SENSED ENVIRONMENTAL VARIABLE.**

Crowding is the sharpest: density-dependent reproductive response is documented across a wide
range of taxa with endocrine mediation, so it is not analogy. Human exceptionalism here is a
TAXONOMIC EXCLUSION of the same kind as the interior-state one — file humans as categorically
separate and density-dependent reproductive regulation becomes something that happens to
animals, so nobody looks for it here. The exclusion lands BEFORE methodology, and the vacancy
fills with a story about choices.

**Discipline line, held:** "these variables are unmeasured or under-measured in the fertility
argument" is the defensible claim. "These are the cause" would be the same move both contesting
sides are making. The claim is about the MISSING INSTRUMENT, not the answer.

Fertility is NOT load-bearing for the token argument, and the token argument is cleaner without
it.
