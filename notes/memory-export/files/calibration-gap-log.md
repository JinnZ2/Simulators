## G1 — the calibration layer

**TWO distinct axes, not one.**

**(1) WAKE-CYCLE AXIS — VALIDATED.** Time-since-entrained-awakening, chronotype-anchored,
replicated 2015 / 2018 / 2023. Evening types peak around 11-12.6 h after wake; morning types
around 5.5-6.7 h. **This is ALSO the axis carried in oral tradition** — so it is proof that an
oral-tradition timing axis survives rigorous testing.

**(2) NEUROSTEROID-MODULATION AXIS.** Mechanism REAL — allopregnanolone gating GABA; estrogen
acting on serotonin, dopamine, ACh; testosterone modulating dopamine — all modulating sensory
threshold, i.e. APPARATUS GAIN. But USE-AS-INSTRUMENT is unstudied.

What is folklore is NARROWER than convergence summaries suggest: *cycle phase predicts athletic
PERFORMANCE* is folklore; *cycle chemical state modulates SENSOR SENSITIVITY* is a different,
real construct nobody measured as a trained instrument. Do not conflate the two.

### Three findings now established, no longer gaps

**(a) The driver is RATE OF CHANGE, not level.** Rapid progesterone/ALLO decline produces the
effect; gradual does not (Doornbos 2009, rodent). Controls wane gradually about 8 days
pre-menses, while symptomatic hold stable then drop sharply at about 3 days (Lovick 2017).
Causal test: dutasteride blocked 5-alpha-reductase and stabilized ALLO while progesterone still
fluctuated — symptoms fell (Martinez 2016, RCT crossover). **THE GRADIENT IS THE VARIABLE.**

**(b) SENSITIVITY TO NORMAL CHANGE is the model; levels are normal.** Schmidt 1998 (NEJM): GnRH
suppression removed symptoms, and estradiol/progesterone add-back re-triggered ONLY in affected
women, not controls. No consistent evidence of abnormal circulating gonadal steroid levels.
Field phrasing: neurobiological sensitivity to natural change, not imbalance — **the calibration
layer's premise, established, observed at the pathological end.**

**(c) GAIN IS ALTERED, not simply up or down, and can INVERT across range.** Less sensitive to
low and subphysiological GABAergic doses; MORE sensitive at luteal-range and above, with
paradoxical worsening (Timby 2016, saccadic eye velocity; Bäckström 2014). "Sensitivity is
higher today" is too coarse — **the response curve can change SIGN.**

### The coupled second axis

The stress axis does NOT add a third axis — it MULTIPLIES with the neurosteroid one.

Cortisol alone had no effect on GnRH pulsatility in ovariectomized ewes; with estradiol and
progesterone co-administered, GnRH secretion fell 70%. Reviews state directly that estradiol
sensitizes the hypothalamus to cortisol's effect.

It runs BOTH directions: estradiol impairs glucocorticoid negative feedback on the HPA axis via
ER-alpha near the PVN, cutting dexamethasone suppression of corticosterone from about 63% to
about 34%.

**So the same stressor lands differently depending on where the neurosteroid axis sits** — which
supplies a MECHANISM for the sign-inversion finding, arriving from an unrelated literature.

**SCOPE, the weak link:** direct GnRH-pulse causality is ewe, monkey, and mouse. Human evidence
is correlational plus exogenous-glucocorticoid and Cushing's. The feedback-impairment result is
ovariectomized rat with stereotaxic peri-PVN implants, and rodent findings run both directions
by dose and duration. Human proof-of-mechanism exists only at the extreme — functional
hypothalamic amenorrhoea, where HPA activation plus energy deficit shuts down GnRH pulsatility
and reverses on behavioural correction.

### Residual gap — now sharper and WIDER

**[gap]** All of this was learned by studying DISORDER. Nobody has run the instrumentation on
healthy trained carriers deliberately reading their own gradient — and the gap now covers a
COUPLED TWO-AXIS SYSTEM rather than a single axis. **Mechanism is no longer in question; the
trained-use case has never been measured.**

### Discipline note

The famous "75% symptom reduction" (sepranolone) is a POST-HOC SUBGROUP, n=60 of 126, and the
larger confirmatory trial FAILED its primary endpoint, with the compound out of production.
Brexanolone was placebo-adjusted only −2.5 to −5.5 HAM-D despite "dramatic" framing; FDA
approval withdrawn April 2025.

**The memorable number is repeatedly the one that did not replicate.** Check before citing this
field's headline figures.

## G2 — CLOSED against the MN 10 mapping

The Satipatthana Sutta's vedananupassana section maps to the emotion spec at four points, three
of them STRUCTURAL rather than thematic:

1. **VERB, NOT NOUN.** The practice is grammatically an operation — *vedanānupassī viharati*,
   "dwells contemplating feelings-as-feelings." The verb-first requirement, in a 5th-century BCE
   text.
2. **DISCIPLINE IS THE DERIVATIVE, NOT THE LEVEL.** The refrain trains contemplation of arising
   AND vanishing factors (*samudaya* / *vaya*) — rate of change as the object of practice.
   **Same result as G1's gradient finding, reached from the opposite end about 2,400 years
   earlier.** Strongest cross-substrate agreement in the catalog.
3. **STRUCTURED CONTENT FIELD:** 3 valences x 2 source-qualities (*sāmisa* / *nirāmisa*,
   of-the-flesh / not-of-the-flesh).
4. **ENDPOINT SEPARATES CONTENT GAIN FROM RESPONSE GAIN.** What is trained down is *upādāna*
   (appropriation), not *vedanā*. Feeling is still felt and classified; the RESPONSE to it is
   what changes.

### Resolves the strongest trained-carrier null

Stated at full strength, not cushioned. Khalsa 2020: 15 meditators averaging 10.8 years and
about 4,947 hours, mostly vipassana, vs matched controls under DOUBLE-BLIND PLACEBO-CONTROLLED
ISOPROTERENOL INFUSION — pharmacological amplification closing the "signal too weak" escape. No
higher detection rates or accuracy at any dose. Meta-analysis of 724 participants shows
negligible difference.

**Read through the mapping, the null points at an axis the training does not target.** The
tradition trains RESPONSE DECOUPLING and never claimed cardiac acuity. Corroborating: Desbordes
2012 found reduced right amygdala reactivity across valences INCLUDING POSITIVE — response-gain
reduction, not improved detection.

**[open]** Held in the quarantined tier: beat-counting asks a DISCRETE-TEMPORAL question ("how
many"). If a trained observer's actual percept is continuous and spatial — distribution or
extent rather than count — they would score badly while reporting accurately about something
else. A cardiac field or EM channel was floated as a candidate; cardiac fields are measurable at
the body surface but NOT established as sensed channels, so it sits with magnetoreception in the
open tier.

**The structural point does NOT depend on it: A PERCEPT OF THE WRONG SHAPE FOR THE TASK SCORES
AS ABSENCE.**

## G3 — sort before searching

The category does not behave as one thing.

### REFUTED as historical carriers — do NOT use as leads

1. **Contemporary Red Tent** — the seed text is a 1997 novel whose author states plainly that
   there is no historical evidence biblical women used a menstrual tent, and that she invented
   it as plausible. Invented tradition. Does real social work; carries no historical knowledge.
2. **Generic "moon lodge"** of the Goddess/spirituality milieu — modern pan-Indian syncretism
   from popular teachers and audio, with practitioners conceding they could not find source
   information.
3. **Adjacent folklore refuted** — menstrual synchrony (three statistical errors in the original;
   prospective nulls; the largest dataset, 360 pairs, found cycles DIVERGE — 10 d to 38 d mean
   onset difference).

### DOCUMENTED AND REAL — different objects from the refuted ones

- **Navajo Kinaaldá** (Frisbie 1967, 400+ pp with song texts, eHRAF primary-data rating 5). Note
  it is a PUBERTY rite at first menses, community-affirmative, structurally unlike either Dogon
  huts or Red Tents.
- **Wabanaki Strawberry Ceremony / moon lodge** — first-menses seclusion, teaching transmitted by
  mothers, grandmothers, and aunts. Source is a 2025 community-revival publication rather than
  historical ethnography: a living or renewed tradition with plausible roots.

**CRITICAL DISTINCTION: the finding is NOT "moon lodges are invented."** The generic marketed
version is; specific tribal practices are documented.

### Tension — state both sides

**AGAINST:** the best hormone-verified menstrual-hut case in the record (Strassmann, Dogon, 93
women, urinary steroid assays, 86% hut use during confirmed menses) is interpreted by its own
investigator as PATERNITY SURVEILLANCE and honest signaling under sexual conflict — not women's
rest or instruction. The prior "calibration content undocumented rather than absent" framing does
NOT survive this.

**FOR / LIMITING:** Buckley & Gottlieb 1988's canonical position that the menstrual taboo AS SUCH
does not exist; that rules vary cross-culturally with opposite purposes; and that the possibility
such practices ENHANCE women's influence has generally been ignored by investigators.

**CORRECTED STATUS:** not "knowledge likely present but undocumented." Meanings are locally
specific, and the question cannot be settled at the level of "menstrual seclusion" as a category.
**Search tribally-specific documented practice, not the category.**

Still-standing leads: curandera / midwife / herbalist traditions (documented as pharmacology, not
as perceptual training) and female monastic branches.

## G4 — interoception: the instrument, not the spec

The emotion spec needs NO change. What is missing is an INSTRUMENT.

**Both measurement channels are compromised**, and the field's central dissociation — self-report
vs objective heartbeat accuracy, r about 0.06, instrument-dependent — may be an ARTIFACT of the
two failures rather than a fact about interoception.

A common BLUF in this literature — "accuracy irrelevant, build content on attention, beliefs, and
granularity" — does NOT hold. It reads MEASUREMENT FAILURE as CONSTRUCT IRRELEVANCE, and supplies
the evidence against its own inference: the three dominant tasks do not intercorrelate and
"likely assess partly different abilities"; of 73 participants, exactly ONE was a perceiver on
all three. **You cannot declare a construct irrelevant using instruments that disagree with each
other.**

### Objective-channel failure = MEASUREMENT INVERSION

Routed to [[tool-off-metrology]] as its third instance.

Heartbeat counting is passable with ZERO interoception, by knowing typical heart rate and
estimating (Ring & Brener 1996/2018). Expectation predicts performance at beta = .595, r = 0.78
on replication (Desmedt 2021). "Count only beats you feel" instructions cut scores by about 50%.
**The task rewards population-norm knowledge over sensing.**

**Softened against a second source** after the first proved one-sided: Zimprich 2020 reanalyzed
the critique's own data and argued the defects are largely artifacts of analyzing ratio variables
with bivariate correlations — "not as serious as they might appear." Corneille 2020 rebutted;
Ainley 2020 called the critique misconceived.

**FIELD'S ACTUAL LANDING:** the task is compromised; do not abandon it; do not trust it as a pure
measure; prefer discrimination and phase-adjustment tasks. **The INVERSION survives; the stronger
"objective channel is simply broken" reading does not.**

### Self-report-channel failure — the seed of clause 3

Sensibility questionnaires measure **CULTURAL PERMISSION TO SPEAK, not sensing.**

Mechanism: where a culture separates rational from emotional, files interoception under
emotional, and follows the rational side religiously, the TERMINOLOGY ITSELF limits expression —
and a division appears in self-report that is SEMANTIC, not perceptual.

The same reading has two renderings — "noticed a change in my body" vs "felt something" — and
only one is credentialed. **Which one a person reaches for tracks vocabulary, not sensing.**

Connects to the render-target problem in [[unnamed-instruments]]: the store is one thing, the
available output words are another, and the questionnaire only sees the output.

Demonstrated instance: the konenki / skin-conductance case in [[sensing-spine]].

**Cuts both ways:** do not read self-report differences as capacity differences, and do not read
low self-report as absent capacity.

### What is missing

**[gap]** NO instrument currently exists that could test a content-field claim. A real test needs
(a) an objective task that cannot be passed by arithmetic, and (b) a report channel that does not
require the subject to first categorize their own reading as emotional. **Neither exists.**

### Do not give the spec a neural substrate

A patient with bilateral insula, ACC, and amygdala destruction retained pain affect, emotion, and
self-awareness (Feinstein 2016). The anterior insula is a salience-network hub that activates for
anything salient — so it is evidence for nothing in particular.
