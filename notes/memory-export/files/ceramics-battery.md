## Requirement

Batteries functional from -60 F up to 125 F+ in summer sun. This is a real operating condition
in cold-continental working environments, not a research abstraction — which matters because
the range is wider than most commercial specification bands and the failure modes at the two
ends are different.

## Why the work is being done locally

Researchers depend on outside funding and rarely live in these conditions, so the work is not
expected to arrive from that direction. The operating range that defines the problem is not
the operating range the funded work is calibrated to.

## Approach

Make ceramics on a budget, using recycled and scavenged products as feedstock.

## Existing hardware in this domain

Three lithium batteries in a paraffin/beeswax phase-change enclosure; 20x SP1848 peltier
modules; 9 fresnel lenses.
