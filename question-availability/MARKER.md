# MARKER — question availability

Status: uncoalesced. Marker to explore, not a position under defense.
Date: 2026-08-24
License: CC0

## What is being typed

Not absence of data. Absence of an askable question.

Existing exclusion mechanisms in [[uninstrumented]] cover cases where
the instrument's constitution prevents a quantity from appearing.
These three sit upstream of that: they prevent the measurement from
being attempted at all.

## The three

### Q1 — unasked

Question never posed. No data, and no gap visible in the literature,
because a gap is defined against an existing line of inquiry.

  case: corporate single-executive form vs alternatives
        controls exist in the world (cooperatives, partnerships,
        mutuals); comparison not run as a comparison
  tell: retention explained by function, adoption explained by
        history, no trial requested
  distinguishing feature: nothing was lost. It was never held.

### Q2 — unaskable

Posing the question costs the asker standing. The label operates on
the question, not on any claim contained in it.

  case: "why is this arrangement retained" filed as conspiracy-
        adjacent; the empirical version then carries the cost of the
        non-empirical one
  case: driver diagnostic question typed as complaint by reporter
        position — see [[report-typing]]
  mechanism: the label is applied prior to content, so the content
        never reaches evaluation. Answering the label does not clear
        it; the pre-emptive denial imports the frame.
  distinguishing feature: the question is formable but not
        enterable. Cost falls on the asker, not the claim.
  status: candidate ninth exclusion mechanism for [[uninstrumented]]

### Q3 — superseded but current

Correction published; prior reading still operating in practice.

  case: captive-wolf alpha hierarchy. Corrected by the original
        author. Still in circulation.
  distinguishing feature: this one IS decay. Knowledge existed,
        degraded in transmission, and the corrected version did not
        displace it.

## Why the split matters

"Knowledge decay" as a single container is wrong for Q1 and Q2.
Nothing decayed in either. Q1 was never held; Q2 is actively held
out. Only Q3 is decay.

Container name: question availability — what is measured is whether
the question can be entered, not whether an answer was lost.

## Measurements

  A1  Q1: does a control condition exist in the world, and is there
      any published comparison. Two booleans. Cheap.
  A2  Q2: does the labeled version of the question appear in venues
      the unlabeled version does not. Requires venue-typed corpus.
  A3  Q2: cost to asker — does the asker's subsequent submissions
      route differently. This is the residue measurement from
      [[report-typing]] and is the same quantity.
  A4  Q3: citation ratio, corrected version vs superseded version,
      by year since correction. Directly computable.

A4 is the cheapest real measurement in the set and is runnable now.

## Open

- Q2 and Q3 may compound: a correction that exists but whose
  invocation carries a standing cost. Not yet a separate class.
- Q1 has no negative evidence by construction. Need a criterion for
  distinguishing "not asked" from "asked and not found by me."
  Until that exists Q1 cases are provisional.
