# CLAIM_TABLE — envelope-asymmetry

Every number here is computed by `protocol_audit.py` on constructed
rows labelled so, and pinned by `selftest_env.py`; the render is in
`samples/protocol_audit.sample.txt`. No document has been coded and
nothing here is a statement about any vendor, filing or regulatory
regime. `[CHOICE n]` refers to the choices printed on every render.

## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

A falsifier that fires updates the claim's status in place with the
evidence that fired it; the checks in `selftest_env.py` are not
loosened to keep a claim. `PROTOCOL.md` is not edited; a change to the
instrument is recorded as a claim rather than folded in.
