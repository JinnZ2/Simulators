# CLAIMS — `incentive_blindspot_sim`

## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

If a claim check fails, you **update the claim or the stated coupling
topology** to match reality. You do **not** retune the frozen weights
to force a falsified claim to pass.

- The `WEIGHTS` dict in `incentive_blindspot_sim.py` is the frozen
  estimate of *coupling shape*.
- The `SCENARIOS` are the inputs (init + params).
- The claims below are what carries the falsifiable content.

If you find yourself reaching for a weight knob to make a verdict
flip, stop. That is the failure mode the protocol exists to block.

The unit tests in `sim/tests/test_incentive_blindspot_sim.py`
enforce this mechanically: any silent retuning of `WEIGHTS` surfaces
as a test failure that demands an honest update to the documented
topology rather than a hidden tweak.

## Adding or refuting a claim

If you write a new claim function `claim_BS_005(...)`, also:

1. Add it to the `CLAIMS = [...]` registry at the bottom of
   `incentive_blindspot_sim.py` so the demo prints its verdict.
2. Add a section here mirroring the four above (statement,
   refutation condition, where it lives).
3. Add a `test_claim_BS_005_supported` assertion in
   `sim/tests/test_incentive_blindspot_sim.py` so the test suite
   enforces the verdict under the frozen weights.

If you believe a shipped claim is refuted by new data: update the
**claim's stated condition** or the **stated coupling topology** in
the README. Do not edit `WEIGHTS` to make the check pass.
