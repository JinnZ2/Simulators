lower-operand-count one survives and its value appears in the higher

that is not merely "bigger relationships break more".

DIVERGED exceeds that of HOLDS + MAINTAINED.

which is not counted as support or refutation. `SSS_043` is why that

those as refutations would have counted a workbook that cannot state a
relationship as evidence about whether relationships are maintained.
