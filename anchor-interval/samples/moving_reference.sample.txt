
MOVING REFERENCE: A DIFFERENCE BETWEEN TWO MOVING THINGS

  The published number cannot tell them apart, and there is no

========================================================================
2  a held-fixed benchmark identifies shape, not level
========================================================================
  gen   contemporary     on fixed B_0     divergence      
  --------------------------------------------------------
  0     0.3000           0.3000           +0.0000
  1     0.3775           0.3500           +0.0275
  2     0.4600           0.4000           +0.0600
  3     0.5475           0.4500           +0.0975
  4     0.6400           0.5000           +0.1400
  5     0.7375           0.5500           +0.1875
  6     0.8400           0.6000           +0.2400
  7     0.9475           0.6500           +0.2975

  The divergence between the two curves is the criteria-drift
  term, isolated: (a_k - a_0) c_k + (b_k - b_0).

  What it does NOT give: c itself. Scores on B_0 are a_0 c_k + b_0
  and a_0, b_0 are unknown. So ratios of DIFFERENCES are
  identified and levels are not:

    (B0_3 - B0_0)/(B0_7 - B0_0) = 0.428571
    (c_3  - c_0 )/(c_7  - c_0 ) = 0.428571
    identified: True

  So the measurement is worth running and its output is a SHARE,
  not a capability. And if the old benchmark reads as obsolete
  rather than as a control, that judgment was made inside the
  thing being measured.

========================================================================
3  how much of the reported improvement is the ruler moving
========================================================================
  Stated assumptions -- these are a worked illustration, not a
  measurement. The point is the decomposition, not the numbers.

  ruler steady         reported +0.3500   capability +0.3500   ruler share   0.0%
  ruler +5%/gen        reported +0.6475   capability +0.3500   ruler share  45.9%
  ruler +9%/gen        reported +0.8855   capability +0.3500   ruler share  60.5%

  Nothing here needs anyone to cheat. Every benchmark revision
  was made for a stated reason, by people who thought the old one
  had stopped discriminating. The share is what it is regardless
  of why the revisions happened.

========================================================================
4  seven terms moved, one number reported
========================================================================
  7 terms co-move between releases. The published delta is one
  number, attributed to the first term.

      model architecture
      training corpus
      curation criteria
      eval benchmark
      annotation guidelines
      rater pool
      definition of neutral

  Effective number of independent directions in the design,
  by participation ratio of the correlation spectrum:

  co-movement    N_eff        top eigenvalue reading
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  0.00           6.409        1.548          terms separable
  0.30           6.434        1.499          terms separable
  0.50           5.500        2.184          terms separable
  0.70           3.239        3.616          partly confounded
  0.85           1.818        5.130          one direction
  0.95           1.216        6.342          one direction

  The load = 0 row reads 6.41 rather than 7: with 40 releases the
  sample correlation matrix carries its own noise floor. That is
  the apparatus reading, and every row below sits on it.

  What makes the co-movement non-removable by better design:
  the architectural term was CHOSEN against the corpus.

      attention shapes fitted to language statistics
      tokenizers fitted to the writing system
      context lengths fitted to document lengths
      objectives fitted to what the corpus can score

  So the term and the data are not independent variables. Varying
  the term while holding the corpus fixed does not separate them,
  because the covariance was built in before the experiment
  started. Ablations at small scale DO isolate -- and small-scale
  results are known not to transfer reliably upward, so the
  isolated result is not the one that ships.

  Falsifier for the selection claim: an architectural term chosen
  WITHOUT reference to the corpus -- transferred from another
  modality, or fixed before the corpus existed -- decorrelates the
  pair, and the load parameter above should drop for it.

========================================================================
READING
========================================================================

  From a contemporary score alone, capability and criteria are not
  separable -- one equation, two unknowns, and section 1 exhibits a
  flat-capability trajectory that reproduces a rising one exactly.

  Holding one benchmark fixed across generations is the right measurement
  and it buys a SHARE, not a capability: c is identified only up to the
  fixed benchmark's own unknown gain and offset, so differences and their
  ratios are identified and levels are not.

  Seven terms move between releases and one number is published. At high
  co-movement the design carries close to one independent direction, and
  the co-movement is not a nuisance a better experiment removes: the
  architecture was selected against the corpus, so the two were confounded
  before any ablation was designed.
