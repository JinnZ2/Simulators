READING
==========================================================================
  SIM-B reported  |D_f(AB) - D_f(Cascade)|  = 0.334
  SIM-B baseline  |D_f(AB) - D_f(Poisson)|  = 0.021   (matched-N)

  Artifact 1 — unmatched sample size. Largest shift across the
  three known-truth probes when N drops 12x: 0.137
    Poisson +0.137   Line +0.050   dust(base2) -0.021   dust(base3) +0.045

  Artifact 2 — box-ladder commensurability. The same 12,000-point
  Cantor dust, same estimator, only the ladder ratio changed:
    base 2 (incommensurate) D_f = 1.304   error +0.042
    base 3 (commensurate)   D_f = 1.189   error -0.073
    ladder shift = 0.115

  Combined artifact budget: 0.252 against a reported
  separation of 0.334 — roughly 75% of it.

  Read the budget as an UPPER bound, not an expected error: it adds
  the worst observed shift from each artifact, and the two need not
  align in sign on any real point set. The residual below is
  correspondingly a LOWER bound on how much is structure.

  A residual of 0.082 survives the budget,
  still well above the 0.021 matched-N baseline.
  SIM-B's DIRECTION survives: the Cascade does read lower, and
  not only because it is sparser. Its MAGNITUDE does not — the
  0.334 figure is quoted to a precision the method does not
  support, and 'decisive' is doing more work than it has earned.

  What would settle it: rerun SIM-B with Ammann-Beenker subsampled
  to 1,024 points, and with a Poisson control also at 1,024 in the
  Cascade's own bounding box. Both are cheap. Neither was run.

  This control cannot recover the true Cascade dimension, and does
  not claim to. It sizes the artifact, nothing more.
