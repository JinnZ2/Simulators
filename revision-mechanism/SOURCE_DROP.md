# Gap: revision mechanisms in transmitted knowledge under high environmental change

Study design. Companion to the hazard-knowledge decay study; uses its
M1–M8 coding scheme. Runnable independently, but the comparison is the point.

CC0. No rights reserved.

## Inversion

Knowledge-transmission research draws mainly on the written record. That
record is one frame's output, so studying it measures that frame's retention
characteristics. It is not an independent sample.

Living transmission systems in HIGH-CHANGE environments are the independent
sample, and they are the informative one:

    stable environment  ->  good and bad transmission both survive.
                            No discrimination. Nothing is being tested.
    high change         ->  transmission is load-tested continuously.
                            What failed is gone; what persists was
                            revised or was robust.

Change is the selection pressure. It is the only condition under which the
adaptive mechanism is visible at all.

## Object of study

Not the content. The REVISION MECHANISM.

    content   what is known                  (companion study measures decay)
    revision  how the system updates what is known when conditions move

A tradition with no revision mechanism and one with a strong mechanism look
identical until conditions change. Sampling only stable systems guarantees
the mechanism stays invisible.

## Site selection

    CRITERIA
      documented environmental change within living or 2-3 generation memory
      an INDEPENDENT physical record of that change
      continuous human occupation across the change
      transmission still active, not reconstructed from archives

    CANDIDATE CLASSES (independent physical records exist for all)
      cryosphere change        sea ice, glacier retreat, permafrost
      hydrological shift       regulation, aquifer decline, channel migration
      fire regime shift        suppression era and after
      coastal change           subsidence, erosion, salinisation
      post-disaster reoccupation
      species range shift      subsistence species arriving or leaving

    Stratify by RATE of change, not only magnitude. Rate mismatch between
    environmental change and the transmission cycle is a candidate failure
    mechanism and must be a measured variable, not a confound.

## Measures

For each knowledge component (M1–M8 from the companion study):

    STATUS
      held unchanged
      revised          content changed, function retained
      extended         new content added for new conditions
      dropped          no longer transmitted
      held obsolete    still transmitted, no longer matches conditions

    REVISION PROVENANCE
      who is licensed to revise
      what evidence triggers a revision
      how a revision propagates to other holders
      how disagreement between holders is resolved
      explicit procedure, or undeclared

    LATENCY
      time from observable environmental change to revised transmission

    HELD-OBSOLETE RATE
      fraction of still-transmitted components that no longer match
      conditions. This is the transmission system's own error rate and the
      single most comparable number across sites.

    ROBUSTNESS FORM
      retained because revised, or retained because stated generally enough
      to survive the change

## Core comparisons

    1  high-change vs low-change site, same domain
       PREDICTION  revision machinery explicit and named in high-change
                   sites, undeclared or absent in low-change ones

    2  fast-change vs slow-change sites
       PREDICTION  held-obsolete rate rises when environmental change rate
                   exceeds transmission cycle length. A rate-mismatch
                   threshold should appear as a discontinuity, not a slope.

    3  component form
       PREDICTION  mechanism-level components (M3) survive change at higher
                   rates than rule-level components (M7). If instead the
                   action rule survives and the mechanism does not, the
                   system is carrying forward instructions that no longer
                   fit — the failure mode worth warning about.

    4  written vs living transmission, same domain and region
       PREDICTION  written records show LOWER revision rates and HIGHER
                   held-obsolete rates, because the medium has no mechanism
                   for retraction. Tests the framing claim rather than
                   assuming it.

## Why this beats the archive

    archives       capture what one frame recorded, at the granularity that
                   frame valued, with no record of what it declined to
                   record. Retention is measurable; revision usually is not,
                   because superseded versions were not kept.

    living system  revision is directly observable and can be asked about.
                   Holders can state the procedure. Archives cannot supply
                   that quantity at all.

## Confounds

Carry over all confounds from the companion study — official backflow,
out-migration survivorship, interviewer frame import, language and register,
prestige asymmetry — plus:

    ATTRIBUTION OF REVISION
      a revision may be adopted from outside and later held as internal.
      Ask provenance for every revision, not only for every component.

    SURVIVOR SITES ONLY
      sites where transmission failed completely are not available for
      interview. The sample is truncated at the failure end. State it;
      it bounds every conclusion about failure rates. Partial remedy:
      sites where occupation continued but transmission is known to have
      broken are the closest observable case to the truncated class.

    CHANGE AS FRAME
      "high change" is defined from an outside physical record. Holders may
      partition change differently. Record their partition separately and do
      not overwrite it. A mismatch between the two partitions is a finding.

## Ethics and ownership

Same requirements as the companion study, binding harder here. Revision
procedures are governance, not only content — who is licensed to revise is
often internal authority structure.

    community consent at the collective level
    communities decide what is publishable
    co-authorship where wanted
    deliver results back in usable form before publication

Publishing a group's revision procedure without consent can damage the
mechanism being studied. That is a hazard, not a formality.

## Falsifiers

    no difference in revision machinery between high- and low-change sites
      -> revision is not change-driven; the framing is wrong

    held-obsolete rate uncorrelated with change rate
      -> rate mismatch is not the mechanism; look elsewhere

    written records show revision rates equal to living systems
      -> the medium claim is wrong and should be retracted

## Ask

One site pair, one domain. Publish the held-obsolete rate, the component-form
result, and the provenance coding. Report the truncation at the failure end
rather than estimating around it.
