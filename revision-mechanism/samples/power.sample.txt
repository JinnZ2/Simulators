REVISION MECHANISM -- what the ask's sample size delivers

SOURCE_DROP.md asks for "One site pair, one domain." This computes
which of its four comparisons a pair carries. It uses no field
data, touches no community, and says nothing about any real site or
any real body of knowledge.

THE STUDY IS NOT RUN HERE AND IS NOT SIMULATED. It requires
fieldwork and collective consent, and its own ethics section says
publishing a group's revision procedure without consent can damage
the mechanism being studied. No synthetic site stands in for a real
one anywhere below. The only objects are a line, a step, and
binomial noise.

0. THE COMPANION SCHEME
   M1-M8 named in the drop: True
   present in this repository: True
   reconstructed: False
   components: M1, M2, M3, M4, M5, M6, M7, M8
   source: transmission-decay.scheme.COMPONENTS, imported
   comparison 3's M3 and M7 resolve: True
   measures that key off it:
     - STATUS (per component)
     - REVISION PROVENANCE (per component)
     - LATENCY (per component)
     - HELD-OBSOLETE RATE (denominator is components)
     - ROBUSTNESS FORM (per component)
     - comparison 3 (M3 vs M7 by name)
   a coding scheme is data. Inventing M1-M8 would have put a
   category system in the author's mouth and every number
   downstream would have been about the invention. It landed
   instead, and is imported.

1. WHAT ONE SITE PAIR CARRIES
   #   comparison                                 kind           at a pair
   1   high-change vs low-change, same domain     CATEGORICAL    yes
   2   fast-change vs slow-change                 SHAPE          NO
   3   component form, M3 vs M7                   WITHIN_SITE    yes
   4   written vs living, same domain and region  CROSS_MEDIUM   yes

   Three of four. The one that does not is comparison 2, and it is
   the one whose prediction is a THRESHOLD.

2. WHY n=2 CANNOT SEE A DISCONTINUITY -- exact, not statistical
   arbitrary pairs tested: 500
   largest residual, either model: 2.47e-32
   both fit exactly: True
   a line has two free parameters and a step has two. Two points
   determine both exactly, so the residual carries no information
   about which generated them. No per-site precision changes this.

3. THE SITE COUNT COMPARISON 2 REQUIRES
   accuracy at telling a step from a slope, by site count
   and by components measured per site (M)

   n     M=10   M=30   M=100
   2     tie    tie    tie  
   3     0.66   0.80   0.93 
   4     0.70   0.81   0.94 
   6     0.73   0.85   0.98 
   8     0.75   0.88   0.98 
   12    0.84   0.93   0.99 
   20    0.87   0.97   1.00 

   `tie` is not low power. At n=2 both models fit every dataset
   exactly, so nothing is decided at any M -- the row is empty
   rather than weak.
   Reading across: comparison 2 becomes decidable at roughly four
   to six sites when about a hundred components are coded per site,
   and eight to twelve at thirty. At ten components per site,
   twenty sites still do not reach 0.9.

4. AN ASYMMETRY IN THE HEADLINE NUMBER
   held-obsolete rate, true value 0.35
   unassessed share   observed       understated by
   0.00               0.350          0.000  (0%)
   0.10               0.315          0.035  (10%)
   0.20               0.280          0.070  (20%)
   0.30               0.245          0.105  (30%)
   0.50               0.175          0.175  (50%)
   the bias runs one way. An unassessed component enters the
   denominator and cannot enter the numerator, so the transmission
   system's own error rate is reported lower than it is, by exactly
   the unassessed share.
   WHAT WOULD CLOSE IT: a sixth STATUS value -- held unchanged,
   match confirmed -- distinct from held unchanged, match not
   assessed. Two states, currently one.
