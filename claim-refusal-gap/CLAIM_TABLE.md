## CRG_001 — the tilde on the baseline

The anchor table gives liability at ~35 → 45 and physical damage at
~25 → ~25. The document's +10 is 45 minus ~35. Reading a tilde as ±2
points `[CHOICE 1]`: the treated arm moves +8..+12, the control arm
−4..+4, and the difference of deltas — the held-constant design's
residual — is +4..+16. The direction survives any band under 5 points;
the magnitude is what the tilde is carrying. The document's argument is
about the direction (both arms or neither), and that holds.

## CRG_002 — one reading, two causes

`UM rate = U / B`. A claimant refused on BI who files on UM moves the
pair from (U, B) to (U+d, B−d). A world with d more uninsured drivers
and d fewer BI claims is the same pair. From the ratio alone the two
are not distinguishable, which is the document's claim stated as
arithmetic. Its NOTE that BI rose and the ratio rose anyway is the
log-derivative: with BI +10% and the ratio +10%, UM claims grew 21%.

## CRG_003 — the seam

Four rows, four stated deltas, all reproducing from the two vintages;
mean seam 1.45, the document's "~1.5". On the newer basis 2023 (15.4)
is below 1993 (16.0); restated by the seam it is 16.9 and above. The
document says both readings live; the instrument shows the seam is the
only thing that decides between them, and E-5's reconstruction is the
seam measured.

## CRG_004 — the netting bound

Litigation rose from 10% to 18% of claimants, +8, against a CWP move
of +10. If every added lawsuit were a re-routed payment the netted CWP
move is +2; if none, +10. "Same order of magnitude" is a ratio of 0.80,
and the open quantity E-3 estimates is where in [2, 10] the netted move
sits.

## CRG_005 — the factor is the appeal rate

Total wrongful refusals = appealed × overturn + unappealed × (rate a
blind audit would find). Under E-1's two readings — selective on merit
(unappealed rate 0) and capacity (rates converge) — the totals differ
by exactly 1/appeal_rate: 100 at 1%. The document's "~100x" is that
identity. The instrument returns None for the total while the
unappealed rate is None, which is the delivered state; a default of 0
would be the "selective on merit" reading assumed rather than measured.

## CRG_006 — a second reading, and the design that separates them

Overturn 38 → 52.5 with appeals flat under 1%: the document says this
has no benign reading, the decisions got worse. A review standard that
moved — reviewers overturning more of the same decisions — produces the
same two series and says nothing about the decisions. That is the
`criteria-drift` shape, the ruler moving. E-1 as designed already
separates the two: reviewers blind to the original determination,
applying policy language only, on a random sample. So the sentence
overstates and the experiment beneath it does not.
