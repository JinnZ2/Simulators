
[WHY]
self-report is unreliable in BOTH directions for this corpus

[OVERWRITE_1]
status devaluation — holders were taught the knowledge marked them as primitive/broke. Retrieval fails even for the holder. Nostalgia reports the STATE, not the METHOD.

[OVERWRITE_2]
present-day reading imposed backward (esp. onto union material)

[OVERWRITE_3]
the era's own organizational self-presentation (solidarity framing was recruitment copy)

[RULE]
prefer PROCEDURE and RESIDUE over TESTIMONY. Bylaws, dues schedules, benefit administration records, inventories, terms sheets. Not accounts of how it felt.

[ROLE_BINDINGS]
era assigned functions by sex and age. Role bindings are SUBSTITUTABLE SUBSTRATE — strip them. The function (contribution scheduling, rotation, gap coverage, small-scale fund solvency, transmission-by-doing) is the extraction target. Extract mechanism and ratios, drop role bindings. Same as reading branching rules off the lung without importing the lung.

[LABOR_NOTE]
what went into it was unpaid and largely women's and children's hours. Therefore residue sits where nothing was logged: kitchen equipment, storage volume, outbuildings. NOT in records or membership rolls.
