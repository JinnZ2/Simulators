  radius ratio, area-preserving   beta  = n^-1/2 = 0.7071
  length ratio, space-filling     gamma = n^-1/3 = 0.7937
  radius ratio, Murray's law      beta  = n^-1/3 = 0.7937

  DELIVERED: 'area-preserving junctions, r_ratio = 2^(-1/3) ~= 0.794'
  0.7937 is the space-filling LENGTH ratio, or equivalently the
  Murray's-law RADIUS ratio. It is not the area-preserving radius
  ratio, which is 0.7071. The value is right; the label names the
  wrong quantity under the wrong assumption.

  metabolic exponent, derived over 20 levels: 0.750000  (3/4 = 0.75)
  It needs BOTH ratios. One number cannot stand for both.

AGGREGATE CROSS-SECTION, n * beta^2 per generation
  area-preserving    1.0000   CONSTANT
  Murray's law       1.2599   widens x1.2599 per generation

  DELIVERED: 'area-preserving ... => aggregate cross-section WIDENS
  every generation'. Under area preservation it is CONSTANT -- that is
  what the name asserts. It widens under Murray's law, the small-vessel
  regime. So 'trunk never wins by construction' holds in the Murray
  regime and is NEUTRAL, not supporting, in the area-preserving one.
  The conclusion survives; the stated route to it does not.
