# gaps

Open measurables. Each states what would measure it, per the audit contract:
a claim without a measurement is not an answer.

---

**G-PORT** — highest priority, named in the README.

Old systems used geographic proximity as the enforcement and verification
mechanism. Does the verification cut port without proximity?

*What would measure it:* two populations matched on custody, parallel path,
slack and horizon, differing only in whether the contributor can physically
inspect the node. Default rate, or residual retention, as the readout. The
comparison exists in principle wherever a mutual has both local and remote
membership on the same book.

*State:* UNRESOLVED. No case in `cases.json` distinguishes proximity from
verification, because every SEED case bundles them.

---

**G-CUTS** — the criterion reads two of five cuts.

*What would measure it:* already computed. `extract.py` reports disagreements
in both directions, and both are present in the seed set. What is NOT settled
is which reading is right — whether the three unread cuts are missing
conjuncts or diagnostics that should not gate.

*What would settle it:* a case where slack or horizon alone flips an outcome
that custody and verification agree on. `owner_operator_trucking` is written
to be that case and is a SEED, so it settles nothing yet.

---

**G-REGIME** — which branching regime the analogy is drawn from.

The aggregate cross-section widens under Murray's law and is constant under
area preservation. Real vasculature is area-preserving in large vessels and
Murray-like in small ones. The anchor's conclusion needs the small-vessel
regime.

*What would measure it:* nothing in this folder, because the anchor is a
physical fact about vessels and the claim it is anchoring is about economic
layers. **The transfer is the unmeasured step, not the physics.** No
measurement here licenses the transfer, and none is proposed.

---

**G-BINARY** — is the criterion binary because the world is?

Kaiser 2020 gives a discontinuous loop transition, which is the one anchor
arguing for a binary criterion rather than a graded one. The criterion is
binary.

*What would measure it:* whether cases cluster bimodally on the five-cut
count or spread evenly. Six SEED cases cannot show a distribution. Needs
enough CITED cases to have a shape.

---

**G-CITED** — no case is CITED and no source is read.

*What would measure it:* reading them. The corpus is four sources and the
README says months, not sessions.
