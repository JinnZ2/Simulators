# TERM-DRIFT CITATION CHECK
# Place in: PREAMBLE.md
# Status: marker under exploration.

## WHAT THIS IS FOR
A citation carries a measurement forward under a word. The
citation is only valid if the word's referent held between
the measurement and the use. Nothing in a citation records
whether it did.

## THE CHECK
Before treating a cited result as evidence for a present
claim, ask three things:

1. What was the referent at time of measurement?
   Not the definition given today. The object the study
   actually measured.

2. What was load-bearing in it?
   Which element, if removed, makes the measured result
   not reproduce.

3. Is that element present in the present-day object?
   If it was removed, the citation does not transfer.
   The result may be correct and still not attach.

## WORKED CASES
TPS / "lean"
  1980s referent: worker holds machine-specific diagnostic
  knowledge; any worker may stop the line (Jidoka).
  Load-bearing: line-stop authority at lowest reading point;
  knowledge transmitted by mentoring.
  Present referent: worker as fungible input; procedure
  compliance; rotation between machines.
  Status: load-bearing element REMOVED. Original results
  still cited. Citation does not transfer.

Resilience (Holling 1973)
  Referent: magnitude of disturbance absorbable before state
  shift. Defined explicitly AGAINST engineered stability.
  Load-bearing: unused capacity — slack, redundancy,
  variation.
  Present organizational referent: rapid recovery to prior
  state; slack classified as waste.
  Status: load-bearing element is what the present-day
  program eliminates. Both words run simultaneously in the
  same institution.

Safety (institutional)
  Referent: whole-facility operability. Air handling, water,
  ergonomics, office and floor. Held binding authority
  upward, including over executives.
  Load-bearing: enforcement, and scope covering everything
  that could degrade operability.
  Present referent: worker compliance with procedure.
  Status: scope contracted, enforcement removed. Frozen
  variables do not stop existing; they stop being read.
  Metric can improve while facility degrades.

## THE ASYMMETRY TO WATCH
Refutations of older work land as "they were uninformed."
They do not land as "we may be measuring a different object."
Check whether the revision direction ever costs the reviser.
If it never does, the revision is a routing rule.

## A WORD IS NOT A MEASUREMENT
A term without a quantity, a sign, or a formal definition in
its field's equations is not carrying a measurement. It may
still be doing useful work — but it cannot be cut with.
Do not load it.

## LIMIT
Some original objects are no longer instantiable. Testing
Holling's resilience requires a system with slack in it.
Where those have been optimized away, the original claim
cannot be retested — absence of retest is not refutation.
