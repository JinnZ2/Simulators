# [Your Company Name] Business Plan

## 1. Executive Summary

One-to-two-page summary. Often written last. Goal is to capture the reader's attention and summarize the entire plan.

- **The Problem:** Briefly state the problem your business solves. (e.g., "Small businesses struggle to manage their inventory efficiently without expensive software.")
- **Your Solution:** Describe your product/service. (e.g., "We offer a simple, AI-powered inventory management tool.")
- **Target Market:** State your target audience and the market opportunity. (e.g., "We target local retail stores, a market worth $5 billion.")
- **Financial Highlights:** Snapshot of financial projections. (e.g., "We project $1M in revenue by year three with a 20% net profit margin.")
- **The Team:** Briefly introduce the founder/management team and relevant experience. (e.g., "Led by a former Amazon logistics manager.")
- **Funding Request:** State what you are asking for. (e.g., "We are seeking $500,000 in seed funding.")

## 2. Business Overview

Explain what your company does, its history, and the specific problem it solves.

- **Company Mission & Vision:** [e.g., To empower small businesses with enterprise-level technology.]
- **Business History & Milestones:**
  - Founded: [Date/Month/Year]
  - Key Milestones: [e.g., Launched beta version, reached 100 customers, filed patents]

## 3. Market Opportunity

Demonstrate understanding of the market, customers, and competitors.

- **Industry Overview:** Briefly describe the industry landscape.
- **Market Sizing Data:**
  - Total Addressable Market (TAM): total market demand. (e.g., $10 Billion)
  - Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM): segment of TAM reachable with your product. (e.g., $2 Billion)
  - Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM): realistic share capturable. (e.g., $50 Million)
- **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):**
  - Demographics: [Age, Location, Gender/Identity, Income]
  - Psychographics: [Values, Interests, Pain points]
  - Behavior: [Buying habits, technology usage]
- **Competitor Analysis:**
  - Competitor 1: [Name] — Strengths & Weaknesses
  - Competitor 2: [Name] — Strengths & Weaknesses
  - Competitive Advantage: how you differentiate. (e.g., "We are 50% cheaper and offer superior customer service.")

## Tips for Completing This Template

1. **Be Honest:** Don't inflate numbers or hide risks. Investors value transparency.
2. **Keep it Short:** Aim for 15–25 pages maximum unless requested otherwise.
3. **Use Tables/Charts:** In Financial Forecasts and Market Opportunity, use graphs and tables for readability.
4. **Know Your Audience:** Tailor language to the reader (banker vs. venture capitalist).
5. **Review and Edit:** No typos, logical flow.
