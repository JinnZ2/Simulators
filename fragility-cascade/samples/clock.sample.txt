Volatility classes registered: 6
  constant     no expiry  holds until the substrate changes
  structural       9125d  holds across a generation of practice
  regime           1825d  holds while the governing conditions hold
  annual            365d  holds within a cycle, not across it
  seasonal           90d  holds within one phase of the cycle
  event               1d  holds only near the occasion

Freshness bands (remaining fraction thresholds):
  FRESH     >= 0.75
  DECAYING  >= 0.35
  STALE     >= 0.1
  EXPIRED   >= 0.0
