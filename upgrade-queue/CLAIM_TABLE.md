# CLAIM_TABLE — upgrade-queue

Claims are properties of the delivered queue and of the checker, **not
adoptions** — the queue's own first rule is that nothing is adopted by being
written, and this folder adopts nothing. `UQ_*` prefix. Every structural
number is computed by `selftest_uq.py` from `UPGRADE_QUEUE.md` as the source
of truth; the render is in `samples/`.

## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

A refuted claim is updated forward with a new id; the old id keeps its text
and gains an UPDATE paragraph. `UPGRADE_QUEUE.md` is delivered verbatim and
is not edited. Moving any `U-NN` entry to ADOPTED is out of scope here — it
requires the entry's adopt-test to have run, or an explicit recorded
decision, per the queue's own rules; `queue_check.py` reports status and
never sets it.
