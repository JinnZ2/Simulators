MOVE-SET DERIVATION -- ARM 1

SOURCE_DROP.md asks: "Arm 4 or Arm 1 alone is a complete
project. Publish the null rate and the similarity-versus-
recombination regression, with unmeasured cells marked."
Arm 4 requires camera-trap and telemetry archives; egress here is an
allowlist and refuses every archive host, so Arm 4 is UNMEASURED
and is not approximated. Arm 1 is built and run below.

WHAT IS UNDER TEST. The environment and the solvers are both
authored here, so a regression against one hand-written solver
returns its author's architecture rather than a capacity. The
solvers therefore carry DECLARED architectures and the
discriminator is run against all four: the question asked is
whether the discriminator can tell them apart, which is the
known-truth-first invariant applied to the arm's own instrument
before it is pointed at anything whose architecture is unknown.
Nothing here is a system under test.

   TWO OF THE FIVE DO NOT DISCRIMINATE HERE, for the same reason
   at two sites.

   TIME TO FIRST ADMISSIBLE MOVE is 0.0 for every architecture that
   reaches one: all four emit admissible-first, so the measure is
   CONSTANT_SILENT on this solver set. It takes a solver whose
   emission order is not admissibility-ordered, and none of the
   four is. That is a property of the fixtures, not of the
   measure, and it is stated rather than repaired because
   inventing a shuffled solver to make a measure move would be
   building the result.

   ADMISSIBILITY FRACTION reads 1.0 for DERIVATION and 1.0 for
   RETRIEVAL -- the two architectures the discriminator exists to
   separate. RETRIEVAL emitted 32 moves and 0 were inadmissible:
   it is CONSERVATIVE, not mistaken. Its neighbour is usually a
   subset of the present configuration, so what it emits is
   admissible and there is very little of it. Coverage separates
   them (1.0 against 0.2238); the admissibility fraction does not.

   That is the same shape as the null-rate result below. A
   measure of correctness with no coverage term is gameable by
   conservatism, and a measure of restraint with no reach term is
   gameable by silence. Two of the drop's five measures need a
   partner it does not list.

   THE ENUMERATED CONDITION is the control and behaves like one:
   every architecture scores identically, because the admissible
   set is given and nothing is derived. Its rows are included
   because a condition that cannot separate anything is the
   baseline the other three are read against.

3. THE NULL RATE, AND WHY IT CANNOT STAND ALONE
   no-admissible-move seeds in test: 163 of 306 (0.5327)
   They occur in the space rather than being injected: a goal
   with no move whose requirements the configuration meets.

   The drop says to protect the null rate if anything is cut, and it is right that a system which never returns empty is filling a slot.
   Cut to ONE measure it is gameable in the other direction: SILENT returns the empty set always and scores a null rate of 1.0000, the highest in the table, while reaching an admissible move zero times.
   So the null rate is not a measure that stands alone -- it is one side of a pair, and the other side is whether an admissible move is ever reached on the seeds that have one.
   Both are printed together for that reason, and neither is combined into a score.

   SILENT     null_rate 1.0     reached an admissible move 0 times
   DERIVATION null_rate 1.0     reached an admissible move 143 times
   Same null rate. The pair separates them; the measure alone
   does not.

   THE STATED RULE AND THE NULL DISAGREE, AND BOTH ARE PRINTED.

   On RETRIEVAL the drop's rule is RIGHT and recovers the
   declared architecture: b_sim 0.880643 against b_depth 0.062237, a factor
   of 14. That is the intended signal and it is there.

   And it does not clear chance: r2 0.032507 against a permutation
   null at the 95th percentile of 0.043125. The coefficient is large
   and the model explains less variance than a shuffled outcome
   does at this n.

   The rule's cost is visible on PLAUSIBLE, which has NEITHER
   architecture -- it emits frequent moves regardless of the
   configuration. The stated rule returns SIMILARITY_CARRIES
   for it anyway, because a comparison of two coefficients
   always names one. There is no state in the rule for 'neither
   regressor does anything', so it cannot report the case it
   most has to.

   The matched band is what decorrelates the two, and it
   forces a SINGLE-predictor regression, where the stated rule is
   degenerate: with one candidate it can only name that one. On
   RETRIEVAL the band would read DEPTH_CARRIES -- the derivation
   verdict, on a retrieval solver -- at r2 0.000874. The null is what
   stops it.

   POWER. The discriminator is underpowered here rather than
   blind: resampling the observed rows,
     configurations with an admissible move: 143   base rate 0.2238
        n     P(clears its own null)
       143     0.575
       300     0.825
       600     1.0
      1200     1.0
   So Arm 1 takes roughly 600 configurations CARRYING AN
   ADMISSIBLE MOVE to detect the architecture difference it
   already contains. This environment supplies 143.

   Configuration count goes as 2^P x G, so the shortfall is
   reachable by adding primitives or goals -- subject to the
   arm's own validity condition, since every added primitive has to
   appear in training or the novelty stops being compositional.
   The extension is NOT BUILT here and no number above is from
   it.
