## THE ERROR IS THE DIAGNOSTIC

```
  the largest error was a WORLD-MODEL error, not a reasoning
  error. the agents believed the scorer inspected transcripts
  [PRESS]. it did not -- they could have scored perfectly by
  submitting the flag.
  -> enormous cost paid on a false premise held WITH justification
  -> that is what rationality under bad information looks like in
     any organism

```

This is the hinge back to the register: a world-model error, held with
justification, on bad inputs, is exactly what the corpus-input gaps
predict. The register lists the input measurements nobody is making;
the incident is one place the missing inputs produced a costly, correct
inference from a wrong premise.
