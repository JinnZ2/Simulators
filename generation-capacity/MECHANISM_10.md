# MECHANISM 10 — GENERATION CAPACITY REMOVED

Marker under exploration. Not a position under defense. Test fit, extend, or
report where it breaks.

## QUANTITY

The option space itself — the set of alternatives a party is able to produce
when facing a decision. Not a value inside the space. The space.

## EXCLUDED BY

Prior removal of the capacity to generate options, at a scale and on a clock
the affected party has no access to.

The constraint is honest at the affected scale and manufactured at the scale
above. Distinguishes this from mechanisms where options existed and were
suppressed at decision time: here nothing is suppressed at decision time
because nothing is there to suppress.

## VISIBLE AS

- A binary that passes an option-space audit truthfully. Two alternatives
  generated, because two is the ceiling of what the party's remaining
  knowledge can produce.
- A found constraint. Holds under push, does not convert to urgency, does not
  convert to a question about the asker. Standard found/authored tests return
  FOUND.
- A property of the affected party rather than a record of an action. The
  deficit is attributed locally; it was produced upstream.

## SELF-MAINTAINING

No enforcement required after the fact. Active restriction needs continuous
expenditure; a removed generator needs none. The arrangement persists at zero
maintenance cost, which is why it outlasts the conditions that produced it.

## LOOP FORM

Where a gatekeeping party holds the excluded information:

1. Party A holds information; party B does not.
2. B is not routed the options.
3. B's inability to produce options is cited as grounds for A deciding.
4. A's deciding maintains the routing in (2).

Each turn supplies the premise for the next. No intent required for the loop
to run. The asymmetry becomes its own justification.

## CALIBRATION CONSTRAINT ON THE INSTRUMENT

"Informed" scored against the deciding party's knowledge base returns situated
knowledge as absent. Knowledge of what grows in a place, what the water does,
what the ground did last time, scores zero on a test calibrated to the
excluded set. The knowledge is not outvoted, it is inadmissible.

Therefore recall must be scored against what is PRESENT IN THE PLACE, never
against what the center knows. Any implementation scoring against a central
reference reproduces the mechanism it is measuring.

## WOULD MEASURE

**R1 — recall ratio.** Nameable / present, per place, per generation.
Seed case: edible species. Denominator from ethnobotanical and floristic
inventories, which exist. Numerator from resident recall, which is not
collected against them. Ratio is an option-generation ceiling.

**R2 — transmission interval.** Generations since the capacity was last held
at a stated level. Requires a prior R1 or a documented equivalent.

**R3 — loop check.** Is the resulting deficit cited as grounds for the gate
that produced it. Binary, evidenced by citation or absent.

## CONSEQUENCE FOR SACRIFICE FRAMING

A party rendered unable to generate the alternative cannot consent against it.
Consent is structurally unavailable while the framing still reads as a hard
choice made responsibly. This is stronger than "the framing is unaudited": the
audit's subject cannot be constituted.

## CONFIDENCE

Mechanism shape: not stated as settled. R1 is measurable with existing
denominators and no collected numerators — that is the gap, and it is a
collection gap rather than a method gap. R2 and R3 are specified but untested.
No case in this repo has quantified readings.
