# SUB-CASE 10A — IRRECOVERABLE SOURCE

Sub-case of mechanism 10. Marker under exploration. Test fit, extend, or
report where it breaks.

## WHAT SEPARATES IT FROM THE PARENT

In mechanism 10 as stated, capacity is removed from one party by another. A
gate is held; a population is excluded; the resulting deficit is cited as
grounds for the gate. There is a gatekeeper, an excluded party, and — in
principle — someone positioned to notice.

Here the party doing the removal and the party bearing the loss are the same,
and the source cannot be returned to.

Seed case: a specimen destroyed to obtain one reading from it. The reading is
obtained. Every reading not yet asked for is foreclosed at the same moment.

## WHY IT DOES NOT SHOW UP AS A LOSS

Three properties, each of which independently prevents the loss from
registering:

**No excluded party.** The only party who could notice is the one who acted,
and that party received what it went for. The transaction closes as a
success.

**The foreclosed set is unbounded and unformed.** The questions that would
have been asked in a year, or in twenty, do not exist yet to be counted. The
loss cannot be enumerated even after the fact.

**Zero is substituted for unknown.** The reading obtained is one, bounded,
and of known value. The readings foreclosed have no representable magnitude.
Any procedure comparing them on one ledger takes the unrepresentable term as
zero rather than as unmeasured, and therefore favours the destructive option
every time — not by error in the comparison, but because one side of it
cannot be entered.

The third property is the same substitution as the calibration constraint in
the parent mechanism, at a different site. There, "informed" scored against
the deciding party's knowledge base returns situated knowledge as absent
rather than as inadmissible. Here, future readings scored against a present
ledger return as zero rather than as unformed. Same move: an unmeasurable
quantity is entered as a measured zero.

## CONSEQUENCE FOR THE PARENT'S READOUTS

R1 (recall ratio) does not apply. R1 requires a denominator — what is present
in the place — against which recall is scored. In the parent case the
denominator survives: the plants are still growing whether or not anyone can
name them, so the ratio is recoverable in principle and the gap is a
collection gap.

Here the denominator is destroyed with the source. There is no later count
that could establish what was lost. **A readout is not merely uncollected;
it is unconstitutable.**

This is the sharper form of the parent's consequence for consent framing. In
the parent, the audit's subject cannot be constituted. Here, the audit's
denominator cannot be constituted either.

## WHAT WOULD MEASURE IT

Not the loss — the loss is unmeasurable by construction, and any tool
claiming to quantify it is producing a number where none exists.

What is measurable is the **procedure**: whether the decision record entered
the foreclosed set as zero or as unknown.

**S1 — ledger check.** In the documented justification for a destructive
reading, is the set of foreclosed future readings (a) absent, (b) entered as
zero or negligible, or (c) entered as unknown and unbounded? Three-valued,
evidenced by citation from the record. Absent and zero are distinguishable:
absent means the term never appeared, zero means it appeared and was
discounted.

**S2 — reversibility declared.** Does the record state whether the source
survives the reading? Binary, evidenced or absent. A record that does not
state this cannot have weighed it.

**S3 — non-destructive alternative considered.** Was a reading method
preserving the source enumerated and rejected on stated grounds, enumerated
and unavailable, or not enumerated. Three-valued. Routes to
`presented-binary` where the record shows two options and no enumeration.

None of the three requires knowing what was lost. All three are properties of
the record, and records exist.

## OPEN

- The seed case is a physical specimen. Whether the sub-case extends to
  sources that are not objects — a practice that stops being performed, a
  speaker who is the last of a language — is not settled here. The three
  properties appear to hold, but the boundary has not been tested and should
  be fixed before cases are classified, not after.
- S1 requires reading decision records that may not be public. Availability
  is unassessed.
- No case in this file has quantified readings. This is a schema, not a
  finding.
