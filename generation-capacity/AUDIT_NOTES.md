## What the drop is

**MECHANISM 10 — GENERATION CAPACITY REMOVED.** A proposed tenth
exclusion mechanism for `uninstrumented/`, arriving one drop after
`category-weld/`'s ninth.

The quantity is the option space itself — the set of alternatives a party
can produce — not a value inside it. What excludes it is prior removal of
the capacity to generate options, at a scale and on a clock the affected
party has no access to. The distinguishing sentence, from EXCLUDED BY:

> here nothing is suppressed at decision time because nothing is there to
> suppress

Three readouts specified: **R1** recall ratio (nameable / present, per
place, per generation), **R2** transmission interval, **R3** loop check.
The drop states its own confidence: R1 measurable with existing
denominators and no collected numerators, R2 and R3 specified and
untested, no case in this repo quantified.

Marked in the drop as a marker under exploration, not a position under
defense.

## 1 — GC_001, the claim is checkable and it holds

VISIBLE AS makes a claim about instruments rather than about the world:

> A binary that passes an option-space audit truthfully. Two alternatives
> generated, because two is the ceiling of what the party's remaining
> knowledge can produce.

`presented-binary/binary_audit.py` — landed in this repo one commit
earlier — is an option-space audit with eleven checks. Answered honestly
by a party under this mechanism, every answer true and every answer with a
record behind it:

    O1  [documented] two
    O2  [documented] three weeks
    O3  [documented] generated by the affected party; all inside the set
    O4  [documented] attempt recorded, not only result
    O5  [documented] widened twice; returned nothing new
    O6  [documented] each constraint held under push; FOUND, not authored
    S1..S5 documented

    documented 11   asserted 0   absent 0   documented_share 1.000

**O3 is the cell the mechanism is aimed at.** It asks *"who generated
them, and are they inside the affected set?"* and it exists to catch
options generated by people who do not carry the consequences. Under
MECHANISM 10 the options ARE generated by the affected party — so the
answer that reassures is the mechanism's signature. **O5** is the second:
widening a search over a reduced generator returns nothing new, which
reads as confirmation that the set was complete.

**The mechanism understates its own case.** `binary_audit` computes no
verdict; `documented_share` is the share of checks with a *record* behind
them and never reads what the record says. Measured:

    favourable   documented 11   documented_share 1.000
    damning      documented 11   documented_share 1.000

Identical. The pass is over-determined — a framing under MECHANISM 10 does
not need favourable answers, because the instrument's only number does not
grade answers.

That is one reading, not two findings. `binary_audit`'s docstring is
explicit that it reports how much of the framing has a record behind it,
which is a deliberate and defensible scope (and `PB_007` already records
what the scalar merges). The consequence for the drop is that "passes an
option-space audit" is a weaker statement than it sounds — and MECHANISM
10 would pass an instrument that *did* grade answers, for the reason the
drop gives.

Either way it is not a defect in any check. All eleven ask about the
decision episode; the removal happened before it, on a clock the answering
party has no access to.

## 2 — GC_002, ANSWERED: the drop built a router, not a check

The first pass argued that adding a check to `binary_audit`'s
option_space block cannot work, because `documented`/`asserted`/`absent`
resolve against the answering party's own record and **an absent generator
produces an absent record of itself** — so the check returns `absent` and
reads as a documentation gap. The conclusion was that MECHANISM 10 needs a
second instrument beside `binary_audit`, not another row.

Drop 2 delivered exactly that: `capacity.py`, plus a `handoff()` router in
`binary_audit` that hands a case across when O1 comes back honest at a low
count. Measured on the delivered router:

    documented, count 2 -- the signature   -> generation-capacity/capacity.py
    documented, count not stated           not routed: O1 documented but count not stated
    absent                                 None

The router's docstring states the reasoning independently: *"An
option-space audit closing clean on a low DOCUMENTED count is the
signature of removed generation capacity, not evidence of its absence."*
And it refuses to estimate — O1 documented without a stated count returns
not-routed **with a reason** rather than a guess, which is the branch that
does the most work in the design.

GC_002 is ANSWERED rather than refuted: the argument was that the repair
is a second instrument, and the repair delivered is a second instrument.
What the router does with its own negative branch is separate, and is
recorded in `presented-binary/` `PB_012`.

## 3 — GC_003, the denominator fork

> Denominator from ethnobotanical and floristic inventories, which exist.
> Numerator from resident recall, which is not collected against them.
>
> ... it is a collection gap rather than a method gap.

The two named sources break in different places, and neither is the
independent denominator the ratio needs as stated.

**Floristic inventory** — Linnaean, produced by the center, independent of
resident recall. A genuine independent denominator, which is what the
ratio needs. But scoring resident recall against it requires mapping local
names onto species: a resident naming one plant where the inventory splits
three, or three where it lumps one. That mapping *is* scoring against a
central reference, which the CALIBRATION CONSTRAINT forbids.

**Ethnobotanical inventory** — collected from residents, so the mapping
problem does not arise; the units already match. But it inherits the
recall of whoever was surveyed when it was made. Measuring recall against
it is measuring recall against earlier recall, which is **R2**, not R1. It
cannot be an independent denominator because it is a previous
generation's numerator.

The independent denominator needs a translation step the constraint
forbids; the translatable denominator is not independent.

This does not sink R1. It **relocates the gap**: part of what the drop
files under collection is a method question — what counts as one nameable
item — and it has to be settled before a numerator is collected, not
after. The drop's own constraint is what surfaces it.

**Against the delivered code.** `capacity.py` implements the calibration
constraint as a `scored_against` field: set to `center`, the reading is
flagged invalid and dropped from the slope, and the selftest covers it.
That closes the case the CALIBRATION CONSTRAINT names explicitly.

It is a **declaration, not a unit check**. Nothing reads `source_present`
or `source_nameable`, so a case can declare `place` and still count its
denominator in Linnaean species and its numerator in local names:

    declared place, mismatched units -> slope -0.23, invalid_scoring 0

The drop's DISCLOSED WEAKNESSES names the **numerator** half of this — free
recall against prompted against recognition against demonstrated use give
different numbers and the schema does not distinguish them. The
**denominator** half is not named, and it is the half the CALIBRATION
CONSTRAINT is actually about: what counts as ONE present item is a
question the center answers.

**The check the schema can already make.** R1 is nameable / present and
cannot exceed 1 when both are counted in the same units. Local categories
splitting finer than the inventory, or an incomplete inventory, push it
over:

    present 40, nameable 55 -> ratio 1.375, valid True, no warning

A ratio above 1 is a unit mismatch, computable from two fields the schema
already carries, needing no new concept — `detail()` already prints a
WARNING block for center-scored readings and could carry this beside it.

G7's stated falsifier is *"a methodological barrier to collecting resident
recall against a floristic inventory that is not a funding or attention
barrier"*. The name-mapping problem is proposed as one. Whether it fires
that falsifier turns on whether settling the unit of a nameable item
counts as methodological or as part of collection — a question for the
author, not one this audit settles.

## 4 — GC_004, R3 can confirm and cannot disconfirm

> R3 — loop check. Is the resulting deficit cited as grounds for the gate
> that produced it. Binary, evidenced by citation or absent.

A citation never searched for and a citation that does not exist both
return `absent`. There is no state meaning *"looked, and the deficit is
not cited"*. `null-harness/` grades this `CONSTANT_SILENT` on the negative
branch.

The repair is the one R1 already has: a denominator. Name the corpus
searched — which decision records, over which interval — so that zero
citations *in a named corpus* is a measurement and zero citations in no
corpus is a gap. `binary_audit.py`'s O4 already works this way; it asks
whether a record EXISTS, not what it says.

R1 and R2 are declared untested. R3 is declared specified, so this is a
statement about the specification rather than about a missing run.

## 5 — GC_005, distinct from the nine, and mirror to one

Checked against structure rather than label, against the eight in
`uninstrumented/README.md` plus `category-weld/`'s ninth. The two that get
closest run in opposite directions.

**AUDIT ASYMMETRY** — "the guard fires on one side of a comparison only".
The LOOP FORM is an asymmetry that becomes its own justification, which is
close. But AUDIT ASYMMETRY is about a guard's behaviour at audit time, and
here no guard misfires: §1 shows the audit running correctly and returning
a clean result. The asymmetry is upstream of the guard.

**AUTHORED REFERENCE** — "the reference is produced by the MEASURED
party", a model authoring the benchmark it is scored on. The CALIBRATION
CONSTRAINT names the mirror image: the reference is produced by the party
whose gate created the deficit, and the *measured* party is the one scored
against it. Same defect in the reference, opposite direction of
authorship.

The mirror is worth its own line rather than folding into either, because
**the remedies are opposite**. AUTHORED REFERENCE is repaired by an
external fixed reference — that is `anchor-interval/` `ANC_005..008` and
`criteria-drift/` `CD_008`, and both build one. MECHANISM 10 is made
*worse* by an external fixed reference if the external reference is the
center's, which is exactly what the CALIBRATION CONSTRAINT says.

What would fold MECHANISM 10 back in: a case where the generator was
reduced AND the reduction is carried somewhere in the record — but that is
suppression at decision time, which the drop's own EXCLUDED BY paragraph
excludes as a different mechanism.

## 6 — GC_006, the rate claim already has instruments

> Active restriction needs continuous expenditure; a removed generator
> needs none. The arrangement persists at zero maintenance cost, which is
> why it outlasts the conditions that produced it.

The strongest sentence in the drop, because it is a claim about cost
asymmetry rather than about anyone's conduct — and this repo has two
instruments already shaped for it.

`rigidification-sensor/simulator.py` records per tick a `continuation`
cost and a `reversal` cost and reports `locked_at`, the first tick where
reversal exceeds continuation AND the derivative does too. Zero
maintenance cost is `continuation → 0`, which reaches the lock condition
with nothing being done. That folder states throughout that "control"
means control PARAMETER, not a hand on a knob — the same "no intent
required" the LOOP FORM states.

`sustained-activation-gate/` is a tilted double well: the system stays in
its basin at zero drive and leaving costs a Kramers escape. A removed
generator is the well that needs nobody holding the system in it.

Neither is a metaphor for the other; they are one rate comparison in three
vocabularies. What connects them concretely: **R2 is the time axis those
simulators run on**, and a measured R2 would give the lock check an
interval instead of a tick index.

Recorded as an available instrument, not a result. Nothing has been run.

## 7 — GC_007, where the load sits

> A party rendered unable to generate the alternative cannot consent
> against it. ... the audit's subject cannot be constituted.

§1 supports the second sentence as far as the instrument reaches: 11 of 11
documented, so "reads as a hard choice made responsibly" is a number here
rather than a characterisation — with §1's qualification that the number
grades record completeness, so what it establishes is that the framing
survives the audit intact, not that the audit approved of it.

The first sentence is a different kind of claim. *Cannot consent against
an option it cannot generate* is close to analytic — consent to forgo X
requires X to be available to consider. What makes it empirical is the
antecedent: **was** the party unable to generate it. That is R1.

    R1 (unmeasured) -> inability (unestablished) -> consent unavailable (follows)

The last step is the cheap one. The drop's CONFIDENCE section says as
much; what this records is that the load is concentrated entirely in R1's
numerator rather than spread across three readouts.

The seed case is chosen well for that: edible species has an existing
denominator and a collectable numerator, and the collection is a survey
rather than an experiment. What it does not have yet is §3's units
decision.

## 8 — GC_008, G3 names an instrument the repo has

> **G3.** The arrangement requires no ongoing enforcement once capacity is
> gone.
> *Status:* untested. Requires an enforcement-cost series, which this repo
> has no instance of.

§6 named two instruments for this rate comparison, and one of them returns
the series G3 says is absent. `rigidification-sensor/simulator.py`,
`run()`, records per tick:

    continuation   : current total imbalance -- the bill this tick
    reversal       : cumulative eroded regen -- capacity you'd rebuild to undo
    d_continuation, d_reversal
    locked_at      : first tick where reversal outpaces continuation AND exceeds it

`continuation` **is** an enforcement-cost series: the per-tick bill for
holding the arrangement where it is. G3 says that bill drops to zero once
capacity is gone; G3's falsifier says a case where it stays flat or rises
refutes the claim. That is a statement about the shape of a series `run()`
already produces.

What the repo does not have is a **measured** series — a real arrangement
with a real enforcement cost per interval. The simulator derives one from
declared node parameters, so wiring the LOOP FORM into it would test the
claim's internal consistency, not the world.

G3's status is right in substance and wrong in its reason: not *"requires
an instrument this repo has no instance of"* but *"requires a measured
series; the instrument is in `rigidification-sensor/`"*. The distinction
changes the next step — collect a series, rather than build a simulator.

## 10 — GC_010, the repair, designed in

`SUBCASE_10A.md`'s S1:

> is the set of foreclosed future readings (a) absent, (b) entered as zero
> or negligible, or (c) entered as unknown and unbounded? ... **Absent and
> zero are distinguishable**: absent means the term never appeared, zero
> means it appeared and was discounted.

That last sentence is the repair this audit has recorded four times, in
four folders, against instruments that shipped without it:

    PB_004   frame_sim option_gain        0 options found == never ran
    PB_012   binary_audit handoff()       above ceiling == never checked
    GC_004   MECHANISM_10 R3              not cited == no corpus searched
    MD_002   moral-decomposer reduces_to  irreducible == routed elsewhere

Each is one value standing for a measurement and for the absence of the
measurement. S1 separates them in the specification and says why in the
same sentence.

Precisely what this does and does not show: S1 has no implementation and
no reading, so it is not evidence the distinction survives contact with a
record. What it shows is that the distinction was designed in rather than
retrofitted — the only point in the cycle where it is free.

S2 and S3 carry the shape too. S2's negative branch is a finding rather
than a gap — *"a record that does not state this cannot have weighed it"*
— and S3 is three-valued on the same axis, routing its third value to
`presented-binary`.

## 11 — GC_011, a third denominator state

`GC_003` found R1's denominator problem in the parent: floristic inventory
is independent but needs a local-name-to-species mapping the CALIBRATION
CONSTRAINT forbids; ethnobotanical inventory is translatable but is a
previous generation's numerator.

The sub-case names a different failure at the same site, and distinguishes
the two correctly:

> In the parent case the denominator survives: the plants are still
> growing whether or not anyone can name them, so the ratio is recoverable
> in principle and the gap is a collection gap.
>
> Here the denominator is destroyed with the source. ... **A readout is not
> merely uncollected; it is unconstitutable.**

Three states, not two:

    collectable        the denominator exists, nobody counted it
    units-blocked      the denominator exists, does not share units with
                       the numerator                    (GC_003, parent)
    unconstitutable    the denominator does not exist and cannot be made
                       to                               (SUBCASE 10A)

`GC_003` stands and is now bounded — a statement about the parent, where a
denominator exists to have a units problem. It does not transfer to the
sub-case, and the sub-case does not rescue it.

The sub-case's own move is the stronger one and it is stated plainly: do
not measure the loss, measure the procedure. *"Any tool claiming to
quantify it is producing a number where none exists."* All three readouts
are properties of the record, and records exist.

## 12 — GC_012, three readouts with no schema slot

    foreclosed         in capacity.py: False
    reversibility      in capacity.py: False
    non_destructive    in capacity.py: False
    source_survives    in capacity.py: False

`capacity.py` scores R1, R2 and R3; its `SKELETON` carries `generations`,
`transmission_gap`, `loop` and `found_constraint_tests`, and nothing holds
a record-level three-valued answer.

The sub-case declares this state — *"No case in this file has quantified
readings. This is a schema, not a finding"* — and it is the same shape as
`MF_017`, `CW_015` and `DL_004`: a rule stated in prose with no schema
slot to express it.

Cheaper here than in any of those. S1 and S3 are three-valued enumerations
over fixed vocabularies and S2 is a boolean with a named negative branch;
all three are properties of one record, so a sub-case entry is flat and
needs no generational structure. The distinguishing work — absent versus
zero — is already done in the specification.

The OPEN section fixes its own boundary before classifying, the third
folder to state that discipline: *"Whether the sub-case extends to sources
that are not objects ... the boundary has not been tested and should be
fixed before cases are classified, not after."*
