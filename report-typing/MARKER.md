# report-typing — MARKER

CC0. Marker under exploration, not a position under defense.
Test fit, extend, or report where it breaks.

Referenced as the canonical shape by: criterion-symmetry,
question-availability, blame-attribution-by-role.

---

## THE MECHANISM

Reports get **typed by the reporter's position**, not by content. The
operation runs on routing, not on evidence. It never has to claim
anything about the report — it only assigns it to a channel where
reading is optional.

That is why it transfers across every form of inequality without
needing a new theory each time:

    testimony rules       account inadmissible rather than false
    standing gates        literacy, language, filing form
    diagnostic categories complaint becomes a symptom of the complainer
    occupational intake   structural report becomes "a complaint"

It is cheap. No argument to win, no enforcement, no visible act.
Everyone experiences themselves as making an ordinary credibility
judgment, so it needs nobody's bad intent and survives the removal of
whoever set it up.

### Register is not the discriminator

Symmetry case: an astronaut's self-report is not a complaint. Nor an
athlete's, a politician's, a CEO's. Same speech act, different type
assigned.

The astronaut case also kills the intake-channel explanation.
Air-to-ground was voice, unrehearsed, colloquial, under load, and
vague — a problem, not a specification. The response was to work the
problem from the report. Same channel properties as an operator on a
phone. So neither register nor format is doing the work.

### Two mechanisms, keep them apart

    ROUTING       per-report. Where does this one go.
    MAINTENANCE   controls the ENTRY condition — who can accumulate the
                  recorded kind of credential, which channels bracket
                  experience into a document, which roles have tiers at all.

They interlock:

    no tiers -> no recorded accumulation -> unbacked report
             -> routes to complaint -> no correction -> tiers stay unbuilt

Maintenance has an older name in the literature: **occupational
closure**. Professionalisation establishes qualifications and a body to
oversee them, demarcates qualified from unqualified, and the occupation
closes to outsiders.

## INSTRUMENT 1 — the credential-correction test

**Signature: a discount that does not move when the content moves.**

When a discount is challenged, a reason gets stated — usually
credentials or experience. Supply the correction. If the assessment
does not update, the stated reason was not the operative objection; it
was produced after the fact for a discount already applied.

    TEST FORM
      hold the claim constant
      vary the stated reason's input
      measure whether assessment updates
      no update => routing rule, not evidentiary judgment

Done in the literature for register and for names. **Not done for the
post-hoc-justification property** — where you correct the stated
objection and watch whether anything changes.

Already run inadvertently, twice:

- Rafferty, 30 European countries: after controlling for education,
  experience, occupation and other categories, racial and ethnic
  minorities remained more likely to be in skill-underutilised jobs at
  lower pay. Controls remove the stated reason; the discount survives.
- BC deskilling study (Springer 2026): deskilling associated with
  non-recognition of foreign credentials, and holding a master's was
  among the factors associated with MORE deskilling. Adding credential
  made it worse.

## INSTRUMENT 2 — the seat-change natural experiment

Within-subject. The strongest available design, because content,
competence and credential are all held constant and only the seat moves.

Prediction: the discount arrives **with the seat**, not gradually, and
applies to reports **in the person's original domain** — the sharp
test, because content sits squarely inside prior expertise and routing
ignores it.

### The sharpest instance found

    Viscelli   doctorate, 17 yrs studying the industry, SIX MONTHS
               trained and worked as a long-haul driver as part of
               research. Expert opinion filed with FMCSA. Admissible
               expertise in a federal proceeding.

    Belzer     TEN YEARS long-haul tank driver, then Cornell PhD, now
               an internationally recognised trucking expert. His own
               note: the driving directly shaped his research.

Same knowledge. It becomes **citable at the moment the seat changes**.
Ten years of driving qualified as background; the doctorate made it
testimony.

Nobody has run the credibility measurement on this pair.

### Self-discounting is visible from inside

Occupational forum posts asking whether an advanced degree would block
hiring arrive wrapped in preemptive disclaimers — not saying I'm too
smart, it's just a piece of paper, much respect for all drivers, not
bragging. The routing rule anticipated and defended against before
anyone applies it. Separate threads in the same forum run the discount
the other way, claiming an inverse relationship between education and
intellect. Both directions, same population.

Scale, different country, same structure: the Dangote driver program
drew >13,000 applicants for 100 positions — roughly 6 PhDs, 65 MBAs,
649 master's, >8,460 bachelor's.

## INSTRUMENT 3 — the reverse arm (R1, and the one that is runnable now)

Reality television built the experiment and never scored it.

An executive works disguised on the floor. Two arms:

    FORWARD   boss learns from floor
              CONFOUNDED: new attention, intent to find something,
              produced-TV selection and editing toward episodes where
              a reveal lands.

    REVERSE   disguised boss reports UP to a supervisor
              CLEAN: same person, same competence, same content, and
              the supervisor doing the dismissing DOES NOT KNOW they
              are dismissing the CEO.

The reverse arm is blind on the assessor's end, which most designed
experiments cannot manage. No strategic behaviour is available to them;
they run their ordinary rule on camera.

### Why it is the strongest form the result could take

The person with the most organisational standing that exists — able to
fire the manager dismissing him — is typed as not-worth-listening-to
the moment the seat is stripped. So under normal conditions it is not
authority, competence, or track record doing the work. It is the
position alone.

**And it kills the charitable reading.** The defensible version of the
discount is that it is a rough proxy: operators statistically know
less, so discounting is lossy but not baseless. This is the case where
the proxy is maximally wrong, and it fires at full strength anyway. A
proxy that behaves identically on its worst case is not tracking what
it claims to track.

### The comedy beat is itself the datum

If the show's standard structure is "the boss is bad at the floor job,"
that is the reverse discount happening on camera and played as
entertainment: maximum organisational standing, stripped of the seat,
immediately read as unskilled. Filmed, repeatedly, unscored.

Confirmed in print. Critics name it as a through line and a source of
audience satisfaction; later reviews repeat it. Also documented in the
same critical literature: employees who voice a critique are
reprimanded, retrained, retired, or fired — the routing rule with
consequences attached, on camera.

### Status of the observation

One observer reports that across episodes watched, the disguised
executive was not listened to by managers. **That is an observation of
the pattern, not a scored count.** The transcript pass has not been
run. See `reverse_arm_score.py`.

## THE AUDIENCE QUESTION — nobody asked

Every published explanation of the show's appeal is one of two:
propaganda-that-works-on-people, or catharsis/fairy-tale. The nearest
dissent argues the audience sees through it because of what they know
from having worked a job.

Scholarly analyses are described in that literature as surprisingly few
given the show's popularity, and at least one author concedes that
close studies of audience response may generate findings not explored.

**The two hypotheses are distinguishable in the wording:**

    schadenfreude / wish-fulfilment   "not so easy, is it"
    routing-rule-failing              "he said what I said and they
                                       didn't listen to him either"

Nobody collected the wording.

A convenience sample of second-hand accounts from working-class viewers
points at the second. Non-random, direction not rate, provenance stated
so it can be discounted appropriately. It is a reason to run the
comment-corpus pass, not a result.

Nielsen cannot answer this. Panels collect gender, household income,
ethnicity, and the buying demos. **Occupation is not a standard
demographic, because advertisers buy on income.** The variable exists
and nobody paid to measure it — the pattern again, in the measurement
apparatus itself. Income blurs exactly the distinction that matters: a
20-year mechanic and a junior manager can share an income band on
opposite sides of the routing rule.

## R-REGISTER — gaps with no proper instrumentation

Each is a standalone work order. Take one.

    R1  CREDIBILITY EFFECT OF THE SEAT CHANGE
        The WAGE effect of downward occupational mobility is documented.
        Whether the SAME REPORT from the SAME PERSON is typed
        differently after the seat change is measured nowhere.
        Instrument: reverse arm, above. Runnable now.

    R2  POST-HOC-JUSTIFICATION TEST
        Correct the stated objection, hold claim constant, measure
        update. Never run as a designed experiment.

    R3  COST-OF-READING GRADIENT
        Prediction: discount strength tracks WHAT THE REPORT WOULD COST
        IF READ, not the reporter's stratum. Cheap-to-act-on reports
        type as data even from low positions; expensive ones type as
        complaint even from fairly high ones.
        Test: hold stratum constant, vary the implied bill. Untested.

    R4  NON-EVENT OUTPUT
        Prevention produces the absence of an output. No counter
        increments. Near-miss rates by shift and crew ARE computable and
        some industries do it — but the measurement is only ever built
        AFTER a loss forces it.

    R5  INDIVIDUAL ATTRIBUTION IN CREW OUTCOMES
        Firefighters and EMTs produce discrete, timestamped, witnessed,
        documented events with named survivors — every property the
        hard-to-measure cases lack — and are still not tiered like
        athletes. So OBSERVABILITY is not the variable. What is missing
        is EXTRACTION of a per-person differential from crew outcomes.
        Prediction that holds: where individual attribution IS
        extracted, tiers appear (surgeons, airline captains, specialist
        paramedics).

    R6  CAPTURE vs CREATION
        Value created and value capturable are different quantities and
        pay tracks the second. Athlete output is sold and buyers bid
        against each other for that specific person. A rescued patient
        generates no revenue attributable to the medic, and a municipal
        employer is a single budget-capped buyer per geography.
        Monopsony as a structural term, unmeasured against skill.

## SUPPORTING MEASUREMENTS

Pointers only — re-queryable, not stored.

- Araki (OECD, ~30,000 individuals, 26 countries): both credentials and
  skills pay, but the CREDENTIAL premium far outweighs the SKILL
  premium, measured separately. The market prices the document, not the
  capability.
- StatCan: immigrants who stayed in their intended occupation earned
  ~50% more than occupational movers, largest gaps among the most
  highly educated. Same person, same capability, position changed.
- arXiv 2602.21369: skill requirements held in physical occupations are
  least able to propagate upward across the domain boundary,
  reproducing occupational immobility INDEPENDENTLY of any individual
  worker's retraining effort or employer's hiring intent. Gradient
  strongest in credential-intensive, compliance-heavy, safety-critical
  domains where valuation regimes actively police boundaries. The
  no-intent-required property, formalised.

## FALSIFIERS

- Assessment updates when the stated objection is corrected → the
  objection was evidentiary; the marker fails.
- Discount tracks stratum and not implied cost, under R3 → the
  cost-of-reading extension fails; the core may still hold.
- Reverse-arm transcripts show the disguised executive's reports acted
  on at the same rate as an equivalent floor worker's → R1 fails, and
  the seat-change prediction fails with it.
- Belzer/Viscelli citation patterns show the pre-doctorate operating
  knowledge cited as expertise → the seat-change instrument fails.
