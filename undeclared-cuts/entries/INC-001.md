title: Material dating against textual attribution, stratified by writing-system incentive
status: NOT_MEASURED
term_status: UNNAMED
measurand: For technologies with both a material date (dated artifact, residue, stratigraphy, dendrochronology, isotope, metallurgical signature) and a textual attribution (a document naming a person, place or period of origin), the offset between the two, stratified by whether the writing system in which the attribution was recorded rewarded origination claims -- carried a named-author convention, a priority dispute mechanism, a patent or privilege, a dedication economy.
control: Technologies whose material date and textual attribution fall in the same writing tradition and whose attribution predates any priority-rewarding convention in that tradition, so the offset is measured where the incentive is absent. Second control arm: technologies with material dates and no surviving attribution, which bound the rate at which attributions exist at all.
scope_limits: Material dating carries its own error band and its own selection -- artifacts survive by material, climate and reuse value, so the dated set is not a sample of the made set. Writing-system incentive is a coded variable, not a measured one, and the coding scheme is the entry's weakest joint. Restricted to technologies where both measures exist, which is a set selected on having been written about. Says nothing about technologies with no text and nothing about technologies with no surviving material.
thin_links: Treating an attribution's date of recording as the date of the claim is an inference over an inference where the text survives only in later copies. Reading a writing system's convention as an incentive on an individual scribe or author is a second: the convention is a property of a corpus, the incentive a property of a person, and the entry has no instrument for the second. Offsets computed across traditions with different calendar reconstructions inherit those reconstructions' uncertainty without stating it unless the run carries the band through.
would_change: A tradition with a priority-rewarding convention and no systematic offset would separate the incentive account from a simpler one (attribution is late because writing is late). A pre-registered coding scheme for writing-system incentive, written by a party who has not seen the offsets, would move the coded variable off its weakest state. Any technology where the material date is revised by a new method changes its row and the entry states that its rows are revisable by construction. A survey establishing the rate of attribution-free technologies per tradition would supply the missing denominator.

## The two measures

```
    MATERIAL DATE                    TEXTUAL ATTRIBUTION
    artifact, residue, stratum       a document names an originator
    error band from the method       a date of recording, or of a copy
    survives by material and         survives by being copied
      climate                        selected by what a tradition wrote
    no claim attached                down
```

They are different instruments answering questions that are related and
not the same. The offset between them is the measurand.

## The stratifier

Writing systems differ in whether they reward an origination claim. A
tradition with named-author convention, priority disputes, privilege
grants or a dedication economy produces attributions under a different
pressure from one with anonymous compilation.

Rows are sorted on this variable. The entry does not assert what the
sort will show.

## Row schema

```
    technology
    material date        value, method, error band
    textual attribution  value, source, date of recording, copy chain
    tradition
    incentive coding     value + the rule that assigned it
    offset               material minus textual, with both bands
```

A row missing either measure is recorded as a row with a gap, not
dropped. The gap rate per tradition is itself a readout.

## Not in scope

Whether any particular attribution is true. The entry measures the
offset between two dating instruments and its distribution across
traditions; it adjudicates no attribution and awards no priority.
