measurand: The set of qualification rows a field records for a physical unit or a manufacturer, mapped against the qualification rows that same field records for a knowledge claim. The entry is the gap list: every row type present on one side and absent on the other, enumerated, with the absence stated as an absence rather than as an argument.
control: Domains that already carry qualification rows on both sides -- where a method, instrument or procedure is itself qualified through the same register as the hardware. Any such domain is a worked instance and bounds the claim that the gap is universal.
scope_limits: Row enumeration is bounded by what a register publishes; a qualification step performed and not recorded does not enter the list, so the gap list is a floor on the hardware side and a floor on the claims side, and the two floors are not the same distance from their ceilings. Unit rows here are drawn from a flight-sensor list, one domain; manufacturer rows from the same domain's supplier qualification. The entry does not establish that either transfers. Mapping a hardware row onto a claim is a stated correspondence, not a derived one, and the correspondence rule is declared per row or the row is not mapped.
thin_links: Reading an absent row as an absent practice is an inference over an inference -- the register is a proxy for the practice, publication a proxy for the register. A practice can be universal and unpublished. Treating a hardware qualification row as having a claim-side analogue at all presumes the two are the same kind of object under a different substrate; that presumption is the entry's largest thin link and is marked rather than argued.
would_change: A published qualification register for knowledge claims in any field closes rows off the gap list one at a time and each closure is recorded with its source. A second unit domain (not flight sensors) whose rows differ substantially would show the hardware side is domain-specific rather than general. A stated correspondence rule authored outside this entry would test whether the mapping is a property of the rows or of the mapper. Evidence that a listed row is performed and unrecorded moves it from gap to unpublished, which is a different row state and is carried separately.

## The two registers

```
    UNIT (a flight sensor)              KNOWLEDGE CLAIM
    part number, revision               ?
    lot traceability                    ?
    environmental qualification         ?
      range, margin, test evidence
    calibration interval                ?
    failure modes enumerated            ?
    re-qualification on change          ?
    service bulletin channel            ?
    retirement / life limit             ?

    MANUFACTURER                        CLAIM PRODUCER
    approval scope                      ?
    audit interval                      ?
    surveillance findings, open/closed  ?
    scope suspension mechanism          ?
    subcontractor flow-down             ?
```

The right column is what the entry enumerates. Where a field does record
something in a right-hand cell, the entry records what and where. Where
it does not, the entry records the absence.

## Procedure

1. Enumerate the unit rows from a published qualification register for
   one flight-sensor family. Rows verbatim, with the clause reference.
2. Enumerate the manufacturer rows from the same domain's supplier
   qualification register.
3. For each row, state a correspondence rule to the claim side, or mark
   the row NO_CORRESPONDENCE_STATED and stop.
4. For each row with a correspondence, search current research practice
   for a published register carrying it. Record PRESENT with a source,
   ABSENT, or PERFORMED_UNRECORDED with evidence.
5. The gap list is rows 4 returns ABSENT.

## Why the gap list is the entry

The comparison is already asymmetric: one side has a published register
and the other is being searched for one. The list of rows that do not
transfer is the output. No ratio is computed, because the two
denominators are not the same object.

## Not in scope

Whether knowledge claims should carry qualification rows, and what a
claim-side register would cost. The entry produces the list; it argues
for nothing.
