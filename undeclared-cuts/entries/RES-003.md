## What a design entry is

It specifies an object and enumerates what the object requires. It runs
nothing, measures nothing and concludes nothing. Its output is the
element list plus, per element, whether it is specifiable in advance.

## The object

```
    SEALED SYSTEM
    the operator may not open it in normal operation
          |
    DECLARED EMERGENCY  (trigger -- exists? timely? reaches operator?)
          |
          v
    PRE-AUTHORISED OVERRIDE
      authority       who grants it, in advance, in writing
      trigger         what state opens it, stated as a condition
      envelope        what is permitted, bounded -- not "as required"
      recording       what is logged, by whom, at what point
      termination     what closes it, including a time limit
      after-action    what happens to the system afterwards
```

Six elements. The design's question is whether each can be written
before the event without becoming a routine bypass.

## Precedents, as text

```
    AVIATION -- minimum equipment list
    A pre-approved document stating what may be inoperative, under what
    conditions, for how long, with what procedure. The authorisation is
    written before the dispatch, not during it. Approved per operator
    against a master list. Carries an explicit expiry.

    MARITIME -- master's override
    Authority vested in a named role to depart from procedure where the
    master judges it necessary for safety, with a recording obligation
    and a report afterwards. The authorisation is a standing grant to a
    role, bounded by a duty to record.
```

The two are structurally different and that difference is the design's
main content:

```
    MEL                              MASTER'S OVERRIDE
    enumerates the permitted set     grants judgement to a role
    bounded by a list                bounded by a duty to record
    expires by clock                 ends when the master says
    no judgement required at use     judgement is the mechanism
```

A sealed-system override could take either shape. They fail differently:
the enumerated form fails when the situation is not on the list; the
judgement form fails when the role, the training or the record is absent.

## Open questions the design exposes

1. Does the target domain have a role to vest a judgement grant in?
2. Can the envelope be bounded without enumerating situations?
3. Who verifies the emergency declaration reached the operator?
4. What happens to a system operated outside envelope -- inspection,
   retirement, or nothing -- and who pays?
5. Is there any element that cannot be pre-written without becoming a
   bypass available in normal operation?

## Not in scope

Advocating for the override, estimating how often it would be used, and
any claim about any manufacturer's sealing decisions.
