title: Corpus ratio per domain -- protocol text against documented justified deviation

measurand: Per domain, the ratio of two corpus volumes: text stating what to do (protocol, procedure, checklist, standing order, standard) against text documenting a deviation from it with the justification recorded and the outcome attached. Measured in a declared unit -- pages, words or clauses, one unit per run, stated before counting.
control: Domains that publish a deviation register by requirement -- aviation, marine classification, regulated pharmaceutical manufacture, nuclear operations -- where the deviation corpus exists by mandate rather than by an individual's choice to write it down. These fix the ratio's upper reach and show the measurement is constructible before it is applied to a domain with no mandate.
scope_limits: One unit per run and the unit is not interchangeable: a clause-count ratio and a word-count ratio are different numbers about the same corpora and are not comparable across runs. Counts what is published, so a domain with a large internal deviation record and no publication reads the same as a domain with none, and the entry records that the two are indistinguishable at this instrument. A justification with no outcome attached is a different object from one with an outcome and the two are counted separately, never merged. Says nothing about the deviation RATE -- a domain may deviate constantly and document rarely, or the reverse.
thin_links: Reading a low ratio as a low tolerance for deviation is an inference over an inference: the corpus is a proxy for the record, the record for the practice, the practice for the tolerance. Three hops and the entry establishes none of them. Classifying a text as protocol or as deviation-with-justification is a coding judgement, and in domains where deviation is itself proceduralised the two categories overlap; those texts are recorded BOTH and carried in both counts with the overlap stated.
would_change: A domain publishing its deviation register for the first time moves its ratio and the entry records the date, because a ratio is a reading of a publication regime and not of a practice. A coding scheme written by a party who has not seen the corpora would move the classification off its weakest joint. An internal deviation record made available under any condition converts that domain's reading from published-only to recorded, which is a different measurand and gets a separate row. A domain where protocol text is machine-generated inflates the numerator without a corresponding practice change and is flagged rather than counted.

## The two corpora

```
    PROTOCOL TEXT                    JUSTIFIED-DEVIATION TEXT
    what to do                       what was done instead
    written before the event         written after
    published by default in most     published by exception in most
      regulated domains                domains
    grows by revision                grows by incident
```

The ratio is the measurand. It is not a quality score and it is not a
compliance reading.

## Counting rules, declared before the run

1. One unit -- pages, words or clauses -- for both sides of one ratio.
2. A deviation text counts only with a justification recorded. A
   deviation logged with no reason goes in a third count, kept apart.
3. A justification with an outcome attached and one without are counted
   separately and reported as two numbers.
4. Text that is both protocol and deviation procedure (a documented
   process for deviating) is counted in both and the overlap volume is
   reported, so any reader can subtract it.

## Output shape

Per domain, four numbers and one overlap:

```
    protocol volume
    deviation-with-justification, outcome attached
    deviation-with-justification, no outcome
    deviation logged, no justification
    overlap (counted in both of the first two)
```

No single ratio is emitted where the numerator has three parts. The run
states which parts it is dividing.

## Not in scope

Whether a domain deviates too much or too little, and whether any
particular deviation was justified. The entry measures corpus volume
against corpus volume.
