## GR-0002  (T2, UNKNOWN)

- quantity: the difference between the analgesia floor written into veterinary procedure guidance and the analgesia floor written into human procedure guidance, for procedures of comparable tissue involvement
- excluded by: the two guidance literatures, each of which sets its floor inside its own species scope and declares no separation from the other
- measured instead: an analgesia floor per procedure inside each literature separately
- venue check: a journal, committee or table where veterinary and human procedure guidance appear on one page
- closes when: a published paper or guideline that places the two floors in one table and states that the comparison was made
- not a gap if: either literature already cites the other on this point, which would make the separation a declared one
- confound: the comparison class rests on treating two procedures as comparable in tissue involvement; that judgement is not itself a measured quantity, and two coders may partition it differently
- index terms: analgesia, pain management, veterinary, human medicine, procedure guidance
