# WO-6 — Assessor–assessed coupling as a recurrent known failure mode

CC0. Instrument, not an argument. Status tags: OBSERVED / DERIVED / PROPOSED.
Companion to WO-1 (load-bearing criterion) and WO-4 (the shape).

NOT ABOUT ANY NAMED ORGANIZATION. The object is the structural class.
Contemporary instances are mentioned only as the current position on a long
record, and no claim of capture is made against any specific party.

## Measurand

Whether an assessor is structurally independent of the party it assesses —
distinguished from whether a direct payment runs between them.

The two are being treated as one question. They are not.

```
  hop-1 test:  does the assessed pay the assessor?
               ASKED, ANSWERABLE, ROUTINELY ANSWERED

  pool test:   is the funding source, credentialing body, career
               path and governance of the assessor independent of
               the OUTCOME being assessed?
               NOT ASKED. NO STANDARD EXISTS FOR IT.

  the first is a routing question
  the second is the independence question
```

## The structural claim (OBSERVED)

A pool cannot audit itself by relabeling its outflows.

Whether funds arrive as a grant, a service contract, a fellowship, a
donation or a salary line is a routing detail. If the pool has an interest in
the outcome, every node drawing from that pool inherits the interest
regardless of which pipe it came down.

Stated case: creating an independent body of bridge inspectors who are all
credentialed, funded, and career-pathed out of the same pool as the bridge
builders. No inspector takes builder money. The arrangement is still not
independent.

Same shape as the rest of the set: every transfer locally clean and
genuinely so, nobody lying, and the aggregate unowned — no party is
positioned to assess the POOL, because each can only see their own clean
transfer.

## Non-financial couplings with no disclosure field (OBSERVED)

Disclosure instruments have fields for money. They have no fields for:

- shared ideological formation
- overlapping boards and advisory positions
- a common recruitment and career pipeline
- shared conference and professional circuit
- future employment prospects inside the assessed sector

Because there is no field, these read as ABSENT rather than unmeasured. Same
never-encodable structure as the maintenance app in WO-5: what matters has no
place to be recorded, so the record shows nothing and the nothing gets read
as a null result.

## THE PRECEDENT RECORD — this is the instrument

The failure mode is not predicted. It is documented, repeatedly, with
fatalities, across unrelated domains and across recorded history.

### Ancient / pre-industrial

- **Code of Hammurabi, builder clauses (~1750 BCE).** Personal liability
  imposed on the builder for structural collapse. The earliest surviving
  written remedy for exactly this coupling: the party who benefits from the
  work cannot be the party who certifies it safe, so consequence is attached
  instead.
- **Roman and medieval guild inspection.** Guilds both trained and certified.
  Recurrent documented failures where certification protected members rather
  than purchasers. Remedy attempted via external charters and civic
  inspectors.
- **Assay offices and hallmarking.** Created specifically because sellers
  certifying their own metal content did not work. The remedy is a separate
  body with statutory authority — invented for this failure mode.

### Industrial

- **Steam boiler explosions, 19th c.** Self-certification by owners.
  Thousands of deaths. Remedy: independent boiler inspection with statutory
  authority — a direct structural response to owner-assessor coupling.
- **Food and drug adulteration, pre-regulatory.** Producers attesting their
  own product. Remedy: external inspection with right of access.

### Modern, well-documented

- **Nuclear regulation pre-Three Mile Island.** The promoting body and the
  regulating body were combined. Split into separate agencies BECAUSE
  combining them was understood to be structurally unsound — a remedy adopted
  in advance of, and reinforced by, incident.
- **Arthur Andersen / Enron.** Audit and consulting revenue from the same
  client. Every individual engagement defensible. Remedy: Sarbanes-Oxley
  forced separation of audit from consulting, plus rotation requirements.
- **Credit rating agencies, 2008.** Issuer-pays model — the rated party
  selects and pays the rater. Each rating locally defensible on its own
  methodology. Systemic failure.
- **Boeing ODA.** Certification authority delegated to employees of the
  manufacturer being certified. 346 dead across two crashes. The
  investigative finding was STRUCTURAL, not individual misconduct.
- **Grenfell Tower.** Testing and certification bodies commercially dependent
  on the parties whose products they certified. 72 dead.

### The common prior defense

Every one of the above ran the same argument BEFORE the failure:

- no direct payment contingent on a favorable outcome
- safeguards and firewalls in place
- professionals of integrity, individually verified
- no demonstrated instance of capture

In each case those statements were TRUE at the transfer level. They were true
and the structure failed anyway. That is the finding: hop-1 cleanliness has
never once been sufficient, in any domain, at any point in the record.

## Substrate-independence (DERIVED)

The recurrence spans roughly four millennia, unrelated technologies,
unrelated economies, unrelated legal systems, and unrelated professional
cultures. A pattern that regenerates across that range is a property of the
STRUCTURE — assessor coupled to assessed — not of any industry, era or set of
people.

This is the same substrate-independence argument as WO-4, with a much longer
baseline.

## The remedy set — already written, repeatedly

Not an unsolved engineering problem. A solved one, re-opened.

```
INDEPENDENCE CONDITIONS
  1  AUTHORITY NOT REVOCABLE BY THE ASSESSED
     licensure/mandate held by a body the assessed cannot remove
  2  FUNDING NOT ROUTED THROUGH THE ASSESSED
     including second-order: investors, boards, shared donor pools
  3  MANDATORY ENGAGEMENT
     assessment not optional for the assessed
  4  PUBLICATION NOT CONTROLLED BY THE ASSESSED
     findings published regardless of who is unhappy
  5  ACCESS AS OF RIGHT
     not granted at the assessed party's discretion
  6  PERSONAL LIABILITY ON THE ASSESSOR
     the stamp carries consequence for being wrong
  7  ROTATION / TENURE LIMITS
     against capture by familiarity
  8  CAPACITY PROPORTIONATE TO SCOPE
```

Condition 6 is the one least discussed in contemporary arrangements. A
structural engineer's seal makes them personally answerable. An assessor with
no exposure to being wrong is running the same decoupling as every other node
in WO-5's chain: coupled to the description, not to the consequence.

## Current position in AI evaluation (OBSERVED, from public statements)

Stated without attribution to any organization:

- assessors commonly state hop-1 independence (no payment from assessed
  parties) — this is the condition that CAN be stated and IS stated
- access to systems is typically granted at the assessed party's discretion
  (fails 5)
- engagement is voluntary (fails 3)
- publication terms are commonly negotiated (fails 4)
- no personal liability regime exists (fails 6)
- no rotation requirement exists (fails 7)
- capacity is publicly acknowledged by practitioners as insufficient to scope
  (fails 8)
- pool-level funding independence is unmeasured, and no standard defines what
  it would require (2 unverifiable as stated)

Also on record from within the sector: there are currently no established
standards governing what access embedded evaluators should receive, how they
should report findings, or how independent evaluation should be funded.

Status: PRE-INCIDENT, with a structure matching the documented precedent.
Note that every case in the table above was also pre-incident until it was
not, and "no capture demonstrated yet" was the defense in each.

## Risk-mitigation statement (DERIVED)

Applied consistently, and this is standard practice in engineered
infrastructure:

```
known failure mode
  + fatal precedent across multiple domains
  + available, previously-implemented structural remedy
  -> remedy is MANDATORY, not optional
  -> absence of a demonstrated local instance is NOT a defense
     (it was never a defense in any precedent case)
```

Connects to WO-1's load-bearing criterion: if the system is to be
infrastructure, engineer it to infrastructure standards. Installing a
component with a known fatal failure history, in a domain that describes
itself as safety-critical, is the specific case that criterion was written
for.

Connects to WO-4 face 7 (description decoupled from referent): the remedy
survives as procedure and vocabulary — "independent," "third-party,"
"external review" — while the structural reason those words existed
deteriorates away. The next arrangement installs the original coupling while
quoting the safeguards written to prevent it.

## Scope limits

- The precedent table is illustrative, selected for documentation quality.
  Not a systematic sample, and no base rate is claimed.
- Ancient cases are read from surviving legal and administrative texts.
  Surviving remedies are evidence the failure occurred; the frequency is not
  recoverable.
- No claim is made that any specific contemporary assessor has been captured,
  has produced a biased finding, or acts in bad faith. The claim is about
  structure and precedent only.
- Condition-by-condition scoring of current AI evaluation is from public
  statements. It should be re-verified before publication; arrangements are
  changing.
- Whether pool-level independence is MEASURABLE in practice is untested. The
  metric below is proposed, not validated.

## Runnable next steps

1. **Pool-level independence metric.** Define and compute: what fraction of
   an assessor's funding originates from sources that also fund, hold equity
   in, or are governed by parties holding equity in, the assessed sector.
   Runnable on public 990s and grant databases. Apply to ALL assessors in a
   field, not selectively — the output is a distribution, not an accusation.
2. **Systematic precedent survey.** Replace the illustrative table with a
   systematic one: search accident and failure investigations across domains
   for findings that named assessor–assessed coupling as contributing. Code
   for remedy adopted and whether it persisted.
3. **Remedy decay study.** For each historical remedy (assay offices, boiler
   inspection, SOX separation, NRC split), track whether the structural
   condition survived or eroded, and over what period. Tests WO-4 face 7
   directly, with a measurable decay time.
4. **Disclosure-field audit.** Take existing conflict-of-interest disclosure
   instruments across fields and score which of the eight conditions they
   have a field for. Expected result: money only. A null result on the other
   seven is the finding.
5. **Score a field other than AI** against the eight conditions, blind, as a
   calibration check on the instrument itself.

## Who could run this

Steps 1 and 4 need public records only and no lab. Step 3 is an archival
study suitable for a historian of regulation or a post-graduate in
science-and-technology studies. Step 2 is the largest and the most citable.
