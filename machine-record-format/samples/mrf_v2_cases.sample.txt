MACHINE-RECORD-FORMAT v2 -- RULE 8 + TEST-CASE FORMAT (CONSTRUCTED)
==================================================================

Rule 8 -- no payment field in the base layer:
  BaseEntry has a payment/compensation field: False
  write path on a payment keyword -> paid: refused, unpaid: refused, wage: refused, compensation: refused
  payment as a Rule 2 view instead -> groups ['paid', 'unpaid']

test-case format -- every case carries the triple:
  Case A -- Amish barn raising  [citable=True]
    tests:         both entries present on identical footing, summable, neither carrying a payment field (Rule 8 end to end)
    does not test: whether the willingness structure is transferable outside a community that already has it; also does not test large-scale or non-structural output classes
    why not:       the community supplying the labor is self-selected and has standing agreements predating any given raising. The comparison holds output class constant, not social substrate. It answers 'does the record handle both,' not 'would this work elsewhere.'
    format verdict: {'both_present': True, 'summable_joules': 1500000000.0, 'no_payment_field': True, 'payment_field_refused': True}

  Case B -- terra preta  [citable=True]
    tests:         whether the record can carry a strong output figure against an absent exposure figure without either discarding the entry or fabricating the exposure
    does not test: output per unit of labor, or any efficiency claim at all
    why not:       the exposure column is absent. A ratio needs both terms, and inferring the missing one from the present one makes the ratio circular. Durability is available; efficiency is not.
    format verdict: {'accepted_with_absent_exposure': True, 'exposure_value_is_none': True, 'ratio_flag': 'NOT_COMPUTABLE', 'durability_readable': 750.0}

  Case C -- Machu Picchu / Inca mit'a  [citable=True]
    tests:         whether labor-unit records can be ingested and compared WITHOUT conversion to a monetary or hour-equivalent common unit
    does not test: the willingness structure, community capital, or unpaid coordination of any kind
    why not:       mit'a was a labor TAX. Coordination was state-directed and compulsory. Absence of money is not presence of willingness -- those are separate axes, and this case is non-monetary and non-voluntary simultaneously, which is exactly why it isolates the units question.
    format verdict: {'compared_on_native_unit': 9200.0, 'same_exposure_class': True, 'convert_raises': True}

the three does-not-test boundaries are distinct, so a pass on one is not a pass on another (the cases are not merged).
