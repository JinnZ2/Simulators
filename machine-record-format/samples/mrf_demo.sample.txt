  a : {'v1': 'hot', 'v2': 'primary'}
  c : {'v1': 'cold', 'v2': 'primary'}
  e : {'v1': 'cold', 'v2': 'primary'}

  view v1 (metabolic_class):
    cold       joules=30.0  measured=1 zero=1 unmeasured=1
    hot        joules=150.0  measured=2 zero=0 unmeasured=0
  view v2 (process_stage):
    primary    joules=130.0  measured=2 zero=0 unmeasured=1
    secondary  joules=50.0  measured=1 zero=1 unmeasured=0

vintages retained; a revision does not overwrite (Rule 4):
  entry a, versions: [('2026-01-10', 100.0), ('2026-03-10', 118.0)]
  as of 2026-02-01: joules=100.0 (the vintage live then)
  latest:           joules=118.0

boundary declared always (Rule 5). two boundaries with no declared reconciliation do not sum:
  gate-to-gate + cradle-to-gate -> refused (no reconciliation)
  with a declared reconciliation -> sums to 150.0

bisection as a structure test (Rule / diagnostic). the span is a methodology registry, not calendar time:
  one change carries the signal -> SINGLE_LOCUS, address=m5
  signal on both halves          -> NOT_A_LOCUS (no address reported)
