# COINAGE LOG

A log of NAMING GAPS: referents that recur across domains and have no
word, where existing terms each fail for a stated reason. The thing
being tested is whether **the absence of a word is itself a finding** —
the same shape the `../uninstrumented/` register runs on a quantity an
instrument cannot hold, run here on a referent a vocabulary cannot
hold. An entry is left UNNAMED rather than forced; a coinage is adopted
only when it earns adoption, and "none adopted" is a legitimate and
common state.

Provenance is separated per the ecosystem's co-production rule
(`../AUDIT_CONTRACT.md`): lines marked **[Kavik]** are the operator's,
verbatim or close; the cross-links and the null caveat are the render's
and add no claim about the referent. `check_coinage.py` computes the
structural facts and adjudicates nothing — it does not decide whether a
word is genuinely missing or merely unsearched.

**Referent [Kavik]:**

```
  multiple bodies jointly producing the local conditions each then
```

**Why existing terms fail [Kavik]:**

```
  cooperation   imports intent
  symbiosis     taken; implies a paired-species relationship
  mutualism     requires a benefit ledger per party
  aggregation   describes POSITION only, drops the
                conditions-producing
  facilitation  closest existing term, but scoped to one organism
                easing another, not JOINT production
```

**Candidates raised, none adopted [Kavik]:** `load-sharing`,
`phase-locking`.

**Kavik's note:** the naming gap creates confusion about something
that is a system process.

**Left unnamed rather than forcing one [Kavik].** The log is testing
whether the absence of a word is itself the finding — and this is a
clean instance, because the competition vocabulary is dense and the
counterpart is empty.

## THE REFERENT'S EVIDENCE — carried, not adjudicated

**Insects aggregating on one face [Kavik]:**

```
  competition model  no explanation; a resource gradient has to be
                     invented, and often is not there
  geometry model     thermal, humidity, boundary-layer -- and each
                     body MODIFIES those for its neighbours
  -> the aggregate is the habitat
  -> proximity is not preference; it is the local physics they are
     jointly producing
```

**The grading to keep [Kavik]:** "cooperation" here is a PATTERN
CLASS, not an intent claim — the geometry is cooperative whether or
not anything intends it, so the objection "but they do not MEAN to"
does not reach it.

**The woodpecker marker [Kavik]:** independent confirmation from a
SECOND trophic level — a predator's search pattern reads the prey
distribution, so face-specific foraging means the aggregation is real
and stable, not an observer artifact. **Cheap to verify:** you do not
need the insects, you need the woodpecker.

**On the literature [Kavik]:** captivity effects and observer effects
are both documented, but as NOISE to control (a methods problem to
minimize), not as a variable carrying information about the setting —
and there is no cross-taxon synthesis making the containment geometry
itself the independent variable.

## THE CONNECTING INSTRUMENT — the absence set, applied to behaviour

The delivery names the instrument the behavioural side runs on
[Kavik]: **repertoire minus what fires.** In the novel-container case
(a smooth vertical pile, no evolutionary history with it), the absent
behaviour is the tell — digging is in the repertoire and does not
fire, because the surface does not afford it; the collective structure
is derived from the geometry present, not retrieved from a matching
template. That is the `../uninstrumented/` absence set applied to
behaviour: the omission is the honest side, the same as with charters.

## STATE — the null caveat, the render's

The log holds ONE entry and it is a naming gap, so the log has not yet
been shown to DECLINE — a coinage log that only ever admits naming
gaps is `CONSTANT_FIRES` in `../null-harness/` terms until it records a
referent that DOES have a good word and correctly adopts none. That
known-negative is not here yet, so "the word is missing" is not yet
separated from "the word was not searched for" (the
`question-availability` Q1 distinction; the
`../uninstrumented/` "do not fill this in with an approximation"
device). The entry is a clean candidate; the null is unrun.
