# coinage-log

A log of NAMING GAPS. Each entry is a referent that recurs across
domains and has no word, with every existing term failing for a stated
reason. The instrument under test is the log itself: **is the absence
of a word a finding, or only an unfinished search?** That is the
`../uninstrumented/` register's thesis — a quantity the apparatus
cannot hold — run one level over, on a referent the vocabulary cannot
hold, and it is `../question-availability/`'s Q1 (unasked) with the
naming gap as the unasked question.

## Files

- **`COINAGE_LOG.md`** — the log. Entry 001 is `UNNAMED`, source
  Kavik: *multiple bodies jointly producing the local conditions each
  then occupies — the aggregate IS the habitat, with no intent, no
  benefit ledger, and no coordination channel.* Five existing terms
  (cooperation / symbiosis / mutualism / aggregation / facilitation)
  each fail for a named reason; two candidates (`load-sharing`,
  `phase-locking`) were raised and none adopted. Left unnamed rather
  than forced.
- **`check_coinage.py`** — `--selftest`. Computes the structural facts
  (entry well-formed with no adopted name, every rejected term
  reasoned, candidates raised none adopted, the woodpecker second-
  trophic-level cross-check and the absence-set-on-behaviour instrument
  both named) and states the null discipline. Adjudicates no coinage.

## Why it is a register and not a finding

The entry does not claim the word is missing; it claims the referent
recurs and the existing terms each miss it, and it leaves the name
open. Two disciplines the repo runs everywhere hold here:

- **Left unnamed, not approximated.** "none adopted" is a legitimate
  and common state — the `../uninstrumented/` *do not fill this in with
  an approximation* device (`UNI_022` / `UNI_049`), and the empty-noun-
  slot of its `020` / `021` markers (`UNI_096`): the difference between
  "we have no word" and "the word is the wrong shape".
- **The null is unrun.** A coinage log that only ever admits naming
  gaps is `CONSTANT_FIRES` in `../null-harness/` terms until it records
  a referent that DOES have a good word and correctly adopts none. That
  known-negative is not here yet, so "missing" is not yet separated
  from "unsearched". The `STATE` section says so on the page.

## The cheap confirmation

Entry 001's referent has an independent check from a **second trophic
level**: a predator whose search pattern reads the prey distribution.
Face-specific foraging (the woodpecker) means the aggregation is real
and stable rather than an observer artifact — and you do not need the
insects to see it, you need the woodpecker. That is the entry's
strongest support and the cheapest to run.
