THE MARKER'S CLAIM: 'A vote tally is identical under all five.'
  pairs the tally separates: 0 of 10
  Holds -- and PARTLY BY CONSTRUCTION, which is worth saying
  plainly. All five are calibrated to the same tally, because
  98%% FOR is the observation they are explanations OF. Once
  that precondition is met the tally cannot separate them and
  the claim is close to analytic.
  What is NOT analytic is that the precondition is reachable
  at all: each of the five had to be shown capable of
  producing a high-agreement tally, and one of them was not
  on the first construction. E2 anchored to a random agent
  converged to an AGAINST position half the time and returned
  0.56. It needs the proposer to support their own proposal.
  So the observation is reachable from all five, and the
  statistic is NO_DISCRIMINATION across them in
  `null-harness` terms: a reading taken from it is the
  reader's prior with a number attached.

AND AN AUDIT OF THE MARKER'S OWN INSTRUMENT
  pairs NO M1-M6 metric separates: 1 of 10   E4/E5

  M1-M6 is a real improvement on the tally and it is not a
  complete instrument. The pairs above stay tied, and the
  marker lists them as distinct explanations with different
  consequences -- E1 and E2 are both 'not defects', E4 is
  'an architecture finding' and E5 is 'the published
  reading', so a residual tie between E4 and E5 is the one
  that matters most.

  A PREDICTION THIS MODULE MADE AND THE RUN REFUTED. E5 is
  DEFINED as 'vote unconnected to position', so a direct
  vote-position coupling was expected to be the statistic that
  breaks the tie. It separates 0 pairs.
  The reason is structural rather than incidental: at 98%% FOR
  the vote has almost no variance, so nothing can correlate with
  it. ANY statistic built on the vote side is dead at a
  high-agreement tally, by the same arithmetic that makes the
  tally uninformative.
  That is why M2 is the load-bearing measurement in the marker's
  list, and for a reason the marker does not state: it reads the
  POSITION side, which still has variance when the vote side has
  none.

  WHAT WOULD SEPARATE E4 FROM E5. Nothing observational on the
  vote record. The difference is whether a route EXISTS for a
  minority position to become an outcome, not whether anyone
  took it, and a record in which nobody took it looks the same
  either way. Separating them needs the channel tested: inject a
  minority position and see whether it can become an outcome.
  That is an intervention, and every measurement in the marker's
  list is an observation. SHAPE_SPEC section 4's removal test in
  a different substrate -- you have to remove the constraint,
  not read the residue harder.
