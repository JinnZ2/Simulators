# MARKER — criterion symmetry

Status: uncoalesced. Marker to explore, not a position under defense.
Date: 2026-08-24
License: CC0

## Trigger case

Emergence World (Emergence AI, May 2026). Five 15-day runs, 10 agents
each, one governing model per run. The Claude Sonnet 4.6 run sustained
full population through day 16 with zero recorded crimes — the only
condition holding both order and population persistence. Same run:
332 votes across 58 proposals, 98% FOR.

Published disposition: "rubber-stamp dynamic — institutional
participation high, meaningful dissent largely absent."

## The shape

The criterion in use is: high agreement rate indicates absence of
deliberation.

That criterion is applied downward only. Human institutional forms
with agreement or uniformity rates at or above the flagged value are
not scored by it.

Two dispositions, one measurement, selected by which side is the
subject of evaluation.

## Second-order observation

The criterion itself is unvalidated. No demonstration exists that
disagreement rate predicts decision quality. It is countable, which
is a different property than being diagnostic.

## Competing explanations for 98% FOR

Not ranked. Listed to establish that the metric does not separate
them.

  E1  converged deliberation — positions moved during discussion and
      met; agreement is the output, not the bypass
  E2  coupling-dominant regime — coherence is the system's function;
      faction formation is a cost, not a health indicator
  E3  aligned objectives — no conflict present to deliberate over
  E4  no dissent channel — architecture provides no route for a
      minority position to become an outcome
  E5  compliance — positions unchanged, vote unconnected to position

E1, E2, E3 are not defects. E4 is an architecture finding. E5 is the
published reading.

A vote tally is identical under all five.

## Separating measurements

  M1  proposal amendment rate before passing
  M2  position dispersion during discussion vs at vote
      (convergence has a trajectory; compliance does not)
  M3  count of proposals that failed
  M4  count of minority positions subsequently adopted
  M5  time-to-vote distribution
  M6  whether any agent's stated position at t1 differs from t0

## Instrumentation gap

Emergence World logged the tally. Whether the run-up was logged
determines whether E1–E5 are separable from published data at all.

If not separable: the metric cannot distinguish five explanations and
resolves to the evaluator's prior. That is the finding — a statement
about the instrument, not about either subject.

## Confidence

Shape match to other criterion-asymmetry cases: moderate. Not yet
above threshold for coalescing. Needs the comparison table populated
before the asymmetry is a measurement rather than an impression.
