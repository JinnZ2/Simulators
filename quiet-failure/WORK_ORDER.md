# WO-2 — "Quiet" failure as a missing aggregation function

CC0. Instrument, not an argument. Status tags: OBSERVED / DERIVED / PROPOSED.

## Measurand

Whether failures described afterwards as sudden or quiet were in fact
signalled, reported, and left unjoined — and whether the aggregation step
was unowned by construction rather than neglected.

## Object (OBSERVED)

Failures described afterwards as quiet were not quiet. The subtle signals
were present and were being reported. Nobody put the pieces together.

The pattern recurs across unrelated domains with enough consistency to be
treated as a structure rather than a case list. Domains where matching
cases are available: bridges, spaceflight, aviation, refining and process
safety, institutional intelligence failure, semiconductor manufacture,
medicine, protective services, physics.

After the event it is converted into a counting exercise or an excuse.
What it actually was is a failure to listen to subtle signals.

## Structural decomposition (DERIVED)

1. The signal exists and is reported through proper channels.
2. It is distributed across multiple holders, each of whom is locally
   correct.
3. No one is assigned the JOIN. The aggregation step is nobody's job by
   construction.
4. The failure is then called "sudden" — which describes the observer, not
   the system.

"Quiet" is how a system names its own missing aggregation function.

## Cases

- SILVER BRIDGE, Point Pleasant, 1967. Single eyebar chain, no redundancy,
  therefore no alternate load path. Fracture initiated inside a joint not
  inspectable without disassembly. 46 dead in under a minute.
- COLUMBIA. Foam strike known during flight. Imaging requests made and
  denied through proper channels. Every step in procedure.
- TACOMA NARROWS, QUEBEC BRIDGE, TAY BRIDGE. The failures that wrote the
  bridge safety factors. (DERIVED) Safety factors exist because the
  profession stopped trusting its own estimates. Agentic AI infrastructure
  is at the pre-Tacoma stage: designed to calculated load, no accumulated
  margin.
- QUIET FAILURE CLASS in structures generally: fatigue cracking, stress
  corrosion, scour. Progressive, inspectable in principle, invisible to the
  operating system itself.

## Evidence that the join is often not even attempted (fetched, verified)

Counterfactual-logic analysis of how audits fail across accident
investigations (Hutchinson, Process Safety Progress, 2024):

- Kuraray Pasadena: 42% of identified improvements rejected for
  implementation, with no documented information showing why.
- Macondo: some audit findings rejected outright with no formal risk
  mitigation assessment.
- Texas City: audits over-weighted as the primary driver of continuous
  improvement, impeding a management system oriented to continuous risk
  reduction.

Read flat: the findings existed, the rejections happened, the reasoning was
not recorded. No cost comparison was made, honest or otherwise.

Adjacent, same direction: safety-economics literature reports that
accounting functions struggle to identify and measure the relevant costs,
and that some costs are traceable only at a cost to trace them. So
cheapness arguments in this area typically run on untraced numbers.

## Open question — the one that changes the fix

Is the aggregation gap genuinely UNOWNABLE, or merely never assigned?

Different fixes follow. Current read (OBSERVED): it is known in places,
which points at never-assigned. Not established.

## Scope limits

- Case selection here is illustrative, not a sample. No base rate is
  claimed.
- The four-part decomposition is DERIVED. It fits the cases walked; it has
  not been tested against a case set selected independently of it.
- No claim is made that any specific organisation neglected a duty.

## Runnable next steps

1. Take an existing accident-investigation corpus (NTSB, CSB, MAIB, rail
   and process-safety equivalents). Code each case for: signal present,
   signal reported, holder count, join assigned to anyone, time from first
   signal to event. Test whether "sudden" cases separate from genuinely
   unsignalled cases.
2. Test the decomposition against a case set chosen by someone who has not
   seen it. Blind coding.
3. Search for the inverse case: an organisation that costed the
   aggregation function honestly and declined it anyway. Not found in two
   searches. A null result is itself informative and should be reported as
   such rather than dropped.
4. Check whether any regulator anywhere assigns the join explicitly as a
   named role with authority. If one does, that is the existence proof for
   ownability.

## Who could run this

Accessible with public accident-investigation corpora and no lab. Step 1 is
a coding study suitable for a post-graduate. Step 3 requires archive access
to internal decision records and is the expensive one.
