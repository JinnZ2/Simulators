Below is a published claim and the method that produced it.

claim: First-draw tap water in the service area exceeds the lead action level.

method: One-litre first-draw samples collected at kitchen taps after at least six hours of stagnation, from a fixed panel of homes served by lead service lines, in two monitoring periods. Samples acidified and analysed for lead by ICP-MS. Each sample reported as micrograms of lead per litre. The 90th percentile of sample concentrations is computed per monitoring period and compared with the action level.
