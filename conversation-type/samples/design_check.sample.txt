1  the residue window, quantified
   The marker's central design move: measure the 30 min AFTER
   the call, not during. If arousal decays with time constant
   tau after the call ends, the share of total excess exposure
   that falls OUTSIDE a call-window measurement is:

   call (min) tau=5   tau=15  tau=30
   ----------------------------------
   2             71%    88%    94%
   5             50%    75%    86%
   10            33%    60%    75%
   20            20%    43%    60%
   45            10%    25%    40%

   A 5-minute call with a 15-minute decay puts 75% of the
   exposure outside the window a call-window study measures.
   The marker's design choice is not a preference; on its own
   stated mechanism a call-window measurement is looking at the
   minority of the effect, and the shorter the call the worse
   it gets. tau is unmeasured -- P1 and P3 are what would
   estimate it -- and no value here is data.

   It also makes a PREDICTION the marker does not state: a
   literature that measures inside the call window should
   return small or null effects for hands-free, and finding
   that would be consistent with the mechanism rather than
   against it. Not checked here; the egress gate refuses.

2  'binary, and easy to type per call class'
   If suspendability is graded and typed as a binary, the cost
   is attenuation. Recovered correlation vs using the graded
   quantity itself:

   threshold    uniform    bimodal    reading
   ----------------------------------------------------
   0.3          0.784      0.939      
   0.4          0.834      0.969      
   0.5          0.858      0.969      best
   0.6          0.848      0.968      
   0.7          0.825      0.938      

   Two readings, and the second is the marker's defence.
   On a UNIFORM spread of call types the binary recovers about
   85% of the graded signal at the best threshold -- a real but
   modest loss. On a BIMODAL spread, where most calls are
   clearly one kind or the other, it recovers nearly all of it.
   The marker's own three-state list is bimodal in shape:
   obligated calls and podcasts are not near a boundary.
   So the binary is cheap IF the distribution is bimodal, and
   whether it is, is itself an empirical question P4 answers on
   the way to answering the main one.
