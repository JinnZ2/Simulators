# MARKER — conversation type as an uninstrumented driving variable

Status: uncoalesced. Marker to explore, not a position under defense.
Date: 2026-08-24
License: CC0

Second worked example for the confidential-reporting gap. First was
the stability-sensor engagement-logic question. Same structure: a
real hazard, no channel typed to receive it.

## The regulatory category vs the actual variable

Motor carrier distraction rules are written on CHANNEL: handheld vs
hands-free, device manipulation, texting. Same phone, same channel,
two conversations produce entirely different attention states.

Proposed variable instead: SUSPENDABILITY WITHOUT DEBT.

  can the exchange be dropped mid-sentence, unilaterally, with
  nothing owed on return

  binary, and easy to type per call class

## Why suspendability is the load-bearing dimension

Breaking off with a person incurs repair debt. The repair is
performed while driving, or queued to the next stop and performed
then. Either way the cost lands downstream of the call window —
which is why measuring inside the call window misses it.

An exchange that can be dropped and resumed with no accounting
carries no such debt. The driver's attention is not being managed on
behalf of another party's state.

## The arousal channel, distinct from cognitive load

Two different mechanisms, currently collapsed into one category.

  cognitive load    competes for road-relevant resources: visual
                    attention, spatial processing, working memory.
                    Measured. In the literature.

  emotional arousal occupies the system rather than competing for a
                    slice of it. Attentional narrowing, longer hazard
                    detection latency. Does not terminate with the
                    call — persists after.

Arousal degrades SPATIAL reasoning specifically. For an 80,000 lb
vehicle on grade, spatial reasoning is the safety-critical channel.

Steady-pace verbal reasoning with no stake and no obligation is the
third state: does not spike arousal, and on a monotonous task can
counter vigilance decrement. That is established in the
vigilance-decrement literature. Never connected to conversation type.

## Three states, one regulatory bin

  1  emotional / obligated       arousal high, residue after
  2  intellectual / unobligated  arousal flat, suspendable
  3  silence, hour nine          vigilance decrement

Also in the same undifferentiated space: video, podcasts, anything
producing emotional response or tunnel vision. Not equivalent to 2.

## Population exposure

Consequence scales with mass. The exposed population is the one where
the outcome of degraded spatial reasoning is an 80,000 lb vehicle in
a curve on a grade.

## Known in pieces, connected nowhere

The arousal findings exist. The vigilance findings exist. Neither
literature is aimed at conversation type, and neither reaches motor
carrier rulemaking. No submission path exists to say so — the
reporting-gap shape with a different payload.

## Measurements available

  P1  heart rate variability by conversation class, in cab
  P2  incident data cross-referenced against call type in the
      preceding 30 min (residue window, not call window)
  P3  hazard detection latency, post-arousal, on a simulator
  P4  self-report of call class, driver-tallied — no cooperation
      needed to run

## Adjacent finding — the desk-worker default prior

Separable from the above and more general.

General-advice corpora appear to assume a default subject: seated,
low physical risk, socially isolated, for whom more social contact is
the correction. Advice is emitted against that default regardless of
stated operating conditions.

Where the operating condition is an 80,000 lb vehicle in motion, the
default inverts: social contact during the shift is the hazard, and
unobligated reasoning is the mitigation. The prior does not carry a
context check, so it fires anyway.

Instance: this session. Applied twice, corrected twice.

Not filed as a claim about corpora in general — filed as a marker
with one clean instance and a mechanism that would predict more.
