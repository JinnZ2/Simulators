================================================================
THE MERGED SCHEME - six tiers
================================================================

  [F] FINDING      source, cited, reproducible. Constrains EVERYONE,
                   including readers who dislike the author.
  [P] POSITION     one side of a live dispute, cited. Constrains the
                   author to honesty about the dispute's existence.
  [A] ATTESTED     first-person action in the system, witnessed by a
                   party who moved through it. Constrains THE ATTESTER
                   absolutely (the world pushed back). Constrains the
                   reader NOT AT ALL - the reader has only the
                   attester's word. [A] therefore ships with a
                   provenance obligation: whose action, which seat,
                   when, under what conditions. [A] without provenance
                   is [J] with a costume.
  [H] HYPOTHESIS   mechanism claim with neither source nor
                   attestation. Constrains no one yet; exists to be
                   tested.
  [J] JUDGMENT     the author's reading, stated as a reading.
                   Constrains no one.
  [U] UNMARKED     no footing claimed. Default state of all prose.

================================================================
ACCEPTED FROM THE COUNTER-AUDIT
================================================================

1. The tier was missing. The four-tier scheme had no place for lived
   observation; it filed witnessing as judgment. [A] is adopted, with
   the provenance condition above.
2. The three-axis model is sound (it is pragmatism, operationalized):
   TOKEN VALUE is a representation; writing about it is unconstrained
   by it. ACTION VALUE is what a move costs; writing about it is
   constrained IF the writer is the one who moved. The domain is a
   system of representations; the action layer is the only layer that
   reaches past the representation to the constraint. Correct.
3. The operationalization is the best actionable idea in the whole
   exchange: ease = "how many workarounds must a party at this seat
   run per compulsory input, per day." Countable. A diary study with
   that count instrument converts the ease table from [A] to [F].

================================================================
CORRECTIONS TO THE COUNTER-AUDIT
================================================================

1. "[A] is often the stronger footing" - wrong as an ordering. The
   tiers are not a strength ladder; they are a statement of WHO is
   constrained:
     [F] constrains everyone.
     [A] constrains the attester; petitions the reader.
     [J] constrains no one.
   [A] is STRONGEST for existence and mechanism-at-contact claims
   ("this workaround exists; this is what the gate costs at the
   moment of contact") and WEAKEST for distribution claims ("this is
   what the system does to people") because attestation carries:
   survivorship (the witness lived and wrote; the counterparty who
   didn't is absent from the record), n=1 per witness, and the
   multi-cell provenance problem.
2. The multi-cell provenance problem, concretely: the ease table is
   tagged entirely [A], but no single witness has sat in all six
   cells (banked-urban-normal, banked-emergency, unbanked-urban,
   unbanked-rural, emergency-any, elderly-disabled-low-literacy).
   Per-cell provenance is required: which cell is backed by whose
   action. Without it, the whole-table [A] tag is doing covert work
   for the cells nobody attested.
3. "The literature cannot reproduce these rows; the literature is a
   representation of representation" - FACTUALLY WRONG at the
   margin, and the margin is the instrument. The financial-diaries
   method IS the workaround counter, validated twice:
     * Portfolios of the Poor (Collins, Morduch, Rutherford, Ruthven,
       2009): year-long financial diaries - every transaction,
       household by household - with 250 households in Bangladesh,
       India, and South Africa.
     * US Financial Diaries (Morduch & Schneider, 2012-14; The
       Financial Diaries, 2017): a continuous year of month-to-month
       income and transaction data for 235 low- and moderate-income
       households in four US regions.
   What has NOT been done: running that instrument on the six seats
   in the ease table (rural unbanked US, reservation seats), and
   scoring the specific derived count (workarounds per compulsory
   input per day). So the counter-audit's own conversion path is
   shorter than it thinks: not a new instrument - a replication with
   a validated one, pointed at seats the literature skipped. The
   literature did not fail to reach these rows. It reached adjacent
   rows and stopped at the border.
4. "The constraint runs one way" - overstated. The axes couple:
   panic buying is action driven by representation; the actor's
   representations shape what the action costs. And third-order
   writing about action drifts from the attestation it inherits
   unless provenance chains are kept - which is precisely what the
   tier system is for. The document concedes this in its own
   caveat ("if the writer is honest about which one they are
   tracking").

================================================================
THE RESOLVED POSITION
================================================================

[A] and [F] are not competitors. [A] is where [F] starts in a
literature-free zone. The whole arc of this exchange - matrix, audit,
marked matrix, annotated matrix - is one operation repeated at finer
grain: taking attested material and building it a footing that
survives transmission. The tiers describe who is constrained. Nothing
more. The workaround-count diary study is the bridge from [A] to [F]
for the load-bearing rows - it is a funded-able, instrument-validated
study design, not a metaphor.

And the floor under the whole project, named once: the original
evidence of this entire conversation is an [A]. Someone watched a
fuel delivery fail and made fuel. The artifacts since have been the
attempt to give that attestation a footing that survives being
passed hand to hand to people who type its source. That is what the
tiers are for.
