FOOTING KEY (merged scheme)
  [F] FINDING     sourced, reproducible; constrains everyone
  [P] POSITION    one side of a live dispute; constrains the author
                  to honesty about the dispute
  [A] ATTESTED    first-person action in the system; constrains the
                  attester absolutely, the reader not at all
  [H] HYPOTHESIS  mechanism claim awaiting test
  [J] JUDGMENT    the author's reading, stated as a reading
  [U] UNMARKED    no footing claimed; default state of all prose

[A] PROVENANCE RULE: every [A] cell ships with whose action, which
seat, when - or carries the standing note: "attested by the matrix
author's direct observation; single witness; survivorship applies;
not independently reproduced."

THE THREE AXES (from the counter-audit, adopted)
  TOKEN VALUE   a representation. Writing about it is unconstrained
                by it.
  ACTION VALUE  what a move costs. Writing about it is constrained if
                the writer is the one who moved.
  WRITING ABOUT a third-order object; constrained only by the writer's
  EITHER        honesty about which axis they track.

F1  RATIONING / ALLOCATION
================================================================
  purpose       allocate scarce goods without violence or permanent
                queue                                        [J]

                as violence - Galtung, Graeber               [P])
  beneficiary   everyone, in genuine scarcity                [J]

THE BOUNDARY THEOREM (from the addendum, marked)
================================================================
  Token-free interiors at community scale (hundreds to ~1-2k
  people) provision all eight compulsory inputs for decades
  to generations, carrying high internal connectivity, and
  connect to the token world through ONE OR TWO boundary
  channels.                                               [F cases]
  Retreat mechanism documented twice: Amana 1932 (narrow
  boundary squeezed on several fronts + demand beyond the
  austere bundle -> dissolution into joint-stock); kibbutz
  1985-2001 (outside-currency debt -> interior tokenized
  with differential wages -> population decline halted).   [F]
  Interpretation: the gate is not abolished by token-free
  interiors; it migrates to the boundary and narrows.       [J]
  Depression detail: Amana members were shielded through
  the 1930s while the Society died of trade debts - the
  interior out-survived the token economy around it; the
  failure was at the boundary, not the provisioning.        [F]
  Binding frame: durable token-free coordination requires
  religion/kinship and pays its gate in belonging - exit
  costs, conformity, austere-bundle scope limits.
  "conformity_required" was one of the eleven gate tags in
  the earlier collapse analysis: the gate structure
  persists; its currency changes.                          [F existence;
                J interpretation]

CORRECTED DESIGN STANDARD [J synthesis; standard itself cited [F]]
================================================================
  1. Re-intern the compulsory inputs at community scale -
     documented, generations-deep precedent.
  2. Keep TWO OR MORE boundary channels (single-boundary
     failure mode documented in Amana).
  3. Carry the capability tail collectively (mutual-aid
     pooling), not by per-person credentialing gates.
  4. N-1 and decoupled fallback at every intolerable vertex
     (reliability-engineering standard; HOT consequence).
  5. Restore the cash fallback: its deletion removes
     fallback rows across F1, F2, F5, F7.
