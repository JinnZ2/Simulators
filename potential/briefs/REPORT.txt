# Fold-in report: "the 19 token-free avenues aren't free"

Companion to the critique document (uploaded). Encodes the critique's
per-avenue annotations as gate tags on the shipped gate/domains.py channels
and recomputes what the instrument can then claim.

## Results (executed, domains_gated.py)

Cumulative survival of the 19 token-free avenues as the critique's gate
classes are applied in its own cross-cutting order:

    layer                        surviving
    0  no token node only             19
    + legal                           12
    + movement                         6
    + time                             4
    + token_pre                        1   (only medical/self-care)
    + capacity                         1
    + conformity                       1
    + envelope                         0   (self-care: "works for minor things")

No single gate class kills all 19 (intersection of tag sets is empty), but
every avenue carries at least one gate, and the union covers all 11 tags.
Widest-net single gate: envelope (10 of 19), then token_pre (8).

## Corrections to the critique's factual frame

* Martin v. Boise: panel decision Sept 2018 (902 F.3d 1031), amended on
  denial of rehearing en banc April 2019 (920 F.3d 584), cert denied
  Dec 16, 2019 — "2019" is defensible. BUT City of Grants Pass v. Johnson
  (June 2024) reversed it: the Eighth Amendment no longer limits
  criminalization of public camping anywhere in the US. The critique's
  shelter/rough-sleeping entries lean on Martin as the live constraint;
  the constraint is gone, so those avenues' legal gate is HARDER than
  stated, not softer.
* Steel v Houghton (1788) confirmed: gleaning held a privilege, not a
  right — "English legal right ended in 1788" is accurate.

## Schema recommendation

Add `preconditions: frozenset` to Channel (done in domains_gated.py).
Booleans re-create the overclaim one level down; production tags should be
parameterized by jurisdiction/season/severity, because most gates are
regional and temporal (rain-harvest bans, Grants Pass vs. Martin-era
9th Circuit, winter closure).

## Epistemic status (the critique's item 12, applied recursively)

The tags are AUTHORED claims, same status as the channels they annotate.
The fold-in lets the instrument say something true and modest:

    "Of 19 edges without a token node, 0 carry none of the 11 documented
    gate classes; weakest single additional gate removes 10."

It cannot say "0 free avenues exist in the world" — the tags are readings,
not measurements. That claim would need a falsifier and field data, in the
repo's own claim-table discipline (suggested entry: POT_021, HYPOTHESIZED).
