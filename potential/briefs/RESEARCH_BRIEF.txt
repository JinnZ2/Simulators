# Research brief: token-gate -> bandwidth tax -> capability ratio

Companion to the uploaded document ("The mechanisms are documented. The
causal claim is testable."). Verifies the documented layer, upgrades three
claims with current evidence, runs the cross-jurisdictional sketch, and
implements the ratio schema.

## 1. Documented layer — VERIFIED (with one citation sharpening)

* Bandwidth tax: primary source is Mani, Mullainathan, Shafir & Zhao,
  "Poverty impedes cognitive function," Science 341(6149):976-980 (2013) —
  NJ mall experiment + Tamil Nadu sugarcane farmers; ~13-IQ-point
  equivalent; survives controls for stress, nutrition, time, effort.
  "Mullainathan & Shafir, Scarcity (2013)" is the book version; cite Mani
  et al. for the effect itself.
* Chronic stress substrate (McEwen allostatic load; Sapolsky
  glucocorticoid/hippocampal): standard, uncontested. Not re-litigated here.
* Stagnation trends: Bloom, Jones, Van Reenen & Webb, "Are Ideas Getting
  Harder to Find?", AER 110(4):1104-44 (2020) — VERIFIED: research
  productivity falls ~5%/yr economy-wide (half-life ~13 yrs); 18x more
  researchers per Moore doubling; semiconductors -7%/yr.
  CONTESTED: Fort et al. (Census CES WP 25-21, 2025) argue "Growth Is
  Getting Harder to Find, Not Ideas" — patent-based innovation measures
  hold up; the leakage is translation/commercialization, not idea
  production. The stagnation fact is solid; its cause is not assigned.

## 2. Trials — the open question is HALF-closed

The document says trials show bandwidth effects but "haven't shown whether
the freed bandwidth goes to invention, care work, creative production, or
rest." Hours-allocation IS now measured in several trials; invention is not.

* Kenya general-equilibrium cash transfers (Egger et al., Econometrica
  2022): overall labor supply did NOT fall; hours shifted away from wage
  work toward recipients' own businesses/livestock; $1,000 transfer ->
  ~$2,500 local economic activity; recipients +0.26 SD psychological
  well-being. Freed hours went to OWN-PRODUCTION, not rest.
* Uganda Youth Opportunities Program, 12-year follow-up: recipients +22%
  earnings, more likely in skilled trades, more businesses, more savings.
* Stockton SEED ($500/mo): full-time employment 28% -> 40% vs +5pp
  control (pre-pandemic); spending on training/coursework; catching up on
  deferred health needs (dental). Peer-reviewed follow-up: employment
  shift positive but not statistically significant.
* Finland 2017-18: well-being up, mild positive employment effect;
  freelancers/artists reported starting businesses.
* Kenya 12-year UBI study (Banerjee et al.): long-run transfers ->
  food security, assets, own-enterprise revenue; wage-labor hours down.

Direction of the evidence: freed bandwidth goes disproportionately to
production, enterprise, care, and health catch-up. Nothing yet measures
invention/creative output directly. Nearest proxy: new-business formation.

## 3. Cross-jurisdictional sketch (World Bank, latest available, executed)

x = out-of-pocket health expenditure share (% of current health spending)
    = inverse proxy for uncompensated provisioning of one compulsory input
y1 = new business density (formal registrations / 1,000 adults), n = 55
y2 = researchers in R&D per million (log), n = 42

    y1: Pearson r = -0.36 (p=0.006), Spearman rho = -0.46 (p<0.001)
    y2: Pearson r = -0.40 (p=0.009), Spearman rho = -0.33 (p=0.031)
    OOP < 12%  (n=8):  median new-business density 5.0
    OOP > 30% (n=16): median 1.0

Direction is the prediction's direction. Confounds, stated: income level
drives both axes; new-business density counts only FORMAL registrations
(systematically undercounts informal economies, which correlates with
high-OOP countries — bias direction unclear); OOP is one of eight
compulsory inputs, not the gate itself. Status: consistent-with, not
causal. See wb_gates_scatter.png.

## 4. Ratio schema — IMPLEMENTED (ratio_model.py)

Adds hours_cost and capability_yield per channel of the eight domains.
Every number [ASSUMED]; the structure is the contribution:

  token-seeking hours return token, not capability -> gated yield 0.
  direct-production hours return capability > 0 by construction.
  a gate converting the second bucket into the first is pure deadweight
  BEFORE counting the bandwidth tax.

Illustrative run: total capability yield under gate = 0.014 units/hr;
if all compulsory-input hours were freed = 1.20 units/hr; deadweight
factor 84x. Two domains (defecation, sleep) have gated capability yield
of EXACTLY zero — the ratchet is not a small ratio, it is division by
zero: effort in, nothing out, next payment still due.

## 5. Honest state after this pass

Mechanisms: DOCUMENTED (primary sources verified).
Trends: DOCUMENTED, cause CONTESTED (Bloom vs. Fort).
Bandwidth -> hours-allocation: MEASURED, leans production/enterprise/care.
Bandwidth -> invention/creative output: STILL OPEN. This is the test the
document calls for and nobody has run: guaranteed-availability trial with
invention/creative-output instruments, not just employment and wellbeing.
The ratio schema now makes the individual-level version computable; what
it needs to become evidence is field time-use data per channel.
