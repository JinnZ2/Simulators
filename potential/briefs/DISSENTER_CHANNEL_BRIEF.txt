2. THE GRAPH READ: FOUR ATTACKS ON ONE EDGE
================================================================

The office is a feedback edge from the counter-signal to the
decision node. Ashby, again: the sitting deliberation's model of
the world is a compression; the office exists to inject variety
the compression lacks. The four failure modes named in the
document are four distinct attacks on that single edge:

  capture          edge intact, rerouted to the defended point;
                   signal flows, direction wrong
  ritualization    edge throttled: objection heard, filed, never
                   reaches the decision node (dead-end node inserted)
  fiat removal     edge cut (1983: veto and mandatory-answer
                   obligations removed; occupant retained as
                   consultant - the channel properties, not the
                   person)
  reclassification edge physically intact; the receiver's decoder
                   is retrained: objections discounted BECAUSE they
                   come from that source. Signal still transmitted;
                   SNR degraded at the receiver. The only attack
                   invisible on an org chart.

Reclassification is the one worth the most attention, because it
preserves every appearance of the channel: the objection is heard,
recorded, sometimes even praised - and weighted to zero because it
is expected. A community that labels its highest-hit-rate sensor
"the one who always objects" is updating its prior against its own
best instrument.

4. THE CLOSING CLAIM
================================================================

"The role doesn't require the seat." Verified against the record:
every tradition that kept the role gave it duties and standing;
every system that lost it kept (or typed) the person and cut the
edge properties. The Promotor Fidei timeline is the controlled
observation: edge properties removed 1983, throughput response
immediate and sustained. The findings of a seatless channel remain
true whether or not anyone heard them; what the seat bought was
timing - the failure used to arrive as a question, and after the
edge was cut it arrived as an event. That is all "on schedule" has
ever meant.
