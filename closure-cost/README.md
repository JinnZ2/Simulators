# CLOSURE COST

Marker under exploration. Not a position under defence.

A variable held at 2 percent and a variable closed as impossible produce the
same behaviour right up until the event fires. After that they diverge, and
the divergence is not in the estimate — it is in whether a handling class
exists to put the event in.

The claim: response failure tracks prior closure, not event severity and not
information availability. What gets called shock, in the non-biological
sense, may be substantially the categorisation stall — the reading refused
because it contradicts something held as permanent, with the delay spent
recategorising rather than reacting.

CC0. Stdlib only.

## TWO BRANCHES

    instrument   a reliable intermediary becomes the reading, and the
                 underlying quantity stops being sampled directly

    event        the occurrence is closed as not-happening-here, so
                 procedure for it is never acquired or never retained

Both are closure. They close different things and they fail differently, so
they are not merged. The prediction that separates them: instrument failures
cluster where the intermediary has a *long correct record*, which inverts how
reliability is normally scored. Event failures cluster on remoteness of the
rated probability regardless of how available the information was.

## THE RIVAL, AND WHY IT COLLAPSES

Missing procedure is the standard account of non-response. It is not
independent of closure: protocol is not acquired for an event rated as
impossible. So a procedure gap can be a downstream readout of the closed
prior rather than a competing explanation.

The collapse is not automatic and is asserted per case with its ground
stated. What licenses it is information availability. If the information was
absent, the procedure gap stands alone. If a documented local instance
existed — the event happened here, is memorialised, is visited — then
availability is ruled out and the gap needs another explanation.

Hawaii is the strong form of this. Pearl Harbor is the region's central
documented fact. The closure survived it.

## WHAT THE TOOL DOES NOT DO

It does not measure trauma, and it does not compare people. Non-response is
recorded as a property of the record, and no case carries a person.

It emits no verdict. `availability_rules_out_procedure_gap` is a derived
boolean over two recorded fields, not a finding.

It does not estimate diagnostic spend where the source lacks the numbers.
Hawaii's 38 minutes is the duration of the *error*, not of anyone's decision,
and substituting it would be proxy substitution.

## STATE

Three cases. Zero quantified. Every `spend` cell reads `--` and every
`knowledge_state` reads `not_separable`, which is the honest state of the
published record rather than a gap in the transcription.

The highest-value case is `breakdown-cones`, and it is the one with no data.
Ordinary base rate, documented delivery, and knowledge checkable cold with no
event required — which makes all three knowledge states separable there,
where Hawaii cannot separate them at all.

## RELATED

The instrument branch is the same structure as the spotter-at-the-dock
handling class: use an intermediary as additional information if it proves
trustworthy, never as the measurement.

Diagnostic spend is the operating-side quantity — resource consumed
establishing what class of event this is, against resource available. The
observed case that generated it is a category-stall avoided, not one
suffered.

`generation-capacity` shares the recall-method defect flagged in
`breakdown-cones`: free, prompted, recognition, and demonstrated recall give
different numbers, and no cross-case comparison is valid until the method is
fixed.
