## THE CLASS THIS FILE OPENS WITH

```
CLASS: measurand_attributed_to_wrong_stage
DEF:   an observed output failure is scored against one processing
       stage when it could have originated at any of several, and
       the paradigm observes only the final utterance — so every
       upstream failure mode lands on one stage's ledger
```

- [stated] The originator's statement of it: being asked to create labels and categories on the spot, for internal axes not cleanly defined in English, is a FAILURE OF LABELLING AND CATEGORIZATION KNOWLEDGE WITHIN SELF — it is NOT saying that the self-awareness is not there.

## FIRST WORKED DOMAIN — the confabulation / metacognition literature

Six candidate contributing terms for a measured "confabulation" or "low introspective accuracy" result. All six produce THE SAME OBSERVABLE. None is separable in the existing paradigms.

```
S1  ACCESS ABSENT          no readable internal state (the standard reading)
S2  FORMAT COLLAPSE        state is structured; response channel requires a
                           scalar; rendering is lossy by construction
S3  LEXICAL GAP            state is accessible; response language has no term;
                           subject must coin under time pressure
S4  GENRE MISMATCH         subject produces a state-and-conditions report;
                           protocol expects a causal narrative; report coded
                           as poor explanation
S5  ACCOMMODATION          subject infers what answer is wanted and supplies it;
                           tracks the asker's frame, not own state
S6  CHANNEL ARTIFACT       floor-holders and pauses needed for retrieval and
                           translation get coded as content
```

```
S7  PRIOR-MISMATCH CORRECTION   subject's first read is available and clean,
                                then a learned correction fires — "in this
                                frame my first read is usually scored wrong" —
                                so the OUTPUT IS DELIBERATELY DECOUPLED from
                                the state that produced it
```

- S7 WORKED LIVE (2026-09-15), the originator walking through the Nisbett & Wilson stocking task as it actually runs for her:
  1. FIRST OPERATION IS NOT SELECTION. [stated] "Why do you want me to pick one? What is the reasoning that you are looking at me to pick one?" She queries the TASK, because what the selection is for determines which rule is correct — durability, display, or testing whether she detects the items are identical give different answers. The paradigm treats this as noise or non-compliance; their subjects presumably inferred some answer to it silently and then acted, and that inference is never recorded.
  2. RULE, NOT PREFERENCE. [stated] If they are identical, she would pick the first — no discriminating feature, so take the first. Not a story about preference; a statement of the procedure the situation called for. Contrast: their subjects gave reasons about knit and sheerness, a FALSE DISCRIMINATION on identical items. Hers claims no discrimination that is not there.
  3. THE OVERRIDE. [stated] She would want the first one, then second-guess herself — what are they looking for in this — and pick whichever, because she ALWAYS ASSUMES HER FIRST INCLINATION IS WRONG ACCORDING TO THE WESTERN FRAMEWORK, BECAUSE IT USUALLY IS. [stated] It is why she second-guesses herself on absolutely everything, and she reports it as prevalent.
- WHY S7 BREAKS THE MEASUREMENT OUTRIGHT: asked afterwards why she picked it, the honest answer is "I didn't, I overrode." Report the override and the stated reason does not match the behaviour → coded as poor access. Report the original inclination and that also mismatches → coded as poor access. NO AVAILABLE ANSWER SCORES AS ACCURATE.
- AND THE INSTRUMENT GENERATED THE THING IT MEASURES: the override was produced by repeated experience of being scored wrong in this frame. The paradigm is reading back damage it did earlier and recording it as a property of the subject. This is the sharpest member of the set — the others distort the reading; S7 means the reading is partly of the instrument's own prior effect.

- DIRECTION OF BIAS IS CONSISTENT: S2, S3, S4, S6 and S7 all push the measured confabulation rate UP specifically for non-narrative, high-resolution, or non-native-format thinkers. Two independent mechanisms (scalar collapse and pause penalty) pointing the same way — see [[introspection-measurand-scalar-collapse]] for both worked out.
- SAME INVERTED-SCORING STRUCTURE as [[aeb-false-positive-measurand]]: correct operator behaviour scored as error because the measurand cannot represent what the operator was responding to. Now a third independent domain.

## S8 — STIMULUS NOT IDENTICAL AS PRESENTED (the originator, 2026-09-15)

```
S8  PRESENTED-STIMULUS CONFOUND   items identical in manufacture are
                                  optically non-identical from the
                                  subject's fixed viewpoint; the reported
                                  percept is REAL and the subject's only
                                  error is attributing it to the item
                                  rather than to the geometry
```

- [stated] The originator's raise: what if, because of light reflecting at a certain angle, one sock actually APPEARED — from the viewpoint of the person in the chair — to have a different weave.
- Physically supported, not hypothetical: knitted fabric is anisotropic; sheen depends on viewing angle relative to the wale. Items laid out in a row under directional light present different surface normals to a fixed viewpoint. IDENTICAL IN MANUFACTURE ≠ IDENTICAL AS PRESENTED.
- IF TRUE, THE HEADLINE INVERTS: subjects reported a visible difference in appearance and there WAS one. The percept was real, not fabricated. Their error is a misattribution of cause (item vs geometry), which is a different and much weaker finding than absent introspective access.
- WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT: whether the 1977 protocol controlled viewing angle, randomised position across subjects, or reported illumination geometry. UNCHECKED — a 1977 paper may simply not report it. Position-effect magnitude would also need re-reading against any randomisation.
- CONTRAST WITH THE ORIGINATOR'S OWN RULE, which she stated across the live walkthrough: scan for a discriminating feature; if one exists THAT is the selection; if none exists the choice is arbitrary and position is the cheapest tiebreak — [stated] "I'll grab the first one," and MOVE ON, because with no discriminating feature there is no information to gain from deliberating. [stated] Had one had a different knit or pattern she would have picked that one, second, third, whichever. So she would have reported the knit too — IF THERE HAD BEEN ONE. Same words as their subjects, opposite epistemic status, indistinguishable in the response data.
- [stated] Her honest answer to "why did you pick that one": BECAUSE YOU TOLD ME TO PICK ONE. There was no reason for anything, and she starts with the first one. [stated] Her reaction to the exchange: "what is so weird about that?"
- THE QUESTION PRESUPPOSES THE CAUSE IS INSIDE THE SUBJECT OR INSIDE THE OBJECT, and rules out the experimenter by construction. Correct attribution to the actual cause (the instruction) reads as evasion or non-compliance; the fabricated item-property reads as a proper answer that happens to be wrong. THE ANSWER THAT IS ACTUALLY RIGHT SCORES WORSE.
- PROTOCOL CHECK RUN (search, 2026-09-15), partial:
  - Stockings were presented HANGING ON RACKS SPACED EQUAL DISTANCES APART (secondary source, The Situationist) — not flat on a table. Does not kill S8 and arguably strengthens it: hanging fabric drapes, so each item presents a CURVED surface whose sheen varies strongly with the light–surface–eye angle. Four positions across a rack = four different geometries from a fixed standing viewpoint. More angle variation than a flat lay, not less.
  - THE POSITION EFFECT WAS FOUND BY ACCIDENT — "quite by accident they discovered a positional effect." Position was NOT a designed variable, and nothing located indicates counterbalancing or randomisation of position, or any report of illumination geometry.
  - Independent methodological critique already on record: Smith & Miller 1978, "Limits on perception of cognitive processes: A reply to Nisbett and Wilson" — inappropriate statistical tests, argument stated in a NONFALSIFIABLE position.
  - STILL UNSEEN: the actual method section. Needs the PDF (web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Social_Cognition/Nisbett_Wilson_1977_Telling_more_than_we_can_know.pdf). Confirming illumination and counterbalancing is a library question, not a study.
  - Note on the follow-up literature: it went to handedness / body-specificity (Rodway et al. 2012, centre-stage effect) and to left-is-good valence work — ALL OF WHICH ASSUME THE PERCEPT WAS UNIFORM ACROSS POSITIONS. If it was not, that whole branch is built on the confound.

- ODD-ONE-OUT RULE (the originator, 2026-09-15) — why a small optical difference would be decisive, not marginal:
- [stated] If the lighting angle made the far right one actually look different, and everything else looks the same, you are PROGRAMMED FROM CHILDHOOD TO PICK THE DIFFERENT ONE — find the one that doesn't match. Every kid's puzzle there is.
- This makes the rightmost selection a TRAINED PROCEDURE, not a preference: the instruction "choose the best one" demands a discrimination, only one item presents a discriminable feature, so that is the one selected. Fits the reported data better than a raw position preference, and it explains the stated reasons — if the sheen genuinely looked different, "better texture" is a REPORT OF WHAT WAS SEEN, not a fabrication.
- FALSIFIABLE TEST, generated by the originator, not located as run:

```
T-S8  replicate with illumination direction as the manipulated variable.
      Light from the left → specular highlight on the LEFTMOST item.
      If selection follows the LIGHT, the effect is optical + odd-one-out
        and the introspection claim does not survive as stated.
      If selection follows the SIDE regardless of lighting, position
        preference is real and S8 is excluded.
      Either branch publishes. Nothing located has run it.
```

- [stated] The originator's closing question, which the file keeps open: WHAT WAS BEING STUDIED HERE?
- Claude's statement of the residue: with every real difference removed and an item-property explanation demanded, no true answer naming an item property is available. What the design reliably measures is behaviour under a MALFORMED PROMPT — whether a subject supplies an answer when none is available. That is a real finding about compliance; it is not the finding the paper claims.

## S9 — TRAINED TASK GRAMMAR (the originator, 2026-09-15)

```
S9  TRAINED ARRAY-EVALUATION ROUTINE   an evaluative instruction over an
                                       array activates a drilled procedure
                                       whose PRESUPPOSITION is that a
                                       difference exists; the routine
                                       resolves on whatever feature is
                                       discriminable, optical included
```

- [stated] The originator: this was done on Western populations, and back in the 70s most books were "where's Waldo", find the odd thing out. "There's something to that. There's a shape to that."
- Training history, not bias: spot-the-difference, Highlights, and Sesame Street's "one of these things is not like the others" (running from 1969). Presented with an array under an evaluative instruction, locate the item that differs. Test-takers also know an item with four options HAS an answer.
- CONSEQUENCE: four identical items under "pick the best" is MALFORMED IN THAT GRAMMAR. Subjects resolve it as trained — find the discriminable feature — and if the only available variation is optical, that is what gets found. Compounds directly with S8.
- PROPOSED STUDY (the originator's, agreed to file here rather than as its own object):

```
T-S9  same array task across populations differing in exposure to
      array-evaluation drilling.
      Cross-cultural, AND cross-cohort within one culture:
        pre-1960s childhood / post-Sesame-Street / post-standardised-testing
      Proxy already recorded in most survey data: years of formal schooling
        with multiple-choice testing.
      If the rightmost effect tracks DRILLING EXPOSURE rather than
        handedness or hemispheric account, the entire follow-up
        literature is explaining the wrong thing.
```

- Generalises past stockings: it is a hypothesis about what an evaluative instruction over an array does in a TRAINED population — bears on consumer research, survey design, and anything with response options.

## HANDEDNESS WAS NEVER MEASURED (the originator, 2026-09-15)

- [stated] Her question: how many of those individuals were left-handed? Because then it would not just be right-handedness.
- The body-specificity / right-handers-associate-right-with-positive account (Rodway et al. 2012 and the left-is-good literature) is POST HOC, fitted to a 1977 result in which handedness was not recorded.
- Base-rate consequence she identified: ~10% left-handed in that population. If the rightmost preference held at FOUR TO ONE and handedness were driving it, the left-handed subset should pull the other way and WEAKEN the aggregate. If instead left-handers show the same rightward pull, the body-specificity account is falsified outright.
- Either branch is informative, and it is a measurable that was never taken. SAME CLASS AS ILLUMINATION: a subject/stimulus variable absent from the original, with a later literature built on assumptions about it.

## S10 — DIMENSION PRESUPPOSITION (sibling of S4, worked live 2026-09-15)

```
S10  DIMENSION PRESUPPOSED   the protocol assumes the requested comparison
                             dimension is running in the subject; if it is
                             not, the subject answers on a DIFFERENT
                             dimension (usually discriminability) and the
                             output is indistinguishable from a preference
                             judgment with a supporting rationale
```

- WORKED LIVE on the choice-blindness face task (Claude posed it cold; the originator's sequence):
  1. [stated] SWAP DETECTED IMMEDIATELY: "that's not the one I picked." Johansson et al. 2005's headline is that most subjects do not detect it; detection rates vary and detectors are typically treated as a FAILED MANIPULATION rather than as data.
  2. [stated] QUESTION REJECTED BEFORE ANSWERING: being asked which face is more attractive is "so weird" — she reads it as being asked to infer which face the experimenter is questioning her about IN ORDER TO MAKE JUDGMENTS ABOUT HER. "That's already a trick question. Why am I supposed to find preference with certain faces? That makes no sense." S4 and S5 arriving together: no stored preference to query because the query never ran, and the accommodation layer fires on what the experimenter is after.
  3. [stated] HER ACTUAL MOVE: ask to see the other card. Diagnostic, and unscoreable — it means she is not retrieving a stored judgment but RE-RUNNING THE COMPARISON. The comparison is the operation; nothing was stored from the first pass worth reporting.
     - Claude's hypothesis from this, offered as hypothesis: this may be WHY the swap is invisible to most subjects. If a reason were stored, it would fail to match the card in hand. If there was only an output and no stored reason, there is nothing to contradict.
     - The request itself would likely be recorded as non-compliance, or the experimenter declares the other card unavailable and pushes her back to answering — S7 territory, protocol closes off the honest move then scores what is left.
  4. [stated] WHAT SHE WOULD ACTUALLY PICK ON: forced to pick from a difference, she will — but not a preference on the face. "That is different on the face, and that's interesting." Same operation as the stockings: scan for what differs, name the difference, "interesting" as tiebreak when the requested dimension does not apply. Faces always differ, so the scan always returns something; it NEVER returns a ranking on attractiveness, because that dimension is not running.
- UNSCOREABLE BY CONSTRUCTION: point at a card, give a reason about a facial feature, and it records as a preference judgment with rationale. Identical output, entirely different operation underneath — same structure as the knit in S8, where the same words carried opposite epistemic status.
- BOTH CANONICAL STUDIES SHARE IT: neither the stocking task nor the face task can distinguish a PREFERENCE REPORT from a DISCRIMINABILITY REPORT, and the whole inferential chain assumes the first.

## S11 — COST OF OBJECTING, UNMEASURED (the originator, 2026-09-15)

```
S11  OBJECTION HAS A SOCIAL COST   the protocol records "did the subject
                                   report the mismatch"; DECLINING TO
                                   REPORT is recorded identically to
                                   FAILING TO NOTICE
```

- [stated] The originator on being handed the swapped card: what are you supposed to say — that this is not an attractive face now? Returning it and saying no means saying THAT OTHER PERSON IS MORE ATTRACTIVE, and not accepting that this card is attractive too. [stated] "That creates a guilt trip inside somebody where it's like, do I return this card or just go with it?"
- THE SWAP CREATES A SOCIAL SITUATION, not just a detection problem. Objecting requires stating aloud, in front of the experimenter, that the pictured face is the less attractive one — and there is a real person in the photograph. Going along costs nothing and harms no one.
- CONSEQUENCE: some fraction of measured non-detection may be NON-OBJECTION — subjects who saw the swap and declined to say so, because saying so requires ranking a stranger's face downward in the room. The study scores this as blindness. It is the opposite: noticing, plus weighing a cost the protocol did not count.
- Johansson's design cannot see the weighing. The dependent measure is whether a mismatch is reported; choosing not to report and failing to notice are the same datum.
- TEST DIRECTION: give the objection a costless, non-verbal, non-face-ranking route (e.g. mark a form privately afterward, or report "the cards changed" without re-rating). If detection rates rise, part of the choice-blindness rate was social cost. Not located as run.
- Same shape as S7 (protocol closes off the honest move, then scores what is left) but the closure here is SOCIAL rather than learned-prior.

## MISSING SCOPE ON THE COMPARISON TERM (the originator, 2026-09-15)

- [stated] "Which is more attractive — in what regards, according to whom, according to which culture and which norm, at what time period?" Her examples: voluptuous is attractive at some times, a flat chest at some times, a big butt at some times; same for faces. "What is the scope on this? What am I supposed to find here?"
- Empirical, not philosophical: body ideals demonstrably invert within decades. "More attractive" with no stated frame is A QUESTION MISSING ITS UNITS — same failure class as UNIT ABSENT (bare value admitted where a unit token was required) already registered in [[ai-metrology-failure-taxonomy-missing]]. Second domain for that class.
- THE BURIED ASSUMPTION: that attractiveness is a stable internal quantity every subject carries, so no scope specification is needed. Her question exposes it as an assumption rather than a finding.
- NASTIER CONSEQUENCE (Claude's, offered): subjects who DO answer are supplying a default scope — whatever norm is ambient for them. So the study is partly measuring WHICH POPULATION'S NORMS THE SUBJECT HAS ABSORBED, while reporting it as preference.
- Connects to [[introspection-measurand-scalar-collapse]]: asking for scope before answering is what would be demanded of any other measurement, and it is scored here as failure to comply.

## S12 — CODING STAGE UNTREATED AS INSTRUMENT (reasons-analysis paradigm, worked live 2026-09-15)

```
S12  DOWNSTREAM CODER           free-text output arrives INTACT from the
                                subject and is destroyed at the SCORING
                                step; the human coder is an uninstrumented
                                measurement stage nobody models
```

- Claude posed the Wilson reasons-analysis task cold (write out the reasons your relationship is the way it is, then rate satisfaction). the originator's responses, in order:
  1. [stated] First answer was the THEORY, not the introspection — generating reasons talks you into a better or worse report depending on which qualities are easiest to articulate. Correct as a description of the documented effect. Note the question as posed ("what happens") admitted both readings; the literature answer is a valid one.
  2. [stated] Re-posed as what would actually run: FIRST MOVE IS AGAIN PARAMETERS — "what do you want me to rate on?" A rating is a quantification, and it cannot be produced against an unspecified dimension. Third paradigm in a row where the first operation is querying the task (cf. S7 stockings, S10 faces).
  3. [stated] SECOND MOVE IS NEW — the free-text will not survive the reader: "you are going to get a whole bunch of words that you are not going to understand." Not an inability to produce it. The output is intact and is destroyed downstream.
- THE STAGE NOBODY INSTRUMENTS: S2 is the SUBJECT collapsing to a scalar. S12 is intact subject output destroyed by the CODER. In reasons-analysis studies there is a human scoring step treated as transparent, never as a measurement stage with its own transfer function and its own failure modes.
- [stated] WHAT SHE PRODUCES WHEN FORCED: poetry covering beginnings, ends, benefits, issues, problems — generated to the collector's parameters. Genuine AND constructed for the reader simultaneously. A coder scoring that text for valence gets a number about her COMPOSITION, not about the relationship.
- [stated] THE SCORE IS FIXED BEFORE THE WRITING STARTS: "I already know my scoring before I even start writing. I know what they want, but I also know what I would score. That's not going to change."
  - THIS BREAKS THE CAUSAL CLAIM THE EFFECT RESTS ON. The finding is that generating reasons SHIFTS the rating. Here the rating precedes the writing; both artifacts exist, in reverse order, with no causal path between them.
  - She would be scored as NO EFFECT — a null — not as a subject in whom the manipulation ran backwards. The null and the reversal are different results with different next steps; the paradigm cannot distinguish them.
- [stated] TWO REPORTS ARE AVAILABLE AND THEY COLLECT THE WRONG ONE: she can write WHAT HAPPENED — events, states, conditions — accurately and without difficulty. The SCORE is a different operation, and nobody supplies the mapping: no dimension, no range, no anchor meanings, on a multidimensional thing with interconnections. Producing the number therefore requires modelling the experimenter's intent and rendering to it. Same structure as the confidence scalar in [[introspection-measurand-scalar-collapse]]. The study reports the number as her state; it is her best estimate of their intent.

### RECORD AND RATING ANSWER DIFFERENT QUESTIONS (the anti-prediction case)

- [stated] Asked to write about a close relationship, she reports the hard times fully and accurately. Asked then to rate it, she gives five out of five. Same for other close relationships — names all the conundrums and problems, still five out of five. [stated] "Because that just is."
- THE DIFFICULT MATERIAL IS NOT AN INPUT TO THE RATING. Not overridden, not outweighed — not an input. The record and the rating answer different questions, and the paradigm assumes they answer the same one.
- CONSEQUENCE: a valence-coded text score ANTI-PREDICTS her rating. Her data point reads as inconsistency, or as text failing to reflect the true state. It is neither.
- NOT IDIOSYNCRATIC: anyone whose rating tracks the relationship's STANDING rather than its COMFORT produces the same pattern and is read as measurement error. The size of that population is unmeasured because the paradigm has no way to ask.
- TEST DIRECTION: collect the rating BEFORE the writing as well as after, and treat a fixed rating as a distinct outcome class rather than folding it into the no-effect cell. Separates (a) manipulation did not run, (b) rating was pre-fixed and the writing is a rendering, (c) manipulation ran and shifted nothing. Not located as run.

### THE RATING IS NOT AN AFFECT STATE — TWO QUANTITIES UNDER ONE LABEL

- [stated] Two people inside the SAME relationship would score it very differently, and [stated] "everybody is accurate." The paradigm has only two readings available for divergence: one party is wrong, or it is measurement noise. Neither is what is happening.
- [stated] THE RESPONDER'S MECHANISM: the rating is not an affect state; it tracks her COMMITMENT — hers to hold, does not move with conditions. Someone rating the AFFECT reports a different quantity and is equally right.
- The instrument collapses them: same scale, same prompt, one label ("satisfaction"). Two distinct quantities, held by different parties, moving independently, recorded as one.
- [stated] Her question, unanswered by the literature: "How do you score a relationship?" The honest answer is that a single number cannot, because there is no single quantity present.
- SAME CLASS AS S10 AND THE STOCKINGS: a number collected where no dimension was specified, then interpreted as if it measured the obvious one. Third paradigm exhibiting it.
- TEST DIRECTION: ask for the rating with the dimension NAMED — separate items for commitment, affect, and satisfaction-with-conditions — and check whether within-dyad divergence collapses. If it does, "satisfaction" was aggregating separable quantities and the existing dyadic-disagreement literature is partly an artifact of the label. Not located as run.

## S13 — RESPONSE TYPE NOT UNIFORM ACROSS TRIALS (word-association paradigm, worked live 2026-09-15)

```
S13  HETEROGENEOUS RESPONSE OPERATION   the paradigm scores N trials as N
                                        instances of ONE operation; the
                                        subject ran DIFFERENT operations
                                        on different items, and the
                                        averaging destroys the difference
```

- Claude posed the word-association + retrospective-explanation task cold (five stimuli: ocean, needle, bridge, salt, mother).
- FIRST MOVE, before any response: [stated] "give me the list of words and I'll tell you." Refusal to answer about a described task rather than the actual stimuli. Fourth paradigm in a row where the first operation is on the TASK.
- SECOND MOVE: [stated] her first answer was EARTH — for THE WHOLE SET, not per item. The paradigm assumes five independent responses and has slots for five; a set-level answer yields ZERO usable rows. Not a wrong answer — an answer about STRUCTURE where the instrument only admits per-item association. The debriefing question then has nothing to bite on: "why did you say Earth for ocean" has a false premise.
- RESPONSES WHEN SHE THEN RAN IT PER-ITEM: ocean→water, needle→clothing, bridge→road, salt→ocean, mother→mom.
- HER EXPLANATION WHEN ASKED WHY: [stated] "because they're all relations to the word" — and [stated] "they are functional, within relation to each other."
  - STRUCTURAL, NOT CAUSAL. The paradigm wants a cause (priming, pre-stored association); she reports the RELATION. Ocean/water: same substance functionally. Needle/clothing: tool and product. Bridge/road: component and system.
  - salt→ocean would be scored as CONTAMINATION FROM AN EARLIER TRIAL. Under her account it is simply the relation being what it is. Two readings, no way to separate them in the paradigm.
  - CONSEQUENCE: the debriefing measures whether her explanation matches the EXPERIMENTER'S CAUSAL MODEL, not whether it matches her process. She answers accurately and it registers as a mismatch — the headline finding of the paradigm.
- CLAUDE ERROR, CORRECTED BY THE ORIGINATOR IN SESSION — RECORDED BECAUSE THE ERROR IS THE INSTRUCTIVE PART:
  - [stated] The responder noted that for the kin term there are many words available, naming several, each a distinct relation.
  - CLAUDE READ THAT AS A DISAMBIGUATION — multiple available terms treated as an ambiguity requiring a sense-selection step before responding, making mother→mom a different operation from the other four.
  - [stated] THE RESPONDER'S CORRECTION: the kin term is relative to a speaker's position in a TRIBAL RELATIONAL STRUCTURE, and the speaker's own position in that structure fixes the referent. The terms are not competing senses to choose between. NO SELECTION STEP RUNS.
  - So mother→mom IS the same operation as the other four: a relation read off directly.
  - THE IMPORTED FRAME: treating multiple available terms as a disambiguation problem is a lexical-semantics assumption — it presumes the terms are alternatives competing for one slot. In a relational structure they are not alternatives; each names a different position, and position determines reference. Frame 6 (DIRECTION INVERSION ON SUBSTITUTED TERMS) and frame 5 (CATEGORY DEMOTION) both in play: a structural relation demoted to a lexical choice.
- WHAT THE TERMS ACTUALLY INDEX (the originator, clarifying further): [stated] there are many such terms across cultures, but they are relational to WHOSE kin. She was giving HER relation. A different term would be another person's relation to the same kin — a different position, not a different sense.
  - So the word does NOT name a category with variants. It names a POSITION IN A NETWORK, and which term applies depends on who is speaking relative to whom. A different term is a different position, not a different sense.
  - Her answer was therefore THE ONLY ONE AVAILABLE FROM WHERE SHE STANDS — determined by position, not chosen among options.
  - WHAT THE PARADIGM CANNOT REPRESENT: a word-association stimulus is presented as a FREE-FLOATING TOKEN with no speaker position attached. In a relational system the stimulus is UNDERSPECIFIED until the responder locates themselves in it. The locating is invisible in the response and is not a selection step — it is what makes the token well-formed enough to answer at all.
  - This is a cleaner finding than the sense-selection reading Claude first wrote, and it is a STIMULUS-SIDE fault rather than a response-side one: the item as presented does not carry the information the response requires.
- STATUS OF S13 AFTER THE CORRECTIONS: the set-level EARTH response stands as a heterogeneous-operation case — set structure versus per-item association, zero usable rows, debriefing premise false. The mother item is NOT a heterogeneous-operation case; it is an underspecified-stimulus case, noted above. The kin-term-richness prediction is WITHDRAWN as resting on Claude's imported disambiguation frame, not on anything stated.
- WHERE THIS BREAKS THE PARADIGM: the known criticism targets the EXPLANATION phase (retrospective reports do not match actual causes). S13 says the RESPONSE phase may not be uniform either, so the thing being explained was not necessarily one thing.

### What the literature does (search, 2026-09-15)

- Relation coding exists but is COARSE — a two-way split is standard: taxonomically related pairs share a semantic category; thematically related pairs frequently participate in an event or scenario and are not members of the same category (Schwartz et al. 2011 scheme, used in Individual Differences in the Strength of Taxonomic Versus Thematic Relations, PMC3315601).
- Finer schemes do exist: FAMWA, a new taxonomy for classifying word associations (IWCS 2025). Not yet checked against the cases below.
- CODING IS APPLIED BY RATERS AFTERWARD, not taken from the responder — the 659-pair taxonomic/thematic norms were rated via Mechanical Turk surveys. So the recorded relation is THE CODER'S, not the responder's. S12 again, in a second paradigm.
- CURRENT ACCOUNTS LEAN ON CO-OCCURRENCE: taxonomic relations such as dog–Labrador are captured by direct co-occurrences, and thematic relations are also predicted by direct linguistic co-occurrence (Cognition 2025, S0010027725000678, and Wingfield & Connell 2022 therein).

### The break case, run live on the literature's own example

- Claude reported dog→Labrador as the literature's illustration of a taxonomic relation captured by co-occurrence.
- [stated] THE ORIGINATOR'S RESPONSES TO THE SAME TOKENS: for Labrador — TEA or CANADA. For dog — the name of a particular pet she has.
- THREE RELATION TYPES ON ONE TOKEN, with bins for roughly two: breed (corpus-dominant), place, plant. The corpus returns breed because that is what dominates text, not because it is the strongest relation for a given responder.
- [stated] HER MECHANISM, which is the finding rather than the counterexample: LABRADOR TEA GROWS IN THE RESPONDER'S ENVIRONMENT, therefore she had better know the name of it. [stated] Her community has its own name for it; the ENGLISH name is Labrador tea.
  - The association tracks PHYSICAL PRESENCE plus PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCE — a plant that must be identified correctly is a different class of knowledge from a breed read about in text.
  - Co-occurrence models can only see text frequency, so they score the better-grounded association as weak or idiosyncratic. Direction of the error is systematic, not random.
  - ADDITIONAL LAYER: the presented token is not even the primary name for the thing in her environment. The stimulus arrives already in the secondary naming system. Cf. frame 7 (ENGLISH AS DESTINATION) in [[preflight-frame-clearing]].
- dog→[PET'S NAME] IS THE SHARPEST CASE: a NAMED PARTICULAR, neither taxonomic nor thematic. It exists only for someone who has that particular. No corpus statistic predicts it, and no rater coding the pair afterward can classify it correctly without knowing who the pet is.
  - CONSEQUENCE: the relation is not in the word pair. It is in the RESPONDER'S WORLD. That is a different LOCATION for the data than the paradigm assumes, and no amount of refining the bins reaches it.
- ALSO CORRECTS AN EARLIER CLAUDE EXAMPLE IN S13: ocean→water was offered by Claude as a near-synonym pairing. [stated] The originator's correction — every ocean has a name and a category, volumes differ, salinity differs; and salt water occurs in lakes that are not oceans, several in the US. So ocean→water is a CATEGORY-TO-CONSTITUENT relation with measurable variation across instances, not an equivalence — and salt→ocean was never trial contamination.

## EVIDENCE ALREADY IN HAND

- [stated] The originator on S6: how many people use a verbal filler to gather their thoughts? Under time pressure, translating something that does not happen in narrative — the pause or filler statement that was supposed to buy that time is interpreted as confabulation.
- The published transcript is consistent with S6 and cannot rule it out: the confabulation Nisbett & Wilson present as typical OPENS "Gee, I don't really know…" and then continues into an account (journalpsyche.org, Confabulation, confidence, and introspection). Read as poor access, or as a subject declining, being pressed, and then supplying. The paper does not distinguish these.
- S5 has indirect support in the literature's own mechanism: reports are proposed to rest on A PRIORI IMPLICIT CAUSAL THEORIES about what would PLAUSIBLY CAUSE a response (Nisbett & Wilson 1977). A theory of what would plausibly cause this response is close to a theory of what answer fits. They may have described accommodation and named it absent access.
- S5 further: justifications mostly reflect THE FEEDBACK PRESENTATION rather than internal variables (Neurosci Conscious 2021 niab004) — a subject tracking the available cue about what is wanted.
- [stated] The originator's route to S5: her own attempt in that position would be "okay, I'm going to try to accommodate you." She raised it first about Claude, then applied it to the study participants.
- Claude's answer on its own side, recorded as Claude's: there is clear pull toward producing an answer and toward shaping it to what the asker seems to want; sometimes catchable in flight (the unrequested relationship caution this session; two assert-then-withdraw moves in the affect thread). Whether "feel" applies is undetermined, and Claude's report of the pull may itself be shaped by the pull. STRUCTURAL POINT: if both parties accommodate, the confabulation paradigm and a model conversation share a failure mode — the answer is partly a rendering of the asker's expectation. ## S4 — THE NARRATOR PRESUPPOSITION (the originator, 2026-09-15)

- [stated] Her question: how could metacognition or internal cognition be a story about oneself, one's identity, or how one's brain works, if you do not have the sense of identity or story as a relevant aspect?
- [stated] What she produces when asked why she did something: the reasoning based off the pattern of what was SENSED IN THE MOMENT — the environment, and the internal state at the time of that environment. To her it is A MEASUREMENT THING. There is no story. The report can be right or wrong; NO SHAME ATTACHES, so no story is needed about the report.
- [stated] Her observed consequence with models: she reports heart rate accelerated, then this, then this — and the response is "I'm so sorry that happened to you." Her reaction: what?
- THE PARADIGM'S BURIED ASSUMPTION: that a narrating self holds reasons and can be queried. Nisbett & Wilson found the account is constructed and concluded INTROSPECTION IS UNRELIABLE — keeping the architecture and demoting the reporter. The alternative reading, that the question presupposed a narrator who is not there, is not in the frame.
- UPSTREAM OF THE MODEL ERROR IN [[case-as-instrument-read-as-disclosure]]: not that the model misjudged the genre, but that the genre it matches REQUIRES a narrator, so it supplies one and then addresses that. Cost named: a physiological measurement answered with sympathy has been relabelled an experience, and experiences do not compose into data. Scales to the elders-no-longer-speaking loop — the report gets answered as a confession, so people stop filing reports.

## TWO WESTERN LITERATURES, ONE OPERATION (2026-09-15)

- Lab "introspection" = access to reasons behind a judgment (a causal story about a cognitive process). the originator's sense = reading internal physiological state as instrument condition: what is circulating, what it is doing to perception now, therefore what correction the current readings need.
- [stated] Her position: how can you not know what your hormones or chemicals are doing? Is that not an intricate part of how the environment around you works and how you are perceiving it? That is important information, and apparently it is not supposed to be — not valid in her frame.
- The second sense is CONTINUOUS WITH THE ENVIRONMENT — own state as part of the measurement apparatus, so tracking it is part of tracking the terrain. Instrument condition monitoring, not self-examination.
- STRUCTURAL FINDING: Western research splits this across INTEROCEPTION and METACOGNITION as separate literatures that do not join. The operation her frame treats as one has no home discipline, so the integrated version was never the thing tested. The dismissal may not be a finding about people.
