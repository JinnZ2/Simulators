## THE CASCADE MECHANISM UNDER THE DETECTOR — four separate-literature results, never assembled (originator, 2026-10-05; web-sourced OBSERVED). THE ASSEMBLY IS THE DELIVERABLE.

1. INTERDEPENDENCE CHANGES THE KIND OF COLLAPSE (Havlin/Buldyrev/Parshani line; Nature 2010, PNAS 2016 "Universal behavior of cascading failures in interdependent networks", arXiv 1211.2330). ISOLATED network loses nodes → degrades GRACEFULLY, second-order (continuous) transition. COUPLED/INTERDEPENDENT networks → ABRUPT, first-order transition. The coupling itself converts gentle degradation into a sudden catastrophic snap. Mechanism: failure in one net triggers failure in the coupled net, which feeds back — recursive cascade bouncing between them. DIRECT MAP: this is the originator's AI↔finance↔governance fragility loop ([[when-to-decouple-triage-and-fail-safe]]) with the math on it — coupling is what turns "decline" into "collapse." (1211.2330: a hidden second-order transition with long branching trees near criticality controls the cascade and explains the power-law distribution of blackout sizes.)
2. NO INTERMITTENT STATES OF HEALTH (Nature Sci. Rep. 2020, cascade mitigation). When these networks collapse, they collapse COMPLETELY — stated flat as "lack of intermittent states of network health." No halfway. Holds, holds, holds, then goes all at once. MAP: exactly the originator's slow-latency-then-sudden-bill / efficiency-trim-looks-free-until-it-isn't ([[blind-spots-not-enumerable-from-inside]], efficiency=resilience section). The warning is NOT in the system's STATE (which looks fine till the snap) — it's only in the TIGHTENING, i.e. the Gini-singularity/Kolkata-crossing from the first search. The two results interlock: state gives no warning, concentration does.
3. DIVERSITY = UNCORRELATED NEIGHBORS = CASCADE RESISTANCE (Cornell, Blume/Kleinberg/Easley/Kleinberg, FOCS 2011, "Which Networks Are Least Susceptible to Cascading Failures?"). Key condition: diversity among a node's connections so shocks stay UNCORRELATED. Correlated neighbors (monoculture) fail TOGETHER — one shock hits everything at once, no independent path to absorb it. Diverse/independent neighbors fail independently; the system routes around the failure. MAP: this is the originator's angular-spread / redundant-load-path / contrast-set requirement PROVEN FROM THE COLLAPSE SIDE and as the formal condition for cascade resistance. Homogenization = correlating all nodes = deleting the exact structure that stops the cascade. (Blume et al. also show complete-graph vs tree can each be more resilient depending on the threshold distribution — i.e. the right structure is regime-dependent, but uncorrelated shocks is the through-line.)
4. SCALE-FREE / HETEROGENEITY vs TARGETED ATTACK (Cohen/Havlin; robustness-of-complex-networks line). Heterogeneous (scale-free) nets are robust to RANDOM failure but fragile to TARGETED removal of the high-degree hubs. MAP: a funnel concentrates connectivity into few hubs (the exempt deciders, [[calhoun-exemption-not-crowding]]); that makes the system look robust to noise while becoming maximally fragile to a hit on the concentration point — the single-point-of-failure read, formalized.
