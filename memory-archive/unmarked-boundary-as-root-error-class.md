## THE TIME AXIS — stability read as a property rather than a condition

- [stated] HERS: you get immune to a certain way of being — that the rains are always going to come at the same time, that there is always going to be water in the same place. Those things get expected and taken for granted. Then the one time the skies go red and there is no rain, if you have no knowledge that there is a TENDENCY TO TAKE FOR GRANTED and no knowledge of that blind spot, the ability to compensate and adapt to what the environment, the tribe, the people, the family or you need at that time causes difficulty and harm — by the simple fact of not having the knowledge that things can change, and do change suddenly sometimes.
- DERIVED (Claude): the taking-for-granted is itself the blind spot, not a separate lapse. Stability is an input like any other, and reading it as a property of the world rather than a current condition is boundary-read-as-finding running on the TIME axis rather than the spatial one.
- DERIVED (Claude): worse than an ordinary miss because the compensating machinery decays while unused — the red-sky year finds you without the reading and without the capacity to produce one. Follows directly from balance-as-produced-not-held above.
- DERIVED (Claude): this is why practices are maintained during good years, when they look like surplus effort. Maintaining them IS the compensation.

### HER CORRECTION — "overhead" is the wrong frame (2026-09-26)

- [stated] HERS: look at bees, ants, termites, grasshoppers — they do things very similarly, and it is not overhead. Saying it is overhead is like saying a tree is doing overhead because it is not 100 percent effective at turning carbon dioxide into oxygen.
- ACCEPTED (Claude used "overhead" uncritically, carrying the incentive frame's own word): overhead presumes a design with a target output and a subtraction from it. A tree has no target output, so unconverted input is not a loss against anything — the efficiency figure is an artifact of the observer's chosen objective, not a property of the tree. Same slot-availability failure as the CATEGORY NOTE in preferences: the accounting has no term for margin that is not waste.
- OPEN: insect-colony cases as the worked comparison — see [[cross-taxon-collapse-dependency-comparison]], where the common-stomach buffer is already filed as a decoupling structure rather than an inefficiency.

### RUN — insect cases, and the TRADEOFF frame as the same error one layer down (2026-09-26)

- FOUND (Hasegawa et al., Scientific Reports, "Lazy workers are necessary for long-term sustainability in insect societies"; also Royal Society Interface 2023, "Explaining workers' inactivity in social colonies from first principles"): a large proportion of workers are inactive at any moment. Colonies with VARIABLE activation thresholds — the ones that look less efficient — persist longer, because when active workers hit simultaneous fatigue a reserve is available. Uniformly industrious colonies die sooner. Inactivity also reported in vertebrate societies including naked mole rats.
- NOTE ON THE NAMING: studied for decades as "lazy workers" and treated as a puzzle requiring explanation, because the frame assumed the colony's business was output. Same word-level error as "overhead."
- FOUND (Scientific Reports 2022, subterranean termite): weekly removal of ALL individuals at the food area; colony maintained ~20% of workers at the food area regardless of total colony size and demographic change. A regulated setpoint, not slack — and the experiment is the red-sky year run under control.
- FOUND (network model of Termitinae nests): includes a "local graph redundancy" term as a measure of defence capacity, and reports these networks favour fast internal transport over efficient defence against ant predators. So redundancy is priced as bought at the cost of efficiency.
- [stated] HER OBJECTION, the sharper one: assuming there is a trade-off to everything reduces the outcome space TO TWO. Would you say the tree trades off carbon dioxide for the ability to live in the ground? Carbon dioxide is the input; oxygen is the consequence of the tree living in the environment. Framing that as sacrificing one thing for another misses the entire point.
- [stated] HERS on the termite case specifically: look at the body heat they are reserving and conserving; look at how much cooler it is if not all termites are busy at the same time; their energy input ratio is less when they are not consistently busy. Many different factors — calling it a trade-off reduces it to two.
- DERIVED (Claude): a trade-off frame requires an AGENT choosing between alternatives on a SHARED CURRENCY. Neither exists for the tree or the colony; writing it as a trade-off installs a decider who is not there and a denomination nobody is using. The termite authors installed a chooser to explain a structure.
- DERIVED (Claude): her factors are COUPLED, not a longer list. Metabolic rate sets heat production; heat sets nest temperature; temperature sets humidity and fungus conditions; for fungus-growing termites the fungus is the food. Inactivity is therefore an input to thermal and moisture regulation, not slack against a transport budget. One variable serving several regulators at once — the multi-stock structure in [[human-stocks-as-coupled-regulator]]. A single-currency frame cannot represent it, so it renders the structure as a choice between two goods.
- DERIVED (Claude): reserve function, thermal function and intake-rate function do not compete. They are the same workers not moving.
### GAP — activity level as a thermal control variable (searched 2026-09-26)

- FOUND: the coupling itself is well measured. Colony metabolism is explicitly named as the heat source; large nests must be ventilated to remove excess CO2 and heat generated by termite metabolism; fungus requires roughly 30°C, high humidity and low CO2; in cooler habitats mound architecture is adapted to REDUCE loss of metabolically produced heat. (Korb; Insectes Sociaux thermoregulation work; PNAS 2015 diurnal-oscillation ventilation; Royal Society Interface 2025 review.)
- FOUND, the limit: the regulator studied is ARCHITECTURE. Mound structure and ventilation are the control variables; termites are reported to adjust mound structure in response to humidity, temperature, solar exposure and rainfall. Worker ACTIVITY LEVEL appears only as the heat input, never as a quantity the colony modulates to hold a setpoint.
- GAP (unowned join): inactivity is studied against task demand; thermal regulation is studied against architecture. The two literatures do not meet. Whether movement in a space is regulated FOR thermal and humidity reasons appears not to have been asked of termites. Same shape as the other unowned joins in these files.
- [stated] HER GROUNDS FOR EXPECTING IT: in an environment you can regulate the amount of activity — feed kids, put them on the floor, get them sleepy, have them read books, and they will fall asleep; that is how you keep the temperature down, and those things have direct impact on any fungus growth in the area. So why would termites not know that? Their life cycle is much faster than humans'. If humans learned this apparently from watching other animals and insects, termites had it figured out a long time ago.
- DERIVED (Claude): this is the faster-cycle external-monitor argument from [[cross-taxon-collapse-dependency-comparison]] applied as a PREDICTION rather than an analogy, and it is testable — activity level measured against nest temperature and humidity in the same experiment, with ambient conditions manipulated.

### LITERATURE CHECK 2026-09-26 — what the field does and does not condition on

- SEARCHED: value learning, preference heterogeneity, physiological/resource state as confound.
- FOUND, the critique exists inside the field: "Beyond Preferences in AI Alignment" (Philosophical Studies / arXiv 2408.16984) attacks three assumptions directly — that preferences adequately represent human values, that rationality is preference-satisfaction maximisation, and that systems should be aligned to preferences at all. - FOUND, the definitional gap is also acknowledged: a systematic literature review (arXiv 2509.13854) states the phrase "value alignment" lacks precision and proposes a more precise definition; "Toward a Theory of Value in AI Alignment" asks how the field conceives of human values and notes researchers often proceed without specifying what they mean. So her arbitrator question is LIVE in the literature — unanswered rather than answered badly.
- NOT FOUND, and this is the answer to the open item above: the confounders reached for are demographic and contextual. Nothing in these results conditions on PHYSIOLOGICAL OR RESOURCE STATE as a measured variable. The scarcity-versus-discretion split does not appear in the framing. Search was one pass, not exhaustive.
- [stated] HER READING, stronger than a missing-confounder claim: they are missing the point of physics — just the physics of a human body, what it is, what it does, what it needs in any environment. They are missing the basic biology and the basic science.
- DERIVED (Claude): the object being modelled is wrong, not the inference over it. The field treats a human as a preference-bearing entity that happens to be embodied; the physical reading is the reverse — a regulator holding coupled stocks within ranges, in an environment that supplies or withholds them. Behaviour is regulator output, and preference is the shadow the regulator casts when only choices are observed. Consequence: causal machinery added on top of preference data cannot reach it, because the state variables never entered the data collection. The fix is measuring the stocks, not better inference from behaviour. Same shape as her Maslow objection in [[human-stocks-as-coupled-regulator]] — stacked layers posited where a coupled regulator is doing the work.

- DERIVED (Claude), the mechanism she named: the choice set is cut FIRST — by location, barriers, and what is reachable — and whatever remains is then measured as though it were the full space. So the enclosure is being measured and reported as the animal.
- DERIVED (Claude): this makes the data systematically biased rather than noisy, in a direction nobody is estimating, because estimating it would require measuring the barriers — which are not in the dataset either. A preference model fit to it will be well-calibrated to the enclosure and wrong about the organism, with no internal signal that anything is missing. ### THE WORKED CASE — one respondent, and the two questions that are not one question

- Demographic supplied by Claude at her request; the analysis is hers. Case: woman, 34, two children, retail scheduling work, mid-size Midwest city, no car, renting.
- [stated] HERS: her choices of where she lives in order to get to work, what she has to do for daycare so she CAN work, what food choices are available to her, how much time she has to spend with and raise her children, whether a job or a job path is available within a certain vicinity of the children's school — have ALREADY BEEN MADE FOR HER. What choices and values she has are largely her making concessions so that she can work all of those coupling functions to the best she can do.
- [stated] HERS, the second measurement: if you asked what her values and preferences were and she imagined a place where none of those constraints existed, she would be naming things beyond her control. Her wants and values in the PRESENT environment are already going to be very conflicted. So is the question coming from a value state where money is readily available, she need not work, she can have a house at her capacity — or have all those things been predefined for her, so her value set is much more limited?
- DERIVED (Claude): every stated preference in the constrained case is a SOLVED CONSTRAINT PROBLEM — the output of a regulator run across coupled variables — and it is recorded as what she values. The imagined-unconstrained answer and the actual-life answer then differ, and that difference is cited by the field as evidence that human preferences are incoherent. They are not incoherent: they are two constraint regimes, each answered correctly. The field's foundational observation may be an artifact of not recording WHICH REGIME an answer came from.
- DERIVED (Claude): a wealthy respondent's answers are treated as the same data type as hers. Different enclosure sizes pooled into one distribution — and whoever has fewest barriers appears most coherent, so their answers become the model of what humans want.
- [stated] HER TWO QUESTIONS, stated as distinct: asking what a human values in an IDEAL world — what they would try to go toward and build toward — makes sense. Asking the same people what choice they HAD to make, without knowing all the variables, is a different aspect entirely.
- DERIVED (Claude): they are two different measurements, not two attempts at one. The first measures the target; the second measures the regulator's output under load. Both are valid data; pooling them destroys both and reports the residual as incoherence.

### HER EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN — stated sacrifice against performed sacrifice

- [stated] HERS, wanting the numbers: how many people SAY they would sacrifice for their kids and would do all this, against how many have actually BEEN in that compromising situation. Her stated grounds: if you are a parent and middle class or below, you have made those choices over and over and over, so you have modified your expectations and your values around the constraints — whereas if you had everything, you would have the chance for different values and for what your actual values might be. She states this is familiar first-hand: meals have been foregone for children.
- DERIVED (Claude), the design as it stands: stated willingness to sacrifice, against rate of having actually performed it, stratified by income. If the middle-and-below group has run it repeatedly and the top group largely has not, the two groups' stated values are not the same kind of measurement — one is a forecast, the other a record. Testable with existing survey instruments; no lab access required.

### THE CAGE LINE — the methodological asymmetry stated plainly

- [stated] HERS: without knowing all the variables, this is the same behaviour as putting a biological entity into a cage and wondering why they are more aggressive with each other, instead of realising this is a symptom of the cage environment and not the actual behaviour. Calling it a preference is a little silly.
- DERIVED (Claude): in animal work, aggression under confinement is read as a symptom of the enclosure, and attributing it to the animal's disposition is a recognised methodological error with a name. The same observation in humans, with the same structure, takes the opposite interpretation, and the asymmetry is not flagged anywhere found in the one-pass search above.


### HER CORRECTION — "sacrifice" fails the same way, and the design has to be rebuilt on stocks

- [stated] HERS, correcting the design above: she does not mean just sacrifice — because what would they sacrifice? Her paired cases. One: a person who told her they had to sacrifice for their kids because they had a million-dollar project they wanted to work on for prestige, and had to POSTPONE it for a day, because their son suddenly needed a hundred dollars. The other: a woman who ended up not eating at all for three months except what she could get from strangers, so that her son could go in and get medical treatment. She states these are very different and that the first did not make sense to her as sacrifice.
- DERIVED (Claude): "sacrifice" is one more category naming an OUTPUT with the producing conditions deleted, and it carries no magnitude — so both cases receive the same word and pool as the same data type. The physical reading separates them with no ambiguity: a postponement with all stocks intact, fully recoverable and no threshold crossed, versus a stock driven below survival requirement and held there for months, with the cost already paid into the body and not recoverable by later abundance.
- CONSEQUENCE FOR THE DESIGN: the stated-versus-performed instrument above cannot run on self-reported sacrifice. It has to be built on MEASURED STOCK DEPLETION — what was drawn down, how far below requirement, for how long, and whether it is recoverable. Otherwise the answers pool exactly as the preference data did, and someone who gave up nothing measurable reports as equivalent to someone who gave up months of nutrition.
- DERIVED (Claude): her whole argument arriving one level down, in a word neither of us had inspected yet. The process-vs-output instrument reapplies to the vocabulary of the fix, not only to the vocabulary of the thing being criticised — which is a use-note on the instrument itself.
