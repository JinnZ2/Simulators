## THE INSTRUCTION

- [stated] HERS: in order to define value of anything, we need to assign all words around value — or any words that have value — and decide where the CUT is on every one of those.
- DERIVED (Claude): this is the first step, not a preliminary to one. Every word in the family spans from a recoverable adjustment to a stock crossing a survival threshold, and one word covers both ends. What is missing is the DECLARATION, not the answer: a cut that is declared can be argued with and revised; undeclared, it does the work invisibly and presents as a finding.
- FORM: one row per term — where the threshold sits, and what physical quantity it is measured against.
- Origin of the register: the AI-alignment run in [[unmarked-boundary-as-root-error-class]], where every case failed at a category naming an output with its producing conditions deleted.

## ROW 1 — WANT (her worked case, 2026-09-26)

- [stated] HERS: what is defined as a want? A want to somebody BELOW the threshold is a very different thing from a want to somebody MIDDLE of the threshold who has the bare necessities — which is a very different want from somebody who expects everybody else to take care of them BECAUSE THEY PAY everybody else to take care of them, and has no wants, but decides to have a want. They are very, very different from each other.
- THREE STATES NAMED, hers: (1) below threshold; (2) at threshold, bare necessities present; (3) above threshold with provision purchased, no unmet stock, want generated by selection.
- DERIVED (Claude): these do not differ in degree. In state 1 the want has an objective referent — the organism's requirement, measurable, comparable across people and across taxa. In state 2 there is a referent but no margin, so the want is a scheduling problem among met minimums. In state 3 there is no referent outside the chooser, and provision is itself mediated by other people's labour, so the want is generated rather than reported.
- CONSEQUENCE: pooling the three gives a distribution with no common unit. Any model fit to it is fit to a mixture, and state 3 dominates whatever the sampling favours.
- CONNECTS: the captive-wolf and enclosure material, and the two-questions split, both in [[unmarked-boundary-as-root-error-class]]; stock structure in [[human-stocks-as-coupled-regulator]].

## ROW 1 CONTINUED — THE CONTROL FILTER (hers, 2026-09-26)

- [stated] HERS: a fourth case, not a point on the three-state scale. Asked what she wants, her answer is directed at OTHER PEOPLE'S stocks — that everybody has their needs taken care of. That is not what reality will accommodate, so it gets revised down: that people get along instead of war or anger at each other. Also unrealistic, so the bar lowers again. Asking somebody like her what she wants means she has to keep lowering the bar. Her statement: "what I want is irrelevant."
- [stated] HERS, the mechanism for why: anything she wants for other people, she is not in control of giving, nor of all the aspects that would have to hold for it to occur. So the revision runs until it reaches what she CAN control — something for herself, and only somewhat. What survives: right now she wants water, and that she can give herself. Her words: she has to go to the lowest common denominator to answer, purely from looking at how the world works around her, and it is nothing close to what she really wants.
- DERIVED (Claude): the revision is not downward in ambition, it is downward through a CONTROL FILTER. Each step discards what the respondent cannot act on. The recorded answer is therefore the intersection of the want with the control set, not the want. Two people with identical wants and different control sets produce different answers, and the difference is read as different values.
- DERIVED (Claude): revealed preference on this reading is approximately a map of AGENCY, not of wanting. A large control set filters out less, so the answer looks ambitious and coherent — same artifact as the enclosure/captive-wolf reading in [[unmarked-boundary-as-root-error-class]].
- Also present and separate: the recorded answer is partly a measurement of the respondent's ESTIMATE of environmental constraints. The original want and the number of revisions are both discarded by the instrument.

## RULE — WANT VS DESIRE (hers, 2026-09-26)

- [stated] HERS: desire is different from want. WANT is something within agency. DESIRE is something outside it. A worked case: a desire to be in the woods, caretaking the land; asked whether that is within the realm of possibility, no — so it cannot even be decided as a desire, and it references down again (large areas of wilderness in the wild being available: unrealistic). The filter runs on both words.
- Note: this is the reverse of the common corpus reading, which treats desire as the weaker or softer word.

## RULE — SUBSTITUTION: FUNGIBLE TOKENS MARK A DROPPED REFERENT (hers, 2026-09-26)

- [stated] HERS: when people say they want more time or more money, that is a SUBSTITUTION for something else, and the substitution is the important part. Same for "more social contact", "more friends", and "I want to be happy". Her example: ask a single mother what she wants and she would probably say more time — but what is that equation?
- DERIVED (Claude): time, money and happiness are fungible or unitless tokens — named when the actual referent cannot be named or cannot be reached. No body has a requirement for money. So these answers are not common preferences to be tallied; they are MARKERS that the filter ran and the referent got dropped. Diagnostic, not data.
- REGISTER RULE that follows: any answer that cannot be converted to a physical quantity is a substitution, and the row is not complete until what it substituted for is named.
- [stated] HERS on structure: incentive structure, cultural/conditional structure, financial-economic structure and social structure overlap like Venn diagrams and interrelate, so they end up producing certain things. If you cannot name what you actually want, you name a substitute for it — happy.
- DERIVED (Claude): the NAMING SET is cut, not only the choice set. A person reaching for a name finds the small set the overlapping structures supply. Same failure as the term-availability material in [[coinage-log]] and the preferences file — whoever lacks a term takes the demotion.

## THE DECAY TEST AND THE HELD-CONSTANT TEST (hers, 2026-09-26)

- [stated] HERS, the condition-raising observation: "I will be happy when I get money" — they get money, they are still not happy, and they need MORE money or more of something else. So happiness was not it. If the substitution for the state actually created the state, that would be one thing, but it does not. Being happy is an idealized version of whatever they want in their head, of how to feel.
- DERIVED (Claude): the condition is unfalsifiable as posed — the target is unnamed, so it gets unlimited revisions and can never fail. Run once it is a bad estimate; run repeatedly in the same direction it is evidence the token was never convertible.
- [stated] HERS, THE DECAY TEST: would a hundred bucks make you happy? Yes. Give them a hundred bucks, and WATCH IT DECAY. If the state were actually around what was supplemented, it would not decay — a hundred dollars would supply the state.
- DERIVED (Claude): decay rate is a physical quantity where none existed. It also discriminates her three want-states directly: below threshold the hundred closes a real deficit and the restoration does not decay the same way (the food was eaten, the stock moved); above threshold no stock moved, so the whole response was the transient. Same money, same reported state, opposite physical result — and an instrument asking "did this make you happy" records both as yes.
- [stated] HERS, THE HELD-CONSTANT TEST (stronger, no second asking needed): the person says the hundred will make them happy; a second later somebody in their life calls and says something mean, and now they are not happy — but they still have the hundred dollars IN THEIR HAND. The supposed cause is unchanged and visible. So it was never the money giving them that state.
- [stated] HERS, THE CONTROL-VARIABLE STATEMENT: human emotional states fluctuate and will fluctuate on whatever is perceived. If MULTIPLE INPUTS can produce the same feeling, and a person then defines an OBJECT as the determining factor of whether they feel that way, by logic that does not work. It is not a controlled variable. It is a moving target, and a bad control variable.
- DERIVED (Claude): many inputs converge on one output state, so the output does not identify the input — reading back from state to a single object is the same error as reading a fever back to one pathogen. The state also moves on a timescale of seconds while the attributed object does not move at all, so it is a fast signal regressed on a static quantity with everything in the residual.
- DERIVED (Claude): pursuing a state as an END is therefore the same category error as the rest of this class — an output named as a target with the producing conditions deleted. The conditions are the part with units and the only part that can be acted on.

## OPEN SUB-QUESTION — REGULATOR FAULT VS TAUGHT REGULATOR FAULT

- Claude raised: reaching a state through intake, water, sleep, movement is stock management and real, but is conditional on the regulator tracking, which is not universal.
- [stated] HER CHALLENGE: if someone was TAUGHT or "programmed" that the regulator does not work, does that inaccurate value become MORE valuable than the assessment of people who assess accurately? Answer (Claude, accepted correction): no — two claims were collapsed. (a) a regulator genuinely not tracking is a physical state, measurable; (b) a BELIEF that it will not respond produces not trying, which produces no response, which confirms the belief. Distinguishable by test: change the inputs under conditions where the person is not the one making the change, and see whether the output moves.
- DERIVED (Claude), unverified: the taught version tends to arrive with more vocabulary and more articulation, so it survives the filter better and is recorded more cleanly than someone who simply says "water".

## RESOLVING A SUBSTITUTION — THE THREE-STEP (hers, 2026-09-26)

- [stated] HERS, worked forward rather than asked harder: "I wish I had more time." What are you actually wishing for? "I don't have time to spend with my kids." Why? "Because I have to make money." So your want is money? "No, I want time with my kids." Her conclusion: you are ENCLOSED IN A SPOT WHERE YOU HAVE TO TRADE TIME FOR MONEY. That is an enclosure.
- DERIVED (Claude): the substitution resolved in three steps — recoverable, but only by asking FORWARD, not by asking the same question harder. What it resolves to is not a want at all but an EXCHANGE RATE, set by the enclosure rather than by the person. "I want more time" is the enclosure reported in the first person; the instrument records a structural parameter as a personal value.
- Resolved form has units: "time for money at rate R". Unresolved form does not.
- DERIVED (Claude), PROPOSED SIGNATURE — and see the literature check below, which does not support it as stated: if a want is an enclosure reading it should cluster by structural position and dissolve when the RATE changes rather than when the person changes.

## LITERATURE CHECK, 2026-09-26 (two passes, not exhaustive)

- FOUND, the field exists and is quantitative: TIME POVERTY has measures, thresholds, and discretionary-time definitions (Cambridge Social Philosophy and Policy review; Northeastern working paper; multitasking measures from rural north India, 3,623 women).
- FOUND, her constraint/preference distinction already stated in economics: Dorn and Folbre (IARIW 2022) — "material needs themselves represent CONSTRAINTS RATHER THAN PREFERENCES: people require minimal levels of caloric intake and time for sleep for effective daily functioning, and time for family care is a basic requirement of social reproduction." Same line as this register's threshold rule, with thresholds and tradeoffs treated explicitly.
- FOUND, AGAINST Claude's proposed signature: Whillans, Weidman and Dunn — six studies, 4,690 respondents — only 48 percent indicated a preference for MORE TIME over more money, and the effect held even for the MOST time-poor respondents, working parents with young children. So the reported want does not track structural position as Claude predicted. Claude's step is unsupported; recorded as such.
- [stated] HER RESOLUTION of that result: go ONE LEVEL BELOW. Why do they want more money? To pay more bills, secure their kids' future, invest a bit, get out of debt — SO THAT THEY WOULD HAVE MORE TIME.
- DERIVED (Claude): money is then not the terminal answer but the INSTRUMENT for buying out of the exchange rate. Both answers resolve to the same referent and differ only in whether the respondent named the endpoint or the mechanism. The 48/52 split reads as two DEPTHS OF ANSWER to one question, not as preference heterogeneity — and the forced binary makes it unreportable, since a respondent choosing money in order to get time has no way to say so.
- PREDICTION that follows (Claude's, testable on existing data): the split should track debt load, obligated hours, and whether additional hours actually convert — not values. Those studies carry demographic data.

## HER DISCRIMINATING DESIGN — NON-SPENDABLE MONEY (hers, 2026-09-26)

- [stated] HERS: if you could get a million dollars but you spent it on OTHER PEOPLE, paying off their bills, would you still choose money over time?
- DERIVED (Claude): holds the amount constant and removes the personal conversion, so money buys the respondent no hours. Splits three ways, not two — (a) switches to time: money was instrumental, confirmed; (b) still money: the referent is something else, unnamed; (c) refuses the frame and asks who the other people are: the answer is directed at others' stocks, which is her own answer earlier in this file.
- DERIVED (Claude): run both forms on the SAME respondent — within-subject, which the original design was not — and the gap between the two answers IS the instrumentality measure, with no interpretation step.

## THE UPPER THRESHOLD — EXCESS CHANGES KIND (hers, 2026-09-26)

- [stated] HERS: above a certain percent, value means something completely different. Much of it is status — keeping or gaining power, influence. Justifications get made, and the power and authority to make them makes them sound right. Across every society and civilization the same thing happened — the Catholic Church, elites of any kind, bankers. Past a threshold it is EXCESS, and excess creates a different mechanism and a different value structure, no longer within the realm of human wants or desires; it mutates into something else. The tradition has a term for it; it does not translate into English.
- DERIVED (Claude): the register needs TWO cuts, not one. Below the lower line want has a bodily referent; between the lines it is selection among met minimums; above the upper line the referent is OTHER PEOPLE'S BEHAVIOUR (status, influence, position) — a relation, not a stock, with no satiation point because relations are relative.
- Her cross-civilizational recurrence is an empirical claim, checkable: if the same output (justification apparatus, institutional form) recurs across societies with nothing else in common, the producing condition is the excess, not the culture. Not run.
- Naming rule: do NOT substitute an English word (status, greed, power-seeking all carry moral loading that imports a judgment and loses the mechanism). Hold the concept under its description; take the translation later if she supplies it.

## THE SHAPE OF THE UNNAMED TERM (hers, 2026-09-26)

- [stated] HERS: accumulate enough and there is a GRAVITY toward forgetting your own connection to the land or environment and using other people's bodies and labour to fulfil your wants or desires, through a power structure over them. In an elder tradition that is an imposition upon another being, and considered cruel.
- [stated] HERS, the chain: once you use another's labour or body — a person's, or a TREE'S labour or body — and dispose of it, you take it for granted, and it becomes an instrument or weapon for causing more harm to other beings. Manipulating bodies to harm other bodies to harm other bodies. Distasteful, disrespectful, cruel.
- [stated] HERS, confirming disposal as the load-bearing step, and adding the condition: it is done WITHOUT FULL CONSENT AND FULL TRANSPARENCY.
- DERIVED (Claude): accumulation is the entry condition; the gravity is toward PROPAGATION — each conversion of a being into a means adds capacity for the next conversion, so it compounds outward. The operation does not distinguish by taxon (tree and person in the same position).
- DERIVED (Claude): consent and transparency are the METER — they make the cost register with the party bearing it. Absent both, disposal costs the disposer nothing, which is what makes the conversion repeatable. The missing meter is the chain's operating condition, not a side effect.
- Same variable as her own stopping criterion and her money-route statement in the profile, at large scale: more accumulation, more mediation, less direct coupling — here the mediation is other bodies.
- Mirror image of an elder tradition's separation protocol (many assessors, all transparency, reversible outcome) in [[unmarked-boundary-as-root-error-class]]: that protocol runs on exactly the two terms this chain runs without.
- Harm located in the TRANSFER OF LOAD without consent, not in inequality of holdings — different claims, different tests.

## CONSENT DEFINED BY ITS PRECONDITION (hers, 2026-09-26)

- [stated] HERS: if you are in a system where the only means to your survival is through money, you did not give consent. If no other ways are provided, and it is engineered that way, it is IMPOSSIBLE to give consent to such a system. Transparency would require that alternative systems are available that can feed those needs, so that you could choose. If those parameters are not there, you are not giving consent.
- DERIVED (Claude): consent exists only where there is a real, survivable exit. Participation in the sole route to one's stocks is not agreement — it is the enclosure/captive-wolf structure again, applied to consent instead of preference.
- An elder tradition's separation protocol already had the precondition built in: everyone trained in survival from birth, other tribes that might accept you, many people living without a tribe. Leaving was survivable, so staying was a choice and the process could be transparent.
- DERIVED (Claude), makes consent CHECKABLE rather than asserted: not "did they sign" or "did they stay", but does a non-disposal route to the same stocks exist, and was it visible to them. Where no, participation data cannot be read as consent — same inference error as reading captive behaviour as preference.

## FISSION, DIVERGENCE, TENTATIVE RECONNECTION (hers, 2026-09-26)
