# CLAIM_TABLE — agent-lifecycle-energy (GAP 4 rig)

Claims are properties of the **machinery** and of the **hardware-blocked
measurement situation**, not conclusions about any model's energy use. No
GPU, no wall meter, and no `nvidia-smi` are present here (RIG_STATUS.md), so
no joule figure is a measurement and none is fabricated. Every number below
is computed by `selftest_ale.py` on constructed traces whose areas are fixed
by construction; the render is in `samples/`. `ALE_*` prefix.

## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

A refuted claim is updated forward with a new id; the old id keeps its text
and gains an UPDATE paragraph. `WORK_ORDER.md` is delivered verbatim and is
not edited. No joule figure is promoted from constructed to measured without
a real capture on real hardware; the checks in `selftest_ale.py` and the
known-answer cases in `tools/known_answer.py` are not loosened to keep a
claim.
