The delivered folder carries its own claims in `CLAIMS.md` (`RC_001..008`,
delivered verbatim and not edited). The ids here are `RCT_`; every number
is computed by `audit.py` on the delivered scripts, imported and never
edited, and pinned by `selftest_rail.py`. The render is in
`samples/audit.sample.txt`. Nothing here is a statement about any real
car, device, transit line or report; the FSRI figures are carried from
the delivered README at its own SOURCE CAVEAT status.

## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

A falsifier that fires updates the claim's status in place with the
evidence that fired it; the checks in `selftest_rail.py` are not
loosened to keep a claim. The delivered files — README, CLAIMS, the
three screens, `run_all.py`, the params file — are not edited; a
repair the author makes lands as a new version beside the original.
