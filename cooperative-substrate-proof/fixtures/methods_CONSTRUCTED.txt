METHODS
Soil cores were collected with a 5 cm split-tube sampler at 10 cm depth. Samples were dried at 105 C for 24 h in a laboratory oven and weighed on an analytical balance calibrated against a 100 g reference mass traceable to NIST. Organic carbon was measured by loss on ignition following Heiri et al. (2001). Elevation was taken from the USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM. Bulk density was calculated using the core volume. Sample identifiers were assigned using the site database maintained by the field station.
RESULTS
Organic carbon declined with elevation across the transect (r = -0.62); cores from the upper sites held half the carbon of the lowest. The decline was steeper on north-facing slopes than on south-facing slopes.
