# CLAIM_TABLE — model-deprecation-backcast

`WORK_ORDER.md` asks for an **instrument specification, not a findings
document, not a critique** — "unmeasured cells are the content." These claims
are properties of the **instrument** (the seven columns, their required
nulls, and the runnable tests), each checked on **constructed** data with a
known answer. No vendor calendar, poll, eval, or dataset is read (all
egress-blocked), and every observed instance in the spec is carried, not
verified. `MDB_*` prefix.

## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

A refuted claim is updated forward with a new id; the old id keeps its text
and gains an UPDATE paragraph. `WORK_ORDER.md` is delivered verbatim and is
not edited. `lag_of_peak` is registered in `tools/known_answer.py` and its
cases are not loosened to keep a claim; each column's null is checked in both
directions so no column is a constant readout. Nothing is promoted from
carried to verified without a fetched source.
