SIM-A  structure factor
==========================================================================
  DENIED at pre(). the sim never runs.

  [G-RES] resolution not declared or instrument coarser than feature: output is not admissible | k-grid vs Bragg peak width: instrument 0.39 x margin 2 > feature 0.063

  The k-grid is 6.2x coarser than the peaks it must resolve.
  At the gate's default 2x margin that is a 12.4x shortfall.
  A run in this configuration cannot see Bragg peaks whether
  or not they are there, so its null carries no information.

  This is the audit's Finding 4 — 'no Bragg star visible in
  the S(k) figure' — arriving before the figure exists, as an
  arithmetic check on two declared numbers.

SIM-B  fractal dimension  (the decisive sim)
==========================================================================
  DENIED at pre().

  [G-RES] resolution not declared or instrument coarser than feature: output is not admissible | estimator artifact floor vs separation: instrument 0.252 x margin 2 > feature 0.334

  Artifact floor 0.252, separation 0.334. The floor alone does
  not swallow the effect — but the gate's default 2x margin
  requires 0.504, and the effect is 0.334.

  Read this as the gate's policy dial rather than a verdict:
  at margin 1.0 the declaration passes, narrowly. That is the
  audit's Finding 2 restated — direction survives, magnitude
  does not — as a margin the operator has to choose out loud
  instead of a precision quietly implied by three decimals.

  Rerunning below at margin 1.0 to reach the post-stage guards.

GATE SIM-B-margin1
  expected : if the classes are distinct, |D_f(AB) - D_f(cascade)| exceeds the estimator's artifact floor
  observed : two estimators shipped. box-count separates by +0.334, sandbox by -0.023 with the sign reversed. the sandbox estimator fails the Line control at 1.913.
  control  : Line, true D_f = 1           run
  control  : matched-N Poisson at 1,024 pts NOT RUN
  resolve  : estimator artifact floor vs separation ok
  claim    : [supported] the quasiperiodic and cascade dimensions do not coincide
  claim    : [unsupported] the two classes do not share a common fractal geometry
  finding  : [G-SUP] claim has no named support
  finding  : [G-CTRL] no controls declared: a confirming result cannot be distinguished from an artifact

  The gate cannot know the sandbox numbers exist unless they are
  recorded, and it cannot stop an author from omitting them. What
  it does force is the shape of the record: the Line control has
  one slot, both results go in it, and the disagreement is in the
  report next to the claim that rests on it. Finding 1 becomes a
  line item rather than something to notice in an unreferenced
  figure.

  G-CTRL flags the matched-N Poisson run as declared-never-run.
  G-SUP marks the geometry claim unsupported: it names nothing.

SIM-C  threshold sweep
==========================================================================

  g.ratio(...) returned None

GATE SIM-C
  expected : if the two correspond, the knee sits at the cascade's own branching fraction
  observed : no sharp threshold found. splitting grows gradually. curvature has comparable peaks at f = 0.45 / 0.55 / 0.65 / 0.70 / 0.80 / 0.90 and the winner lands on a local minimum of the curve it is meant to find the knee of.
  control  : threshold inserted at a known f NOT RUN
  resolve  : f-grid vs knee width         ok
  VOID     : ratio_54x (tight-binding lattice model vs cascade set)
  claim    : [unsupported] the two systems show different threshold behavior
  finding  : [G-DIM] ratio operands are properties of different objects: void, no interpretation permitted
  finding  : [G-SUP] claim has no named support
  finding  : [G-CTRL] no controls declared: a confirming result cannot be distinguished from an artifact

  G-DIM voids the 54.1. Both operands are real, both are
  dimensionless, the division runs without error — and the
  quotient is a property of the lattice model over a property of
  the cascade set, which denotes nothing. The report reaches the
  same place in words: 'the two systems operate on very different
  normalized energy scales.' The gate reaches it as a void.

  G-CTRL: the positive control was declared and never run, so the
  null cannot be entered as evidence. That is Finding 3, caught
  structurally rather than by noticing a null promoted to a
  positive three sections later.

  G-SUP: the threshold claim names no quantity. Recorded
  unsupported.

SIM-STACK  the overall conclusion
==========================================================================

GATE SIM-STACK
  expected : if the lines are independent, agreement multiplies confidence
  observed : the three lines are not independent. SIM-A and SIM-B are two statistics on one pair of point sets, and SIM-C's contribution is a null.
  control  : independence check           run
  resolve  : matched-N baseline vs claimed separation ok
  claim    : [qualified] convergence across SIM-A, SIM-B, SIM-C

  G-IND does not forbid the convergence claim. It requires the
  shared input to be named, which downgrades 'three independent
  simulations converge' to a qualified claim about two statistics
  over one pair of point sets. The report's word is 'independent'.
  Nothing in the drop establishes that.
