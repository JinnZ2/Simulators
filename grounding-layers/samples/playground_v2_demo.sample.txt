── What changed since the previous sample ─────────────────────────
The former "I can lift 200 kg passes as grounded" hole is closed.
Mass claims now route through L4's `lift_mass` distribution combined
with the six-factor scope profile from `scope_profile.py`. With the
default (all-UNKNOWN) profile — as in this demo — the verdict is
UNSCOPED: the sim admits it cannot assess without knowing the
person's physical state, nutritional state, health, career, living
conditions, or environment.

The demo below runs each claim WITHOUT a scope profile. To see the
three achievable verdicts (UNSCOPED, EMBODIED_TRUE_UNVERIFIED,
MOST_LIKELY_UNTRUE), see `scope_profile_demo.sample.txt`.

A fourth verdict, EXTERNALLY_VERIFIED, is reserved for verification
injected from OUTSIDE the sim (a scale reading, a witness, a video).
The sim itself cannot grant this verdict — that's the architectural
ceiling.

── Under-the-hood fix that surfaced ───────────────────────────────
L4's `probability_of_feasibility` had an inverted sign: the sigmoid
formula computed "probability value exceeds mean" instead of
"probability random person can achieve value." Applied Step 2 of
the REFUTATION_PROTOCOL (Check the instrument): the docstring's
promised semantics is the phenomenon claim, the formula is the
instrument, and the instrument was wrong. Sign flipped. Under the
fix, 200 kg vs (mean=35, std=15) now returns ~0.004 (was 0.996).
