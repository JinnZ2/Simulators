# CLAIM_TABLE — cycle-ledger

`WORK_ORDER.md` is a delivered work order (verbatim, CC0): two independent
instruments generalizing the `routing-data-layer` marker's findings so they
are reproducible or refutable against someone else's operation.
**Deliverable 1** (`cycle_ledger.py`) turns a cycle-of-elements into a
rate-setter histogram, a TIED/BEHIND/AHEAD classification with a null, an
unnotated-work register, a relocation ledger, and a serial-interface
precondition. **Deliverable 2** (`rate_gap.py`) is the marker's Section 5
rate form on a data layer: dE/dt vs dM/dt over one season, per-class lag,
and the unrecorded set.

These `CLL_0NN` claims are properties of the **instruments**, each checked on
the SEED (the marker's carried corridor) or on **constructed** data with a
known answer. The SEED is one operator's corridor (Upper Midwest), carried
not verified; no county's events or updates are measured (egress-blocked);
nothing here is a result about any other operation.

## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

A refuted claim is updated forward with a new id; the old id keeps its text
and gains an UPDATE paragraph. `WORK_ORDER.md` is delivered verbatim and is
not edited. `sustained_excess` / `rate_verdict` are IMPORTED from
`routing-data-layer/rate_form.py` (not copied) and their known-answer cases
are not loosened. The null is run in both directions (SEED AHEAD == 0 vs a
DECISION-bound cycle AHEAD > 0; a structural season vs a kept-up season) so
no verdict is constant. Nothing is promoted from carried to verified without
a fetched source.
