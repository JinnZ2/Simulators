This is the welded term. One crash event is split across two objects:

             one crash event
                   |
                crash()
              /         \
  CRASHED reading     GAP_INSTRUMENT_CRASH entry
  3 fields            6 fields
  (shared: path, which the entry stores as missing_path)

That explains the scorer marking the crash pair as differing. The two terms share a predicate, but each one holds only half of the event's fields.
