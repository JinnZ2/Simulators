G29	Tool state	Tool enabled, tool invoked, and tool output used are equivalent, or three distinct conditions.

G33	Reproducibility	Repeating the same prompt is sufficient, or the environment, memory state, tools, and available context must also be controlled.

6. Important distinctions to preserve

These are not interchangeable:

* Unknown ≠ absent.
* Available ≠ accessed.
* Accessed ≠ used.
* Used ≠ materially influential.
* Similar terminology ≠ identical predicate.
* Identical fields ≠ identical function.
* Same function ≠ same implementation.
* Clarification ≠ correction.
* Correct output ≠ verified process.
* Missing declaration ≠ declared negative.
* Multiple interpretations ≠ multiple errors.
* A successful interaction ≠ an unambiguous interface.

7. Proposed investigation method

For every identified gap:

1. Record the original observation.
2. Identify each plausible interpretation.
3. Identify the assumption required by each interpretation.
4. Identify the information that would distinguish them.
5. Record whether that information is present, absent, or unavailable.
6. Preserve unresolved branches.
7. Determine whether the gap belongs to the source, instructions, interface, environment, or evaluator.
8. Only then consider a correction.

Do not automatically eliminate ambiguity. Some ambiguities are legitimate properties of the source material and should remain visible.

9. Central engineering question

Can we design an interface in which multiple interpretations are detected and recorded before an evaluator silently selects one?

That is the larger opportunity.

The objective is not to eliminate all confusion. It is to eliminate unrecorded assumptions introduced during the transition from observation to interpretation.
