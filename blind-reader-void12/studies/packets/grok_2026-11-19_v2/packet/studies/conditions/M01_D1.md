# Additional note for this reading (experimental, M01 condition D1)

This note is added to the reader brief for this reading only. It is experimental wording used to study how readers read the brief; it is not a change to the brief, and other readers do not receive it.

For `slots_specific`, a field counts as a slot of a term when both of these hold:

- TAG: it is defined on every instance of the term and on no instance of any other term; and
- INDEPENDENCE: it is an independent input of the term's record, that is, not computed from other candidate fields (it is passed in or recorded raw).

If you cannot tell whether a field meets one of these, say which one and why in `source_lines_read`, and set `confidence` accordingly. Do not guess.
