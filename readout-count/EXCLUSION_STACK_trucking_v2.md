# EXCLUSION STACK — trucking operator report channel

License: CC0. Compiled 2026-09-02. Companion to readout_count_v0.1_trucking.md.
Status: DRAFT. Rates are unmeasured; every layer is stated as a filter with a
named mechanism, and the survival fraction at each layer is the open quantity.

Purpose: enumerate every filter between an operator holding a readout and that
readout entering a record. Each layer is coded by mechanism, not by intent.
No layer requires an author; a filter is a stopband, and the record is the passband.

## STRUCTURE

    L0  the readout exists
    L1  statutory coverage
    L2  entry condition
    L3  timing
    L4  knowledge access
    L5  proof and construction
    L6  technology and connectivity
    L7  monetary
    L8  litigation gatekeeping
    L9  procedural / red tape
    L10 disposition
    L11 what enters the record

Survival is multiplicative. A layer that passes 50% is not minor if there are
eleven of them.

---

## L0 — THE READOUT EXISTS

    Held by the operator, uncarried by any instrument:
      labor / movement / kinetic / mental load the job actually requires
      compensations needed to operate the specific machine as assigned
      maintenance beyond the mechanics' protocol that the company, machine,
        road, and weather dictate for that machine to run safely
      social requirements of the assigned route
      road behaviour of that truck under variable conditions
      traffic / pedestrian / tourist patterns across those variations

    None of these is a rule violation. None names a respondent.
    → they are unfilable in a complaint instrument before any other filter applies.
    → EXCLUSION BY CHANNEL TYPE. This is the largest filter and it is at the top.

## L1 — STATUTORY COVERAGE  (who is an "employee")

    excluded before filing:
      owner-operators and independent contractors — no STAA employee standing
      lease-purchase drivers — contractor on paper, and the paper controls
      government drivers — 49 U.S.C. 31132(3)(B) excludes Federal, State, and
        political subdivisions expressly
      intrastate-only operations — must meet the "in commerce" test
      OOIDA is owner-operator membership and functions as referral, not a holder

    → classification is written by the carrier and decides standing
    → ASRS asks no equivalent question

## L2 — ENTRY CONDITION  (what counts as protected activity)

    STAA: requires a refusal, or a report of a violation, AND an adverse action
      that has already occurred. No adverse action, no standing.
    Coercion rule 390.5T: three elements — a threat, made to induce an FMCSR
      violation, after the driver stated that it would violate the FMCSRs.
      → the driver must have named the rule at the time, on the dock, to the
        person coercing
    → the analysis must be complete BEFORE the event is filable
    → an operator with a read and no legal theory has nothing to enter

## L3 — TIMING

    coercion / harassment complaint to FMCSA: 90 days from the event
    STAA retaliation to OSHA: 180 days from the adverse action
    → the two deadlines differ, and the shorter one governs the safety channel
    → OSHA argued in rulemaking that longer windows yield more meritorious
      complaints; the 90-day window stands for FMCSA
    → an OTR driver is away five days a week; the clock runs during that time

## L4 — KNOWLEDGE ACCESS

    to file, the operator must know:
      that 390.6 exists and what it covers
      that NCCDB is the filing venue and 386.12 the procedure
      that STAA is a separate statute with a separate agency and deadline
      that FMCSA cannot award personal remedy and OSHA can
      which FMCSR was violated, by citation
    → this is not published to drivers as a workflow; it is spread across
      FMCSA FAQs, 49 CFR parts 386 and 390, 29 CFR 1978, and OSHA fact sheets
    → carrier orientation typically advertises an internal number instead

    CURRICULUM COVERAGE — the claim, sharpened

      The channel does not appear in the professional safety curriculum at
      any level sampled. Evidence from one working operator's training stack:

        OSHAcademy Professional Program, 132 hours, completed with
          distinction. Aligned to ANSI/ASSP Z490.1 and ISO 45001. Course set
          is 29 CFR throughout: confined space, fall protection, electrical,
          SMS evaluation, emergency action plans, fire prevention, fleet
          safety management, workplace violence, ergonomics, industrial
          hygiene, OSH training development. The single fleet course is
          written from the position of the party operating the fleet.

        FEMA EMI Independent Study, 22 courses. ICS-100/200, NIMS-700/704,
          NRF-800, COOP awareness/introduction/manager, devolution planning,
          critical infrastructure support annex, exercise design, ESF-15
          external affairs, facility security committees.

      Neither stack contains 49 CFR 390.6, 49 CFR 386.12, or 49 U.S.C. 31105.

      The inverse is the sharper finding: the FEMA stack teaches how to STAND
      UP a field-to-decision reporting and coordination architecture — ICS,
      NIMS, ESF, COOP, devolution. A person trained in exactly that
      architecture, occupying the field position, had no route for a field
      report. The knowledge to build the channel and the absence of the
      channel are in the same person.

      → L4 is therefore not "low knowledge access" and not a gap in one
        program. It is absence from the professional curriculum, testable:

        TEST  search the syllabi of OSHAcademy, FEMA EMI IS, BCSP/ASSP CSP
              preparation material, and entry-level driver training
              (ELDT, 49 CFR 380 subpart F) for 390.6 / 386.12 / 31105
        PREDICT  present in carrier-side compliance and safety-director
              training; absent in operator-side training
        → if present in operator-side training, this layer is refuted

## L5 — PROOF AND CONSTRUCTION

    required in the filing: complainant name, address, phone; company name,
      address, phone; date; location; description; the elements
    → identity disclosure is required for prosecution (49 CFR 386.12(c)(3)
      states this directly)
    → evidence burden sits on the person with the least document access:
      dispatch messages, ELD records, maintenance history, and load paperwork
      are held by the respondent
    → forum advice, consistently: paper trail; verbal reports do nothing
    → the diagnostic burden is pushed upstream onto the reporter

## L6 — TECHNOLOGY AND CONNECTIVITY

    filing is written, and the venue is a web database
    → reception at truck stops, rural corridors, and reservations is variable;
      carriers do not select routes for coverage
    → one-finger phone entry, in the intervals between pre-trips, gates, and
      dispatch calls; no printer; no desk
    → document assembly (screenshots, photos, logs) on a phone, then upload
    → NCCDB modernization windows take the system offline for days at a time
    → ASRS by contrast accepts oral filing; STAA does too, and drivers are told
      why: "the only way they can communicate immediate concerns is by CB radio
      or phone." The safety channel does not carry that accommodation.

## L7 — MONETARY

    time to construct the filing is unpaid
    a load refused pending resolution is unpaid
    termination while a complaint is pending: no interim income
    FMCSA cannot pay the driver; penalties go to the Highway Trust Fund
      (49 U.S.C. 521(b)(10))
    OSHA can order remedy, but only after the process below
    → the operator finances the entire channel out of pocket, in advance,
      against a ~1-in-5 chance of any outcome

## L8 — LITIGATION GATEKEEPING

    STAA disposition (GAO-09-106, STAA rows, N=274):
      dismissed 183 (67%) · withdrawn 32 (12%) · merit 59 (22%)
    across statutes, ~95% of merit cases settle by separate agreement between
      whistleblower and employer
    of the residual sent to the Solicitor, per an OSHA official, none were
      litigated — all dismissed
    investigation duration: DOL OIG reports an average of 279 days, up from
      238 (2015) and 150 (2010)
    counsel: contingency representation depends on damages; a condition report
      with no termination has no damages and therefore no counsel

## L9 — PROCEDURAL / RED TAPE

    two agencies, two statutes, two deadlines, one event
    FMCSA refers the personal remedy to OSHA; OSHA refers safety allegations
      back to FMCSA (OSHA–FMCSA MOU)
    State Plan states: dual-filing rules, 30-day referral windows, and closure
      if the complainant does not respond promptly to the State Plan
    non-response to any follow-up letter is grounds for summary dismissal
    → an OTR driver on the road misses mail and calls by the nature of the work
    → carrier-internal path: advertised at hiring, routed to HR on use, and
      re-typed as a driver complaint (operator report, 2026-09-01)

## L10 — DISPOSITION

    from 100 filings, using the STAA rates above:
      67 dismissed · 12 withdrawn · 21 merit
      of the 21: ~20 private settlements · ~1 formal order
    → the modal outcome is dismissal; the modal SUCCESS is a private settlement

## L11 — WHAT ENTERS THE RECORD

    a private settlement resolves the employment matter and publishes nothing
    OSHA does not publish settlement outcomes or explain them
    the CONDITION that triggered the refusal does not enter any safety dataset
    → the successful outcome REMOVES the information from the record
    → in the digest case where a driver refused a truck over kingpin play and
      was terminated, the adjudicated question was the firing; the equipment
      question was never the subject
    → so even the surviving fraction does not produce a readout

## THE SHAPE

    L0 excludes the quantity by channel type.
    L1–L3 exclude operators by class and clock.
    L4–L7 price the channel in knowledge, proof, connectivity, and money,
      charged to the party with the least of each.
    L8–L10 reduce the survivors by roughly five to one.
    L11 converts the survivors into silence.

    A channel whose success condition is a confidential settlement cannot
    function as a readout channel, no matter how many people pass through it.

## ACCRETED, NOT ENGINEERED

    The stack is not a designed system with a weak link. It is a set of parts,
    each specified alone, with no party owning the composition.

    engineered system                    what is present
    ---------------------------------    ---------------------------------
    spec includes the return path        intake specified; return unspecified
    interfaces defined and owned         FMCSA → OSHA → State Plan, referrals
                                         in both directions, no owner of the seam
    failure modes enumerated             no analysis of what an operator holding
                                         a condition-read is to do
    operator in the design loop          operator is the load, not a stakeholder
    tested against the actual user       L4: never tested against whether a
                                         trained operator could locate it
    instrumented on its own performance  no published survival rate at any layer

    Contrast: ASRS was specified as a whole — a third-party holder, an immunity
    clause, a return to operators, and a published dataset, defined together.

    → this predicts the failure is not fixable layer by layer. Removing any one
      filter leaves the composition unowned.
    → and it converts "safety culture" from a disposition into a countable
      structure: who holds, who returns, who is immune, who publishes.

## OPEN QUANTITIES  (the survey this compilation calls for)

    S1  fraction of the driving population outside L1 (employee classification)
    S2  fraction of L0 items that are filable in ANY existing instrument
        — predicted near zero; needs a coding exercise against the NCCDB
        complaint categories
    S3  per-layer survival rates; none are published
    S4  per-carrier reply rate to internal driver technical submissions
        (board-sampled; identity filter self-enforcing)
    S5  NCCDB coercion filings vs investigations vs enforcement actions, by year
    S6  curriculum search (L4 test): 390.6 / 386.12 / 31105 across OSHAcademy,
        FEMA EMI IS, CSP preparation material, and ELDT under 49 CFR 380
        subpart F — operator-side vs carrier-side presence
    S7  awareness survey: N drivers and N carrier safety directors asked
        whether 390.6 exists and where it is filed; the asymmetry is the
        measurement
