# Gap: transmission decay rate of hazard knowledge

Undergraduate-scale study design. No funding required beyond travel or remote
interviewing; no proprietary data.

CC0. No rights reserved.

## The gap

Glacial lake outburst flood (GLOF) hazard in High Mountain Asia is heavily
instrumented: satellite lake inventories, dam-stability modelling, version-
controlled event databases. Local and transmitted knowledge is credited in
that literature as a source that catches events the other sources miss.

The same literature notes that local knowledge degrades as memory fades and
as it passes between generations.

That degradation has never been quantified. It is a caveat, not a
measurement.

    physical hazard channel  ->  instrumented, funded, monitored
    knowledge channel        ->  footnote

No decay rate, no half-life, no transition point. The asymmetry is the gap.

## Question

At what generational distance from a hazard event does a transmitted account
shift from MECHANISM (actionable causal structure) to STORY (narrative
retained, causal structure lost)?

Secondary: does the shift track elapsed time, generational hops, schooling
exposure, out-migration, or the arrival of an official monitoring system?

## Why it is answerable now

Event dates are known independently. GLOF event databases give dated events
per valley over the last century. That supplies the x-axis without relying on
any informant's dating — which is what makes the design work.

## Design

    SAMPLE   valleys with a DATED event in the independent record,
             stratified by years-since-event.
             Within each valley, informants stratified by chain position:
               C0   witnessed it
               C1   heard it from a witness
               C2   heard it from C1
               C3+  no traceable chain to a witness

    Cohort is defined by CHAIN POSITION, not age. A young informant who
    heard it directly from a surviving witness is C1. Coding by age
    measures schooling, not transmission.

    ELICIT   open-ended first: "what happens in this valley"
             never lead with the event
             ask about the dated event only after open elicitation

## Coding scheme

Score each account on components independently checkable against the
physical record.

    M1  source identified        lake, glacier, slope above
    M2  trigger named            avalanche into lake, rain, quake, erosion
    M3  mechanism stated         impoundment fails, water released
    M4  routing correct          which valley, which side, where the wave goes
    M5  timing/seasonality       when in the year, how fast arrival
    M6  precursor signs          lake colour, level change, sounds, springs
    M7  ACTION rule              where to go, what not to build, when to
                                 move stock
    M8  chained consequence      what the flood loads downstream
                                 (the coupling component)

    S1  event retained as narrative with no M-components
    S2  attributed to agency with no physical mechanism
    S3  no temporal anchor

    MECHANISM SCORE = count of M present AND correct against the record
    STORY SCORE     = count of S present

Correctness is checked against the instrumented record, not against the
interviewer's expectation.

## Measures

    decay curve       mechanism score vs chain position C0..C3+
    half-life         chain position at which mechanism score reaches half
                      its C0 value
    component order   WHICH M-components drop first
    action retention  M7 — does the action rule outlive the mechanism
    coupling loss     M8 — does the chained-consequence component drop
                      faster than single-process ones

Component order is the most useful output. If M7 outlives M3, transmission is
preserving what to do while losing why — which fails precisely when
conditions change and the action rule no longer fits.

If M8 drops first, that is direct evidence that chained structure is the
least transmissible component, which matters well beyond this domain.

## Confounds that must be controlled or the result is worthless

    OFFICIAL BACKFLOW
      informants may have learned mechanism from schooling, NGO briefings,
      or monitoring programmes rather than from transmission. Ask provenance
      for every component; code transmitted vs acquired separately. Backflow
      inflates mechanism score at high chain positions and fakes a flat curve.

    OUT-MIGRATION SURVIVORSHIP
      valleys that lost population after an event have lost carriers.
      Absence of C2/C3 informants is data, not a sampling failure.

    INTERVIEWER FRAME IMPORT
      a hazard-trained interviewer supplies mechanism inside the question.
      Open elicitation first, always. Consider running the open phase with
      an interviewer blind to the event record.

    LANGUAGE AND REGISTER
      mechanism may be encoded in idiom, ritual calendar, place names, or
      song rather than expository form. An account coded S1 may be M-rich in
      a form the coder cannot read. Requires a local co-analyst; without one,
      report the coding as frame-limited. Largest threat to validity and not
      fully removable.

    PRESTIGE ASYMMETRY
      informants may withhold or downgrade transmitted knowledge in front of
      an outside researcher because it has been treated as folklore.
      Underreporting is directional, not random.

## Ethics and ownership

Transmitted hazard knowledge belongs to the communities holding it. Design
consequence, not an appendix:

    community consent at the collective level, not only individual
    communities decide what is publishable
    co-authorship where wanted
    the ACTION components (M7) may be more sensitive than mechanism ones —
      do not assume publication is neutral
    deliver results back in usable form before publication

A study that extracts a decay rate while accelerating the extraction is
self-defeating.

## Falsifiers

    no decay   mechanism score flat across chain position
               (check backflow before believing it)
    cliff      mechanism intact to Cn then collapses — implies a discrete
               break (schooling, resettlement, monitoring arrival) rather
               than gradual fade
    inverse    later cohorts score HIGHER — official backflow dominates and
               transmission is not what is being measured

All three are publishable. The cliff result would be the most consequential:
loss caused by an identifiable institutional event rather than by time.

## Generalisation

Domain-portable. Any hazard with an independently dated physical record and a
resident population: flood-stage knowledge on regulated rivers, fire-return
intervals, sea ice, seismic precursors, avalanche paths.

Run it on more than one domain and the component-order result becomes a
general finding about what transmission drops first.
