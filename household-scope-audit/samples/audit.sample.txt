HOUSEHOLD-SCOPE AUDIT -- Arm 1's coding scheme, and what the
design can be checked on without a reading room

SOURCE_DROP.md asks for Arm 1, and for "a reading room and a
coding scheme, nothing else." The coding scheme is built. The
reading room is not available and is not substituted for.

0. ARM 1 IS NOT RUN. THE CORPUS IS UNREACHABLE, MEASURED.

     pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov      000
     onlinelibrary.wiley.com      000
     www.acf.hhs.gov              000
     www.childwelfare.gov         000
     apps.who.int                 000
     www.gov.uk                   000
     github.com                   400
   Every publisher, statutory and archive host returns no
   response through this environment's proxy; only github.com
   answers. Egress is an allowlist, so substituting a publisher
   does not help.

   NO INSTRUMENT ITEM IS INVENTED, PARAPHRASED OR CODED ANYWHERE
   IN THIS FOLDER. These are tools that carry weight in decisions
   about real families; a fabricated E-fraction table would read
   as a result about them. The fixtures used below are authored
   in coding.py, labelled there, and no fraction over them is a
   statement about any instrument.

   A SECOND CONSTRAINT, which binds whoever DOES have the reading
   room: item wording in many published instruments is licensed
   rather than free. An audit publishing its item-level working
   data would reproduce it. So the scheme codes by REFERENCE with
   the text optional -- and that is not free either, because the
   field most in need of checking is the one that then cannot be
   published with the item beside it.

1. THE CODER, NULL-TESTED BOTH WAYS
   items written to have a class:      6 of 6 classified correctly
   items written to have none:         0 of 3 forced into one
   can classify: True    can decline: True    OK
   A classifier that cannot decline forces every item into a
   class, which is the failure the drop's own ask names. One that
   classifies nothing is equally useless. Both directions are
   checked because either alone passes for a coder that is not
   doing its job.

2. X IS A CAUSAL JUDGMENT, AND IT MOVES TWO OF THE THREE OUTCOMES
   LOCUS as delivered has X = "external condition coded AS a
   personal property". Separating P from X takes a claim about
   what causes the underlying variable -- about the world, not
   about the text. Two coders who disagree about whether housing
   instability is externally caused produce different
   X-fractions on identical items.

   Declaring ONE fixture's cause, changing no text:
     X-fraction               0.2308   -> 0.3077
     attenuation denominator  7        -> 6
     attenuation coverage     0.1429   -> 0.1667
     E-fraction               0.1538   -> 0.1538   (unchanged)
   Two of the three published outcomes move together on one
   judgment, and the drop reports them side by side as separate
   readings. The third does not move, which is the point: the
   E-fraction is a property of the text.

   AND THE COUPLING RUNS IN THE FLATTERING DIRECTION. The item
   that moved carried no attenuation rule, so leaving the
   denominator RAISED attenuation coverage. A coder attributing
   more to external cause makes the instrument score higher on
   discounting for external cause, on the same manual. Whether
   that holds in general depends on how attenuation is
   distributed across the items a coder reclassifies -- which is
   measurable in a real audit and is not measured here.

   THE SPLIT IS IN THE SCHEME HERE, not in the write-up:
   LOCUS is DERIVED from two declared fields --
     subject_class      mechanical, from the item's own subject,
                        recomputable by anyone holding the text
     externally_caused  declared per item WITH a stated basis;
                        refused without one
   -- so the causal claim is visible as one and can be disagreed
   with separately from the reading of the text. An item whose
   subject is a person and whose cause is NOT_DECLARED codes P,
   never X: a conclusion nobody declared is not one.

3. REVERSE CAUSATION DOES REACH ARM 1
   The drop's confound section says: "The audit arm is
   unaffected -- it measures representational capacity, not
   causal share."

   True of the E-fraction and false of the X-fraction, from the
   drop's own definitions. Same items, two defensible causal
   readings of one of them:
     external cause read outward   X 0.3077   E 0.1538
     cause read inward             X 0.2308   E 0.1538
   E unchanged: True     X changed: True
   Reverse causation is precisely the case where a person-subject
   item's variable is NOT externally caused, so the confound the
   drop excludes from Arm 1 lands on one of Arm 1's three primary
   outcomes. Section 2 is the same finding reached from the other
   side.

4. ONE CODED FIELD HAS NO OUTCOME, AND IT IS THE ONE THAT
   SEPARATES RECORDING FROM EXPLAINING
   The drop codes four fields and publishes three outcomes.
   DIRECTIONALITY -- "does any item permit an external cause to
   EXPLAIN a household observation, or only to co-occur with
   it" -- is collected and not reported. So is ACTIONABILITY
   TARGET.

   Two item sets, identical text, differing only in whether the
   external items may explain:
     all three published outcomes identical: True
     explain fraction                        0.5 vs 0.0
   An instrument that records external conditions and never lets
   them do any work is indistinguishable, in the three published
   numbers, from one that does. The field is already collected;
   what is missing is one line in the outcome list.

5. WHERE THE UNCLASSIFIED ITEMS SIT IS NOT SPECIFIED
   The ask says mark them unclassified rather than forcing them,
   which is right and is the reason the coder has the state at
   all. It does not say which denominator they occupy.
     unclassified 1 of 13 items
     E-fraction with them in the denominator   0.1538
     E-fraction with them out                  0.1667
   E-fraction is stated as 'E items / total items', so keeping
   them in biases it low by exactly their share. Both are
   reported and neither is picked.

6. WHAT THIS FOLDER DOES NOT ESTABLISH
   No E-fraction, X-fraction or attenuation coverage for any
   instrument. No claim about whether the gap is real -- the
   drop states its own retraction condition (E-fraction
   materially non-zero and attenuation mandatory) and nothing
   here bears on it in either direction.

   Arm 2 UNMEASURED: human scorers, and a simulated practitioner
   panel would be a fabricated claim about practitioners.
   Arm 3 UNMEASURED: administrative records, unreachable and
   not public.

   What is established is about the coding scheme and the
   design, and every demonstration above runs on items authored
   in this folder for that purpose.
