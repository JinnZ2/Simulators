# AUDIT NOTES — SEARCH SUBSTITUTION

What is not established, written down before it can be forgotten.

## The strongest thing here is the absence, not the speed

None of these organisms is fast. Physarum takes over a day. The claim is that
a term is missing from the cost expression, not that the cost is small. If
this folder is read as "biology beats algorithms" it has been read wrong —
the finding is that the enumeration was never in the problem.

## Three figures are stipulations, not measurements

- **64 bits per cache.** Chosen so the ledger has a number in it. Nothing in
  the corvid literature licenses this or any other figure as the neural cost
  of one cache. The *direction* of the transfer is the claim; the magnitude
  is decoration and should not be quoted.
- **100 cm² arena, 26 hours.** Order-of-magnitude, consistent with published
  setups, not lifted from a specific figure in Tero 2010.
- **10,000 candidate sites.** An illustrative territory partition. No source
  gives a site count for a real recovery problem.

The arithmetic on all three is correct; the inputs are chosen. That is a
different standing from C2, C6, and C7, which are reproducible from printed
terms.

## The platypus claim is the one likely to break

C5 has two halves and they are not equally supported. Range-from-offset is a
published proposal with a plausible mechanism behind it. "No intermediate
representation" is a claim about what is *absent* in a nervous system, and
absence is not what the cited work measured — bill representation in cortex
has in fact been described. The second half is carried here because it is the
structurally interesting part, and flagged here because that is exactly why it
should be distrusted.

## Species were conflated in the source material and are separated here

The informal version of this ran three results together under "crow". They
come from three species in two genera, none of the results obtained in the
same animal. The separation is in `README.md` and in `--sources`. The
combined picture — thousands of caches, plus temporal memory, plus observer
tracking, in one bird — is not attested and is not claimed.

## What the ceiling is doing here

The 10^120 figure locates crossovers and nothing else. No organism in this
folder approaches it, no biological process is priced against it, and its
appearance should not be read as a claim that anything here is near a
physical limit. It is a fixed ruler borrowed so the numbers can be compared
to the scaling work.

## Unresolved

The terminal-count sweep for C1 would settle the folder's central claim and
appears to be absent from the literature the file cites. Whether it has been
run elsewhere has not been checked. Until it is, C1 rests on the structure of
the model rather than on an observation, and the folder says so rather than
rounding it up.
