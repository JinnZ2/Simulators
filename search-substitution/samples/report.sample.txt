SEARCH SUBSTITUTION
ceiling: 1e120 operations (Lloyd 1999)

CROSSOVERS -- where a method exceeds the ceiling
  2**k   exceeds at k = 399 components
  3**k   exceeds at k = 252 residues
  N**2   exceeds at N = 1e60 bodies
  N**3   exceeds at N = 1e40 bodies
  Earth carries ~1e50 atoms: N**2 fits, N**3 does not, at one N.
  The exponent decides, not the size.

PHYSARUM -- occupancy instead of enumeration
  Tero 2010 ran 36 terminals.
  Exact Steiner DP carries 3**36 = 1.5e+17 subproblem splits.
  Plasmodium cost has no terminal term at all: it is arena held
  for a duration. A 100 cm2 arena for 26 h is 9.36e+06 cm2-seconds,
  and that figure does not move when terminals are added.

CORVID -- storage instead of search
  5000 caches over 10000 candidate sites.
  Recovered by search:   2.5e+07 probes
  Recovered by memory:   5e+03 probes
  Ratio: 5e+03. The cost did not vanish -- it moved to the ledger,
  3.2e+05 bits at 64 bits per cache, carried the whole interval.
  Species are not interchangeable here; see --sources.

ORNITHORHYNCHUS -- coupling instead of representation
    50 mm of range  ->    33.8 us of arrival offset
   100 mm of range  ->    67.6 us of arrival offset
   200 mm of range  ->   135.1 us of arrival offset
  Recovered by one multiplication: 0.099 m from 67.0 us.
  A map-then-query approach would carry 2.62e+08 cells at 1 mm over
  a 0.5 m hemisphere. The animal carries none of them.

The exponential is a property of the representation, not of the
system. Check which one you are pricing.
