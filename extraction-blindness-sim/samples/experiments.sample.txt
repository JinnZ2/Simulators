an error signal as confirmation of safety. These six experiments
measure how far that reading diverges from the substrate's state.

EXPERIMENT 6 — EBS_006: temporal aliasing, with and without the trend in the loop
======================================================================

  Two conditions govern whether aliasing matters: whether the
  trend steers, and whether an effort ratchet is defending a target.

  regime                                            alias steps  final stock
  trend not in loop, no ratchet, clear                        0       0.0000
  trend not in loop, no ratchet, aliased                      2       0.0000
  trend steers, no ratchet, clear                             0       0.8798
  trend steers, no ratchet, aliased                          13       0.0000
  trend steers, ratchet defending target, clear               0       0.0000
  trend steers, ratchet defending target, aliased             8       0.0000

  (a) trend not in loop      -> outcome delta 0.0000  (aliasing cosmetic)
  (b) trend steers, no ratchet -> outcome delta +0.8798  (aliasing decisive)
  (c) ratchet defending target -> outcome delta 0.0000  (ratchet dominates)
  VERDICT: SUPPORTED — aliasing is decisive only where the trend steers AND no ratchet overrides it
