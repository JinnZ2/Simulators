Section 4 names ONE of these three states. It offers
UNDER_OUTLINED, and reads apparent conflict as 'separate
tangents to one boundary, not competing claims'. But
INCONSISTENT is reachable and is what a genuine conflict looks
like: no point satisfies every statement, so there is no
boundary they are all tangent to. Without that state every
apparent conflict routes to the reassuring reading.

And OUTLINED carries a NUMBER. 'Complete when the gaps
constrain the object to one form' is the area going to zero;
until then the area is how much is still open. Under-outlined
stops being a stated state and becomes a measured one.

  Tangents recover a convex hull and nothing finer. If the

  NEVER be met -- not for want of statements, by construction.
  Worth knowing before an outline that will not close is read
