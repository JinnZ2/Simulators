# Mechanism 11 — DERIVATION DISCARDED

Alternate handle under consideration: UNPRICED PRECONDITION.
Name not settled. If the second reads better in the accounting register,
swap it; the mechanism is the same.

STATUS: open. Marker to explore, not a position under defense.
Correct responses: test fit, extend, or report where it breaks.

---

## STATEMENT

A structure that has persisted is a record of every constraint that had
to be satisfied simultaneously for it to exist. The derivation was never
written down anywhere else — the structure is the only copy.

Removal of the structure is therefore removal of a readout. The
computation cannot be rerun: the inputs are not available and the
conditions it solved under are gone.

The accounting for removal books the structure at extraction or
replacement price. There is no line for the discarded derivation. The
ledger is arithmetically correct and structurally blind, so the removal
registers as a gain.

## DISTINCTION FROM ADJACENT MECHANISMS

- **PROXY SUBSTITUTION (6):** a stand-in quantity is measured instead.
  Here nothing is substituted. The quantity has no register at all.
- **SCORED AS WASTE (8):** the quantity is seen and assigned a negative
  or null value. Here it is not seen — the value assigned is not low, it
  is absent from the schema.
- **GENERATION CAPACITY REMOVED (10):** the capacity to produce a
  quantity is removed from a population. Here the record of a completed
  computation is destroyed. Related in shape; different object.

The distinguishing test: after the removal, can any party — including
one who wanted to — recover what was lost? If the loss is uncountable in
principle rather than uncounted in practice, it is mechanism 11.

## ANCHOR CASE — THE SELF-REFERENTIAL POST-AUDIT

Environmental impact assessment predicts impacts before development.
Post-auditing exists as a named practice and has been run: UK, US and
Australian audits, review literature going back decades. Reported
prediction accuracy in one review: 152 of the auditable predictions
deemed accurate, roughly 73%, spread from about 38% to 92% by project
type.

The denominator is the prediction list.

A prediction never made cannot be scored inaccurate. Unanticipated
impacts therefore do not enter the accuracy figure — they enter, if at
all, as a separate note. One US review states directly that few
forecasts were found inaccurate and fewer unanticipated impacts were
identified.

In the risk-analysis notation: the specified event set A' is scored
against itself. The gap between A' and the actual event set A is the
quantity of interest, and the audit instrument is constituted so that it
cannot appear.

This is stronger than an unchecked assumption. The checking apparatus was
built, staffed and published. It is closed on its own inputs.

## THE NULL TEST

Does pre-removal documentation contain any term for what was not
modelled — applied to the structure being removed, rather than to the
structure being installed?

Expected: structural null. Risk-engineering formalism has the
unmodelled-interaction concept (normal accident theory, epistemic
uncertainty tracking, A' vs A) but points it at the new build.
Natural-capital accounting points at existing structure but values its
output flow — services, yield, provision — not the structure as record.

The two literatures are complementary blind spots. Neither holds the
term. If the null is field-wide, the exclusion is structural rather than
an oversight, which is the condition for entry in this repo.

## SEED MEASUREMENTS

**R1 — Unanticipated-impact ratio.** For a set of post-audited projects:
count of impacts recorded post-hoc that appear nowhere in the
pre-project prediction list, over total impacts recorded. Published
audits report the numerator inconsistently and often not at all.
Recovering it requires re-reading the source EISs against monitoring
data.

**R2 — Denominator disclosure.** Simpler and checkable now: of published
post-audits, what fraction state their accuracy figure's denominator
explicitly as the prediction list rather than as total impacts? This is
a documentation audit, not a field study. Runnable from library access.

**R3 — Removal-side uncertainty term.** Sample of demolition,
clearance, drainage, or decommissioning approvals. Presence or absence
of any documented term for unmodelled function of the removed structure.
Binary per document. States: documented / asserted / absent — no verdict
computed.

Calibration constraint: R3 scored against the removing party's own
knowledge base returns absent-as-nothing-there. Score against the
document's own uncertainty vocabulary, or the reading is invalid and
drops out.

## OPEN SUB-QUESTIONS

1. Does an accounting standard anywhere carry a term for irreversibility
   distinct from valuation difficulty? Farley on critical natural capital
   comes nearest located so far and frames it as a valuation problem.
2. Control-belief before failure is well documented in psychology
   (self-model bias) and named across religious and historical
   traditions (hubris). Neither has been connected to the accounting
   blindness. Is the connection load-bearing or coincidental? Open.
3. Is there a class of structure for which the derivation IS recoverable
   post-removal? If yes, that boundary is the mechanism's actual edge and
   worth more than the central case.
4. Nesting: fast loops persist underneath slow ones — a stable layer is
   what allows a longer loop to exist at all. Does removal at one layer
   have a characteristic signature at the layers above it, and on what
   delay? Not articulated. Do not fill in with an approximation.

## FALSIFIERS FOR THE MECHANISM ITSELF

- Fails if post-audit literature is found that scores against total
  observed impacts rather than against the prediction list.
- Fails if any removal-approval standard already requires a stated
  unmodelled-function term.
- Weakens substantially if R1 comes back small — i.e. if unanticipated
  impacts really are rare rather than merely unlooked-for.
- The "only copy" claim fails for any structure whose constraint set is
  independently documented. Scope the mechanism to those that are not.
