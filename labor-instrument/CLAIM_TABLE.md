# CLAIM_TABLE — labor-instrument

Claims are properties of the machinery and of the egress-blocked data
situation, not conclusions about employment (no real BLS/ALFRED/QCEW data is
fetched or fabricated). Every number is computed by `selftest.py` on
constructed data; the render is in `samples/`. `LI_*` prefix.

## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

A refuted claim is updated forward with a new id; the old id keeps its text
and gains an UPDATE paragraph. `WORK_ORDER.md` is delivered verbatim and is
not edited. No registrar or series fact is promoted from carried-not-verified
without a fetched source; the checks in `selftest.py` are not loosened to keep
a claim.
