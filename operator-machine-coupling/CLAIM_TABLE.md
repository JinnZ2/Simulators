# CLAIM_TABLE — operator-machine-coupling

`MARKER.md` is posted as a **gap, not a finding** — "Nothing here is a
result." These claims are therefore properties of the **instruments** built
for the gap's measurables, each checked on **constructed** data with a known
answer, plus the carried epistemic status of the marker itself. No fleet,
plant, incident, or symbiosis data is read (none is available — egress is an
allowlist), and every literature claim in `MARKER.md` is carried, not
verified. `OMC_*` prefix.

## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

A refuted claim is updated forward with a new id; the old id keeps its text
and gains an UPDATE paragraph. `MARKER.md` is delivered verbatim and is not
edited. The `interaction_fraction` metric is registered in
`tools/known_answer.py` and its cases are not loosened to keep a claim; the
discriminators' verdicts are null-tested in both directions so a constant
classifier is caught. Nothing is promoted from carried to verified without a
fetched source.
