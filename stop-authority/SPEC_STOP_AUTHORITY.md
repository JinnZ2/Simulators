# STOP-AUTHORITY INSTRUMENTATION GAP
# Status: marker under exploration.

## THE PROBLEM
"Stop work authority exists" is currently evidenced by policy
text and by stop COUNT. Both are unsigned. A count of zero is
consistent with two opposite states:

  A. no condition warranted a stop
  B. the authority is not honored, so none were attempted

Published cases treat zero as evidence of A. Nothing in the
measurement distinguishes them. A worker who knows a stop
will not be honored does not attempt one — so under B,
non-use is produced BY the hollowness, and reads as safety.

## THREE MISSING NUMBERS
1. STOPS ATTEMPTED
   Not stops executed. Attempted — including those reversed,
   discouraged, or resolved informally before recording.

2. STOPS HONORED / STOPS ATTEMPTED
   The ratio is the authority measurement. The count alone
   is not.

3. WARRANTED-IN-REVIEW
   Of incidents that occurred, how many had a prior state
   where a stop was warranted and available. Determined by
   post-incident review, not by whether anyone called it.

(3) is the denominator the other two are missing. Without it,
attempts and honors float free of the actual hazard rate.

## THE STRUCTURAL QUESTION UNDERNEATH
Authority is defined by what it can bind, not by who holds it.
Record, per facility:
  - highest organizational level a stop has bound
  - whether any stop has been reversed, and by whom
  - whether the holder of the authority reports to a party
    whose objectives the stop can cost

A stop authority that has never bound upward has not been
tested. Untested is not the same as functioning.

## NOTE ON DISTRIBUTION
Extending stop authority to "everyone" — employees,
contractors, visitors — reads as expansion. It may be
contraction: authority located in everyone is enforced by
no one, and has no identified holder to be reversed against.
Record whether a NAMED position holds it.

## PRIOR ART
Documented case: safety manager reports SWA as a core program
element and cannot recall a single stop in ~10 years.
Offered evidence of function: workers reported having
CONVERSATIONS about safety. Zero attempts, zero honors,
no review denominator. Published as working.
