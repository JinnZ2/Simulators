STOP AUTHORITY -- what it can bind
========================================================================

  Authority is defined by what it can bind, not by who
  holds it. A stop authority that has never bound upward
  has not been tested. Untested is not the same as
  functioning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

  facility                       binding              tested
  prior art: SWA as core program NO_STOPS_AT_ALL      False
  named holder, stops only at pe UNTESTED_ABOVE       False
  named holder, one stop bound a BOUND_ABOVE_HOLDER   True

                                 reversals    named    conflict
  prior art: SWA as core program NOT_LOOKED   False    UNASSESSED
  named holder, stops only at pe NONE_FOUND   True     YES
  named holder, one stop bound a FOUND        True     YES

  functioning: None on every row -- there is no such field.
    FUNCTIONING is not a value this module returns. An untested
    authority is untested; a tested one has a binding record to read
    directly. Collapsing both into a single functioning flag is the
    move the whole instrument exists to refuse

------------------------------------------------------------------------

  EACH AXIS

  prior art: SWA as core program element
    binds      NO_STOPS_AT_ALL
      no stop has been executed, so nothing has been bound and nothing
      has been refused. The authority has produced no trial in either
      direction
    reversals  NOT_LOOKED
      with no stops there is nothing that could have been reversed. An
      empty reversal record here is an empty record, not a clean one
    holder     EVERYONE
      enforced by: no identified holder
      someone to reverse against: False
    conflict   UNASSESSED

  named holder, stops only at peer level
    binds      UNTESTED_ABOVE
      stops have bound only at or below the holder's own level
      (SUPERVISOR). An authority that has never bound upward has not
      been tested, which is not the same as its having failed
    reversals  NONE_FOUND
      4 stops executed and none reversed
    holder     NAMED_POSITION
      enforced by: line safety officer
      someone to reverse against: True
    conflict   YES

  named holder, one stop bound at executive level
    binds      BOUND_ABOVE_HOLDER
      a stop has bound at EXECUTIVE, above the holder at SUPERVISOR
    reversals  FOUND
      reversed by: plant manager
    holder     NAMED_POSITION
      enforced by: line safety officer
      someone to reverse against: True
    conflict   YES

------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ON DISTRIBUTION -- RECORDED, NOT GRADED

    verdict on expansion: None
    authority located in everyone is enforced by no one and has no
    identified holder to be reversed against. Whether that is expansion
    or contraction depends on the facility, and the spec says MAY. The
    structural facts are recorded; the grade is not

------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE DOCUMENTED CASE

    evidence offered of function:
      workers reported having CONVERSATIONS about safety

    is that a measurement? False
      a conversation is not a stop attempted, not a stop honored and
      not a reviewed warranted state. It is not one of the three
      numbers, and offering it as evidence of function substitutes an
      activity for a measurement

    axes returning a measurement: 0
    count diagnosis:              INDISTINGUISHABLE
    VERDICT:                      UNTESTED_ON_EVERY_AXIS

    no stop executed, so nothing bound and nothing was reversed;
    authority in everyone, so no named holder and nobody to reverse
    against; conflict unassessed; and no review denominator, so the
    count is INDISTINGUISHABLE. Published as working, with every axis
    empty

  CONFIDENCE, reported separately and not resolved
    UNTESTED
      returned wherever the authority has produced no trial. It is not
      a soft HOLLOW and not a soft FUNCTIONING
    conflict_axis
      carried as declared. Nothing here checks a reporting line, and
      UNASSESSED is not NO
    distribution
      the structural facts are recorded and the
      expansion-or-contraction grade is not made. The spec says MAY,
      and which it is depends on a facility this module does not
      measure
    functioning
      never returned. There is no such field on the summary, by
      construction
    resolved
      False
    the_facilities
      one delivered case and two stipulated contrasts. The contrasts
      exist to show the axes moving and are not observations

  WHERE IT BREAKS
    - THE DOCUMENTED CASE RETURNS UNTESTED ON EVERY AXIS AND THAT IS
    THE WHOLE READOUT. No stop executed, so nothing bound and no
    reversal record to read; authority located in everyone, so no named
    position holds it and there is nobody to reverse against; conflict
    unassessed; no review denominator, so the count is
    INDISTINGUISHABLE. Published as working with every axis empty. The
    instrument does not say the authority was hollow -- it says the
    published claim rested on nothing it could read
    - 'CONVERSATIONS ABOUT SAFETY' IS NOT ON ANY AXIS, AND THE MODULE
    CAN ONLY SAY THAT. It is not a stop attempted, not a stop honored,
    not a reviewed warranted state, and not a binding event. Recording
    that it fails to be a measurement is different from establishing
    that the conversations did nothing, and nothing here does the
    second
    - UNTESTED_ABOVE IS RETURNED BY A COMPARISON OF TWO DECLARED LEVELS
    AND BOTH ARE SUPPLIED BY WHOEVER FILLS THE RECORD. highest_bound
    and holder_level are inputs, so a facility that files its holder at
    PEER makes any supervisor-level stop read as upward binding. The
    ordering is real and the placement in it is a declaration
    - THE MODULE REFUSES TO GRADE DISTRIBUTION AND THAT REFUSAL HAS A
    COST. It records that authority in everyone has no identified
    holder and nobody to reverse against, then declines to say whether
    that is contraction. A reader wanting a verdict gets two structural
    facts and no number, which is correct under the spec's MAY and is
    less useful than the verdict would be if the verdict were earned
    - NOTHING HERE MEASURES A FACILITY. Two of the three rows are
    stipulated to make the axes move, and the third is the operator's
    documented case carried without independent check. The instrument
    is a set of refusals with a worked example, not a survey
