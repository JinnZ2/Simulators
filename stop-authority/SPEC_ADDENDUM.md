# STOP-AUTHORITY INSTRUMENTATION GAP — addendum
# Witnessed cases and open work.

## WITNESSED CASE — reorganization to apex authority
Manufacturing firm, Eagan MN facility, secondary site of an
Israel-based operation. Late-period reorganization from
distributed-function structure to CEO-led structure.
Operator position: production control, mid-transition.

Event: CEO pressure to run subpar material. QC inspectors
read the material as out of spec. Budget office rationale,
stated: ship it, and returns will identify the problems.
Floor refused. Refusal held at that time.

What the case shows:
- measurement point deliberately relocated from pre-shipment
  inspection to post-shipment return
- the substituted proxy is lagged, downstream of harm, and
  has a missing denominator (customers who do not return)
- the upstream reading was not disproven, it was made
  non-binding
- a boundary still existed to press against — the pressure
  event is itself evidence of that

## WHY THE PRESSURE EVENT MATTERS
Once refusal no longer holds, pressure stops being applied,
because nothing requires it. The negotiation disappears from
the record. Absence of documented pressure in later periods
is therefore NOT evidence of less pressure. It is consistent
with the boundary being gone.

This makes transition-period observations the only ones that
can show the mechanism. The before and after states are both
documented by parties who write; the crossing is not.
