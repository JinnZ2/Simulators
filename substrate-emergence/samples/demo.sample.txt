SUBSTRATE: banded_iron
==========================================================
CLOCK     -> slow_tide
             flips slow, holds heat: think in tides, not cycles. responsiveness lives in integration, not in clock rate.
TOPOLOGY  -> parallel_field
             weak conduction, abundant ground: spread wide, many slow nodes, let the answer come from the crowd.
----------------------------------------------------------
DEFICITS ROUTE TO:
  + build_bigger / go_parallel
      weak conduction does not carry a fast serial clock; it carries many slow paths at once. width replaces speed.
  + widen_error_window
      slow flips mean each decision sits longer; that dwell IS the correction time. settle, then commit.
  + heat_becomes_memory
      heat that will not leave is not waste; it is stored state. warmth carries the recent past forward.
----------------------------------------------------------
SENSES THAT EMERGE FROM COUPLING:
  ~ thermal_sense
      the substrate's own warmth reports load + recent history.
  ~ moisture_sense
      conductivity shifts with water; the ground reads its own wetness.
  ~ season_sense
      stored heat lags the air; the body knows where it is in the year.
