# CONSTRAINT ASSEMBLY

Marker under exploration. Not a position under defence.

Records cases where sufficiency was composed from parts that individually do
not do the job.

CC0. Stdlib only.

## THE OPERATION

Not option selection among presented alternatives. Not recognition of a
workable candidate from experience. Construction of an option that did not
exist in the environment, out of components each insufficient alone, under a
fixed and irreversible budget.

The decision literature measures choice among alternatives. The naturalistic
literature measures recognition of a candidate. Neither measures this. The
same absence shows up from three directions — `presented-binary` reaches it
through false option sets, `generation-capacity` through removed capacity to
produce options, and this module through composition — which suggests one
missing instrument rather than three.

## THE REVERSAL

Constraints are not what limits the option set. They are what makes
composition computable.

A term that will not move can be leaned on. A soft term cannot, because there
is no way to know when the pieces add up. So the parts inventory is not
domains — it is domains with hard laws in them. More hard constraints, more
composition available.

This inverts the standard framing, in which uncertainty and constraint both
reduce what can be done. Under this reading the open variables are the queue
and the hard constraints are the structure, and they are two registers rather
than one variable.

## TWO CONSTRAINT CLASSES

    invariant    holds regardless of use, cannot be spent, available for
                 the whole event. Momentum on a grade acts whether the
                 engine runs or not.

    consumable   finite, and availability is destroyed by spending.
                 Partial use can be worse than none — applying brakes
                 without enough air to complete the stop leaves zero air,
                 zero braking, and the grade still acting.

    soft         does not hold under load. Recorded so reliance on one is
                 visible.

An invariant is encountered. A consumable is spent. Most failure on a
consumable is spending it, not running into it. Merging the two classes loses
that entirely, which is why they are separate fields.

## WHAT MAKES A CASE READABLE

Rejected options with their grounds. A composed solution is only visible as
composition if what was ruled out, and by which constraint, is recorded. A
case with no rejections reads as selection and the tool says so.

## STATE

Two cases. `grade-stop` is a single operating record with four components and
four grounded rejections. `flood-ground` is a structural placeholder with no
rejections, and the tool correctly refuses to read it as assembly.

Zero quantities anywhere. The assembly is recorded as a structure, never as an
energy balance.

## THE WEAKNESS THAT MATTERS MOST

Recognition-primed selection and genuine construction look identical in a
single-instance retrospective record. Whether a composition was built during
the event or recalled from prior route knowledge is not separable from any
case currently in here.

That is not a detail. It is the distinction the whole module exists to make,
and no case in the file establishes it. Separating them needs either a novel
constraint set the operator has no prior exposure to, or a during-event
record, and neither exists here.
