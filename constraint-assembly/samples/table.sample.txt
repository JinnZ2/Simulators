used  components used in the assembled solution
inv   invariant terms. hold regardless of use, cannot be spent
cons  consumable terms. finite, destroyed by spending
soft  terms that do not hold under load
comp  every used component insufficient alone, two or more
rej   options rejected, with the constraint that ruled each out
grnd  every rejection names a constraint

A case with rej 0 is selection, not assembly.
