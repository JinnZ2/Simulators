THE THREE-COLUMN TEST, run against the sim that proposed it

1  the stated form is additive; the quantity is a product
   gap = frag * wake_cost by construction, so `gap ~ count +
   duration` cannot represent it. Both fitted, same data:

   true p   | slope     intercept  R2       | b_count   b_dur     R2
   --------------------------------------------------------------------
   0.00     | -0.0022   0.0008     0.0001   | -0.0005   -0.0005   0.0000
   0.25     | 0.2436    0.0018     0.1096   | +0.0622   +0.4836   0.0951
   0.50     | 0.5036    -0.0011    0.2923   | +0.1281   +1.0066   0.2535
   0.75     | 0.7415    0.0014     0.5296   | +0.1874   +1.4823   0.4557
   1.00     | 0.9983    -0.0009    0.9164   | +0.2505   +1.9922   0.7805

   At p=1.0 the product form explains 0.916 and the additive form
   0.781 of the variance. The additive coefficients scale with p
   and neither of them IS p.

2  the slope of the product form estimates p
   max error across the five levels: 0.0085
   intercept stays at zero throughout, as it must if the
   true-reporters contribute a gap of zero.

   E[gap | product] = p * product exactly: a span-reporter's gap
   IS their WASO and a true-reporter's is zero, so the mixture's
   conditional mean is p times the product. The test does not
   only confirm or deny -- it MEASURES the quantity RESULTS.md
   said nobody reports.

3  the note's 'one flag' is worth a factor of two
   A self-report is a person's USUAL. A single night is one draw.
   Regressing on one night's fragmentation is errors-in-variables
   in the PREDICTOR, which attenuates toward zero.

   true p   1 night      7 nights     1-night      7-night
   --------------------------------------------------------------
   0.25     0.1230       0.2025       49% of p     81% of p
   0.50     0.2519       0.4169       50% of p     83% of p
   0.75     0.3733       0.6457       50% of p     86% of p
   1.00     0.5156       0.8776       52% of p     88% of p

   One night recovers about HALF of p. Seven recover most of it.
   The note offers the seven days as something you can 'also
   check'; they are load-bearing on the headline number, and a
   single-night design would report p at half its value.
