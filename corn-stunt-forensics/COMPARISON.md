### The caveat on pass 1's D4, and it is this audit's finding

3.8% and 33% have **different denominators**. The first is over insects
caught in traps; the second is over leaves somebody chose to submit, and
a submitted leaf is selected on already looking diseased. So the pair is
not a measured discrepancy between comparable quantities, and the "yet"
in the row is doing work it has not earned — `measurement-fork`'s VOID
RATIO, in the row that carries the most weight.

**The conclusion survives the correction.** Counts are not infectivity
regardless of the ratio, and that is what G3 turns on. What does not
survive is reading 3.8 against 33 as a factor of nine.

## The frame-cycle, and a second correction to `CSF_017`

Pass 1's frame-cycle is a six-row table with the columns *what counts as
a signal* and *what that frame can't see*. Those are components **3 (what
counts)** and **6 (blind spots)**, built per frame. With wording and focus
that is roughly **4 of 7**, against pass 2's 2 of 7.

So `CSF_017` was right that pass 2's finding was about a stub, and wrong
about which stub existed: **pass 1 ran the move deeper than pass 2 did,
and ran two of the components that decorrelate.**

**And D3 comes out of the blind-spot column.** Pass 1's `seasonal
calendar` row reads: what counts is *a hard dry or cold season empties
the host every year*; what it cannot see is *modern irrigation and double
cropping removed that break*. That is D3's mechanism, stated as a frame's
blind spot before D3 is named as a dissonance.

Which runs directly against pass 2's original finding. The frame-cycle
did not fail to produce a decorrelated reading; **pass 2 failed to build
the component that produces one**, and pass 1 shows the move working
when it is built. `CSF_022` records it.

## What the comparison establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes.** One convergent dissonance reached by two routes, one
suspicion reached by two unrelated routes, 4 of 5 gaps convergent, and
the decorrelating component identified by where D3 actually came from.
The gap list is the reproducible part; the dissonance set is not — 2 of
5 rows are unique to each pass.

**Does not establish.** Anything about independence (`CSF_011` stands),
anything about whether D3 is real (`CSF_002` stands), and anything about
the method against a control, since neither pass has a no-method arm.

The one thing worth saying about the method from here: **the two passes
agreed about where to dig and disagreed about what was strange.** If that
holds on a second subject it is a property worth knowing, because it
means the gap list travels and the dissonance set does not.
