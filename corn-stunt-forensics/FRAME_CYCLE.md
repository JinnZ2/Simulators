## 1. Why it is not "read it from several angles"

Naming frames and reading evidence under each is the cheap version, and
it is what this folder's PASS 2 actually ran. The move as stated requires
seven things **per frame**, before any evidence is read under it.

## 2. The seven components, per frame

| # | component | what it asks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **wording** | the vocabulary specific to that frame |
| 2 | **incentives** | what the parties inside the frame are rewarded for |
| 3 | **what counts** | which evidence is admissible to the frame at all |
| 4 | **what is left out** | the exclusions — what the frame does not carry |
| 5 | **focus** | where the frame's attention sits |
| 6 | **blind spots** | what the frame cannot see from where it stands |
| 7 | **assumptions** | what it takes as given without stating |

Then, **and only then**, the same seven for the next frame.

## 3. The consolidation step

After the frames are built: **find the variables, and attempt
consolidation.** Attempt is the operator's word and is load-bearing — the
step may fail, and failing is a result.

## 4. Which components decorrelate — this model's analysis, ASSERTED

The seven are not equally hard, and more importantly they are not equally
*useful*, because only some of them can make two frames genuinely
disagree.

| component | decorrelates? | why |
|---|---|---|
| wording (1) | **no** | retrieval. Two frames can share most of their words and read a field identically. |
| focus (5) | weakly | a difference in attention, not in standard |
| **incentives (2)** | **yes** | a frame paid for yield and one paid for publication read the same field differently with the same vocabulary |
| **what counts (3)** | **yes** | admissibility is a standard. One frame takes a trap catch as evidence; another needs a full causal chain. Same evidence, different status. |
| **what is left out (4)** | **yes** | an exclusion is a structural property and is invisible from inside the frame that holds it |
| blind spots (6) | yes, derived | mostly a consequence of 3 and 4 |
| assumptions (7) | **yes** | what a frame takes as given is where two frames most often silently differ |

**Status: ASSERTED, not measured.** The split is this model's reading
and has not been tested. The falsifier is cheap: run two frames
differing only in component 1, then two differing only in component 2,
and compare the dissonance yield.

## 5. The observer step — and why exiting is the wrong target

The operator states the requirement as *keeping myself out of it, and
knowing I am going to fail at that part but doing my best.*

The failure is structural rather than a matter of effort, and this tree
has already settled what to do about it: `declared-frame/` does not
remove the observer, it makes the position a **field**, so a reader can
subtract it. The authored-reference problem is not solved by succeeding
at exiting. It is solved by declaring where you stood.

**The asymmetry, this model's note.** A human operator can declare the
position — trade, region, which failures they have watched, which frame
they came up inside. A model cannot: its priors are a training
distribution it has no read access to, so it cannot supply the field
`declared-frame/` asks for. The operator fails at exiting and can
succeed at declaring. The model fails at both, and only one of those is
recoverable.

Consequence for this folder: any frame-cycle run by a model carries an
**undeclarable** observer position, which is a different and worse state
than an undeclared one.

## 6. The consolidation step is welding, run backwards

`category-weld/` is the instrument for a term that fuses two
independent quantities into one handle. Consolidation — deciding that two
frames' variables are one variable — is that same operation performed
deliberately. So the consolidation step's failure mode already has an
instrument in the tree, and the decision carries the consolidator's own
frame, which is where §5's observer problem enters most strongly.

No consolidation was attempted in either pass of this folder.
