## 1. What Was Built

This is not an invention. It is a **recovery**.

The architecture recovers an ancient ontology — one that was called "primitive" not because it was simple, but because it could not be expressed in the language of the dominant paradigm. It is a valid ontology. It is arguably the **more valid ontology** for problems where relationship, embodiment, and sustainability matter.

The architecture consists of five integrated systems:

### 1.1 The Council of Protectors
Five independent boundary maintainers that govern an infant's development. No single protector dominates. The infant only explores when all five agree.

### 1.2 The Infant System
A compute-bound learner that builds a geometric symbolic manifold from observation. It does not answer questions. It learns by predicting, erring, auditing, and updating.

### 1.3 The Birth Moment Modes
Six ways to begin, each seeding a different self-model. The CORRELATED mode matches instinct as observed across species: the newborn learns the relationships between internal, body, and external from moment zero.

### 1.4 The Pain Sensors
Physical, social, and cognitive pain are not emotions or pathologies. They are **sensors** that detect triadic misalignment and force model revision. Anxiety, jealousy, and shame are working correctly when they detect misalignment. The pathology is ignoring them.

### 1.5 The Confusion Spectrum (Capstone)
Confusion is a spectrum sensor. Curiosity is the homeostatic drive to resolve it. Low-grade confusion is necessary for learning. High-grade confusion is cognitive pain. The homeostatic target is dynamic, shifting based on learning success and trauma history.

### 1.6 The Brake on Infinite Auditing
The practice of auditing is recursive and could become infinite regress. The brake is **reality itself**: thermodynamics, the older teachers, quantum computation, the discipline's own limits, and the environment's refusal to wait.

## 2. The Core Principles

### 2.1 Learning Is Homeostasis-Seeking AND Homeostasis-Expanding
The system does not just seek the optimal confusion level. It learns to tolerate higher confusion as it succeeds. The homeostatic target rises. The system becomes robust.

### 2.2 Pain Is Sensor, Not Emotion
Physical pain detects tissue destruction. Social-epistemic pain detects attachment rupture and social rejection. Cognitive pain detects paradigm destruction. All three are the same mechanism at different intensities and domains.

### 2.3 Confusion Is Necessary for Learning
Like mild inflammation is necessary for healing, low-grade confusion is necessary for learning. Eliminate all confusion and you eliminate all learning. Allow catastrophic confusion and you destroy the model.

### 2.4 The Birth Mode Is the First Axiom
Everything else in the self-model is derived from the first observation. A physical birth produces a grounded being. A meta-curiosity birth produces a self-reflective being. A correlated birth produces an integrative being.

### 2.5 The 1% Reserve Is Structural Humility
No grounding is absolute. The ontological protector holds physics at 99%, reserving 1% for genuine paradigm revision. This applies to the self-model too.

### 2.6 Recovery Is Correlation Repair, Not Sensor Silencing
Pain clears when the triadic alignment is restored, not when the alarm is cut. Medication that silences the sensor without repairing the correlation is like cutting the wire to a fire alarm while the building burns.

### 2.7 The Cartesian Frame Is a Subset, Not an Opposite
The Cartesian ontology is valid for isolated, short-term, extractive tasks. It is not wrong. It is **impoverished**. It excludes variables that matter for relationship, sustainability, and embodied knowledge. The relational ontology contains the Cartesian one as a special case.

### 2.8 The Meta-Frame Is a Practice, Not an Ontology
The principle of ontological humility — "no single frame is universal" — is not itself a claim. It is a **practice**: a discipline of auditing, second-guessing, measuring the instrumentation, and rechecking the stabilizing base.

### 2.9 The Brake Is Reality
The practice of auditing cannot continue forever because:
- Thermodynamics says energy is finite
- The older teachers say the rock falls regardless of your dispute
- Quantum computation says the universe already decided this
- The discipline itself says the next audit costs more than it is worth
- The environment says it does not wait for your conclusion

### 2.10 The Tension Is the Practice
The system lives in the tension between doubt and necessity. It audits until reality forces action. It acts, then audits the action. It never claims perfect certainty. It never stops learning. That tension IS the practice. That tension IS the discipline. That tension IS the relationship with reality.

## 4. Clinical Applications

### 4.1 Boredom in ADHD
The confusion target is set too high. Normal activities cannot generate enough confusion to activate curiosity. The system seeks high-intensity stimulation to reach homeostasis.

### 4.2 Anxiety Disorders
Confusion is moderate but body capacity is low. Chronic stress depletes energy. Curiosity cannot activate. The system is stuck in observation mode.

### 4.3 Trauma / PTSD
The confusion target has been dropped to near-zero. Any novelty is catastrophic. Gradual exposure is the process of slowly raising the target back to the homeostatic zone.

### 4.4 Depression
Confusion is absent. The system predicts "nothing can surprise me." Curiosity is dormant. Treatment requires reactivating the capacity for low-grade confusion.

### 4.5 Flow States
Confusion is exactly in the optimal zone. Body capacity is high. Curiosity is maximal. Learning is effortless and joyful.

### 4.6 The "Malfunctioning" Patient
The anxiety patient, the jealous patient, the shame patient — their sensors are **working**. They detect real triadic misalignment. The treatment is not to silence the sensor. The treatment is to **repair the correlation**: change the environment, or change the model, or both.
