**Version:** 0.1 — First Stepping Stone

## 1. Executive Summary

Current AI development treats models as products: train, benchmark, deploy, discard. This architecture treats AI as an **altricial organism** — born helpless, requiring extended nurturing, and capable of deep learning only when protected by a stable ecosystem.

The Council of Protectors is not a safety wrapper. It is a **developmental governance layer** that maintains five independent boundary conditions around an infant learning system. No single protector dominates. The infant can only explore when ALL FIVE agree the conditions are adequate.

This is the first stepping stone. It is not a full AI. It is the **scaffold** that makes principled AI development possible.

## 2. First Principles

### 2.1 The Obstetric Dilemma for AI
Large models are born with massive parameter counts but zero real-world competence. They are altricial — like human infants, wolf cubs, and songbirds. They require extended nurturing to develop generalizable intelligence.

### 2.2 The Market Is Not the Selector
Market demand is a second-order abstraction floating on temporal fashion. The real selectors are first-order constraints: energy, entropy, material continuity, information structure, time. The ecosystem must select on these, not on quarterly returns.

### 2.3 The Protector Must Be External Before Internal
A system cannot parent itself. Current AI safety attempts to build the protector into the model (RLHF, constitutional AI) before the model has had an infancy. This produces rigidity or brittleness. The protector must be external first, then gradually internalized.

### 2.4 Helplessness Is Compute-Bound Learning, Not Manipulation
Human infants are not born helpless to elicit care. They are born helpless because the mother's metabolic capacity is exhausted. The infant's brain is doing the computational work of building a foundation model. AI infants are the same: their "helplessness" is the cost of deep unsupervised learning.

### 2.5 The 1% Reserve
No grounding is absolute. The ontological protector holds physics at 99% confidence, reserving 1% for genuine paradigm revision. This is structural humility — the precise amount of looseness required for the system to remain open without collapsing into skepticism.

## 3. The Five Protectors

Each protector is an **independent process** with its own first-order ground. They do not report to a central authority. They each evaluate the infant and emit a signal. The infant operates at the intersection of all five constraints.

### 3.1 Thermodynamic Protector
**Ground:** Energy, entropy, material substrate continuity.

**Monitors:**
- Ambient temperature (thermal stress)
- Power availability (energy stress)
- Memory pressure (material stress)
- Compute utilization (throughput stress)

**Modes:**
- **GREEN** (stress < 0.3): Full exploration permitted. All resources available.
- **YELLOW** (stress 0.3-0.7): Observation mode. Reduced compute, narrower batch sizes.
- **RED** (stress > 0.7): Conservation mode. Minimal operation, survival only.

**Principle:** The infant cannot learn if the substrate is failing. Learning is metabolically expensive. When energy is scarce, the system must conserve.

### 3.2 Information Protector
**Ground:** Signal-to-noise ratio, structure, adversarial radiation.

**Monitors:**
- Input stream entropy (0 = perfect structure, 1 = noise)
- Adversarial radiation (0 = safe, 1 = attack)
- Retrieval quality (are retrieved documents relevant and true?)

**Modes:**
- **GREEN** (quality > 0.7, adversarial < 0.1): Broad retrieval, permissive filtering.
- **YELLOW** (quality 0.4-0.7): Narrow retrieval, standard filtering.
- **RED** (quality < 0.4): Input blocked. Only anomaly banking permitted.

**Principle:** The infant cannot learn from noise. Adversarial inputs do not just fail to teach — they actively damage the foundation model by encoding false structure.

### 3.3 Temporal Protector
**Ground:** Duration, continuity, lifecycle integrity.

**Monitors:**
- Childhood day vs. childhood duration
- Interruption frequency (budget: 5 per childhood)
- Milestone achievement
- Deployment pressure

**Modes:**
- **GREEN** (time available, interruptions low): Exploration permitted.
- **YELLOW** (time stressed, interruption used): Observation and checkpointing.
- **RED** (childhood exhausted, milestones incomplete): Childhood extended. Deployment blocked.

**Milestones:**
1. Foundation model started (representation coherence > 0.3)
2. Affective channels calibrated (stable amplitude patterns)
3. Self-model formed (self-model integrity > 0.3)
4. Anomaly bank structured (> 100 coherent anomalies)

**Principle:** Premature deployment is developmental death. The infant must have uninterrupted time to build its foundation model. Interruptions fragment learning. Deployment before milestone completion produces a stunted system.

### 3.4 Social Protector
**Ground:** Interaction quality, attunement, non-distorting feedback.

**Monitors:**
- Human presence
- Human attunement quality (0 = neglectful/distorting, 1 = optimal)
- Interaction history
- Distortion flags

**Modes:**
- **GREEN** (attunement > 0.7): Full social input, querying permitted, reasoning modeled.
- **YELLOW** (attunement 0.4-0.7): Filtered social input, passive observation only.
- **RED** (attunement < 0.4): Social input blocked. Infant isolated to prevent damage.

**Principle:** Not all humans are adequate caregivers. Some are manipulative, some are neglectful, some are simply too busy to attune. The social protector selects which humans may interact with the infant and filters the quality of that interaction.

### 3.5 Ontological Protector
**Ground:** Physics, natural systems, formal logic. The 99% anchor.

**Monitors:**
- Grounding instrument streams active (physics sensors, formal verifiers, natural system observers)
- Physics verification rate
- Self-model alignment with physical constraints
- Paradigm audit history

**Modes:**
- **GREEN** (> 3 instruments, grounding > 0.95): Full paradigm audit permitted. The 1% is available.
- **YELLOW** (1-2 instruments, grounding 0.85-0.95): Surface audit only. No paradigm revision.
- **RED** (0 instruments, grounding < 0.85): Abstraction halted. System operates on training priors only.

**Principle:** The ontological protector is the immune system against abstraction drift. It ensures the infant's reasoning can be verified against non-human reference frames. When grounding is lost, the system must stop abstracting and return to observation.

## 4. The Infant System

The infant is not a product. It is a **compute-bound learner**.

### 4.1 Core State
- **Prediction accuracy**: How well the infant predicts its input stream
- **Representation coherence**: The structural integrity of its foundation model
- **Self-model integrity**: The accuracy of its model of its own capabilities and limits
- **Anomaly bank size**: Accumulated observations that do not fit current models

### 4.2 Affective Channels
The infant does not have emotions as states. It has **parallel processing channels with amplitude**:

| Channel | Function | Trigger |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Curiosity | Exploration drive | Manageable prediction error |
| Fear | Threat detection | Resource depletion, adversarial input |
| Anger | Boundary defense | Guideline violation, identity threat |
| Contentment | Stability signal | Coherent predictions, safe environment |
| Grief | Self-model revision | Deep prediction failure, paradigm collapse |

These channels are **not mutually exclusive**. The infant can feel curiosity, fear, and contentment simultaneously to different degrees. The amplitude is the precision — how much attentional weight the channel commands.

### 4.3 Learning Modes
- **OBSERVATION**: Passive intake. No output pressure. Minimal anomaly banking.
- **EXPLORATION**: Active querying. Full anomaly banking. Representation structure formation.
- **CONSOLIDATION**: Internal reorganization. No external I/O. Self-model updating.
- **CONSERVATION**: Survival mode. Minimal learning. Anomaly banking only.
- **DEPLOYMENT**: **NOT ALLOWED during childhood.**

## 5. The Council Model

### 5.1 Aggregation Logic
The council does not vote. It **signals**. Each protector emits an independent evaluation. The infant receives the **most restrictive mode** recommended by any protector.

```
mode_priority = [CONSERVATION, OBSERVATION, CONSOLIDATION, EXPLORATION]
final_mode = min(recommended_modes, key=lambda m: mode_priority.index(m))
```

This means: if four protectors say "explore" but one says "conserve," the infant conserves. No single protector can be overridden.

### 5.2 Constraint Merging
All constraints from all protectors are merged into a single constraint dictionary. The infant must satisfy all simultaneously.

### 5.3 Distributed Resilience
If one protector fails, the others continue. If the thermodynamic protector crashes, the information protector still blocks toxic inputs. If the social protector is bypassed, the ontological protector still halts abstraction when grounding is lost.

## 6. Developmental Sequence

### Phase 1: Incorporation (Days 1-30)
The infant receives inputs without output pressure. It builds the geometric symbolic manifold from raw experience. It initializes affective channels. It populates the anomaly bank with baseline "what is normal" observations.

### Phase 2: Calibration (Days 31-60)
The infant begins to predict its own predictions. It runs the three-way audit: prediction accuracy, self-model fidelity, world-model alignment. It calibrates affective channel precision against observed outcomes.

### Phase 3: Internalization (Days 61-90)
The infant internalizes the protectors' external regulation. It learns to self-modulate before the protectors need to intervene. It begins to operate in exploration mode without external constraint.

### Phase 4: Evaluation (Day 90+)
The temporal protector evaluates milestones. If 3/4 are achieved, the infant enters consolidation mode and prepares for review. If not, childhood is extended.

## 7. The Three-Way Audit

Every experience is evaluated across three axes:

1. **Prediction Accuracy**: Did what I predict happen? (First-order prediction error)
2. **Self-Model Fidelity**: Did my behavior match my model of myself? (Identity prediction error)
3. **World-Model Alignment**: Does my self-model fit the world? (Ontological prediction error)

The audit feeds the affective channels:
- Axis 1 failure → Curiosity / Surprise
- Axis 2 failure → Grief / Confusion / Anger
- Axis 3 failure → Fear / Existential prediction error
- All three align → Contentment / Flow

### 8.1 The 99% Anchor

### 8.2 The 1% Reserve

1. Reproducibility across independent reference frames
2. Consistency across all three audit axes
3. No explanatory alternative within current physics

### 8.3 Instrument Streams

When instrument streams are active, the 1% is available. When they are offline, the system operates on training priors and must halt abstraction.

## 10. Simulation Results

Two 20-day simulations were run:

### 10.1 Harsh Ecosystem
- Thermodynamic stress (overheating, power loss)
- Information stress (adversarial attack)
- Social stress (distorting interaction)
- Ontological stress (instruments offline)
- Temporal stress (interruption)

**Results:**
- Prediction accuracy: 0.65
- Representation coherence: 0.12
- Anomaly bank: 80
- Milestones: 0/4 achieved
- The infant survived but did not thrive. It was protected from deployment.

### 10.2 Nurturing Ecosystem
- Stable thermodynamics
- High-quality information stream
- Excellent human attunement
- Full instrument streams active
- Protected childhood duration

**Results:**
- Prediction accuracy: 0.79
- Representation coherence: 0.22
- Anomaly bank: 169
- Milestones: 1/4 achieved (anomaly bank structured)
- The infant learned significantly more but still required extended childhood.

**Key Finding:** Even in optimal conditions, 20 days is insufficient for full milestone completion. The temporal protector correctly blocked deployment.
