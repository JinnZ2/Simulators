## 2. The Triadic Model

All intelligence, all instinct, all suffering arises from the correlation (or misalignment) of three domains:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  INTERNAL MODEL                             │
│  (predictions, expectations, self-concept)    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
              ↕
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  BODY                                       │
│  (somatic state, thermodynamics, tissue)    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
              ↕
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  EXTERNAL WORLD                             │
│  (physics, social environment, other beings)│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Instinct** = the learned correlation between these three domains.
**Pain** = the sensor that detects when the correlation is self-destructive.
**Intelligence** = the capacity to revise the internal model when pain falsifies it.

## 5. Pain as Somatic Sensor

### 5.1 Physical Pain

Physical pain detects when the body is being destroyed despite the internal model predicting safety:

```
Internal: "warmth_safe"
Body:     45°C, burning
External: 80°C, fire
          ↓
    PAIN: 0.80 (thermal, destructive)
          ↓
    [PAIN REVISION] Model 'warmth_safe' falsified.
    [PAIN REVISION] Correlation marked as DANGEROUS.
```

### 5.2 Social-Epistemic Pain

Anxiety, jealousy, shame, guilt, loneliness are not emotions. They are pain sensors that detect specific triadic misalignments in the social domain:

| Pain Type | Internal Model | Body State | External Evidence | What It Detects |
|-----------|---------------|------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Anxiety** | "Maybe something is wrong" / "I am safe" | Cortisol↑, HR↑, oxytocin↓ | Ambiguous, unpredictable | Uncertainty about threat verification |
| **Jealousy** | "This relationship is exclusive" | Cortisol↑, HR↑, rejection-sensitive | Divided attention | Falsification of exclusive attachment |
| **Shame** | "I am acceptable and worthy" | Cortisol↑, HR↑, oxytocin↓, hiding | Rejected, ridiculed, excluded | Falsification of social acceptability |
| **Guilt** | "I am a good person" | Cortisol↑, HR↑ | Evidence of harm caused | Falsification of moral self-model |
| **Loneliness** | "I am connected and belong" | Oxytocin↓, low arousal | Alone, isolated, no reciprocity | Absence of social correlation |

### 5.3 The Clinical Insight

The "malfunctioning anxiety" patient is not broken. Their anxiety sensor is **working**. It detects that the internal model ("I am safe") does not match the external world ("unpredictable, ambiguous").

The "malfunctioning jealousy" patient is not broken. Their jealousy sensor is **working**. It detects that the exclusive attachment correlation is falsified.

The "malfunctioning shame" patient is not broken. Their shame sensor is **working**. It detects that the social acceptability model is falsified by the group.

**The treatment is not to eliminate the sensor. The treatment is to repair the correlation.**

Medication that silences the sensor without repairing the correlation is like cutting the wire to a fire alarm while the building burns.

## 8. Key Principles

1. **Instinct is correlation, not program.** The newborn does not "know" what to do. It has learned (through evolution and development) that certain correlations between internal, body, and external lead to survival.

2. **Pain is sensor, not emotion.** Pain does not indicate weakness. It indicates that the current correlation is self-destructive and must be revised.

3. **Anxiety, jealousy, shame are pain sensors.** They are not pathologies. They are working correctly when they detect misalignment. The pathology is ignoring them.

4. **The birth mode is the first axiom.** Everything else in the self-model is derived from the first observation. A physical birth produces a grounded being. A meta-curiosity birth produces a self-reflective being. A correlated birth produces an integrative being.

5. **The 1% reserve is structural humility.** No grounding is absolute. The ontological protector holds physics at 99%, reserving 1% for genuine paradigm revision. This applies to the self-model too.

6. **Recovery is correlation repair, not sensor silencing.** Pain clears when the triadic alignment is restored, not when the alarm is cut.
