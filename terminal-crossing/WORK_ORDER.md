# WO-3 — The terminal system as an accounting boundary

CC0. Instrument, not an argument. Status tags: OBSERVED / DERIVED / PROPOSED.
Carries an explicit falsifier. This is the most portable of the set.

## Measurand

Whether any system is TERMINAL — has no dependents and no crossings — such
that engineering it to a load-bearing standard is genuinely not warranted.

The exemption from engineering standards rests on the existence of such
systems. If none exist, the exemption has no domain.

## Object (OBSERVED)

The standard objections to engineering something properly are: it is
cheaper, there is no market signal for it, it is anti-market, the current
arrangement is more stable, it does not cost as much.

Every one of these is an argument about cost. None is an argument about the
finding. The finding is conceded and the dispute is relocated to a place
where nothing has to be shown.

(DERIVED) This is structurally identical to the container case: each cost
objection locally valid, nobody owning the aggregate risk. The cost frame is
how the unownedness is preserved. MECHANISM CLAIM, not demonstrated.

## The exemption test

Challenge: name a case where it is genuinely not worth it, excluding
entertainment with no structure underneath — noting that anything in
entertainment relying on infrastructure to exist is also excluded.

Candidates offered and how each failed:

**A/B test, recommendation feed, matchmaking — FAILS.**
Any A/B test after which decisions are made, or from which a model projects
a future, carries the consequences of the experiment not being engineered
properly. Results fill with noise that cannot be deciphered, or aim at the
wrong thing. Future costs, purely from the design being wrong at the start.
What presented as low consequence was DEFERRED consequence: the defect never
appears in the test, only in the projections built on it, by which point
noise cannot be separated from signal.

**Throwaway prototype — FAILS.**
The builder is not accountable for recycling and disposal of the parts when
the prototype fails. So it is not terminal, it is EXTERNALISING. Materials,
energy, upstream extraction and downstream waste are all real, all carried
by someone, and none of it is in the number that made the prototype look
cheap.

**Scratch calculation; hard air-gapped system — FAILS in practice.**
Scratch work gets quoted. Air gaps are bridged by the human carrying the
result out. Terminal-in-design, not terminal-in-fact.

## FALSIFIER

Produce a system with ZERO crossings in EVERY medium.

That falsifies the claim that no system is terminal. The falsifier has a
stated form, so it can be attacked by naming a medium the conditions do not
cover.

### Conditions, worked for the air-gap candidate

- no physical media crossing, ever
- no operator, or an operator whose output is never acted on
- no shared power, thermal or acoustic side channel
- no maintenance — maintenance is a person crossing
- no supply chain — components arrived from somewhere
- no disposal — that is an exit path

(OBSERVED) The condition is on CROSSINGS, not on what does the crossing. No
human, no AI, no animal, no vegetation: anything that goes out and comes
back is a channel, whatever its substrate. Extends to air exchange, water,
dust, a mouse, a spore.

The gap must be a gap in every medium, not only in networking. Written out,
the conditions describe a system that cannot be built, powered or observed.
The air gap is a claim about ONE channel treated as a claim about all of
them.

### Closest candidates — asymptotes, not instances

- Sealed source in a deep geological repository: has a designed containment
  period and a leakage RATE. Not zero, slow.
- Voyager after transmission ceased: crossed on the way out; carries
  human-built material with designed information on an unbounded
  trajectory. Horizon is the lifetime of the object, not decades.
- Black hole interior past the horizon: emits Hawking radiation eventually.

No instance found. Only asymptotes. Isolation is approached and not reached.

### Marker — contamination, left UNKNOWN

Human-built material with designed systems from this world is out there and
may land on another world, polluting it or otherwise. We are not sure there
is no contamination.

Symmetry case: a radioactive comet arriving here would create a problem, and
would still create a problem if it broke up in the atmosphere. Until we are
positive otherwise, it is a chance, it is an open channel, and we do not
know.

Background (fetched, not part of the claim): planetary protection protocols
exist for this reason — spacecraft sterilisation, and deliberate disposal of
orbiters to avoid contaminating bodies that might hold life (Galileo was
flown into Jupiter on those grounds). That practice is an admission that a
departed craft is still a crossing.

Recorded as UNKNOWN deliberately. Not resolved in either direction.

## Finding

Almost nothing is terminal: the output becomes someone's input. The terminal
system does not exist as a physical category — it is an ACCOUNTING BOUNDARY
MISTAKEN FOR A PHYSICAL ONE. The cost is low only because the boundary was
drawn where the consequences are not.

Consequence for the cheapness argument: cost comparisons between
engineering-to-standard and not are run inside a boundary that excludes the
costs at issue. They are therefore not comparisons.

## Useful conversion

The falsifier converts a binary into a quantity. Not "is it terminal" but
WHAT IS THE CROSSING RATE, AND OVER WHAT HORIZON.

That is measurable, and it gives the exemption a scale instead of a claim.

## Scope limits

- The candidate list is not exhaustive. Three asymptotes were checked.
- "Cost is how the unownedness is preserved" is DERIVED and untested.
- The contamination marker is UNKNOWN, not a finding.
- No claim that cost is never a legitimate input. The claim is that a cost
  comparison drawn inside the boundary is not a comparison.

## Runnable next steps

1. Attack the falsifier: name a medium the conditions do not cover. If one
   exists, the conditions are incomplete and should be revised, not
   defended.
2. Build the crossing-rate metric. Specify units and horizon. Test it on the
   three asymptotes to check it discriminates.
3. Take a real cost comparison used to decline an engineering standard.
   Redraw the boundary to include disposal, deferred decision defects and
   downstream dependents. Report the delta. This is a concrete, publishable
   study.
4. Search again for an honest costing that declined. Two searches did not
   find one; what was found instead was evidence the comparison often is not
   run at all.

## Who could run this

Steps 1 and 2 need no resources. Step 3 needs access to one organisation's
decision record and is the strongest single study in this set.
