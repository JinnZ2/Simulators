
OVERTHINKING: A DECLINING BRANCH, AND WHAT IT COSTS THE KNEE
No reasoning model was run. Every constant is generator-level.

==========================================================================
1  the model in dial_response.py forbids the phenomenon
==========================================================================
  SOURCE_DROP_2 reports negative marginal utility as the central
  empirical finding: past some budget, more thinking makes answers
  worse. A logistic is monotone, so dial_response.py cannot produce
  that at any parameter setting.

  problem                  min dQ/dlnB over 2..1e7 tokens
  Easy (pattern match)                        1.041e-08
  Medium (multi-step)                         1.687e-04
  Hard (novel mechanism)                      6.270e-03

  Positive throughout. This is a correction to this folder's own
  prior work, not to the drop's: RD_003 picked a response family
  that rules out the effect by construction, and then reported that
  the family behaves well. It does. It is also the wrong family.

==========================================================================
2  with a declining branch the stopping rule becomes well-posed
==========================================================================
  Q(u) = logistic rise - drift accruing at 0.030 per log-token from
  B = 50. Drift does not depend on D_r.

  problem                      argmax Q    Q there   interior
  Easy (pattern match)              444      0.914       True
  Medium (multi-step)              8194      0.805       True
  Hard (novel mechanism)         224214      0.675       True

  dQ/d(ln B) = 0 has ONE solution on the declining side. No tie, no
  choice of shoulder, no dependence on the sweep window -- the
  interior column is the guard and it passes for all three.

  RD_002 found the knee rule degenerate on a saturating curve. On
  the curve the literature actually reports, the degeneracy is not
  merely a defect: it is unnecessary. A better primitive exists,
  and it is the one the surveyed work uses -- marginal utility
  crossing zero, not curvature reaching a maximum.

==========================================================================
3  on this shape the knee rule stops far too early
==========================================================================
  problem                      argmax Q    knee rule   knee/opt
  Easy (pattern match)              444           76       0.17
  Medium (multi-step)              8194          680       0.08
  Hard (novel mechanism)         224214        14024       0.06

  The knee lands at 6%-17% of the optimal budget, always
  early, and the error grows with difficulty -- worst exactly where
  the budget matters most.

  So the knee is not a conservative version of the optimum. It is a
  different point on the curve, and reading it as a stopping rule
  costs the hardest problems most of their budget.

==========================================================================
4  'easier problems flip earlier' is a cross-gradient result
==========================================================================
  SOURCE_DROP_2 quotes the surveyed work: 'easier problems (Level
  1-2) reach negative marginal utility earlier than hard problems.'

  That sentence is a statement about d(argmax Q)/d(difficulty) --
  how the zero of the budget-gradient moves with problem structure.
  It is the mixed partial, reported in words.

  problem                     D_r       argmax Q
  Easy (pattern match)        0.5            444
  Medium (multi-step)         2.0           8194
  Hard (novel mechanism)      4.0         224214

  monotone increasing? True

  And here it is a CONSEQUENCE, not an input. Drift is the same for
  all three problems; only the rise moves. An easy problem finishes
  rising sooner, so the fixed drift overtakes it sooner. The
  ordering falls out of making overthinking-damage a property of
  the reasoning system rather than of the problem.

  That is a structural prediction with a cheap falsifier: measure
  the drift rate on problems of different difficulty. If it is
  flat, the ordering needs no separate explanation. If it varies
  with difficulty, this model is wrong and the ordering is an
  independent empirical fact.

==========================================================================
READING
==========================================================================

  The second drop does three things to the first.

  It REFUTES the response family. A saturating curve cannot produce negative
  marginal utility, so dial_response.py could not have represented the effect
  the surveyed work is about. That is a correction to this folder's model,
  recorded as RD_010.

  It REPLACES the knee. On a curve that declines, the stopping point is where
  the gradient crosses zero -- unique, interior, and independent of the sweep
  window. Every objection RD_002 raised against argmax|curvature| dissolves,
  not because the objection was wrong but because the rule is unnecessary.
  Section 3 measures what keeping it would cost: a stop at 6-17% of the
  optimal budget, worst on the hardest problems.

  It UNDERCUTS the novelty claim. SOURCE_DROP_2 closes by saying no surveyed
  paper computes cross-gradients, and that formalising them is the
  contribution on offer. But the same document quotes a surveyed paper
  reporting that easier problems reach negative marginal utility earlier than
  hard ones -- which is the mixed partial, measured and stratified by
  difficulty, stated in prose. The quantity is already an object of study.
  What is not yet standard is the notation.

  That is a smaller contribution than claimed and still a real one. Section 4
  is the argument for it: written as a derivative, the ordering stops being a
  fact to report and becomes a consequence to derive, with a falsifier
  attached. Prose does not do that.

  None of this checks whether the surveyed papers exist or say what the
  survey says they say. Their citation markers point outside the delivery,
  and the dates sit at the edge of what can be checked from here. Every
  finding above is internal: the framework against its own model, and the
  survey against its own quotations.
