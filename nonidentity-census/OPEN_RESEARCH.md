The Problem / 问题

English 中文
In standard research practice, the unit of analysis is often treated as a given — chosen by the field, inherited from prior work, or determined by data availability. When a study says "the population declined," that is treated as a statement about a thing. When a study says "the market tightened," that is treated as a statement about a thing. 在标准研究实践中，分析单元通常被视为给定的——由领域选择、继承自先前工作或由数据可用性决定。当一项研究说“种群数量下降了”时，它被视为关于一个事物的陈述。当一项研究说“市场收紧了”时，它被视为关于一个事物的陈述。

But population and market are not the same kind of unit. One is a persistent, individuated entity that carries properties across time. The other is a process, a flow, a relation — and whether it counts as identity‑bearing depends on the claim, not the noun. | 但 population 和 market 并不是同一种单元。一个是持久的、个体化的实体，能够跨时间承载属性。另一个是过程、流动、关系——它是否算作具有同一性取决于声明，而非名词。

Six Ways the Connection Gets Lost / 六种连接丢失的方式

1. The "Noun as Unit" Fallacy / “名词作为单元”谬误

English 中文
Many studies treat the noun as the unit of analysis. If the study says "the market tightened," that is treated as a statement about a thing. 许多研究将名词视为分析单元。如果研究说“市场收紧了”，它就被当作关于一个事物的陈述。

But market is resolved at the claim, not at the noun. the labour market tightened predicates a property on a persistent individuated thing: identity‑bearing. prices allocate scarce goods predicates on a mechanism: non‑identity. Same noun, two answers. If the study says "the market tightened," it is not wrong for the noun, but it may be wrong for the unit. The claim was causal — just not represented. | 但 market 是在声明层面解决的，而非名词层面。the labour market tightened 将一个属性谓述给一个持久的、个体化的事物：具有同一性。prices allocate scarce goods 将一个属性谓述给一个机制：非同一性。同一个名词，两个答案。如果研究说“市场收紧了”，对于名词并非错误，但对于单元可能是错误的。声明是因果性的——只是没有被表述。

2. The "Population as Obvious" Fallacy / “种群作为显而易见”谬误

English 中文
Many studies treat population as obviously identity‑bearing. If the study says "the population declined," that is treated as a statement about a thing. 许多研究将种群视为明显具有同一性。如果研究说“种群数量下降了”，它就被当作关于一个事物的陈述。

But the decision that population counts as identity‑bearing moves the count most, and it moves it against the work order's own prediction. Ecology does not get the score its substrate was expected to buy it. If the study says "the population declined," it is not wrong for the population, but it may be wrong for the inference about ecology's substrate. The decision was causal — just not represented. | 但将种群视为具有同一性的决定对计数影响最大，并且它违背了工作指令自身的预测。生态学并未获得其底物预期能带来的分数。如果研究说“种群数量下降了”，对于种群并非错误，但对于关于生态学底物的推论可能是错误的。该决定是因果性的——只是没有被表述。

3. The "Lexical Search as Detection" Fallacy / “词汇搜索作为检测”谬误

English 中文
Many studies use lexical search to detect framings. If the study says "the search found X," that is treated as a finding. 许多研究使用词汇搜索来检测框架。如果研究说“搜索发现 X”，它就被当作一个发现。

But non‑identity work does not announce itself, and identity assumption is PREMISE, not vocabulary. The detector built to escape lexical detection decides most cases lexically — 10 of 12, 83%, decided by a word list. If the study says "the search found X," it is not wrong for the search, but it may be wrong for the framing. The lexical fallback was causal — just not represented. | 但非同一性工作不会自我宣告，而同一性假设是前提，而非词汇。被设计用来逃离词汇检测的检测器，其大多数决定都是通过词汇方式做出的——12 个中有 10 个（83%）是由一个词表决定的。如果研究说“搜索发现 X”，对于搜索并非错误，但对于框架可能是错误的。词汇回退是因果性的——只是没有被表述。

4. The "Binary as Sufficient" Fallacy / “二元作为充分”谬误

English 中文
Many studies treat binary classifications as sufficient. If the study says "the framing is identity‑bearing or not," that is treated as a finding. 许多研究将二元分类视为充分的。如果研究说“该框架具有同一性或不具有”，它就被当作一个发现。

But the verb‑first test produces six states, not two. VERB_CARRIES_IT marks a claim where the carrier may have been folded into a nominalization. Collapsing it hides an identity framing wearing a noun. If the study says "the framing is non‑identity," it is not wrong for the binary, but it may be wrong for the mechanism. The collapsed state was causal — just not represented. | 但动词前置测试产生六种状态，而非两种。VERB_CARRIES_IT 标记了一种声明，其中载体可能已被折叠进名词化中。将其合并会隐藏一个披着名词外衣的同一性框架。如果研究说“该框架是非同一性的”，对于二元分类并非错误，但对于机制可能是错误的。被合并的状态是因果性的——只是没有被表述。

5. The "One Window as Enough" Fallacy / “一个窗口作为足够”谬误

English 中文
Many studies treat a single window as sufficient. If the study says "the window is X," that is treated as a finding. 许多研究将单一窗口视为充分的。如果研究说“窗口是 X”，它就被当作一个发现。

But the dissolution‑window proposal requires two numbers, not one. W_dissolve and W_measure are different quantities, and the case where they do not coincide is the informative one. If the study says "the window is X," it is not wrong for one window, but it may be wrong for the other. The conflation was causal — just not represented. | 但消解窗口提案需要两个数字，而非一个。W_dissolve 和 W_measure 是不同的量，而它们不重合的情况才是有信息量的。如果研究说“窗口是 X”，对于一个窗口并非错误，但对于另一个可能是错误的。混淆是因果性的——只是没有被表述。

6. The "Field as Container" Fallacy / “领域作为容器”谬误

English 中文
Many studies treat the field as a container for its units. If the study says "ecology's units are non‑identity," that is treated as a finding. 许多研究将领域视为其单元的容器。如果研究说“生态学的单元是非同一性的”，它就被当作一个发现。

But under D2, ecology's most common unit — the population — is identity‑bearing. Ecology does not get the score its substrate was expected to buy it. If the study says "ecology's units are non‑identity," it is not wrong for the field's reputation, but it may be wrong for the actual units. The field boundary was causal — just not represented. | 但根据 D2，生态学最常见的单元——种群——是具有同一性的。生态学并未获得其底物预期能带来的分数。如果研究说“生态学的单元是非同一性的”，对于该领域的声誉并非错误，但对于实际单元可能是错误的。领域边界是因果性的——只是没有被表述。

What This Framework Does Differently / 这个框架做了什么不同的事情

English 中文
This framework treats the unit of analysis as something that must be decided at the claim, not at the noun. It builds a detector that reports how it reached each classification, so the size of the unfixed problem is a number rather than a caveat. 该框架将分析单元视为必须在声明层面而非名词层面决定的东西。它构建了一个检测器，报告它如何得出每个分类，因此未解决问题的规模是一个数字，而非一个警告。

The detector that fails its own test: T1's step 3 decides 83% of cases by a word list — the very thing it was built to escape. | 未能通过自身测试的检测器： T1 的步骤 3 通过一个词表决定了 83% 的案例——这正是它被设计用来逃离的东西。

The six‑state verb‑first test: Not a binary. VERB_CARRIES_IT and BOTH_READINGS are informative states that a binary collapses. | 六状态动词前置测试： 不是二元的。VERB_CARRIES_IT 和 BOTH_READINGS 是被二元分类所合并的有信息量的状态。

The two‑number dissolution window: W_dissolve and W_measure. The ratio tells you whether the study resolved well inside the term's stable regime or at the dissolution scale. | 双数字消解窗口： W_dissolve 和 W_measure。该比率告诉你，该研究是在该术语的稳定区间内进行解析，还是在消解尺度上进行解析。

The first‑class boundary decisions: BOUNDARY.md was written before anything was run. The detector imports its decisions from here rather than restating them. | 一流的边界决策： BOUNDARY.md 是在任何运行之前编写的。检测器从这里导入其决策，而非重述它们。

What Is NOT a Valid Epistemic State / 什么不是有效的认知状态

English 中文
NOUN_AS_UNIT is not a valid epistemic state. If the unit of analysis depends on the claim, not the noun, treating the noun as the unit is a measurement error, not an epistemic one. The claim does not care about our noun preferences. NOUN_AS_UNIT 不是一个有效的认知状态。如果分析单元取决于声明而非名词，那么将名词视为单元就是一个测量误差，而非认知误差。声明并不关心我们对名词的偏好。

The framework refuses to record a noun as the unit. Instead, it records the claim's subject as the unit — and reports how the classification was reached, so the size of the unfixed problem is visible. | 该框架拒绝将名词记录为单元。相反，它将声明的主语记录为单元——并报告分类是如何得出的，以便未解决问题的规模是可见的。

The Standard / 标准

English 中文
The question should not be: 问题不应该是：

"Is this noun identity‑bearing?" | > “这个名词具有同一性吗？”

But rather: | 而应该是：

"What does the claim predicate on, and is that thing a persistent, individuated entity?" | > “该声明将什么作为谓述对象，那个东西是一个持久的、个体化的实体吗？”

If the answer is that the same noun gives two answers depending on the claim, the unit is not in the noun — it is in the claim. End of story. | 如果答案是同一个名词根据声明给出两个答案，那么单元就不在名词中——它在声明中。故事结束。

This framework does not pretend otherwise. | 该框架不会假装并非如此。
