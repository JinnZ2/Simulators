WORK ORDER: NON-IDENTITY FRAMINGS CENSUS

OBJECTIVE
  Measure how much documented work models systems
  WITHOUT a persistent identity-bearing unit.
  Hypothesis under test (marker, not position):
  near-total. If so, the absence is constitutional
  — not a coverage gap.

DEFINE FIRST, BEFORE SEARCHING
  identity-bearing unit = a persistent, individuated
  entity that carries properties, interests, or
  continuity across time, and is the thing the model
  predicates on.
  NON-identity framing = analysis whose primary unit
  is process, flow, function, role, or relation, with
  no persistent individuated carrier.
  Write the boundary cases down BEFORE running.
  Borderline: does a "population" count? a "niche"?
  a "market"? Decide and log the decision.

T1 — DETECTION, NOT KEYWORD
  Lexical search fails: non-identity work does not
  announce itself, and identity assumption is
  PREMISE, not vocabulary. Same problem as the
  predicate-asymmetry scan.
  Detect by predicate structure instead: what does
  the paper's core claim attach to? Extract subject
  of the main causal claim per abstract; classify
  as individuated-persistent vs not.
  Valence off. Field-agnostic.

T2 — BASE RATE
  Run T1 over a stratified sample across fields:
  economics, sociology, psychology, ecology,
  systems/control, thermodynamics, law,
  organizational theory.
  Report proportion non-identity PER FIELD.
  Prediction to test: non-zero only where the
  substrate forces it (ecology, thermo, control).

T3 — MARGIN CHECK
  For every non-identity case found, log:
    - field
    - whether it is inside or outside the
      institutions that fund/review that field
    - whether it survived into citation, or died
  Testing whether these are exceptions that prove
  the rate — outside the measuring institutions.

T4 — EXCLUSION MECHANISM
  If the rate is near-zero, log in uninstrumented
  format. NOTE: this is a DIFFERENT CLASS from
  existing entries — not a quantity the instrument
  cannot see, but a UNIT OF ANALYSIS the corpus
  cannot express. May require a ninth mechanism.
  Do not force-fit into the existing eight.
  Report if none applies.

T5 — IDENTITY LOAD DISCRIMINATOR (separate thread)
  Testable asymmetry, from the reform-resistance
  branch:
    self-interest model predicts resistance scales
      with MATERIAL loss
    identity-load model predicts it scales with
      how much self-model the institution carries
  Find cases where these DIVERGE: reforms that
  preserve identity structure while changing
  mechanism, vs reforms that dissolve it, with
  material effect held constant.
  Report whether any such comparison exists in the
  literature. Likely not. If not, that is the result.

OUTPUT
  findings file, reproduction commands, no prose
  padding. shape-index format for T1-T4.
  Report the definitional boundary decisions as
  a first-class result — they determine the count.
