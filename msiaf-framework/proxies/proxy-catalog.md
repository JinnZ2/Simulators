# Proxy Catalog — Early-Warning Indicators by Mode

Proxies let the framework detect systemic risk **before** incidents occur — the
same way paleontologists use proxies to infer facts they cannot observe directly.

**Examples of the principle:**
- High turnover among temp warehouse staff → proxy for hazardous conditions or
  algorithmic over-pacing, even without worker complaints.
- Frequency of late-fee waivers granted to drayage drivers → proxy for systemic
  port congestion raising accident risk.

## By Mode

### Rail
- Unplanned maintenance requests → infrastructure degradation
- Frequent speed restrictions → localized track issues

### Ports
- Prolonged drayage dwell times → terminal inefficiency
- Increased cargo-damage rates → poor material-handling protocols

### Inland Waterways / River
- Water-level fluctuations → navigation hazards
- Unexpected draft limits → reduced cargo capacity

### Light-Duty & Private Vehicles (Car / Van / Pickup)
- Frequency of delivery/service vehicle trips → supply-chain pressure
- Time spent in congestion → strain on local infrastructure
- Emergency roadside-assistance call volume → deferred maintenance / heavy use

### Freight & Intermodal Terminals
- Daily throughput at intermodal terminals → system capacity pressure
- Average container dwell time before transfer → transfer friction
- Freight volume through major corridors → physical load on pavement/bridges

### Behavioral Segmentation (Travel Demand)
- Congestion patterns around entertainment venues vs. retail hubs → experiential
  vs. resource-acquisition trip mix (via anonymized mobile data)

### Aerial / Drone Logistics (Emerging)
- Corridor density near landing zones → airspace-conflict risk
- Requires new infrastructure accounting: landing zones and airspace safety
  alongside traditional ground transport

## Proxy → Physical Reality Mapping (Freight Example)

| Proxy Metric | Underlying Physical Reality |
|---|---|
| Corridor volume spikes | Pavement micro-fractures → potholes |
| High container dwell time | Static point-load damage to terminal slabs |
| Maxed-out cargo weights | Bridge/overpass fatigue-limit consumption |
| Roadside-assistance frequency | Tire failure & brake overheating from degraded roads + heavy loads |
