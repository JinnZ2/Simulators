# Proactive Model: Dimension 4 Redesign for Reefer Freight

**Goal:** transform the financial architecture from a punitive, single-point-of-
failure structure into a shared-risk, dynamically adjusted, safety-incentivizing
ecosystem. The binary liability switch (2°F / 15 min = total load rejection) is
what forces rigid dispatch — distribute both financial stake and informational
power so every node benefits from preventing the incident.

## 1. Graduated, Context-Aware Penalty Curves

Replace all-or-nothing rejection with a degradation curve tied to food-safety
science and real-time data:

- **Minor excursion** (<30 min, <5°F): accepted with small quality discount (~2%)
  funded into a joint safety pool — not a margin hit.
- **Moderate excursion** (30–120 min): sliding-scale discount plus mandatory
  post-trip reefer/telemetry audit.
- **Severe excursion/spoilage:** full loss covered by the pre-funded shared risk
  pool — removing the single-carrier-bankruptcy fear that drives reckless dispatch.

## 2. Shared Risk Pool & No-Fault Safety Fund

- **Contributors:** shipper, carrier, receiver, end-retailer — fractions of a cent
  per mile or per pallet into a neutral trust.
- **Trigger:** pays out when incidents occur under demonstrable systemic risk
  (stale GPS data, unpaved shoulders, missed weather alerts), not simple negligence.
- **Result:** carrier exposure capped; incentive shifts to maximizing *safe
  completions*, which unlock pool-funded bonuses.

## 3. Dispatcher & Carrier Incentive Recalibration

**Safe Trip Completion Score (STCS):**

```
STCS = (On-Time % × w₁) + (No ELD HOS violations × w₂)
     + (Driver-Initiated Rest Compliance × w₃)
     + (No Temperature Excursions × w₄)
     + (Dynamic Risk Adjustment × w₅)
```

Weights set so that forcing a skipped rest break tanks the score far more than a
30-minute delay improves it. **"Rest Now" credits:** a fatigue or weather log
immediately pays a guaranteed rest bonus from the shared pool — overriding the
driver now costs the dispatcher their own bonus.

## 4. Real-Time Dynamic Underwriting

- API-linked telematics + environmental feeds give the insurer the same data used
  in Information Feed Verification.
- **Per-trip premium adjustments:** rerouting around hazards earns instant rebates;
  overriding a rest request spikes the per-mile premium in real time.
- The dispatcher sees a live "financial risk meter" — unsafe dispatch costs
  real-time cash.

## 5. Mandatory Rest Infrastructure & Financial Buffer

- **Rest-period buy-out clause:** if the window can't be met without HOS violation,
  the receiver takes pre-agreed compensation from the pool and the driver rests.
- Carriers reimbursed for healthy, climate-controlled stops via an insurer-audited
  freight-rate surcharge — directly addressing micronutrient depletion and 105°F
  cab sleep.

## Counterfactual Outcome

Graduated penalties remove the bet-the-company incentive; dispatcher bonuses reward
granting rest; insurer-funded live weather updates re-route the truck before the
unstable shoulder; the driver rests at a vetted facility. **The incident doesn't
happen** — the D4→D2→D1→D3 cascade is reversed from pressure to protection.
