# "Natural": two senses, one word

CC0. A reusable reasoning move, not a description of any person.

Companions: `tools/uncounted_observer_isolation.md` (the isolation
ledger) and the efficient / deficient specimen in
`tools/bin_self_test.md` (its twin, see section 5).

Tags: OBSERVED = stated in a source; DERIVED = follows from what is
stated here; PROPOSED = a reading not yet backed by a cited instance.

## 1. Principle

A worked specimen of the welded-word family: headline vs ledger.
"Natural" carries two unrelated senses, and the word slides between
them with the reference unstated.

[DERIVED] SENSE 1 - conforms to physics / SURVIVES UNPAID.
It runs on the gradient and stays itself without continuous energy to
hold it there; it is what a completed experiment certified. It has a
LEDGER behind it: it bills and it pays.

[DERIVED] SENSE 2 - FAMILIAR / customary.
Common, expected, "the way it's always been". It has an unstated
REFERENCE behind it: familiar to whom, and when.

## 2. The test

One line:

    survives-unpaid, or merely-familiar?

## 3. The inversions

[DERIVED] The two senses routinely point OPPOSITE ways:

    case                                sense 1        sense 2
    ----                                -------        -------
    forced lab environment /            un-natural     natural, once it
    isolated system                     (billed        is the familiar
                                        continuously;  setup
                                        collapses when
                                        payment stops)
                                        => FAMILIAR UN-NATURE

    processed food, climate-            un-natural     natural
    controlled building, pristine       (continuous    (familiar)
    lawn                                spend; reverts
                                        when spend
                                        stops)

    repaired tool, off-grid system,     natural        "un-natural /
    worn-but-survived thing             (runs unpaid;  primitive"
                                        certified by   (unfamiliar to
                                        survival)      the judge)
                                        => UN-FAMILIAR NATURE

## 4. The damage

[DERIVED] The weld lets a speaker:

    ATTACK a sense-1-natural thing with sense 2      ("nobody does that")
    DEFEND a sense-1-UN-natural thing with sense 2   ("it's perfectly normal")

Opposite warrants, same word. The switch is invisible because the
reference behind sense 2 is never named.

## 5. Twin of efficient / deficient

[DERIVED]

    efficient   smuggles an unstated GOAL
    natural     smuggles an unstated REFERENCE

Both are RELATIONS wearing the costume of a PROPERTY. "Natural"
describes the thing's distance from what the speaker is used to, then
reports that distance as a property OF the thing.

## 6. Rule

Before "natural" or "unnatural", name which sense.

If sense 2, name the reference (familiar to whom, when) and check that
it is the speaker's, not a given.

A thing whose familiarity is in dispute cannot carry the word. Fix the
reference and the label flips.
