# Inward turn: apply the outward critique to the home record

CC0. A reusable reasoning move, delivered 2026-10-25, co-authored. The
delivered text is kept verbatim in section 2 and nothing in it is edited.

Companions: `tools/glyph_reasoning.md` (E4, the unchecked observer, is
the same move at the level of one reader), `tools/bin_self_test.md`.
Simulators `uninstrumented/` and `internal-reference-boundary/` are
instruments for the gaps this produces.

Tags: OBSERVED = stated in a source or seen in the field; DERIVED =
follows from what is stated here; PROPOSED = untested.

## 1. When to pick it up

- pick up when: a corpus is dismissing outside evidence for a reason
  (anecdote, small n, survivorship, self-report) and the same reason has
  not been checked against the corpus's own record.
- skip when: the critique has already been applied to the home record and
  the result is on file. Cite it instead of re-running.

Step 3 records what survives as well as what fails. A run that records
only failures is selected on its own outcome.

## 2. Delivered text (verbatim)

```text
INWARD-TURN INSTRUMENT                                  [DRAFT / co-authored]
  1. collect a critique the corpus aims outward   (survivorship bias, anecdote, small n)
  2. apply it to the home record                  (science, economics, policy, terms)
  3. record what survives and what doesn't
  4. each failure = a gap the home frame never measured on itself
```
