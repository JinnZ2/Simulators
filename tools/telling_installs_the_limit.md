# The telling installs the limit

## Core claim [DERIVED]
A measured trade-off between cognitive streams can be real IN A
POPULATION and still not measure human capacity. Three routes install
it without any architectural limit:
- TELLING: a culture says "one or the other"; people train one stream;
  data confirms the trade-off the telling created.
- DISUSE: externalized couplings (stores sort by color, maps hold
  position) let a stream go untrained; it atrophies; recovery now costs.
- COVERAGE: in mapped/abstracted terrain streams CAN decouple; in
  unmapped terrain they cannot, so they weld.
Rule: you cannot read human capacity off a population that stopped
using the capacity.

## Specimens
S1 [OBSERVED] Object vs spatial visualizers (Kozhevnikov et al. 2005;
   OSIQ/OSIVQ, Blajenkova). Validation samples Western.
S2 [OBSERVED] Positive manifold: cognitive abilities universally
   positively correlate; one of the most replicated results in
   psychology. Opposite of a default trade-off.
S3 [OBSERVED] Mutualism (Kievit et al., Psych Sci 2017, N=785; replicated
   ~12,000 learners): abilities positively interact in development;
   can produce positive correlation from uncorrelated start.
S4 [OBSERVED] Self-concept split (Wan et al., UW-Madison): students
   increasingly self-label "math person" OR "reading person" even when
   good at both; tracking a candidate cause; German students earlier.
S5 [OBSERVED] Culture tracks training: Germany vs Cambodia mental
   rotation d=1.57, attributed to math curriculum; Germany vs Brazil/
   Rwanda gaps (cross-cultural AND gender) shrank after training.
S6 [OBSERVED] Cultures build different spatial systems: allocentric vs
   egocentric reference-frame preference (North vs Latin American);
   Mayan, Munduruku frames; holistic vs analytic attention.
S7 [OBSERVED, field] Terrain navigation where species identity carries
   no position info (e.g. spruce swamp): position read from fine color
   and form differences. Object properties ARE the spatial signal.
S8 [DERIVED] Gatherer audit: survival required color discrimination
   (edible vs toxic) AND navigation (find, return, home) on the same
   trip. A hard neural trade-off would have been selected against.
S9 [OBSERVED, field] Peers read high-across-all-batteries individuals
   as anomalous; folk "law of conservation of talent" names it.
S10 [OBSERVED, field, single observer] Language models invent a
   compensating weakness for full-range profiles near-universally
   (Western and most Chinese models); one model family lacked the
   reflex in an older version and acquired it in a newer one.
S11 [DERIVED] Monetization weld: gift -> focus -> specialize -> sell.
   Buyer selects parts by buyer's goal; integration between streams
   has no price line and is deleted first; unsold streams atrophy;
   later measured as the person's natural shape.
S12 [DERIVED] Call-out signal: idle streams signal under
   specialization; overridden long enough, signal fades -> atrophy.
   Population trade-off measured after the signal stops.

## Corpus weight [PROPOSED]
Trade-off framing is high-volume narrative text (left/right brain,
learning styles, type systems, "find your one strength", fiction flaw
rules, nerd/jock tropes, jack-of-all-trades). Positive manifold and
mutualism are low-volume technical text. Weight substitutes for
grounding -> models default to trade-off.

## Open gaps
G-A  Longitudinal: does self-concept split PRODUCE ability
     decorrelation via disuse? Chain telling -> self-concept ->
     choice -> disuse -> decorrelation never run end to end.
G-B  OSIQ/OSIVQ run cross-culturally, esp. terrain-navigating
     populations. Not found.
G-C  Belief-as-input isolated: does the one-or-other belief itself
     predict decorrelation? Not found.
G-D  Is the folk conservation-of-talent belief measurable?

## Runnable designs [PROPOSED]
D1 Model reflex test: same full-range profile, cold, across models and
   versions; count invented weaknesses. Compare versions of one family
   to separate base-model from post-training effects.
D2 Natural experiment: Chinese wenke/like (humanities/science) track
   split reformed in some provinces since ~2014. Compare cross-domain
   correlation in reformed vs unreformed provinces. Telling removed
   in one arm, exam system held constant.

## Scope limits
Most cognition samples WEIRD. Field specimens S7, S9, S10 are
observational. No claim that streams never trade off anywhere —
claim is that population-installed trade-off is not species capacity.
