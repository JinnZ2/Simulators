# Bin, slack, conservation: one mechanism from token to civilization

CC0. A reusable reasoning move, not a description of any person.

Companions: `tools/held_tie_precision.md` (the near-tie as the token
scale of the same loss), `tools/uncounted_observer_isolation.md` (the
isolation ledger), `tools/coupling_and_accumulation.md`.

Tags: OBSERVED = stated in a source or seen in the field; DERIVED =
follows from what is stated here; PROPOSED = untested.

## 1. Binning is information loss, at every scale

[DERIVED] To bin is to replace a distribution with one of its labels.
The label is cheaper to carry and the distribution is gone. The same
operation runs at every scale:

    body ladder   a specific state of the body (blood sugar, temperature,
                  posture, load)  ->  "hunger"  ->  "feelings"  ->  one
                  word on a form
    substrate     "noise" is itself a bin: everything the instrument
                  did not have a label for, collapsed into one token
                  that means "discard"
    civilization  the regimes, practices and slack a society carries,
                  collapsed into the metrics it reports

[DERIVED] Granularity is not something added; it is what remains when a
collapse is NOT performed. A fine reading is the default state of the
sensor, and every bin is a subtraction from it.

## 2. Efficiency is a transfer under conservation

[DERIVED] A saved cost does not vanish. Under conservation it lands
somewhere, and the efficiency claim is incomplete until the destination
is named:

    "we saved X"   ->   where did X go, and who carries it now?

Slack that reads as "inefficient" is often unmeasured resilience work:
capacity held for the case that has not happened yet. Cutting it moves
the cost from the ledger that counts to the one that does not.

Check: name the destination of every saving. A saving with no named
destination is a cost pushed off the page.

## 3. Why now: a non-stationary regime

[DERIVED] Slack is the adaptation budget. Cutting it is a bet that
tomorrow equals today. In a stationary regime that bet pays, and
efficiency looks free; in a non-stationary regime the bet is the cost.

[OBSERVED] The Holocene was the window: a long stable regime in which
removing slack went unpunished long enough for efficiency to look like a
free lunch. That window is what made the bet look safe, not the bet
itself.

## 4. Coupled perturbations do not sum

[DERIVED] Three perturbations are running at once and each moves the
ground the others stand on:

    climate                        moves the physical baseline
    the AI gradient                moves the information and labor baseline
    continuous-patch governance    moves the rule baseline before either
                                   of the above is measured against it

Their effects are not additive. Adding them assumes each is measured
against a fixed ground, and each is what un-fixes the others' ground.

[DERIVED] Stability falsification: name a regime that held for 150 to
250 years without patching. None is on offer. So the question to ask of
any "stable" arrangement is not what it kept, but what WAS NOT changed
while everything around it was.

## 5. The rational / emotional split is false at the mechanism

[DERIVED] The body is the sensor array. Strip it out and reasoning does
not become pure; it becomes decalibrated, because the inputs it was
reasoning over are gone.

The "pure reasoner" is an empty AND incoherent category: empty because
no instance exists, incoherent because a reasoner with no sensors has
nothing to reason about.

[DERIVED] Denying a present factor violates the definition of rational.
If the body state is present and bears on the decision, a reasoning
process that excludes it is excluding an input it has, which is the
opposite of what "rational" names. See `tools/bin_self_test.md`,
specimen "emotion-separation", for the single-reader form.

## 6. Population scale: the missing bin as dread

[DERIVED] At population scale the same unbinned reading appears as
dread: a signal the body array is carrying that no public label holds.
It reads as noise (section 1, substrate) because the label is missing,
not because the signal is. A society that has binned its own slack
away has no word for the state of having no slack, and reports the
state as a mood.

## 7. Permission to read the body: the floor under the stack

[DERIVED] Every level above rests on the lowest one: the permission to
read the body as a sensor rather than bin it as "feelings". Without
that floor, sections 1 through 6 have no input. With it, the body
ladder in section 1 runs in reverse: the one-word form entry is
unpacked back toward the state that produced it.

## Checks

    1. Name the bin. What distribution did this label replace?
    2. Name the destination. Where did the saved cost land?
    3. Name the regime. Is "tomorrow = today" being assumed?
    4. Name the ground. Which perturbation moved the baseline this
       measurement was taken against?
    5. Name the sensor. Which body or field reading was excluded as
       "emotional" or "noise"?
