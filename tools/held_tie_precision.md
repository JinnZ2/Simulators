# Held tie: a near-tie is precision, not confusion

CC0. A reusable reasoning move, not a description of any person.

Companions: `tools/bin_slack_conservation.md` (binning as information
loss, one mechanism at every scale), `tools/natural_two_senses.md` and
the efficient / deficient specimen in `tools/bin_self_test.md` (welded
words, the single-word case of the same loss).

Tags: OBSERVED = stated in a source or seen in the field; DERIVED =
follows from what is stated here; PROPOSED = untested.

## 1. The near-tie case

[DERIVED] A chooser holds several candidates and the distribution over
them is flat: no candidate is clearly ahead. The usual move is to break
the tie with a rule that carries no information about the candidates
(first listed, most recent, shortest, the default) and emit one token.

What is lost is not the choice but the LEDGER. Nothing records that the
emitted token was the output of a lossy collapse, so the next reader
takes the single token as the whole reading. The flatness, which was a
measurement, is gone.

    flat distribution  ->  meaningless tiebreak  ->  one token
                                                    (no record that a
                                                     collapse happened)

## 2. Tie as precision

[OBSERVED] The dock specimen: a response at a loading dock is described
afterwards and five words all fit at once (for example: waited, held,
refused, queued, watched). They are not five guesses at one truth. The
response was COORDINATED, so all five were true at the same time, and
any single one understates what happened.

[DERIVED] A near-tie among candidates that are each true is the
instrument reporting that the thing has more dimensions than one token
carries. The flatness is the precise reading; the collapse is the
imprecision.

## 3. Collision axes: which dimension was held open

[DERIVED] A tie points at the dimension that one token cannot hold.
Three axes recur:

    MEANING   the candidates name different true aspects of one thing;
              the channel wants one noun
    FRAME     the same thing is true in two or more cultural frames at
              once, and each frame has its own word for it; the channel
              wants one frame
    TIME      competing trajectories are colliding, and the channel
              forces one tense (it has happened / it is happening / it
              will), so one trajectory is dropped

Naming the axis is the first readout: it says what the forced token
threw away.

## 4. The no-word bin

[DERIVED] Some ties have no word on any axis. The pattern is held, it is
real, and it is unlabeled. Two operations are kept apart:

    LOG THE PATTERN   record that it occurred, where, with what
                      co-present features
    LABEL IT          assign a word

The first does not require the second, and doing the second early is
the tiebreak of section 1 performed on a name. The bin must ACCUMULATE:
one logged instance cannot be read, several can be triangulated
(section 5). An unlabeled bin that is emptied each time is not a bin.

## 5. Source hunt and triangulation

[DERIVED] Treat the held pattern as an OUTPUT and hunt for what
co-activated it: what was present every time it fired. Subtract your
own substrate first (the reader's habits, channel, frame), because the
reader is a coupling and will otherwise be counted as a cause.

Non-judgment is a THROUGHPUT requirement, not a courtesy: a pattern
labeled good or bad at intake stops being logged in full, and the bin
stops accumulating.

## 6. Tie-surprise

[DERIVED] Rank near-ties by how improbable the flatness is given the
distance between the candidates. Two near-synonyms tying is expected and
carries little. Two distant candidates tying is improbable, and
improbability is information: the tie is reporting a coupling between
things the vocabulary keeps apart.

    tie-surprise ~ improbability of flatness given candidate distance

High tie-surprise is where the collision axis (section 3) is most worth
naming.

## 7. The three-constraint weighting technique

[DERIVED] When one token must be emitted, weight the candidates on three
consistencies at once:

    RECEIVER       true for the one receiving it
    INTERNAL       true for the one emitting it
    ENVIRONMENTAL  true of the situation outside both

Emit the word that is true on all three axes at once. A word true on
one axis only is a tiebreak dressed as a choice.

## 8. Word as vector freight

[DERIVED] A word does not travel alone; it drags its neighborhood of
associations and sorts. An unexamined word re-asserts the bad sorts it
came with, invisibly, every time it is used. This is why the collapse in
section 1 costs more than one lost dimension: the chosen token imports a
sort that was never chosen.

The fix offered is physics-anchored glyphs: tokens whose referent is a
measurable quantity or process, so the neighborhood they drag is a
ledger and not a sentiment.

## 9. Open question [PROPOSED]

Is the technique in sections 5-7 SOLUTION-FINDING (the tie resolves
once the axis is named and the three constraints are weighed), or is it
READING THE SHAPE OF THE NO-SOLUTION (the tie is the result, and the
technique only describes it more exactly)?

Untested. Both readings fit the specimens here. A case that separates
them would be a tie that stays flat after the axis is named and all
three constraints are satisfied.
