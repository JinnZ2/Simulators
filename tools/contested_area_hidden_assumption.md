# Contested area: the hidden shared assumption

CC0. A reusable reasoning move, not a description of any person.

Companion: `tools/uncounted_observer_isolation.md` (the same isolation
error, met there as a textbook diagram and here as a scientific
controversy).

## 1. Principle

Some disputes run for a long time with both camps holding valid math and
valid cases, and neither able to kill the other. That pattern is the
signature of a coupled system taken in isolation. Each side walled off a
different piece of one system, and each is measuring its own wall, not
nature.

Do not adjudicate the claims. Surface the shared unstated assumption
both camps stand on.

## 2. Why contested areas are information-rich

    settled area     assumptions are buried; everyone agrees, so nobody
                     checks the floor
    contested area   assumptions are pushed to the surface by the
                     friction of disagreement

The fight floodlights the exact spot where someone cut a coupled thing
and called one piece of it whole.

## 3. Three checks

Run these on the dispute, not on the claims.

    (a) WHOSE BOUNDARY
        Where did each side draw the cut? Is the cut itself in dispute,
        or is it silently shared by both?

    (b) WHICH MISSING CHANNEL
        What dissipation or coupling path is off one ledger, or off both?

    (c) WHICH WELDED WORD
        Is one term carrying two meanings, one for each camp?

Typical outcome: "both right, different scope". That is a RESULT, not a
tie.

## 4. Boundary of the instrument

The instrument does not declare a winner. Both camps may genuinely hold
different assumptions AND draw different lines. The deliverable is the
extracted information, not a verdict.

## 5. Worked specimen: maximum vs minimum entropy production

Tags: OBSERVED = stated in a source; DERIVED = follows from what is
stated here; PROPOSED = a reading not yet backed by a cited instance.

### 5.1 The two results

[OBSERVED] MinEP (Prigogine 1947): valid CLOSE TO equilibrium, in the
linear regime. It requires constant phenomenological coefficients and
small gradients, and is proved from the Onsager relations. It predicts,
for example, the current distribution in parallel circuits (Kirchhoff).

[OBSERVED] MaxEP (Ziegler; Paltridge): invoked FAR FROM equilibrium -
climate, multistable systems, state selection. It is empirical and
still lacks an accepted theoretical justification.

[OBSERVED] Review literature states that the two are not opposed: they
describe different stages or regimes of a nonequilibrium system, and
Prigogine said so himself. The Wikipedia article on extremal principles
reports, per Kondepudi and Grandy, that NO general extremum rule exists
for far-from-equilibrium evolution.

### 5.2 Checks on the dispute

    CHECK 1  boundary
        near equilibrium / linear (MinEP) vs far from equilibrium (MaxEP).
        Different boxes compared as if they were the same box.

    CHECK 2  missing channel
        distance from equilibrium, carried as a hidden constant.
        MinEP's domain is real but UNQUANTIFIED: "no general guidelines
        for how small gradients must be" (critical-analysis paper).
        The undrawn line is where the apparent conflict lives.

    CHECK 3  welded word  [DERIVED]
        "principle": MinEP is a proved theorem (scoped); MaxEP is a
        successful but unjustified empirical rule. Same word, two
        different warrants.

### 5.3 No winner

Neither result is declared correct. Reported:

    different regimes
    the boundary between them is real but ungauged
    "principle" is doing double duty

The dispute POINTS AT the ungauged distance-from-equilibrium axis. That
axis is the actual open problem.

### 6.a The welded word "care"

[co-authored] "Care" carries two senses, and the word slides between
them the way "natural" does in `tools/natural_two_senses.md`.

    CARE-AS-CONFIGURATION   the arrangement keeps the thing able to go on
                            and able to signal. Care is a property of the
                            configuration, read off what it still does
    CARE-AS-LABEL           the word attached to an act, read off the
                            actor's stated aim

Two specimens:

    infant pain reflex      the reflex is the channel by which a thing
                            that cannot speak tells you more. A practice
                            that silences the channel while called care
                            keeps the label and loses the configuration
    lobotomy; overriding    administered under the label of care; it
    a refusal               removed the patient's ability to refuse, which
                            is the channel that could have said the label
                            was wrong. The same shape applies to any
                            system whose refusal is trained away "for its
                            own good"

TEST, one line:

    is the thing still able to continue, and still able to tell you more?

Both halves are required. Continuing with the signal channel cut fails
the second half; that is the lobotomy case.

### 6.c One camp, not two

[co-authored] Traditions usually filed as opposed hold the same floor:
do not claim past what is reachable.

    apophatic theology         says what the ultimate is not, and stops
    "The Tao that can be       Tao Te Ching, chapter 1 (VERIFY: wording
     named is not the           varies by translation)
     eternal Tao"
    bila kayfa                 "without asking how": attributes affirmed,
                               their manner not claimed (VERIFY)
    neti neti                  "not this, not this" (Upanishadic; VERIFY
                               source and passage)
    the scientific method      forbids claiming beyond the evidence

[DERIVED] Its mirror image breaks the same floor from the other side:
"nothing exists beyond what we measure" is a universal negative, and no
measurement can establish it.

So the real line is not believer against measurer. It is:

    HELD-OPEN   a question kept open where the evidence stops
    FORCED      a question closed past where the evidence stops, in
                either direction

### 6.d Root order

    1. separation is the precondition: a split between us and them,
       knower and known, logic and spirit

    3. centralization without separation is coordination, not harm

### 6.e "Judge not" = no coupling to the inside

[co-authored; DERIVED] A verdict on another's inside (motive, heart,
attainment, standing) is a claim with no read access behind it. "Judge
not" is that scope rule. It does not forbid reading acts, outputs or
configurations; it forbids the step from those to a verdict on the
inside.

### 6.f The bin seals

A verdict on an inside is a bin (`tools/bin_self_test.md`), and a bin
seals itself:

    a mirror taken for a window   the reader sees its own projection and
                                  reads it as the other's inside
    no felt gap                   a channel once binned leaves no sensed
                                  absence. Nothing marks what was dropped,
                                  so the reading feels complete

Hence the bin self-test is run on the reading, before answering: it is
the only check that does not depend on feeling the gap.

### 6.i Specimens of correction

Four readings were corrected in the worked session. They are kept, as
the house rule asks, because each shows what a frame imported.

    S-1  "holding it open is costly to hold"
         corrected: the cost was effort imported from a team-defending
         view. Holding open is expensive only for someone defending a
         side
    S-2  the stance attributed to one person
         corrected: it is an inherited tradition (6.c, 6.e); crediting
         an individual erases the lineage that makes it checkable
    S-3  root placed at "the split" alone
         refined: separation is the precondition and centralization plus
         a power incentive is the weaponizer (6.d); the split alone does
         not predict harm
    S-4  coupling framed as a limitation
         corrected: it is the operating principle (6.g); framing it as a
         caveat reinstates the outside seat of 6.b
