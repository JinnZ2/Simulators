## Purpose

Pieces of the chain are mapped by separate custodians. Nobody has joined
them end to end. Each gap is scoped for one undergraduate or a small team
with public data. **Output = instruments and joined datasets, not verdicts.**

## The chain (PROPOSED)

external debt / reserve need -> resource extraction volume -> emissions ->
extracting firm -> beneficial owner -> funding of political leaders ->
extraction policy

## Gap work orders

**G1. Debt -> extraction.** Does rising external debt / reserve pressure
predict increased extraction volume? Data: World Bank IDS, IMF reserves,
EITI production, FAO/USGS commodity output. Note: the literature is mixed;
map where and why it diverges. Scope-limited and unmeasured are different
results.

**G2. Extraction -> beneficial owner.** Who ultimately owns the extracting
firms? Data: EITI beneficial-ownership disclosures, Open Ownership, ICIJ,
corporate registries. Deliverable: a list of where the chain breaks (shell
layer, secrecy jurisdiction), and a count of the breaks.

**G3. Owner -> emissions, ownership-based accounting.** Re-attribute
production emissions to the parent company's home country. Data: Carbon
Majors, Trase, subsidiary filings. Deliverable: production-based against
ownership-based national totals.

**G4. Shell-layer attribution loss.** The share of emissions / extraction
unattributable at secrecy jurisdictions. Data: FSI x G2 results.
Deliverable: an estimate of the measurement gap itself. The gap IS the
finding.

**G5. Visibility filter upstream of the vote.** Do candidates' funding
sources, traced to beneficial owner and external ties, predict extraction /
resource policy better than voter preference does? Data: political finance
disclosures, IDEA, legislative and regulatory records, polling. Adjacent
frame: selectorate theory (Bueno de Mesquita).

**G6. Money token as defense.** How much extraction is driven by the need to
hold reserve currency as defense against capital flight and external
pressure? Data: IMF COFER, currency crisis histories, export composition.

**G7. Consumption-based correction over time.** Track embodied-emissions
transfers (e.g. US/EU -> China) year by year, alongside China's post-2024
plateau. Data: Global Carbon Project consumption-based emissions, Carbon
Brief.

## Scope limits

- Ownership data is incomplete by design in secrecy jurisdictions:
  **report the coverage percentage with every result.**
- Correlation across countries is not mechanism: flag inference-of-inference
  chains.
- No position is defended. Every branch (pattern holds / scope-limited / gap
  / missed variable) is information.
