threshold: onset_frac
value:     0.5
who:       builder (Claude Code session, DISPATCH ESP-1), not an operator
when:      2026-09-24
reasoning: onset is the first exceedance of this fraction of a body's OWN peak
           envelope. Relative to itself on purpose: onset then measures WHEN a
           body started moving and never how much it moved, which is the
           amplitude ratio's job. Mixing the two into one number would let a
           large late body and a small early one report the same.
