INSTRUMENT: BIOSPHERIC REPLACEMENT COST (BRC)

  measurand: energy per year required to perform,
             by engineered means, a cycle currently
             performed by the biosphere
  unit:      J/yr  (NOT currency — see scope)

VALIDATION CASE — the one cycle already partly
replaced, so the number is MEASURED not modelled

  N FIXATION
    Haber-Bosch fixes ~120 Tg N/yr, and consumes
    roughly 1-2% of total world energy use
    biological fixation still supplies about half
    of bioavailable nitrogen on Earth

  arithmetic:
    ~50% of one cycle, partially replaced
    → ~1-2% of ALL human energy
    remaining biological half, unreplaced:
    same order again

    50-70% of the reactive N produced is lost to
    the environment
    → industrial version is OPEN-loop
    → biological version is closed
    → replacement cost is not just the synthesis

  status: this is a REAL measurement of a partial
  biosphere replacement. It anchors every
  extrapolation below.

THE CELL — where the same problem was already
solved, and how

  naive read:   replication aims at fidelity,
                variation is residual error
  actual:       mutation rate is a TUNED parameter
                with an optimum

    too low  → no adaptive variance, lineage dies
               when the environment moves
    too high → error catastrophe, information
               not retained
    proofreading exists AND is deliberately
    incomplete; stress raises the rate

  asymmetric division carries the other half:
    damaged components partition to the mother,
    daughter resets
    → the lineage does not average damage across
      copies, it CONCENTRATES it in one and
      discards
    → error is not diluted, it is localized and
      shed

  variation precedes the environment that will
  select it. The cell does not know which variant
  will be needed. That is the whole design.
