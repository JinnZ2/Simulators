ANCHOR INVARIANT
A boundary drawn by the party inside it, measured from inside.
Instances: disciplinary silo | credential | efficiency metric |
diagnostic criterion | corporate liability shell |
institutional self-investigation.
Assembly order: name invariant -> each instance as a case
(what the boundary is, who draws it) -> exemptions as separate layer.

RADIALS (quantities, not categories)

R1 ENCOUNTER RATE — rate constrained party meets the boundary.
   Range anchors: free-range cattle ~0 ; gated bathroom ~continuous.
   STATUS: ready.

R2 MEASURAND OWNERSHIP — is metric-definer inside or outside.
   Graded by distance, not binary.
   STATUS: ready.

R3 SANCTION BASE RATE — consequence rate / occurrence rate.
   Empirical anchor: ~1 finding per 10,000 researchers/yr vs
   25-50% self-reported incidence.
   STATUS: ready. Strongest empirical leg.

R4 ROUTABILITY — can cost be passed to an entity.
   Predicted sign: INVERSE to capacity to cause damage.
   STATUS: ready.

R5 EXEMPTION PROVENANCE — self-designated vs survived external test.
   Legitimacy test: detailed empirical work + full transparency +
   peer review across different fields and outside peers.
   SCORING (reciprocity): apply the exemption's own logic back on
   the claimant. Count with a direction —
     (a) breadth: count of other cases they extend it to
     (b) benefit-correlation: are extensions sorted by benefit
   High breadth + zero benefit-correlation = actual principle.
   Base rate unknown, worth measuring.
   STATUS: ready.

R6 TRANSFER SURVIVAL — does a result hold outside its origin boundary.
   Four routes:
     1. replication outcomes, split by insider/outsider team
     2. retraction/correction survival curves, same split
     3. textbook persistence at 50yr, by origin breadth
     4. INDEPENDENT ADOPTION COUNT — traditions adopting without
        borrowing from each other (strongest; pure functional selection)
   ORIGIN-BREADTH TAGGING RULE (solved via physics route):
     not a count of fields — EFFECTIVE NUMBER OF INDEPENDENT ORIGINS.
     Fields sharing instruments/funders/journals/training lineage are
     one observation measured twice. Correlated measurements do not
     reduce the error bar. Compute coupling from shared citation
     ancestry, instruments, funders, training lineage; collapse the
     count toward 1 as coupling rises.
   SPLIT TO PRESERVE: dependency depth within a tradition is a
     different quantity from necessity for the knowledge itself.
     (Notation: high depth in the written lineage, zero necessity
     for the physics.)
   STATUS: method complete, needs corpus.

R7 BOUNDARY PERMEABILITY TO INFORMATION — does anything get IN.
   Distinct from R2: R2 = who scores, R7 = what enters.
   METRIC: ratio of confirming-entry rate to contradicting-entry rate.
     equal = permeable ; confirming-only = selection, not evaluation.
     Self-corrects for field tempo.
   DENOMINATOR (the pariah set): use work the field has publicly
     declared quack/pseudoscience/pariah. Declared in writing, dated.
     Code each rejection: cites METHOD (field read it) vs cites
     CONCLUSION / "does not fit what we know" (field did not).
     Ratio of the two rejection kinds is the measure.
     Validity check built in: genuinely-wrong cases belong in the
     sample; a field correctly identifying bad method scores as
     permeable. Scoring the field's REASON avoids needing to
     adjudicate the pariah's correctness.
   SECOND SCORE, same corpus — historical outcome, 3 states:
     stayed rejected | came in WITH credit | CAME IN WITHOUT CREDIT
     Third state is the informative one: content absorbed while
     rejection of the source held = boundary was never about method.
     Lag from labelling to absorption = latency, second direction.
   Existing calibration point: anonymous-post loop, ~1-2yr latency.
   STATUS: method complete, needs corpus.

SECOND INSTRUMENT — GAP TRANSFER ACROSS HOSTS
   Claim: an audit gap survives the end of the practice carrying it
   and reattaches to a new intervention. Track the gap, not the
   intervention. This is the TIME DIMENSION of the radials, which
   measure one boundary statically.
   Prediction: do not look at the discredited practice (already being
   fixed) — look at whatever inherited its population and its
   accounting horizon.
   Worked case: lobotomy -> veteran pain prescribing. Finding true
   inside its boundary; short outcome window inside the measurand,
   downstream course outside it. Later swing to blanket restriction
   = same defect running the other way.
