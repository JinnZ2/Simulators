## The problem, restated as an instrument problem

The Emergence World comparison read a defect off the DIFFERENCE between two
populations with different configurations. A global single settlement medium
has no second configuration to difference against, so any reading taken from
inside it is a reading of the medium plus the defect, inseparable by
construction. The three designs below each supply a partial second
configuration from a different source. None supplies a whole one, and each
says what it cannot separate.

The measurand in all three is the same one FWO-5 builds: for a produced
result, the count of dependencies that carry a route settling outside the
medium, and the conversion points the others route through. A control source
is useful exactly to the extent that it moves that count for the same kind of
result.

```
                    single-medium world            control source
   result R  ---->  routes, conversion points  vs  routes, conversion points
                                     |                    |
                                     +---- difference ----+  (the reading)
   confound: everything ELSE that differs between the two worlds
```

## D1 -- historical / jurisdictional parallel media

**Scope.** Periods or places where a second medium was in LEGAL use for
discharging obligations: tax in kind (grain, labour days, corvee), commodity
money circulating beside coin, local scrip accepted for local taxes. The unit
of comparison is one class of produced result (a public work, a harvest
record, an instrument made) with its dependency chain coded under FWO-5's
schema in both the parallel-medium record and a matched single-medium record.

**Prediction, PROPOSED (Claude).** In the parallel-medium record at least one
dependency category settles outside coin for the same result class; the
conversion-point list is shorter or empties at a different layer.

**Kill condition.** If, for every result class with records in both worlds,
the FWO-5 independence band is the same to within one dependency, the
parallel medium was a unit of ACCOUNT and not of SETTLEMENT, and the design
has no control in it. Also killed if fewer than three result classes carry a
dependency chain recoverable from the record: the reading is then about the
archive, not the medium.

**Cheapest first run.** One result class, one jurisdiction, two records: a
tax-in-kind ledger entry and a coin-tax ledger entry for the same kind of
produced good, coded by two readers blind to each other under FWO-5's schema,
with the coding disagreement reported first. No archive is named here because
none was reached; the choice is the operator's.

**Confound stated.** Everything else differed too (technology, literacy,
state capacity). The design reads only the dependency-chain count, which is
the one quantity the medium touches directly, and claims nothing about output.

**Prior art.** NOT_RUN. Economic-history work on tax in kind and on
commodity-money regimes exists and is not cited here because none was read.

## D2 -- populations whose coordination does not settle in the medium

**Scope.** Household production (already the seed of FWO-5 case (a)), and the
insect-scaling comparison already filed in this tree. Both are populations in
which obligations are discharged in kind, in time, or not at all, alongside
the single-medium population in the same place and time. The control is
CONTEMPORANEOUS, which removes D1's historical confound and adds one of its
own: the household sits INSIDE the medium's jurisdiction, so its legal
compliance routes convert (case (a)'s property-tax row).

**Prediction, PROPOSED (Claude).** The household chain reads INDEPENDENT on
most categories and CONVERTED on exactly the categories the jurisdiction
reaches (legal compliance, and any purchased input). The conversion points
therefore locate the jurisdiction's reach, not the medium's, and the two are
separable by which categories carry them.

**Kill condition.** If household chains coded across several households
convert on categories the jurisdiction does not touch (labour, data access,
energy from daylight), the medium reaches further than legal compliance and
the population is not a control.

**Cheapest first run.** FWO-5 case (a) is already coded and is the first run's
fixture; the run is to replace its CONSTRUCTED household with three declared
real ones, coded by their own members, no person named in the record. Cost:
three coding sessions, no instrument beyond FWO-5.

**Prior art.** NOT_RUN. Household-economics and time-use literatures measure
unpaid work in hours and value it in money; whether any of them records
SETTLEMENT medium per obligation is the unclaimed join the order names, and
it was not searched.

## D3 -- divergence-then-contact cases

**Scope.** Two populations that separated, developed for a period without
exchange, then met. At contact each population's dependency chains are still
denominated in its own media; the contact record shows which conversion
points appear first and at which layer. The fission / divergence object
already filed in this tree supplies the case form.

**Prediction, PROPOSED (Claude).** The first conversion points at contact
appear at SETTLEMENT (one side's obligations begin to be discharged in the
other's medium) before they appear at ACCOUNT. This is the same layer
hypothesis FWO-6 registers, tested on a time axis rather than a cross-section.

**Kill condition.** If the contact record shows account-layer conversion
(prices quoted in the other medium) before any settlement-layer conversion,
or shows both in the same record with no ordering recoverable, the layer
hypothesis has no time signature and D3 tests nothing FWO-6 does not.

**Cheapest first run.** One documented contact with a dated record on both
sides, coded for the first appearance of each conversion mechanism by date.
None is named here because no source was reached.

**Prior art.** NOT_RUN. Contact-period economic history and the anthropology
of first exchange are the obvious literatures; neither was read.

## What none of the three can do

None supplies a world with the SAME technology, jurisdiction and period and a
different settlement medium. Each design's reading is a difference with one
named confound left in it. A result from any one is a marker. Agreement
across two of them on the same dependency category is the smallest thing
that would count as a finding, and no run of any of them exists.
