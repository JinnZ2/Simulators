SHAPE INDEX -- one shape, four bindings, different vocabulary
========================================================================

  If the same predicate shape recurs across all four subject
  classes with different vocabulary, that confirms it is a
  structural signature and not four separate literatures.

  subject classes:
    women
    enslaved and formerly enslaved people
    ethnic groups typed as lower
    disabled people

------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE INDEX

    shapes recorded: 0
    corpus:          NONE_ACQUIRED
    state:           NOT_RUN

    a predicate shape taxonomy is derived from the corpus. None is
    acquired, so the index is empty -- and empty here is the size of
    the work done, not a finding that no shape recurs

------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE RECURRENCE TEST, GRADED ON FIXTURES

  SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE -- invented to grade the recurrence test

  SHAPE FX_1
    fixture shape, all four classes, no shared words
    bindings:              4 of 4
    vocabulary disjoint:   True
    per binding:
      women                                    w_a1, w_a2
      enslaved and formerly enslaved people    w_b1
      ethnic groups typed as lower             w_c1, w_c2
      disabled people                          w_d1
    VERDICT: STRUCTURAL_SIGNATURE
      present in all 4 classes with disjoint vocabulary. The shape
      recurs and the words do not, which is what separates a structural
      signature from a shared literature

  SHAPE FX_2
    fixture shape, all four classes, one shared word
    bindings:              4 of 4
    vocabulary disjoint:   False
    shared words:          w_common
    per binding:
      women                                    w_a2, w_common
      enslaved and formerly enslaved people    w_common
      ethnic groups typed as lower             w_c2, w_common
      disabled people                          w_common
    VERDICT: SHARED_VOCABULARY
      present in all 4 classes and the vocabulary overlaps: w_common.
      Shared words are consistent with four literatures being one,
      which is the hypothesis the extension exists to rule out

  SHAPE FX_3
    fixture shape, two classes only, disjoint
    bindings:              2 of 4
    vocabulary disjoint:   True
    per binding:
      women                                    w_a1
      enslaved and formerly enslaved people    w_b1
      ethnic groups typed as lower             (checked, none)
      disabled people                          (checked, none)
    VERDICT: NOT_ESTABLISHED
      present in 2 of 4 classes. The extension's claim is recurrence
      across all four

  SHAPE FX_4
    fixture shape, all present but two classes never checked
    bindings:              2 of 4
    vocabulary disjoint:   True
    NOT CHECKED:           2 class(es)
    per binding:
      women                                    w_a1
      enslaved and formerly enslaved people    w_b1
      ethnic groups typed as lower             NOT_CHECKED
      disabled people                          NOT_CHECKED
    VERDICT: NOT_ESTABLISHED
      2 of 4 subject classes were not checked (disabled people, ethnic
      groups typed as lower). Presence in the rest cannot establish
      recurrence across all four

  GRADE: OK   distinct verdicts: 3

  FX_1 and FX_2 differ only in whether one word is shared across
  bindings. A test that counts bindings returns the same verdict for
  both, and the difference between them is the whole extension

------------------------------------------------------------------------

  CONFIDENCE, reported separately and not resolved
    disjointness
      treated as a requirement, on the method's own words. A shape
      recurring in shared vocabulary is reported as weaker, not as
      absent
    resolved
      False
    the_format
      INFERRED from scope-bound-shapes, where a shape is defined by its
      structure and not its materials. No shape-index format exists in
      this repo, so if the delivered one differs this is the wrong file
      shape and the contents transfer
    the_grade
      over four synthetic fixtures that separate three verdicts. It
      grades the test, not the extension
    the_index
      empty of real shapes. A taxonomy comes from the corpus and none
      is acquired
    unchecked_classes
      a class never checked is never counted as absent. FX_4 exists to
      hold that case

  WHERE IT BREAKS
    - FULL PRESENCE WITH SHARED VOCABULARY IS WEAKER THAN PARTIAL
    PRESENCE WITH DISJOINT VOCABULARY, AND A BINDING COUNT LOSES THAT.
    FX_1 and FX_2 both appear in all four classes and differ only in
    whether one word is common across them. A test that counts bindings
    returns four-of-four for both. Shared words are consistent with the
    four literatures being one literature -- quotation, a shared
    source, a single tradition restating itself -- which is precisely
    the hypothesis the extension was added to rule out, so the shared
    case returns SHARED_VOCABULARY and not a signature
    - THE INDEX IS EMPTY AND THAT IS THE SIZE OF THE WORK DONE, NOT A
    FINDING. Zero shapes recorded does not mean no shape recurs across
    the four classes. It means no corpus has been read. Naming the
    shapes from here would be easy and would be this module writing the
    finding and then confirming it against its own fixtures
    - THE OUTPUT FORMAT IS AN INFERENCE. The delivered instruction
    names a shape-index format that does not exist in this repository.
    The format used is built from scope-bound-shapes' distinction
    between a shape and its materials, which fits the extension's own
    same-shape-different-vocabulary claim closely. It is still a guess
    at what was asked for, and it is not imported from that module
    because its Binding is a LIVE/FROZEN variable list and forcing a
    predicate shape into it would fit the object to the nearest
    available class
    - A CLASS NEVER CHECKED IS NOT A CLASS FOUND EMPTY, AND THE ENTRY
    SCHEMA IS THE ONLY THING ENFORCING IT. ShapeEntry refuses a
    realisation for a class not in checked_classes, and reports
    unchecked classes separately from absent ones. Nothing stops a
    caller declaring all four checked when two were skimmed -- the
    field records a claim about what was examined, not the examination
    - THE FOUR SUBJECT CLASSES ARE CARRIED AS DELIVERED AND ARE NOT
    PARTITIONS OF ANYTHING. They overlap: a disabled woman is in two,
    and the classes as named are not defined against a stated reference
    class here at all. The predicate difference needs a reference class
    per comparison, and which reference each of the four is measured
    against is not settled by this index
