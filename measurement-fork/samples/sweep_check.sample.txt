
SWEEP: WHICH VARIABLE, AT HOW MANY LEVELS
subject: the delivered arms and the newly specified K11-K16

========================================================================
1  the delivered probes against the new rule
========================================================================
  34 probes emitted by the three arms, 17 of them measuring
  (the widen arm emits design-directed options, not quantities --
  MF_008 / MF_016 -- so the rule does not apply to it)

  measuring probes carrying a `sweep` field: 0 of 17

  quantities.probe() has these fields:
      arm  id  quantity  protocol  reads  blind_to

  `sweep` is not among them, so the rule is not expressible in
  the delivered schema and every measuring probe fails it. That
  is a schema gap, not 17 separate oversights.

  Why it is load-bearing rather than tidy: the spec's own
  falsifiers are statements about a gradient.

      regime variable declared in the spec: provisioning level
      open question: confidence accuracy gap
      open question: act consequence latency
      open question: response magnitude per perturbation size

  A probe run at one setting of the control parameter cannot
  participate in a claim about a gradient. So the missing field
  and MF_010's unreachable falsifier are one gap seen twice.

2  the newly specified probes against the same rule
========================================================================
  id     quantity               sweep                          levels   rule
  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  K11    information_rate       regime.variable                2        PASS
  K12    reliance_weight        regime.variable                2        PASS

  K16    detection_latency      baseline_staleness             3        PASS

  6 of 6 pass.

  The spec's regime variable is 'provisioning level', which normalizes to 'provisioning_level'.
  Resolving the default against it:

      K11    regime.variable                -> provisioning_level
      K12    regime.variable                -> provisioning_level
      K13    provisioning_level             
      K14    provisioning_level             
      K15    time_since_clean_reference     
      K16    baseline_staleness             

  3 distinct sweep variables after resolution, and 4 of 6
  probes sweep the regime variable -- 2 by default and 2 by
  naming it directly. So the field carries information on 2 of 6,
  not 4 of 6, and K13/K14 spelling out the default is a
  redundancy the schema should collapse rather than a choice.

  K15 and K16 sweep at 3 levels rather than 2. Both are on the
  mediation chain, and 2 levels gives a slope with no curvature
  and no way to see a threshold. That is the min being a MINIMUM
  and the probe declaring above it.

========================================================================
3  K13 closes the gap MF_010 named
========================================================================
  MF_010 adjudicated `reversibility after regime shift` as
  unreached, and named the shape of the missing instrument:

      the predicted contrast is a RATE -- fast vs slow relearn
      once the buffer is removed -- and no K-probe returns a
      rate; every one measures a level, ratio, slope or variance
      at fixed regime.

  MF_014 then moved it to PARTIAL on K14: K14 supplied the
  provisioning gradient the stated falsifier needed, but nothing
  measured relearn rate AFTER the buffer is removed.

  K13:

      base       tau
      protocol   error vs trials-since-shift, fit tau
      sweep      provisioning_level at 2 levels
      returns    time constant

  `error vs trials-since-shift` is measured across a regime
  change by construction -- trials-since-shift has no meaning
  without one -- and fitting tau returns a time constant. Swept
  across provisioning level, it supplies both halves the stated
  falsifier needs: the rate, and the gradient to read it against.

  Verdict change: PARTIAL -> CLOSED.

  The prediction shipped with it is falsifiable in the direction
  that costs something: tau RISES with provisioning, and a flat
  tau falsifies. A flat tau is the null and it is reachable, so
  this is not the ../null-harness/ CONSTANT_SILENT shape.

  K12 carries the sharpest single line in the batch:

      Trust is a measurement only if (b) was run.

  which is `whether trust in own sensing is a measurement or a
  belief` -- the gap MF_014 closed on K15 -- restated as a
  precondition on reading K12 at all, rather than as a separate
  probe. Two independent routes to the same distinction.

========================================================================
4  what is still open
========================================================================
  coupling bandwidth

    MF_014 left this OPEN on the grounds that rate-of-use,
    staleness and latency are three quantities and capacity is a
    fourth. K11 `information_rate` is the fourth:

      distinguishable environmental states registered per unit
      time, by the actor's own sensors

    and it is explicitly marked `not_` against K01 delay and K02
    reliability -- 'This is channel capacity.'

    Verdict change: OPEN -> CLOSED.

    Its stated blind spot is the honest one: 'whether anything is
    done with the states'. Capacity measured, use not measured,
    and the probe says so.

  So all three gaps MF_010 named are now reached. What is NOT
  reached is anything that was measured: every probe here is a
  specification, the mediation lags are still ordinal (MF_015),
  and the `sweep` field is not in `quantities.probe()`.

========================================================================
READING
========================================================================

  `sweep` is a schema addition with teeth, and no delivered probe
  satisfies it -- because `quantities.probe()` has no such field, so the
  rule is not expressible in the schema the arms were written against.
  One gap, not N oversights.

  It is load-bearing rather than tidy because the spec's falsifiers are
  statements about gradients, and a probe run at one setting of the
  control parameter cannot participate in one. The missing field and
  MF_010's unreachable falsifier are the same gap from two sides.

  Of the six new probes, 2 of 6 use the default sweep variable, so the
  field carries information rather than restating a default. K15 and K16
  declare 3 levels rather than the minimum 2, which is what a minimum
  looks like when it is working.

  K13 `tau` closes `reversibility after regime shift`: error against
  trials-since-shift is measured across a regime change by construction,
  fitting tau returns a rate, and sweeping provisioning supplies the
  gradient the stated falsifier needs. K11 closes `coupling bandwidth` as
  the capacity term the other three were not. All three of MF_010's gaps
  are now reached -- as specifications. Nothing has been run.
