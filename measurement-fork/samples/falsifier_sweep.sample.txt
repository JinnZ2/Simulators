========================================================================
3  the check cannot be run from the spec
========================================================================
  spec fields: actors, boundary, currently_measured, description, known_latency, open_questions, provisioned, regime, system_id, test_items_source

  `falsifiers` is not among them.

  So the requirement in the PROBES_K11_K18.py header --

      compare.py must flag any falsifier whose terms are not
      swept by any arm

  needs two fields that do not exist. `sweep` on a probe
  (MF_017), and `falsifiers` on a spec. The four falsifiers in
  section 2 are HAND-TRANSCRIBED from prose and from the
  `predicted=` lines of individual probes.

  K13 makes the gap concrete: it declares

      closes=['reversibility', 'falsifier:ratio_flat']

  and `falsifier:ratio_flat` resolves to nothing in any
  delivered file. It is a reference to a registry that has not
  been created. Once it exists, section 2 becomes mechanical and
  this hand transcription goes away.

  The two gaps are the same gap seen at two levels: a probe
  cannot say what it must be run across, and a spec cannot say
  what would refute it. Between them, a generator can emit a
  complete, well-formed design that is incapable of failing.

========================================================================
4  the smallest schema that makes this mechanical
========================================================================
  On the probe, one field (MF_017's):

      sweep = (spec_variable, n_levels)   # or None + a reason

  On the spec, one list:

      "falsifiers": [
        {"id": "ratio_flat",
         "statement": "ratio flat across the provisioning gradient",
         "terms": ["provisioning level"]}
      ]

  Then the check is three lines: for each falsifier, is every
  term in the union of the arms' swept variables. No prose, no
  transcription, no adjudication.

  And it is a PRE-stage check, not a post one -- it decides
  whether the design can fail before anything is measured, which
  is the ../reasoning-gate/ G-CTRL shape: controls sized by
  fragility, declared before the run.

========================================================================
READING
========================================================================

  The delivered arms sweep nothing, so 0 of 4 stated falsifiers are
  reachable by any probe they emit. That is not a badly written probe --
  `quantities.probe()` has no `sweep` field, so the generator cannot emit
  a swept design at all.

  The K11-K16 batch sweeps four variables and makes 4 of 4 reachable.

  The check the drop asks compare.py to run cannot be run from the spec,
  because the spec has no `falsifiers` field. K13 declares
  closes=['falsifier:ratio_flat'] and that tag resolves to nothing in any
  delivered file -- a reference to a registry that does not exist yet. The
  four falsifiers checked here are hand-transcribed from prose.

  Two schema gaps, one shape: a probe cannot say what it must be run
  across, and a spec cannot say what would refute it. Between them a
  generator can emit a complete, well-formed design that is incapable of
  failing, which is what happened.
