  Three open questions no measuring arm reaches:

  [NO PROBE] coupling bandwidth
             no probe. latency and contingency_consistency measure delay and reliability of the loop; neither measures how much can cross it per unit time

  [NO PROBE] whether trust in own sensing is a measurement or a belief
             no probe. confidence/accuracy reaches the confidence-validity gap but not whether the organism's reliance on its own sensor was ever validated against outcome

             no probe. Nothing measures relearn RATE after the buffer is removed, which is the quantity that separates the two predicted trajectories

  'reversibility after regime shift' is the one with a stated
  prediction attached and no instrument. The predicted contrast
  is a RATE -- fast relearn against slow relearn once the buffer
  is removed -- and no probe in the coupling arm measures a rate.
  Every K-probe measures a level, a ratio, a slope or a variance,
  all at fixed regime. The falsifier named in the notes ('ratio
  flat across the provisioning gradient') needs the gradient
  swept; the probes as generated sit at one point on it.

  'reversibility after regime shift' and simultaneously supplies the
  gradient the stated falsifier needs.
