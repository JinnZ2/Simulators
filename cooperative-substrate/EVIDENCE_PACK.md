## E4. SELECTION HISTORY — the load-bearing methodological claim

This is the single claim to carry if only one is carried.

**Target papers:**
- "Obligate mutualistic cooperation limits evolvability" — *Nat Comms* 10.1038/s41467-021-27630-9
- "Mutualism breakdown underpins evolutionary rescue in an obligate cross-feeding bacterial consortium" — *Nat Comms* 10.1038/s41467-025-58742-1

**The construct:** engineered E. coli auxotrophs, biosynthesis pathway deleted, cross-feeding amino acids, **one fixed partner each**, monoculture vs coculture, adaptation scored as growth under stepwise antibiotic. Control is a **prototroph**.

**The confound, stated precisely:**
> The comparison is *coupled-and-unstabilised* vs *autonomous-and-selected*. **Selection history is not held constant.** The result cannot distinguish "coupling costs flexibility" from "recent unvetted coupling costs flexibility."

**Missing arm:** a coupled consortium *with* selection history, or an autonomous strain with an equivalently recent engineered change.

**Also note:** partner substitution is not in the option set. Bypassing the partner is scored as rescue; switching partners cannot be scored because it cannot occur.

### The field has already named the confound

| # | Finding | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| E4.1 | Cross-feeding strains may be maladaptive to each other at the start — competing for non-exchanged metabolites, secreting and taking up inefficiently — **especially when the consortium is constructed from two engineered strains without any coevolutionary history.** | Luo et al. 2025, *Environ Microbiol Rep* 10.1111/1758-2229.70175 (citing Harcombe 2010; LaSarre 2017; Vidal & Segraves 2021) | `[UNVERIFIED-FULLTEXT]` — **verify this one first, it carries the argument** |
| E4.2 | Synthetic leucine/lysine E. coli auxotroph consortium **gained** growth rate and biomass yield after coevolution. | Zhang et al. | `[UNVERIFIED-FULLTEXT]` |
| E4.3 | Model-designed syntrophic co-cultures began at near-zero cooperativity; all three combinations evolved viable syntrophy over ~40 days of ALE. | *PLOS Comp Biol* 10.1371/journal.pcbi.1006213 | `[UNVERIFIED-FULLTEXT]` |
| E4.4 | Interkingdom community evolved enhanced entanglement including **de novo** interdependence for nitrogen metabolism, via pleiotropy and trade-offs, with no group selection. | *Nat Comms* 10.1038/s41467-024-51702-1 | `[UNVERIFIED-FULLTEXT]` |
| E4.5 | Field-acknowledged contradiction: some auxotrophs fail to compensate unless genetically modified to increase metabolite export, while others spontaneously establish stable syntrophy. | *ISME Comms* 10.1093/ismeco/ycaf011 | `[UNVERIFIED-FULLTEXT]` |

**Conclusion this supports:** coupling performance **improves** with selection history. The evolvability result was measured at the worst point on that curve — day zero, pre-coevolution — then generalised as a property of mutualism as such. E4.5's contradiction resolves as selection history plus partner availability, not mutualism per se.

## E6. THE EXPERIMENT NOBODY APPEARS TO HAVE RUN

**A stress gradient with partner substitution available.**

Both constructs jointly imply it; neither ran it. Required design elements:
- partner pool as an explicit environmental variable (not a fixed single partner)
- switching in the option set, so it is scoreable
- **selection history as a controlled arm** — this is what E4 says is missing everywhere

This is the second candidate build target in the work order.

## E7. CROSS-SUBSTRATE TERM SET (for whatever table gets built)

All terms gradeable; none requires a deliberate author; none requires anyone's interior state.

```
1. cost asymmetry
2. whether the aggregate steers (incentive direction)
3. what sits inside vs outside the accounting boundary
4. whether a legitimate other is representable at all
5. does the accounting stance PRESERVE OR DESTROY the measurement it depends on
```

Substrates: foraging/predation ecology · multiagent AI harnesses · human societies and mutual aid · morality/ethics claims · nation-state sovereignty.

Per cell record: **MEASURED**, **MISSING**, or **acts differently because of something in the scope**. The gaps are the payoff — a term well measured in one substrate and unmeasured in another is an experiment sitting there.

Worked example: cost asymmetry is well measured in optimal-foraging work (E1.1–E1.3) and appears unmeasured in multiagent harnesses (E2.1–E2.3).

**Discriminator against self-deception:** a real shared structure makes transferable **quantitative** predictions across substrates. A projected frame produces only analogies that stop at the vocabulary. If a cross-substrate term produces no measurable prediction in the second substrate, it is the second kind.

## E8. WHAT THIS PACK DOES NOT ESTABLISH

- Nothing about interior states, in any substrate. The argument runs on derivation evidence, energy accounting, and dependency structure alone.
- No moral claim. Terms used are incentive direction, cost asymmetry, and whether the aggregate steers — all measurable without anyone's interior, and explicitly not a conspiracy claim.
- No claim that competition never describes anything. It describes the benign-environment case (E0.1) and it describes some contested-resource intervals. The claim is one of **coverage**: it is a narrow subset presented as the whole.
