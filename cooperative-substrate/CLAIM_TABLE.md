## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

A falsifier that fires updates the claim's status in place with the
evidence that fired it; the checks in `selftest_csp.py` and
`v2/selftest_v2.py` are not loosened to keep a claim. Delivered text
(`WORK_ORDER.md`, `WORK_ORDER_V2.md`, the two verbatim blocks in the
README, the contract strings in `p2_substrate_audit.py`, and the
truncated `v2/p4_goal.py`) is not edited. A repair to a shipped check
is recorded as a claim (`CSP_008`) rather than folded in silently.
