## REFUTATION_PROTOCOL

A falsifier that fires updates the claim's status in place with the
evidence that fired it; the checks in `selftest_csp.py` are not
loosened to keep a claim. Delivered text (`WORK_ORDER.md`, the two
verbatim blocks in the README, the contract strings in
`p2_substrate_audit.py`) is not edited. A repair to a shipped check is
recorded as a claim (`CSP_008`) rather than folded in silently.
