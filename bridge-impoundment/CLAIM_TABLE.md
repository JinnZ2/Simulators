## BI_005 — the gain sentence holds at the operator level, and only there

*"It stores and re-releases it, with gain… a rate with gain, not a
static offset."* Conservation arithmetic, no hydraulics: a span that
stores inflow for the accumulation time and releases the stored volume
over the release time has peak-outflow gain equal to their ratio —
above one exactly when the release is faster than the accumulation,
which is what a span giving way is; and a slow release attenuates,
so the negative branch is reachable. The debris budget is a gain of at
least one by construction (arriving load plus the span's own),
with equality only when the structure contributes nothing. Both are
properties of storage-and-release, not of any bridge; the event
coupling the drop names (the jam grows during the event, the carried
ASCE 2024 finding) is what makes the accumulation time event-scaled
rather than a design constant.

**Falsifier:** a storage-and-release configuration violating either
inequality. Mass conservation is the whole derivation.
